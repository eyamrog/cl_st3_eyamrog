Measurement of emerging contaminants requires advanced laboratory capabilities. Solid phase
extraction with Oasis HLB cartridges has recently been the most
common method of extraction and concentration for emerging contaminants in
water. Solventless extraction techniques, such as solid phase microextraction, and
dispersive liquid-liquid microextraction (which uses very little water, ie, ~10
mL) are increasingly being used (Richardson & Kimura, 2016 ).
Liquid chromatography and high-resolution-mass spectrometry, as well as complementary analytical
techniques such as nuclear magnetic resonance spectroscopy, have also been
growing in use for identifying unknown contaminants (Richardson & Kimura,
2016 ).
