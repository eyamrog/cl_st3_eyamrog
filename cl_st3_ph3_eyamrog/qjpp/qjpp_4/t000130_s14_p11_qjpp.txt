For protein biotinylation in HEK293 cells, protein constructs in pHLsec
C-terminal Avi-tag were co transfected with a vector encoding BirA
(biotin ligase). Cell culture medium was supplemented with 100mM biotin.
Proteins were purified as previously, but in ice-cold conditions. For
the nanobody-streptavidin complexes, streptavidin (Streptavidin-Alexa Fluor 594 conjugate, Thermo Fisher
Scientific, or Streptavidin from Streptomyces avidinii , Sigma-Aldrich) was mixed
with an excess of biotinylated nanobodies, incubated overnight at 4°C
and subjected to size exclusion chromatography using Superdex 200 16/60
(GE Healthcare) in 20 mM Tris-HCl (pH 7.5) and 200
mM NaCl.
