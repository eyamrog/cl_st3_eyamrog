Methods: SHARE, a pan-European study, collected 24 000 DBS samples
in 12 countries in its sixth wave. Our new algorithm
uses photographs of the DBS samples to calculate the number
of pixels of the blood-covered area to measure the spot
sizes accurately. We ran regression models to examine the association
of spot size and seven DBS analytes. We then compared
the application of our new spot-size measures to common spot-size
estimation.
