Here, we present data from a pilot study of salivary
DHEA-S levels among Maya girls from Merida, Mexico. The Maya
have experienced long-standing marginalization and adversity, and rank among the
lowest socioeconomic ethnic groups in Mexico (Azcorra et al., 2013
). Despite improvements in living conditions, many children are stunted
(Wilson, 2011 ) with recent experience of the dual burden
of malnutrition (Azcorra et al., 2013 ; Wilson, 2011 ).
Since adrenarcheal age is linked to timing of the BMI
rebound (Remer & Manz, 1999 ), we hypothesized an earlier
adrenarche compared to industrialized populations.
