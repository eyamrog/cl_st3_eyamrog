This measure is the best field-friendly, noninvasive hydration biomarker because
Usg is much easier to measure in the field (Armstrong,
2007 ). It has been shown to work well in
human biology research in remote settings (Rosinger, 2015a , 2015b
, 2018 ). This makes Usg particularly useful for field-based
research on the impact of unmet human water needs on
human health and biology.
