The BMI values of participants were compared to the normative
values of provided by the World Health Organization (WHO, 2007
). Our P50 values ranged between normative P50 and P75
matching for the same gender and age: BMI of our
participants was therefore higher than this reference. We further classified
the children according to the obesity cut-off norms of P97
from the WHO reference (Valerio et al., 2018 ). As
a result, 24.76% of children in our sample had obesity.
