Absorbance was recorded in 1-cm quartz cuvettes on a nanophotometer
np80 (Implen) spectrophotometer. Cu 2+ stock solution was prepared by
dissolving copper sulfate in ultrapure water. Freshly prepared L-ascorbic acid
(Sigma A0278) was added at a final concentration of 120
μM to buffered 8-μM or 4-μM Cu 2+ -protein solutions,
as indicated in the figure for each experiment. Buffer was
50 mM MOPS, pH 7.0, 100 mM NaCl.
