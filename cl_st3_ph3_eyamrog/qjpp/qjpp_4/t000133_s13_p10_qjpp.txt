Urine specific gravity can also be measured on reagent strips.
While this method of measuring Usg is highly correlated to
Usg using refractometers, it is more likely to yield false
negatives when applying a euhydration cutoff (Usg < 1.020 g/mL).
Therefore, refractometry is recommended (Abbey, Heelan, Brown, & Bartee, 2014
).
