The grapho-motor test was performed on a tablet PC, carried
out following an established procedure (Giammarco et al., 2016 )
which involved a blank page with a square and a
diamond represented in four gray segments shown on a tablet
screen (Samsung Galaxy note, 10.1 in., 1280 × 800). The
software was developed by the authors of the cited study
(Giammarco et al., 2016 ) and got by them, who
also positively tested the retest reliability with Primary school children.
The size of the square and diamond were large enough
to cover the entire screen of the tablet (see the
original article for a deeper description and images). Children were
supposed to redraw the shapes with a digital pen.
