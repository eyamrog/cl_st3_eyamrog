Informed written consent was taken from all participants in this
study, and their guardians if they were aged <18 years.
Research ethics approval was obtained from the Nepal Health Research
Council (NHRC) (108/2012) and the University College London (UCL) Ethical
Review Committee (4198/001) for primary data collection, and from the
NHRC (292/2018) and the UCL Ethical Review Committee (0326/015) for
the secondary analyses presented here.
