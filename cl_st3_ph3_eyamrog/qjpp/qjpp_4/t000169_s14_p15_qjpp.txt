Since A and C markers need two punches for analysis,
they could come from different spots. In these cases, we
used the average size of the two spots. If there
was no suitable spot available for the analysis of all
A markers, we used one punch only and restricted the
analysis to HbA1c, referring to it as A* marker. Knowledge
of the punching scheme is therefore important. For each spot,
the dates and the times of punching are stored in
the file names of the images. It allows us to
establish a chronological order of punches for a sample and
to match each punch (and its size) to the respective
marker set.
