Between 2000 and 2006, the results scored by university students
in speed/agility tests (excluding the zig-zag run) improved by 0.52
claps/year (1.7%) in the 8-s—SHC test, and by −0.03 s/year
(0.3%) in the 4x10 m shuttle run on average. Between
2006 and 2018, the results scored by the evaluated subjects
in the 8-s—SHC, 4x10 m shuttle run and the zig-zag
run decreased by −0.31 claps (1.1%), 0.05 s (0.4%) and
0.10 s (0.4%) per year, respectively. The corresponding differences between
the minimum and maximum values were determined at −3.7 claps,
0.58 s and 1.18 s.
