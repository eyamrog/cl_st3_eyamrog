The links between graphonomic outcomes, other fine motor skills, fitness,
and anthropometric status, as well as perceived competence, were addressed
with network models, using correlations as estimators. Further network analyses
were conducted with TPB , TPF , Shuttle , Floppy
, Thumb , BMI and WtHR , splitting by age
and gender. PMSC-2 parameters were excluded from the split networks
because of the smaller sample sizes of the subgroups. For
the details of network analysis, refer to the figure legends.
