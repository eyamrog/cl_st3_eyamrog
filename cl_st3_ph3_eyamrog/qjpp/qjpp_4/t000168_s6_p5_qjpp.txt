Although holidays are unlikely to provide a complete explanation of
seasonality, they have been associated with increased sexual activity in
other studies (Russell et al., 1993 ). In England and
Wales, the trends point consistently to an increase in sexual
activity occurring at or around the Christmas period, and a
longer but less pronounced subsidiary period of increased sexual activity
coinciding with the summer vacation (Wellings et al., 1999 ).
A persistent September local peak in births has been observed
in northern populations (Lam & Miron, 1994 ) which conforms
to long suspected seasonal conceptions in December (Lerchl et al.,
1993 ). More recently it has been stated that conception
dates vary mostly due to cultural factors, such as holidays
(Wood et al., 2017 ).
