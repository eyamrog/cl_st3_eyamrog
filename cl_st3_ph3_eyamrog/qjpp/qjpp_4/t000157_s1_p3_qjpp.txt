Results: Stature in the Holocene prehistory of the Near East
and Europe varied with maxima for women usually ranging below
160 cm, and maxima for men between 165 and 170
cm. Stature never rose above 170 cm. European and white
US-American schoolchildren of the 19th and 20th century were generally
short with average height ranging between −1.5 and −2.2 hSDS,
yet in the absence of any evidence of chronic or
recurrent undernutrition or frequent illness, poverty, or disadvantageous living conditions.
The same is found in contemporary Indonesian schoolchildren.
