This study used data from participants examined as part of
2 waves of the U.S. National Health And Nutrition Examination
Survey (NHANES) in 2011–2012 and 2013–2014. Participants were included if
handedness and testosterone data were available and they were aged
18 and over. Women were only included if they reported
having a menstrual period in the last 12 months. This
excludes post-menopausal women and women who have had hysterectomies.
