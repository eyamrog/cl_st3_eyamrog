Participants wore an activPAL (AP) (PAL Technologies Ltd, Glasgow, UK)
on the left thigh and an ActiGraph GT3X+, (AG) (ActiGraph
LLC, Fort Walton Beach, FL) on the left hip for
a continuous 7-day period, including while asleep, only removing the
devices when coming into direct contact with water (i.e., bathing
or swimming). AP is a uni-axial monitor containing an inclinometer
to determine limb position. Sampling rate was 20 Hz, and
data were downloaded in 15-s epochs (activPAL3™ software version 7.2.32).
The AG device is a triaxial accelerometer, generating activity counts
for each axis and a vector magnitude representing the combination
of all three axes. In the current study, the data
were collected at a frequency of 80 Hz. Details regarding
the AG device have been published elsewhere (Barreira et al.,
2018 ).
