Mean and standard deviation of the raw DBS values for
the SHARE DBS sample are presented in Table 3 .
These raw values are not comparable to standard blood values
derived from routine laboratory assays of venous blood draws. This
has several reasons. First, the raw values have not been
converted to venous standard values according to the conversion formulae
(Börsch-Supan et al., 2021 ). Second, they are based on
a non-fasting state, which is particularly important for the interpretation
of triglyceride and total cholesterol values. For TG (76 obs.)
and CRP (52 obs.) we removed physiologically not meaningful values.
The rightmost columns show the intra- and inter-assay variability measured
as coefficient of variation (CV).
