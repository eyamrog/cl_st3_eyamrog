Drying time (minutes): Interviewers were instructed to let the blood
sample dry until the end of the interview but at
least 15 min. This followed the protocol that was used
at that time by the HRS (Crimmins et al., 2015
). As an estimation of the drying time, we used
the interview length between the blood spot module, programmed in
computer-assisted personal interviewing (CAPI), and the end of the interview
(or the time until the end of the interview session
if the interview was conducted in more than one session
on several days).
