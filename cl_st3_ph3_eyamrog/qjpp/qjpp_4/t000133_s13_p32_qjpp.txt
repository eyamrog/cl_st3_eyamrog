Hydration strategies provide a way to examine how much water
is coming from different dietary components, like plain water, alcohol,
sugar-sweetened beverages, foods, and so forth (Rosinger & Herrick, 2016
). Additionally, conducting multiple 24-hour dietary recalls over different seasons
enables an examination of how total water intake shifts over
the year as the weather changes and dietary availability of
some items change (Tani et al., 2015 ). In terms
of cost, the time it takes to conduct the interviews
and convert dietary data into water is the greatest limitation
to this method.
