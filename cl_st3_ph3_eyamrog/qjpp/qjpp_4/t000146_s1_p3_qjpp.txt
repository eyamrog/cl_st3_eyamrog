Results: The students evaluated in 2018 were 1.7 cm taller
than their peers tested in 2000. Body mass and BMI
values continued to decrease between 2000 and 2006 (by 0.46
kg and 0.15 kg/m 2 per year on average), whereas
a steady and significant increase in both parameters was observed
between 2006 and 2018 (by 0.45 kg and 0.12 kg/m
2 per year on average). The results of motor tests
were strongly correlated with body mass and BMI, and they
continued to improve until 2006, after which a steady decline
was observed up to 2018 when the students scored lowest
in motor tests.
