Body mass values were lowest in 2006 (74.4 kg), after
which they increased steadily to reach 79.7 kg in 2018.
The difference between the minimum and maximum body mass values
was determined at 5.4 kg. On average, body mass decreased
by 0.5 kg/year (0.5%) between 2000 and 2006, and it
increased by 0.5 kg/year (0.6%) between 2006 and 2018. Similar
trends were noted in BMI values which decreased by 0.2
kg/m 2 /year (0.7%) between 2000 and 2006, and increased
by 0.1 kg/m 2 /year (0.5%) between 2006 and 2018
on average. BMI values were within the norm in all
years of the study, and the difference between the minimum
and maximum values was determined at 1.5 kg/m 2 .
