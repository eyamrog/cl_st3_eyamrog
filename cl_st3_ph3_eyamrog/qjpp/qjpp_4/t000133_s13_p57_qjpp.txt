Affordability is another key dimension of water insecurity. Common metrics
for estimating household-level affordability include: (a) water expenditures as a
share of household income, and in low-income countries the affordability
threshold is between 3% and 6%; (b) cost of equipment
needed to access water; (c) financial and economic costs as
a percentage of annual income, and in low-income countries, it
is calculated as 5% or less of the community's median
household income (Hutton, 2012 ). Where water bills are unavailable,
water affordability data are often collected through self-report surveys and
are vulnerable to recall errors and other reporting biases.
