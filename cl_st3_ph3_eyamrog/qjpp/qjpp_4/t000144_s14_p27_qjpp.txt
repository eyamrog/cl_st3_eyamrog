As all models reported here use a logit link function
and are Bayesian, we report results using odds ratios (ORs)
and 90% credible intervals (CIs). We explain how ORs and
CIs work in Supporting Information 3.6 . Briefly, we use
90% CIs because (a) CIs are typically wider than confidence
intervals, as Bayesian models better account for uncertainty than frequentist
models, and (b) the 95% criterion can be unstable for
CIs, requiring extensive sampling during the model-fitting process (Kruschke, 2014
). As the OR estimate for intercepts—including the thresholds for
transitioning from zero to one long-distance relationship, one to two,
and so forth—do not have a straightforward interpretation, we do
not report these here.
