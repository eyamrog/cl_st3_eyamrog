In March to June 2017, A. P. interviewed 119 participants
about their migration histories, long-distance relationships, same-community relationships, household possessions,
self-described extraversion, and demographics. Given variable literacy across participants but
familiarity with signing documents, consent forms were read aloud and
participants provided written (ie, signed) consent. All field protocols were
approved by the Max Planck Institute for Evolutionary Anthropology Department
of Human Behavior, Ecology, and Culture, and declared exempt from
additional IRB oversight. More details on data collection and on
the limitations of the predictors described appear in the Supporting
Information .
