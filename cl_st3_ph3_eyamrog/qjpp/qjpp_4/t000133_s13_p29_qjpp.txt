While DLW allows for an assessment of water throughput, it
does not distinguish what people are actually consuming to meet
their water needs. As many studies in human biology show,
diet is intimately connected to environmental resources, market resources, and
globalization (Dufour, Bender, & Reina, 2015 ; Piperata et al.,
2011 ; Sorensen et al., 2005 ). Dietary recall analyses
reveal many important facets of inquiry to human biology research,
including insight into population level snapshots and trends of water
intake, dietary exposures and adaptations, and health outcomes (Leonard, 2012
; Popkin et al., 2010 ).
