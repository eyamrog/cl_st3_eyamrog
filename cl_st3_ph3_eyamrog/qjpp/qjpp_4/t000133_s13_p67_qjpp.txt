The science of measuring water needs has developed unevenly. Measurements
of hydration and water quality have been well-established for decades,
though there is a significant need for new methods of
water quality measurement for contaminants of emerging concern. In contrast,
the measurement of household water insecurity is a largely new
field, with only a few metrics and little overall agreement
over which are best to apply under what conditions. The
expanding range of measures currently available allows new areas of
research to begin opening up. Table 4 contains useful resources
for further review.
