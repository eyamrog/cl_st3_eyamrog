Individuals from St. Mary Spital Period 17 (c.1400–1539) and St.
Mary Graces are included in the post-Black Death sample. St.
Mary Graces is a burial ground associated with the Cistercian
abbey of St Mary Graces, in operation soon after the
Black Death ended in 1350 until the Reformation in 1538
(Grainger & Phillpotts, 2011 ). Burials in this cemetery include
lay individuals of both high and low status as well
as monks.
