We obtained data of body height, weight, and skinfold thickness
(subscapular, triceps) of 1666 school children aged 5.8–13.9 (mean 9.5)
years in two separate studies, 2018 and 2020 (Scheffler et
al., 2019 ; Scheffler et al., 2021 ). The data
were obtained under standardized conditions (Knussmann, 1988 ).
