Age-associated variation in cycle biomarkers has been reported for some,
but not all, studies. Such age dependency reflects, in part,
the inclusion in some studies of teenagers, peri-menopausal women, and
cycles of unknown ovulation status. Because the samples for our
study comprised only adults during their peak reproductive years (21–41
years old) and only ovulatory cycles were included in analyses,
the absence in both our study samples of a significant
association between age and cycle biomarkers is not surprising.
