One important caveat about antigen stimulation assays is to remember
that the assays measure the response to stimulation, but not
how effective that response is in fighting the infection. Cytokines
are signaling molecules and measuring them is equivalent to measuring
how loud the immune system yells when it feels threatened.
Indeed, in some cases strong inflammatory responses may be detrimental
or represent a failure of specific responses, so care in
interpretation is needed.
