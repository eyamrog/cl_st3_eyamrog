To test our hypotheses, we used data from our studies
of German and rural Bolivian women, chosen because Germany is
a wealthy industrialized country with universal health care and Bolivia's
rural population is resource poor with, at the time of
the study, among the highest fertility and mortality rates in
the Western hemisphere. Also, as noted earlier, the longitudinal study
design in Project REPA reduced selection bias, thereby producing a
study sample of cycles representative of this population.
