First, plasma or serum osmolality (Sosm) is viewed by some
as the best marker of bodily hydration status, which is
buffered against acute changes in water intake (Cheuvront & Sawka,
2005 ). Serum osmolality is not as influenced by recent
food and water intake or physical exertion as Uosm. It
can therefore serve as a more stable indicator of longer-term
hydration status.
