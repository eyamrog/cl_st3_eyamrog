Flow cytometry is used to analyze the relative proportions of
different white blood cells by tagging cells with fluorescently marked
antibodies for specific cell-surface (or sometimes interior) proteins. Cells are
suspended in a salt-based buffer and single cells are then
rapidly analyzed as they flow past a single (or set
of) laser(s). Cells are analyzed based on the presence or
absence of each tag, and the visible light scatter, which
roughly corresponds to size and shape of the cell.
