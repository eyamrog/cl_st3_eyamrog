Coefficient of variance ( V *) values are fairly consistent
across time periods for the tibial and femoral lengths. Considering
tibial lengths, estimated females have a larger coefficient of variance
than their male counterparts for all time periods, however the
value of the coefficient of variance for males fluctuates more
across the three time periods. For femoral lengths, females also
tend to have a larger V * value, though this
pattern does not hold for the early pre-Black Death period.
The fluctuation in coefficient of variance values is also greater
for estimated females across the three time periods than estimated
males for the femoral length data.
