Several studies have found that month of birth is frequently
associated with later outcomes in life such as mortality (Ueda
et al., 2013 ), lifespan (Doblhammer & Vaupel, 2001 ),
number of live-born children (Huber et al., 2004 ), health
(Lokshin & Radyakin, 2009 ; Reffelmann et al., 2011 ),
and childhood disease dynamics (Martinez-Bakker et al., 2014 ).
