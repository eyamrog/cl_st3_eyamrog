Measures related to immunosenescence may also be useful for comparing
individuals and making life history inferences. Naïve cell counts and
sjTREC may be useful in this regard, as well as
counts of senescent T cells (Franceschi et al., 2000 ).
Methylation assays have been used to develop a so-called “epigenetic
clock” related to immune aging (Horvath et al., 2016 ).
