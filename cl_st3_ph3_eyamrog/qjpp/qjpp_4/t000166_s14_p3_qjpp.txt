We randomly selected a subgroup of seven school classes to
administer the Pictorial Scale of Perceived Movement Skill Competence-2 (PMSC-2;
Barnett, Robinson, et al., 2015 ). This was due to
time and logistic constraints, Thus, only a subsample of n
= 95 children (42% of the first grade and 58%
of the second grade, 49% boys and 51% girls, 88%
RH and 12% LH) completed this test.
