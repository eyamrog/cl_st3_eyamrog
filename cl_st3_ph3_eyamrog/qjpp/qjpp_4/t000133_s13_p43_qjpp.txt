The Colilert test by IDEXX, for example, is available in
tubes with predispensed reagent to which one adds a 10
mL water sample and incubates at 35 ± 0.5°C for
24 hours. After incubation, colorless tubes are negative, yellow tubes
are positive for total coliforms, and yellow tubes that fluoresce
under ultraviolet light are positive for total coliforms and E.
coli . These tubes take 1 minute to fill, are
stable at room temperature, and last about a year (IDEXX,
2019 ). When used in combination with IDEXX's Quanti-Tray, the
Colilert tests can quantify coliform and E. coli levels using
most probable number (MPN) tables and special software. Although the
field-based Colilert tests do not require refrigeration, they do require
stable ambient temperatures and must be read promptly after the
24-hour incubation period. The predispensed tubes cost approximately $1.50 per
test when purchased in 100-tube increments.
