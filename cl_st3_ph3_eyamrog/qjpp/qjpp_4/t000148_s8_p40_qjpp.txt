It is not our intention, nor would it be wise,
to examine every possible cycle biomarker that could be defined
from the observed data. Multiple statistical testing runs the risk
of producing spurious significant outcomes by chance alone. Therefore, our
selection of biomarkers for the present analyses is not exhaustive
but rather is intended to reflect a few of the
more common implicit assumptions about cycle physiology, particularly as regards
variation in cycle and phase lengths, and the relationships between
these biomarkers and progesterone concentrations (see [Harris & Vitzthum, 2013
; Vitzthum, 2009 ] for additional discussion).
