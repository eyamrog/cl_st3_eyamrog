Naked Neck strain embryonated eggs were obtained from a local
supplier (Elevage avicole du Grand Buisson, Saint Maurice sur Dargoire,
France). Laying hen’s sanitary status was regularly checked by the
supplier according to French laws. Eggs were housed at 18°C
until use. They were then incubated at 38.5°C in a
humidified incubator until the desired developmental stage, i.e, HH14 for
the graft step (54 h of incubation).
