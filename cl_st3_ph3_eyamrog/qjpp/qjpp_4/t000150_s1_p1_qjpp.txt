Objectives: Human biologists are increasingly interested in measuring and comparing
physical activities in different societies. Sedentary behavior, which refers to
time spent sitting or lying down while awake, is a
large component of daily 24 hours movement patterns in humans
and has been linked to poor health outcomes such as
risk of all-cause and cardiovascular mortality, independently of physical activity.
As such, it is important for researchers, with the aim
of measuring human movement patterns, to most effectively use resources
available to them to capture sedentary behavior.
