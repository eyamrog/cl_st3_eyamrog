Cycle length is properly seen as a biomarker rather than
as an intrinsic attribute of ovarian functioning. Cycle length itself
does not have an inherent biological significance; rather, length in
days is a perceived feature of the cycle that can
be defined and measured. Identical cycle lengths can result from
a wide combination of follicular and luteal phase lengths. By
inference, it is likely that identical cycle lengths are associated
with marked variation in reproductive physiology including ovarian hormone levels.
