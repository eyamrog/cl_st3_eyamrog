From these results, pairwise differences between cohorts were analyzed and
Mann–Whitney's U test was again applied between Cohorts 2 and
3, always as a function of age and height. This
confirmed that in males there are only significant differences between
hand grip values from both cohorts in two out of
the 14 body height categories and age groups ( p
< .05 in both cases). In females, however, no significant
differences are evident for any height category and the only
exception is for the 11 years old group ( p
< .05).
