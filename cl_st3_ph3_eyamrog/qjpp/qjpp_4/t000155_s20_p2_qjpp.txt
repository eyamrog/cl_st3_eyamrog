The prevalence of physical inactivity is very high in Roma
women (95.0%) compared to the control group (50.0%). Smoking is
a more frequent lifestyle habit in Roma women (85.0%) than
in the control group (36.7%, Table 1 ). 80% of
Roma women rated their health status as poor compared with
30.0% of the control group. The prevalence of chronic illnesses
and gynecological illnesses was very similar in the two groups
(Table 1 ) while 90% of Roma women had at
least 3 pregnancies compared with only 23% of the control
group (Table 1 ).
