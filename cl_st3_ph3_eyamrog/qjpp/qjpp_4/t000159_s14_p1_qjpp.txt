Participants were 124 adults (ages 18–40) selected from an online
survey of birth size ( N = 1246). Inclusion criteria:
singleton born at 37–42 weeks gestation; free of current or
previous diagnosis of heart disease, diabetes, cancer; current weight maintained
for a minimum of 3 months. Exclusion criteria: maternal hypertension
or diabetes; underweight (BMI < 18.5) or morbidly obese (BMI
>35); current pregnancy, or less than 1-year post-partum. The Syracuse
University Institutional Review Board approved this study, and participants provided
written informed consent.
