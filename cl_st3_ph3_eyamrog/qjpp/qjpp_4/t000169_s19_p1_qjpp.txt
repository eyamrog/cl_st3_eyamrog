Figure 2 shows the distribution of the spot area that
was calculated using AMoSS per each number of punches that
could be obtained from one DBS. The larger the spots,
the more punches could be collected. For most DBS spots,
only one punch could be taken. We observe the smallest
spots for DBS that yielded only one punch. The distribution
of spot sizes across all spots (“Total” in Figure 2
) is right skewed, which is mainly driven by the
spots with only one punch.
