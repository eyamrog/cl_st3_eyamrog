Methods: To test the hypothesis that a poor nutritional environment
resulted in decreased SSD in medieval London, this study compares
adult individuals from early pre-Black Death ( c. 1000–1200), late
pre-Black Death ( c. 1200–1250) and post-Black Death ( c.
1350–1540) cemetery contexts from London. Maximum tibial,femoral, and lower limb
lengths were used as a proxy for stature, and SSD
was calculated using the Chakraborty and Majumber index.
