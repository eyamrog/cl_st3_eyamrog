Methods: The initial sample included 2418 schoolchildren from 6 to
16 years old (1467 males, 951 females) attending the camps,
but after preliminary analyses, the study was restricted to 1073
boys and 818 girls in the 1900–1925 interval. Three time
periods were established and 13 categories of height at camp
entry, every 5 cm. Normality tests were run as well
as contrasts of means, and both average values and percentiles
were calculated for hand grip strength in both hands, as
a function of age and height categories.
