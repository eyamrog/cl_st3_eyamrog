Participants' median weight and height were 25.9 kg and 123
cm, respectively (24.9 kg and 121 cm in first grade,
26.9 kg and 126 cm in second grade). Manual dominance
was evaluated as a self-report by asking children to write
their names. Splitting by manual dominance, 88.1% of children were
right-handed (RH) and 11.9% left-handed (LH). In particular, the ratio
of LH children was 14.4% in first grade and 8.9%
in second grade, 10.6% in girls and 13.1% in boys.
Because we focused on healthy motor behavior, the results of
the six children with a certificate of disability were excluded
from the data analysis. In this way, we were considering
the setting of an inclusive experience as they were allowed
to be part of the testing experience. In the remaining
sample there were no children with graphic or writing problems.
