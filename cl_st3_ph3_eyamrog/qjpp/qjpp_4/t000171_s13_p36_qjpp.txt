Apart from the cost of the flow cytometer, expenses for
flow cytometry can be minimized by using small samples and
no more reagents than are necessary. Typical flow cytometry protocols
call for 100 μl of blood and 5 μl of
each labeled antibody. However, we have had good results with
as little as 10 μl of blood and 0.5 μl
of each antibody per sample, cutting the sample required and
expense by a factor of 10 (Blackwell et al., 2021
).
