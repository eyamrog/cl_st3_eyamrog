More generally, our findings for physical components of maternal phenotype
are broadly consistent with a previous analysis of Indian data,
where both short stature and maternal high BMI increased the
risk of cesarean section, while low parity and male offspring
also increased the risk (Wells et al., 2018 ). Adolescent
childbearing is well established to increase the risks of adverse
birth outcomes (Ganchimeg et al., 2014 ) but our analysis
adds to growing evidence that this is partly explained by
the fact that such mothers tend to be reproducing for
the first or second time.
