Our Maithili-speaking study population resides in Dhanusha and Mahottari districts
in Province 2 of southern (plains) Nepal, near the border
with Bihar state of India. Women in this context often
suffer limited (Morrison et al., 2018 ) and inequitable (Harris-Fry,
Paudel, Shrestha, et al., 2018 ) access to food during
pregnancy and the postpartum period and 40% were thin during
late pregnancy, defined as MUAC <23.5 cm (Harris-Fry, Paudel, Shrestha,
et al., 2018 ).
