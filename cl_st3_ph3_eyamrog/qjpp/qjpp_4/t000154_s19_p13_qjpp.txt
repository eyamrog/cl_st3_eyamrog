Mothers' dietary diversity was on average low, at 4.4 food
groups out of 10, with only 46% consuming 5 or
more food groups in the preceding 24 h. On average,
women consumed 1.7 main meals and 1.7 snacks per day.
Energy and micronutrient intakes were below requirements and mean probability
of adequacy (MPA) was only 45% (SD 20%) (Table 2
