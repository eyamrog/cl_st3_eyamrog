While measuring hydration biomarkers in 24-hour urine samples provides the
most accurate measure of daily hydration status, this method is
often hard to implement in field settings where only spot
sampling is possible. First morning samples are often used as
well as early afternoon samples, which according to Perrier et
al. ( 2013 ) most accurately reflect 24-hour sample averages.
Another method is to statistically adjust for time of day
and run sensitivity analyses between the different times of day
to make sure that the relationship being tested is consistent
across the day (Rosinger et al., 2019 ; Rosinger, Lawman,
Akinbami, & Ogden, 2016 ).
