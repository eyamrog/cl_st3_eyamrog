I fitted several Bayesian linear models, with T or E
as outcome variables and handedness as a predictor, using the
rstanarm package in R (Goodrich et al., 2020 ). Handedness
was dummy-coded as two binary variables comparing (a) the effect
of being left-handed versus right-handed and (b) the effect of
being mixed-handed versus right-handed. One exception is when analyzing female
estradiol levels, where data on mixed-handers was insufficient ( n
= 16). In this case, left- and mixed-handers were combined
into a single non-right-handers group ( n = 103).
