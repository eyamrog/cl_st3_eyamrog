Methods: Data were collected from 75 healthy individuals (female =
41; ages 18–53) throughout a 3-month long expedition in the
American Rockies. Body mass, body fat, and lean muscle mass
were measured before, during, and after the course. Physical activity
intensity and energy expenditure were also measured in a subset
of participants using the wGT3X-BT Actigraph wrist monitor and an
accompanying Bluetooth heart rate monitor.
