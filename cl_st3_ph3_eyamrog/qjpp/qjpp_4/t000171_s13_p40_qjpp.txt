Finally, with network-based approaches RNA-seq data can be used to
assess the effects of different environmental exposures on the function
of biological pathways. For example, PathOlogist, an analytical tool developed
by Greenblum et al. ( 2011 ), utilizes pathway structure
to integrate biological information, and assesses differences in activity and
consistency states of networks of genes within pathways of known
biological processes and functions. While such network-based genomic approaches tend
to be used to assess differences by disease state (e.g.,
gene expression profiles in healthy versus tumorous tissue), applying them
to questions relevant to eco-immunology opens opportunities for in-depth investigation
of how the environment influences biologic pathways and processes related
to life history processes (e.g., cellular energy metabolism or immune
investment).
