The aim of this study was to examine the anthropometric
characteristics and motor abilities of Polish university students and to
determine changes in the studied parameters between 2000 and 2018.
Anthropometric characteristics such as body mass and BMI in young
men decreased between 2000 and 2006, whereas a steady increase
in these traits was noted between 2006 and 2018. Body
height continued to increase in all years of the study
(2000–2018).
