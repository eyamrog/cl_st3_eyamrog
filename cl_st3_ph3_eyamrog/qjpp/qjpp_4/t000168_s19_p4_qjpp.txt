Furthermore, we studied the crude and adjusted associations between sociodemographic
variables and birth seasonality (Table 3 ). In the multivariate
logistic regression model for period 2016–2019, all variables except 3
rd and 4 th birth order and medium maternal education,
were significantly associated with seasonality when simultaneously entered in one
model. The multivariate logistic regression model for period 2018–2019 shows
similar results but, as the variable maternal employment status is
considered, showing the highest odds ratio, the maternal age variable
is not significant.
