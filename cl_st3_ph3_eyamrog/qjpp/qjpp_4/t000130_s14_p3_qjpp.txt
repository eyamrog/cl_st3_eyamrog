All mice (C57BL/6 background) were housed with a 12h:12h light:dark
cycle and food/water available ad libitum . All animal experiments
were used in accordance with the ethical guidelines (Declaration of
Helsinki and NIH, publication no. 85-23, revised 1985, European Community
Guidelines, and approved by the local ethical committee (University of
Barcelona, 225/17 and Generalitat de Catalunya, 404/18).
