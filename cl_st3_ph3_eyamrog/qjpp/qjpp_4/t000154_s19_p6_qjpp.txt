Odds ratios for stunting and wasting in boys relative to
girls by age are shown in Figure 3 and Table
S3 , for all cases and for children of primigravidae
only. Results support H 0 2, in showing that sons
are at greater risk of stunting in the first year
of life, but did not show that risk of wasting
is consistently higher in sons. The odds of stunting in
boys relative to girls started 21% higher in boys at
0–2 months (adjusted OR [aOR] 1.209, 95% CI 1.089, 1.342)
and increased to a 67% higher at 4 to 6
months (aOR 1.667 [95% CI 1.321, 2.103]). Between 6 and
12 months the higher odds for boys gradually fell such
that they did not differ from girls between 12 and
20 months. Wasting showed a less clear pattern but, analyzing
all cases, boys had 12% lower odds at birth (aOR
0.882 (95% CI 0.779, 0.999) and higher odds at 14
to 16 months (aOR 1.324 (95% CI 1.021, 1.719). There
was no difference in wasting between boys and girls at
any other time point.
