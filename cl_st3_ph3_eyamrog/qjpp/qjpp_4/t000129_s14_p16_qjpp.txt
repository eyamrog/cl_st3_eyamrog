dissected in cold PBS, fixed in 4% paraformaldehyde (PFA) in

in lacZ buffer (2 mM MgCl2, 0.01% sodium deoxycholate, 0.02%
Nonidet P-40 in PBS). Embryos were then incubated in staining
solution (0.5 mg ml−1 X-gal, 5 mM potassium ferrocyanide, 5
mM potassium ferricyanide in lacZ buffer) at 37°C for a
few hours to overnight until desired staining was achieved. Following
staining, embryos were washed in lacZ buffer and stored at
4°C in 4% PFA in PBS. Finally, embryos were imaged
using a ZEISS SteREO Discovery.V12 with cold light source CL9000
microscope and Leica DFC420 digital camera. LacZ signal was scored
in at least 4 replicate embryos and was performed independently
by at least two annotators blinded to genotype.
