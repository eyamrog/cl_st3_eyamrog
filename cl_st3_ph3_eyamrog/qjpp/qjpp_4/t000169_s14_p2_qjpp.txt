We collected about 24 000 blood samples in 12 out
of the 20 countries participating in SHARE Wave 6. Samples
were probability-based and representative of the population age 50 and
older. Countries were selected by ease of ethical approval. All
respondents without medical obstacles were asked to participate in the
blood collection. Consent rate was 77%, ranging from 39% in
Greece and 68% in Italy on the low end to
89% in Slovenia and 91% in Sweden on the high
end. This resulted in the following country samples: Belgium (3690),
Denmark (2861), Estonia (3683), France (578), Germany (3147), Greece (807),
Israel (1073), Italy (2175), Slovenia (2242), Spain (1827), Sweden (3013),
and Switzerland (2123). Sampling process and data cleaning steps are
described in detail in Börsch-Supan et al. ( 2020 )
and http://www.share-project.org/data-documentation/waves-overview/wave-6.html .
