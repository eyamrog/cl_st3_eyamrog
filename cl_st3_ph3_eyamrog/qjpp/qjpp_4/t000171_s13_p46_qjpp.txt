Due to the high volume of RNA in red blood
cells, globin-based reads can consequently comprise a large proportion (>80%)
of sequencing reads. Thus, when preparing whole blood for 3′
RNA sequencing, it may be advisable to include a globin
depletion step in the protocol. However, globin reads can also
be removed from analyses after sequencing, and depletion may introduce
biases to other reads (Harrington et al., 2020 ). Consequently,
there are costs and benefits to globin depletion with the
primary benefit being a reduction in sequencing depth and expense,
and the cost being an increase in protocol complexity and
read bias. Globin reads may also sometimes be of interest.
