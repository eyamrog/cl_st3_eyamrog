Up to the mid-19th century, Dutch, Swiss, German, Italian, and
Portuguese conscripts still reached barely 165 cm (Fredriks et al.,
2000 ; Hermanussen et al., 1995 ; Padez, 2002 ;
Staub et al., 2013 ). Essentially all mid-19th century children
on earth were short compared with modern children, irrespective of
geography, economic wealth, and living conditions (Boyd, 1980 ).
