Questionnaires were administered verbally to pregnant women by trained fieldworkers,
using smartphones. Data were collected on current age, age at
marriage and first pregnancy, education level of the woman and
her husband, caste and parity. Pregnancy refers to any conception
that was detected by the woman, and included miscarriages, abortions,
stillbirths, or livebirths. Data collection from each participant continued through
the trial, and was scheduled to cover late pregnancy, delivery,
and early infancy.
