To this end, we have tested hypotheses regarding variability in
menstrual cycling with data from two very different populations. The
cycle biomarkers selected for our analyses have been widely used
to characterize ovarian cycles and, in some instances, to distinguish
purportedly abnormal/dysfunctional cycles. The hypotheses that we test reflect some
commonly held implicit and explicit beliefs of “normal” menstrual cycling
that have influenced interpretations of within and between population variation
in cycle phenotypes.
