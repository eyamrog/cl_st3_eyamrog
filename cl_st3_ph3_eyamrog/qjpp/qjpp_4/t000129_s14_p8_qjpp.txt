Embryonic stages of opossum originated from the breeding colony of
Monodelphis domestica maintained under permit ZH104 (issued by the local
authority, LAGeSo) in the animal care facility of the Museum
für Naturkunde, Berlin. All opossums were housed in a centrally
controlled environment with a reversed 12 h dark and 12
h light cycle, temperature of 24–26 °C, and humidity of
60-65%. Bedding, food and water were routinely changed. Females were
euthanized using an overdose of Isoflurane under T0198/13 (issued by
LAGeSo) according to national and international standards. Samples were taken
immediately after death was confirmed. To culture opossum embryonic fibroblasts
(OEFs), stage 30 embryos were isolated and the heads removed
by dissection. Following, cells were isolated through trypsinization, filtration (100
μm) and centrifugation. Cell suspensions were then plated on gelatine-coated
plates at 37 o C in 5.0% CO2 in ESGRO
Complete Basal Medium with supplement until a stable line of
OEFs was established.
