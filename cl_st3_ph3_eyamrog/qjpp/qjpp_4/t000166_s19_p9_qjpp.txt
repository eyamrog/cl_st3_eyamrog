There was a difference between boys and girls by sex
in the BMI (gender × age comparison: Q 1,199 =
6.49, p = .011, partial η 2 = .035); post-hoc
analysis revealed no significant difference in the first grade, but
a tendency for girls to show lower BMI values (
Mdn = 16.5 kg/m 2 ) than boys ( Mdn
= 18.0 kg/m 2 ) in the second grade (
p Tukey = .082, Cohen's d = 0.496).
