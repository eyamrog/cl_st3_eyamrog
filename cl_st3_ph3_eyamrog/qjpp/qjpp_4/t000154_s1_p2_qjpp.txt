Methods: We analyzed longitudinal data from a cluster-randomized trial from
plains Nepal, stratifying results by child or gestational age. Children's
outcomes (0–20 months) were weight, length, and head circumference and
their z -scores relative to WHO reference data in 2-monthly
intervals (n range: 24837 to 25 946). Maternal outcomes were
mid-upper arm circumference (MUAC), and body mass index (BMI) during
pregnancy (12–40 weeks) ( n = 5550 and n =
5519) and postpartum (n = 15 710 and n =
15 356), and diet in pregnancy. We fitted unadjusted and
adjusted mixed-effects linear and logistic regression models comparing boys with
girls.
