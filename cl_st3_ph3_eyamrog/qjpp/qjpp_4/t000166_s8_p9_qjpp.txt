Among several determinant factors, Zaqout et al. ( 2016 )
highlighted BMI, age and sex as the strongest determinants of
children's physical fitness, independent of physical activity. Boys often overestimate
and girls underestimate their own competence regarding sports practice; this
mismatch between objective competence and perceived competence may negatively influence
health outcomes and motor skills in particular (Pesce et al.,
2018 ). The perceptual judgment of sports rules is related
to actual play experience, and gender difference influence the amount
of sports experience (Lange-Küttner & Bosco, 2016 ).
