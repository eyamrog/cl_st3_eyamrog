Genomic DNA from E11.5 forelimbs was obtained using Quick-DNA/RNA Microprep
Plus Kit. Bisulfite conversion was performed on 1μg of DNA
using the EpiTect Bisulfite Kit. Bisulfite-treated DNA was PCR amplified
by nested PCR at the Zfp42 promoter and subsequently cloned
into a pbluescript vector and sequenced. 10-20 clones from 2
replicates per samples were Sanger sequenced and a total of
12 CpG were analysed with BiQ Analyzer software ( Bock
