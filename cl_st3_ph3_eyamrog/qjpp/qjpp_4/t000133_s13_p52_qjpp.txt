There is an urgent need to conduct routine, cost-effective testing
of emerging contaminants. Many of these agents—particularly pesticides, pharmaceuticals, industrial
chemicals, and surfactants—have entered the water cycle with suspected toxicity
for humans (and ecosystems), but are rarely, if ever, tested
in municipal water systems (Rosenfeld & Feng, 2011 , p.
215). We know even less about how emerging contaminants may
interact with each other or other human chemical exposures currently
considered to be benign.
