Materials and methods: A total of 3913 Guatemalan, rural, semi-urban
and urban, Maya and Ladino children 6 to 15 years
old were studied. Height, sitting height, leg length, and metacarpal
length were studied in relation to three proxies for living
conditions: height- and leg length-for-age, and maternal education. Estimation statistics
and null hypothesis significance testing were used to analyze the
data.
