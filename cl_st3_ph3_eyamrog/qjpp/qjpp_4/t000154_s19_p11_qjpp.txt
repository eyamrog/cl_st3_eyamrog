Maternal anthropometry (BMI and MUAC) did not differ by child
sex between 12 and 36 weeks' gestation nor at any
of the postpartum time periods. However, adjusted analyses showed that
in the last month of pregnancy (36 to 40 weeks),
mothers of sons had 0.27 (95% CI 0.00, 0.54) cm
higher MUAC and 0.46 (95% CI 0.12 0.80) kg/m 2
higher BMI than mothers of daughters. This provides weak evidence
to support H 0 4 in very late pregnancy only,
but no evidence that mothers of sons are larger earlier
in pregnancy or thinner during the postpartum period.
