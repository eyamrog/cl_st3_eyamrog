Background: Adrenarche involves maturation of the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis and increased
production of dehydroepiandrosterone and its sulfate ester, dehydroepiandrosterone-sulfate (DHEA-S). It
occurs at ages 6 to 8 in industrialized populations, marking
the transition from childhood to juvenility and cognitive development at
middle childhood. Studies in subsistence level populations indicate a later
age (8-9) for adrenarche, but only two such studies currently
exist for comparison.
