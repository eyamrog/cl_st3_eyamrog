Domain-based questionnaires can also be used to ascertain time spent
in specific sedentary behavior(s) of interest, not just total sedentary
time. For example, the health outcomes associated with time spent
watching television (Grøntved & Hu, 2011 ) or time spent
sitting at work (van Uffelen et al., 2010 ) have
long been of research interest. The validity of measuring time
spent in single behaviors is generally high. For example, self-reported
time spent watching television has been shown to have high
agreement ( ρ = .84, P < .001) with objective
measurements of television time (Otten et al., 2010 ; Wijndaele
et al., 2014 ), and self-reported time spent sitting at
work shows good agreement ( ρ = .63, P <
.001) with objective measures (Wijndaele et al., 2014 ).
