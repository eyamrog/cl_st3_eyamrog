The multiple tube method is another traditional indicator in which
a measured water sample is diluted with sterile growth medium,
then a portion is decanted into multiple tubes, and the
process repeated to yield a range of dilutions. After incubation,
the resulting number of positive-growth tubes at each dilution level
is used to calculate the concentration of the original sample.
This technique is time- and labor-intensive, requires a lot of
tubes and cleaning, and costs ~$2 to 5 per test.
While more precise than plate methods, it is ultimately less
precise than membrane filtration.
