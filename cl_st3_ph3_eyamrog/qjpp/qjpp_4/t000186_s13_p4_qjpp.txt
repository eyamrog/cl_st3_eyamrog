Naïve T cells can differentiate into a number of different
classes, including Th1 (type 1 helper T cells), Th2 ,
Th17 , and regulatory T cells ( Tregs ). Th1
and Th17 cells are generally seen as proinflammatory, whereas Th2
cells promote antibody responses and Tregs facilitate immunological tolerance (
Eisenstein & Williams 2009 , Lee 2018 , Maizels &
Yazdanbakhsh 2003 , Tesmer et al. 2008 ). Because these
develop from common precursors and to some extent have antagonistic
effects, they can also be seen as trading off with
one another. However, any immune response will require some coordinated
action from T cells of multiple classes, so trade-offs are
best seen as shifts in the relative balance among different
kinds of response rather than changes in kind.
