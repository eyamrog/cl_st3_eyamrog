RNA sequencing (RNA-seq) allows for the quantification of mRNA transcripts
and is used to compare gene expression between individuals or
experimental conditions. RNA-seq relies on next generation sequencing (NGS), a
technology that sequences multiple DNA fragments (reverse transcribed from mRNA)
in parallel. Because most immunological processes of interest require mRNA
transcription for protein synthesis, gene expression levels are likely to
be correlated with many other biomarkers and are informative of
a wide range of processes. As such, RNA sequencing may
prove a more cost effective and informative method than direct
measurement of isolated biomarkers.
