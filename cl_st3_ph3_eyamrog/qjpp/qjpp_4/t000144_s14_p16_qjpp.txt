(P1) We estimated a participant's exposure to chronic drought and
chronic excess precipitation by taking (a) the average length of
all drought intervals they experienced and (b) the average length
of all excess precipitation intervals they experienced. Note that if
a participant moved from one department to another during a
drought interval, if the second department had an SPI below
0 when the participant arrived, we treat the drought interval
as if it continued, because the participant's exposure to below-average
rainfall continued even though they moved departments; this is consistent
with our reasoning that exposures can affect preferences and behaviors
even after migration (Section 1.3 ). However, if the second
department had an SPI at or above 0 when the
participant arrived, we closed the drought interval. We likewise continued
excess precipitation intervals following a move if the second department
had an SPI above 0 when the participant arrived.
