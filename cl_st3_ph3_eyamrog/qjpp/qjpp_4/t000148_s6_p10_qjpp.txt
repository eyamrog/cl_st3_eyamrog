Cycle length, the most commonly used criterion for evaluating normalcy,
is a poor predictor of variation in the underlying physiology
(at least as regards the progesterone indices examined here). This
lack of much association between these progesterone indices and cycle
length suggests that neither is a principal determinant of the
other. The same can be said for the other nonhormonal
cycle biomarkers observed in this study. A study of reproductive
aging in US women similarly concluded that cycle length is
too variable to predict hormone levels (Ferrell et al., 2005
).
