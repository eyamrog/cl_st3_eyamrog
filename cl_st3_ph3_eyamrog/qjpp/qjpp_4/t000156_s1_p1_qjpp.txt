Objectives: The Spanish National Pedagogic Museum ( Museo Pedagógico Nacional
, MPN), founded by La Institución Libre de Enseñanza ,
organized the first summer school camps in Spain and their
initiative was followed by other institutions in this country. MPN
prepared anthropological forms for those sojourns, which included information about
both metric and physiological measurements of the schoolchildren. The aim
of the current work is to analyze hand grip strength
data and to compare them with recent values.
