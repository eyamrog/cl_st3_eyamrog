Included in these five sequential models are a random effect
for community of residence and fixed effects for all control
variables (Section 2.2.4 ). We use a random effect for
community such that we can pool our estimates at the
community level; this is more appropriate than pooling data across
both communities when estimating parameters, as these two communities differ
in important ways (Section 2.1 ). We use weakly informative
priors for all variables ( Supporting Information 3.2 ).
