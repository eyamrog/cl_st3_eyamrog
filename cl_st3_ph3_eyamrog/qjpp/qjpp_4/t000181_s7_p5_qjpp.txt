With these twinned intellectual and pedagogical aims in mind, this
article aims to introduce readers to the complex histories of
early proto-anthropology and scientific racism in non-Western contexts using my
primary fieldsite, India, as a case study. My hope is
that it will serve as an example of how a
shift toward a more global perspective can be incorporated into
our understanding of the history of biological anthropology and that
it will provide those who teach about anthropology and race
with a resource to broaden their pedagogies.
