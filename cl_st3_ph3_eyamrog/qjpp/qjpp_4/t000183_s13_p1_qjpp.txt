The heterogeneous domain of HSS can be framed as a
subfield of the more established history of science (HS) field—directed
toward its sociocultural counterpart ( Porter & Ross 2003b ,
Isaac 2007 ). HSS also feeds on established disciplinary histories,
is fortified by science and technology studies (STS) investigations of
social knowledge in the making ( Camic et al. 2011
), and stands to gain from anthropological approaches to how
knowledge of the social is made and how it circulates.
