Houghton et al. ( 2014 ) compared adrenarcheal ages using
salivary DHEA-S from 419 girls of white British and Bangladeshi
ethnicity growing up in Bangladesh or London (Houghton et al.,
2014 ). Median adrenarcheal ages were around 7 years, except
for first-generation migrants to London (5.3 years). The authors suggested
rapid catch-up growth and an earlier body mass index (BMI)
(adiposity) rebound might explain this earlier age.
