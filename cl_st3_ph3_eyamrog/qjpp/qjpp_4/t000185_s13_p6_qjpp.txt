In this review, we draw from this rich array of
perspectives on the study of biotechnology and law in contemporary
society and demonstrate their interventions in anthropology's core concepts. Hence,
the following sections on exchange, self, kinship, and community describe
how the biosciences—and their technologies, economies, and infrastructures—engage and disrupt
modern understandings of their Euro-American legal counterparts, namely property, personhood,
parenthood, and identity, with significant knowledge effects for the idea
of sociality.
