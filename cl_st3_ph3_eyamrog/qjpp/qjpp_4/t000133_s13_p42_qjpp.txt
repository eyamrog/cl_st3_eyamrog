The most common microbiological water quality indicators are total coliforms,
fecal coliforms, and E. coli (Bain et al., 2012 ).
These three indicators, along with others such as heterotrophic bacteria
and Pseudomonas counts, are often used as a proxy for
water treatment efficiency and risk of more virulent organisms. The
simplest tests indicate presence or absence of one or more
indicators.
