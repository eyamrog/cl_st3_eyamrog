Our participants attended one of three US Universities (two large
and one medium sized institution). Both offered traditional classroom experiences
focused on 50–75 min courses that were generally physically inactive
but required 5–10 min walks between classes. While at their
home institution, students had access to intense physical exercise (e.g.,
gyms, cardiovascular exercise) and some students were employed in positions
that required walking (e.g., food service and caregiving).
