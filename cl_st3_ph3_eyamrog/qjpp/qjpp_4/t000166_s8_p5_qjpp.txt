From a kinesiological point of view, we can consider grapho-motor
skills as special tool-related fine motor skills. Drawing, tracing, and
writing result from an integrated input of visual and motor
modalities (eye-hand coordination) during precise, small-scale movements. Defining fine motor
skills, beyond grapho-motricity, other clusters involved are dexterity, manipulation of
small objects, and speed-dominated skills in simple and very likely
repetitive movements (Martzog et al., 2019 ).
