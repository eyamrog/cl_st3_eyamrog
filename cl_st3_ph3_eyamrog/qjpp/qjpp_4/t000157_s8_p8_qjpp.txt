Rather than asking, why are people in LMIC quite regardless
of wealth and social status, shorter than modern global growth
reverences, we discuss the role of phenotypic and developmental plasticity
as a primary means of adjustment to environmental changes. We
ask, what may have caused the modern industrialized democratic European
population to so extraordinarily exceed the millennia-old historical frame of
human body size. Given the widespread assertion that diet is
the main cause of short stature, we focused particularly on
the clinical signs of food shortage and micronutrient deficiency.
