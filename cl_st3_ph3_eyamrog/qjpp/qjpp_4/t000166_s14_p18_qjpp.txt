Considering the assumption check, robust ANOVA and robust t -test
were chosen to run the comparison tests. In particular, a
series of robust ANOVAs (median method) were carried out to
test the gender × age effect on TPB , TPF
, Shuttle , Floppy , Thumb , BMI , WtHR
, Control , Locomotor , Active play . In case
of a significant interaction effect, post-hoc analyses with Tukey correction
for multiple comparisons were conducted. Robust t -tests (Yuen's test
with 0.1 trimmed proportion and median method), in addition to
the nonparametric Mann–Whitney U test, were carried out for the
left versus right-side manual dominance effect on TPB , TPF
, Shuttle , Floppy , Thumb , BMI , and
WtHR .
