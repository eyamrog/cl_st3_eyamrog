Humidity protection during shipment: The DBS were shipped in a
plastic bag with a sachet containing molecular sieve as desiccant,
which absorbs humidity faster than Silica gel. The indicator for
humidity protection consisted of two dummy variables: one for a
not tightly closed plastic bag containing the DBS sample and
one for a missing desiccant inside the bag. Information on
complete or missing components was registered for each sample on
arrival at the biobank.
