In Table 3 , the descriptive statistics and calculated D
indices from femoral lengths are listed by time period. For
both estimated males and estimated females, femoral length decreased from
the early to the late pre-Black Death periods and then
increased again in the aftermath of the Black Death. Results
of the one-way ANOVA revealed that such changes across time
are statistically significant for estimated females ( p = .0452)
as well as estimated males ( p = .0301).
