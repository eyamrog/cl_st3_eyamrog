Contrary to H 0 5, that mothers of sons would
consume more energy and micronutrients, we found that diets of
mothers of boys during pregnancy did not differ from mothers
of girls, whether measured as meals or snacks per day,
dietary diversity score, odds of adequate dietary diversity (Figure 6
and Table S8 ), energy intakes, dietary iron intakes, or
probability of micronutrient adequacy (Table 2 ). This was true
whether all available data were analyzed together and when 4-weekly
gestational age intervals were analyzed.
