In our models for %MVPA, individuals spent 26.6% ± 11.4%
of time in moderate to vigorous physical activity early in
the course and 24.8% ± 9.6% late in the course
(see Figure 3 ). When adjusting for relevant covariates, there
was no significant change in %MVPA from earlier in the
course to later in the course and sex did not
moderate any differences in %MVPA (all p > .7).
