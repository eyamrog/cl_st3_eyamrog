In the interviews at pre- and post-31 weeks' gestation, we
asked the women to recall the number of eating occasions
of main meals and snacks, and the food groups consumed,
in the preceding 24 h. We categorized foods consumed into
10 food groups and calculated the 24-h Women's Dietary Diversity
Score (WDDS) (FAO & FHI 360, 2016 ). This has
been demonstrated to be a reasonable proxy for micronutrient adequacy
of the diet of pregnant women (Nguyen et al., 2018
).
