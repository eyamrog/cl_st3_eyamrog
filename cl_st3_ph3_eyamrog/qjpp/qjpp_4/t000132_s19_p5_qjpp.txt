In light of the conservation of the histidines and glutamic
acid in other major gel-forming mucins ( Figure 1 D),
we hypothesized that these mucins also coordinate Cu 2+ .
Supporting this hypothesis, D1 of the murine MUC5B ortholog (denoted
Muc5b), a representative respiratory mucin, showed increased thermal stability in
the presence of Cu 2+ as measured by DSF (
Figure S4 E). MST confirmed Muc5b Cu 2+ -binding activity
( Figure S4 F).
