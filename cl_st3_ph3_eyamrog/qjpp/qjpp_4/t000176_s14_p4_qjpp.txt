All students attending the field school ( n = 12)
were invited to participate in the first round of data
collection at the Balinese Macaque Ethnoprimatology Field School. A total
of nine students (Seven undergraduate and two graduate) took part
in both rounds of the study. There were six females
and three males who were between 21 and 29 years
of age. All participants were considered full time students by
their home institutions.
