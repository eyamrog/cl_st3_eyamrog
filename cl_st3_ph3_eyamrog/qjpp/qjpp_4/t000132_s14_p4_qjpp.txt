The human colon epithelial cell line Caco-2 were obtained from
the laboratory of Prof. Benjamin Geiger (Weizmann Institute of Science)
and grown in DMEM (Gibco, Thermo Fisher Scientific) supplemented with
10% fetal bovine serum, L-glutamine, and Pen-Strep (Biological Industries). Caco-2
cells were confirmed to be negative for mycoplasma. The human
colon epithelial cell line LS174T was purchased from Sigma and
grown in MEM (Sigma) supplemented with 10% fetal bovine serum,
L-glutamine, and Pen-Strep (Biological Industries).
