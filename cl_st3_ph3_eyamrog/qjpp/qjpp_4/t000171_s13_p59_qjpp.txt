As should be apparent from the sjTREC protocol above, qPCR
can also be used to estimate the proportion of non-T
cells (and by extension T cells) in a sample. Though
we know of no validations, a similar approach is likely
possible for estimating B cells using primers for the non-rearranged
heavy and light immunoglobulin chains. Assessment of rearrangements is currently
described for monitoring of B cell disorders (Cho et al.,
2017 ). Measurement of non-rearranged sequenced should be considerably simpler.
