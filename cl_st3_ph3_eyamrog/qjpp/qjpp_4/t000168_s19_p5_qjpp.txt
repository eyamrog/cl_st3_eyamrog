Table 4 shows sociodemographic factors importance rank according to each
one of the alternative data mining techniques used for period
2018–2019: logistic regression model, Bayesian Networks, Radial Basis Neural Networks,
CHAID decision tree and discriminant analysis. Among the eight sociodemographic
factors considered, those related to maternal employment status, maternal job
qualification and maternal education were consistently found to be the
most relevant influencing birth seasonality, whereas maternal age and population
size were found to be the less relevant influencing factor
in most models.
