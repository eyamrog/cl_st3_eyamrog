A time series composed of 1 510 817 births in
Spain in the period 2016–2019 was analyzed. The data come
from the Birth Statistics of Vital Statistics elaborated by the
National Institute of Statistics (Instituto Nacional de Estadística, 2020 ).
Vital Statistics collects all births that occur in Spain, including
undocumented migrants, regardless of whether the mothers are residents or
non-residents. In the public dataset the month of birth is
the most detailed temporal variable available.
