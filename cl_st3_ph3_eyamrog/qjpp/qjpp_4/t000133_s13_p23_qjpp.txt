Full methods on measuring vasopressin and copeptin can be found
in Morgenthaler et al. ( 2006 ). In sum, copeptin
is a more useful and practical biomarker to measure vasopressin
for human biology research than directly measuring vasopressin. Nonetheless, copeptin
and vasopressin are more reflective of changes in water intake
than plasma osmolality (Enhörning et al., 2019 ).
