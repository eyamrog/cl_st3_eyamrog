Functional genomics also uses RNA-seq data to study gene and
protein expression and function on a global (genome- or system-wide)
scale. Research by Cole ( 2019 ), focusing on individuals
exposed to chronic psychosocial threats has led to the identification
of a pattern of gene expression that associates with such
experiences. This pattern, referred to as the CTRA, involves upregulation
of pro-inflammatory responses and downregulation of type I interferon antiviral
response genes and antibody synthesis genes (Table 1 ). Differences
in patterns of gene expression across these markers has been
increasingly used to investigate the biological effects of psychosocial stress
(Dieckmann et al., 2020 ; Levine et al., 2017 ;
Powell et al., 2013 ).
