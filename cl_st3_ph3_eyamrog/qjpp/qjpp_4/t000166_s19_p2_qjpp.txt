The WtHR values of our participants were compared to the
recent values reported by Santomauro et al. ( 2017 )
on Italian children from Tuscany, a region in central Italy,
north of Abruzzo region. Our P50 values ranged between their
P50 and P75 matching for the same gender and age.
Therefore, WtHR values of our participants were higher than this
reference. We further characterized the participants according to the health
hazard cut-off of 0.50 (Maffeis et al., 2008 ). We
found 14.35% of children to be at metabolic risk.
