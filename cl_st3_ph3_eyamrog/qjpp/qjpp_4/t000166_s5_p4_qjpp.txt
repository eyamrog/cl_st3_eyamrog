Indeed, also in the current study, strategy and performance on
fine motor control tests were task-related. The link between the
two tablet parameters and the two fine coordinative tests were
only small and changed with regards to age and gender,
as shown in the networks. This dynamic perspective was also
shown by Snapp-Childs and colleagues who reported specific training effects
to eliminate drawing performance differences between ages (7–8 vs 10–12-year
old children) and different coordination skill levels (Snapp-Childs et al.,
2015 ).
