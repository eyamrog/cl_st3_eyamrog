Shuttle performances of participants were compared to international norms for
this task (Kolimechkov et al., 2019 ). Median of boys
in first grade were between P30 and P40 of that
reference, girls in first grade were between P50 and P60;
boys in second grade were between P50 and P60, girls
in second grade were between P60 and P70. Shuttle performance
was therefore overall higher than this reference, except for boys
in the school entrance class.
