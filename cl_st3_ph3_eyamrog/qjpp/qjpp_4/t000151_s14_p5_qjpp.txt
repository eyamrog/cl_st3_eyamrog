R code that downloads the data from the CDC website,
then cleans and analyzes it, is available on the Open
Science Framework ( t.ly/pg6V ). The distributions of both testosterone
and estradiol were strongly non-normal. The data were transformed to
bring both skew and kurtosis as close to 0 as
possible. The distributions of male T and E were both
best corrected by a square root transform. The female distributions
were best made normal by a natural log transform. As
such, each sex was analyzed separately, and model outputs are
not easily comparable. All further references to T and E
refer to their transformed values. T and E were also
both standardized for all analyses, so effects of predictors are
in standard deviation units.
