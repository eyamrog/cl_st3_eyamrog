For cell lines, 50 μg/mL of GPC3 core protein was
mixed with Alexa 568-conjugated anti-hIgG antibody (Thermo Fisher Scientific) in
PBS. Protein was injected into matrices (90 μm width) (17,546,017)
and placed on 60 mm dishes, resulting in red fluorescent
stripes. After 30 min incubation at 37°C, dishes were washed
with PBS and matrices removed. Dishes were coated with 50
μg/mL GPC3 coreUG protein mixed with 120 μg/mL anti-hFc (Jackson)
for 30 min at 37°C and washed with PBS. HeLa,
SY5Y or N2A cells were cultured on the stripes in
2% FBS medium, with 50 μg/mL Strep-Nano break or Strep-Nano
glue , or without nanobody (control). After 16 h cells
were fixed with 4% PFA in PBS for 20 min
at room temperature and nuclei were counterstained with DAPI before
mounting and imaging. The number of DAPI positive pixels on
red or black stripes was quantified.
