There was a main effect of gender on the WtHR
(girls showed a higher WtHR than boys, Q 1,200 =
4.74, p = .029, partial η 2 = .024), and
a significant two-way interaction ( Q 1,198 = 8.17, p
= .004, partial η 2 = .037); post-hoc analysis revealed
no significant difference between boys and girls in the first
grade, whereas boys showed higher WtHR values ( Mdn =
0.48) than girls ( Mdn = 0.45) in the second
grade ( p Tukey = .005, Cohen's d = 0.699).
