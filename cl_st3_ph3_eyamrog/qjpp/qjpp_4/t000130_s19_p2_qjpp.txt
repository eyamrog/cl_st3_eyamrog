We confirmed the interaction using surface plasmon resonance (SPR) binding
experiments with purified ectodomains ( Figure S1 D). To produce
complex crystals, we mixed hGPC3 core , mGPC3 core or
murine GPC3 residues 31–488 (mGPC3 488 ) with Rattus norvegicus
Unc5D residues 32–307 (rUnc5D IgIgTSP ). The complexes crystallized in
two different space groups ( Table S1 ) with different
crystal packing. Strikingly, all three datasets revealed an octameric assembly
( Figures 1 D–1I, S1 E, and Video S1 ).
The center of the octamer is formed by four Unc5D
molecules that are aligned in a “head-to-tail” antiparallel bundle. Two
GPC3 molecules wrap around each end of the Unc5 tetramer.
Each GPC3 chain interacts with three Unc5 chains, forming interfaces
with the Ig1/Ig2 domains of two Unc5 molecules, and with
a TSP1 domain of a third Unc5 molecule ( Figures
1 D–1I).
