Third, it can improve service quality to customers. Service-related training
in a Korean coffee chain created a positive work environment
for employees and improved the quality of service ( Son
et al. 2021 ). There was a strong relationship between
employee training and the quality of service in the Indian
hotel industry ( Dhar 2015 ). Cross-industry studies in China,
including clothing, electronics, and automobiles, have shown that training indirectly
affects the quality of customer service through job satisfaction (
Shen and Tang 2018 ).
