Ethiopia is not immune to this widespread deadly disease. The
first case of coronavirus was reported in Ethiopia on March
13, 2020, since when the number of cases has continued
to rise. With 106,615 tests conducted as of May 30,
2020, there were 1,063 cases reported and eight deaths (
Ministry of Health Ethiopia 2020 ; Worldometer 2020 ). As
of June 14, 2021, 1,487,270 tests were conducted out of
which 274,000 cases are reported and 4,237 died ( WHO
2021 ).
