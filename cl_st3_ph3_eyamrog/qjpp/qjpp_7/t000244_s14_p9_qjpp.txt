To test the additional explanatory value of goal efficacy beliefs,
we further examined constructs that seemed especially relevant for the
collective context of our study: identification with the group of
students (three items, α = .79; Bamberg, Rees, & Seebauer,
2015 ), injunctive ( α = .78) and descriptive (
α = .72) norms with six items each (Rees &
Bamberg, 2014 ), and demographics (age, gender, study program).
