The dimension balance-isolation is characterized by an understanding of the
clients’ feelings. Sincerity is inhibited by group therapy, as it
does not go as deep into sensitive areas as individual
therapy does. The therapists, however, try to identify every bit
of progress clients make, no matter how small, which is
a way of showing appreciation to the clients. The focus
on feelings is hampered as the clients tend to use
psychological defenses such as triangulations instead of uncovering their inner
feelings. In sum, therapists and clients are isolated from each
other in two out of four dimensions, which contribute to
an alienation between them.
