To provide a deeper understanding of the role of social
aspects in the formulation of COVID-19 reports in the early
stages of the pandemic, this paper extends the correlation-based calculations
of Kurbucz et al. (2021) by using the authors’ data
set and regression models with regional dummy interactions. The main
findings not only reflect the importance of foreign travel restrictions
and airport controls but also show that in Europe and
Africa, more corrupt countries reported significantly fewer cases and deaths
than countries in other regions.
