The current study extended prior research on ERS by focusing
on SES and by investigating how adolescents perceive parental messages
regarding cross-wealth peer interactions in Nepal, a country with a
long history of societal and structural inequalities identified by SES.
Given that cross-group friendships foster positive intergroup attitudes and reduce
intergroup biases (Turner & Cameron, 2016 ), negative parental expectations
for cross-group friendships limit the opportunity for reducing bias based
on SES, a pervasive source of marginalization in adolescence (Arsenio
& Willems, 2017 ).
