In this section, we provide an overview of variables that
have been found to predict contact seeking and contact engagement
on a meso-level, that is factors associated with intragroup and
intergroup processes, such as group-based categorizations and emotions. We then
focus on intragroup processes affecting intergroup contact such as social
support by fellow ingroup members. After that, we discuss the
effects of violent intergroup conflicts on willingness to engage in
intergroup contact and our ideas for interventions in such settings.
