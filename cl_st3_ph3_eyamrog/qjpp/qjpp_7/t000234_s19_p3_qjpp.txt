Selena, another student who mentioned that she was thus far
unaffected by the pandemic, stated, “I haven’t noticed it affecting
me. I’ve been able to receive more help of EBT,
but personally, I don’t feel affected with food when it
comes to the pandemic.” Having access to a federal food
assistance program helped Selena ensure that she still had access
to food while the food pantry was shut down. When
I asked C, an international student who was close to
graduation, how the pandemic had affected her eating schedule, she
responded, Mostly I eat two meals a day. Now I
have a car so . . . After I purchased
my car, the time we were going to the food
pantry was reduced. So, now I have enough food at
home, and I think I will not purchase much food
now because I will leave [the city] next month. I
want to make sure that I eat up all the
food before I leave.
