A chi-square test revealed that adolescents who perceived that high-SES
parents would approve of the friendship used different reasons to
justify their answer than students who did not, χ 2
(3) = 332.11, p < .001. Further supporting H3a, Figure
3b (i.e., the expected likelihood for the reasons that adolescents
used to justify parental expectations and their 95% confidence intervals)
shows that adolescents expected that high-SES parents would not approve
of the friendship because of reasons of social hierarchies and
reputation. Since the results showed an absolute difference between using
reasons about reputation and hierarchies over moral concerns to justify
negative parental expectations, no statistical follow-up tests were required. Figure
3b also shows that adolescents justified positive parental expectations for
high-SES parents with predominantly moral concerns.
