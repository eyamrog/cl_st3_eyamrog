While we outlined interventions and social policy measures for most
of the predictors of intergroup contact separately, we want to
emphasize that interventions and policies ideally consider all levels of
analysis together (for a similar point see Paolini et al.,
2018 ). Interventions and policies that are aimed to change
societal norms on the macro-level, for example, must take into
account also inter- and intragroup variables on the meso-level such
as the history of conflict and a sense of community
shared within a group, as well as micro-level factors like
outgroup attitudes and motives.
