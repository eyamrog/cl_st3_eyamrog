When probation officers exhibited remorse bias towards defendants with violence
“signals” through the social cognitive processes, they then discussed crafting
remorse-based narratives in their presentencing reports that encompassed two types
of succeeding information on and evaluations of defendants’ remorse displays
and, correspondingly, evaluations of their character. In using these two
types of evidence, officers’ pre-sentencing reports most often negatively depicted
these defendants in the ways described below, which led to
harsher sentencing recommendations.
