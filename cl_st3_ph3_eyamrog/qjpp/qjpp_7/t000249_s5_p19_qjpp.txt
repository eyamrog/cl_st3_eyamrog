Moreover, with regard to the methodological approach, future research could
further establish generalizability of the AST across different ecological contexts.
A recent study investigating children's hostile attribution biases in 12
ecological contexts, including countries from African, American, Asian, European regions
and the Middle East found reliable results (Dodge et al.,
2015 ). Lastly, since the current study included multiple dependent
measures, we adjusted the p-values hypotheses-wise (for details see the
supplementary file, S5 ). Thereby, the effects regarding students’ SES
remained significant, while the effects regarding grade differences became non-significant
(all below p < .10). Future research should thus replicate
these findings with more power to detect small effects.
