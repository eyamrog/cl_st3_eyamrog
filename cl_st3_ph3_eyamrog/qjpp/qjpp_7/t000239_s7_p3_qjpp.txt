This paper argues that the responses seen across the planet
by various political leaders during the outbreak of COVID-19 were
driven primarily by the heuristics employed by each particular leader,
drawn from their own experience in crisis situations, their backgrounds,
and their generational attributes. Unlike the recommendations of Ansell and
Boin (2019) , political leaders globally tended not to rely
on pragmatism during the early days of the coronavirus, but
instead reverted back to basic ideological heuristics in order to
facilitate cognitive savings ( Sniderman et al. 1986 ) and
make decisions more quickly while under stress ( Gigerenzer 2015
).
