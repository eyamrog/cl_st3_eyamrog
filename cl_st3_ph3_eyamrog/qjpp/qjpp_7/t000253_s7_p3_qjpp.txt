This paper explores the ethical nature of these policy decisions,
and whether or not they represent substantive discrimination against older
people. We set out a simple criterion for establishing an
operationalizable yardstick of fairness. We briefly assess the usefulness of
this yardstick with reference to a range of global health
issues. We then interrogate COVID-19 vaccination policies in greater detail,
with a particular focus on a set of countries whose
health care resources are more limited than in high-income country
settings: Brazil, Bulgaria, India, and South Africa.
