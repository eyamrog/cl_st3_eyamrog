With the COVID-19 pandemic, we have witnessed the pandemic of
ageism in social media discourse and relevant policies (Ayalon, 2020
; Ayalon & Cohn-Schwartz, 2021a ; Clarfield & Jotkowitz, 2020
). This has raised attention to the topic of ageism
and the need to act. Moreover, given that WHO (
2021 ) call to obtain further evidence from different places
around the world concerning social campaigns to eradicate ageism, we
focused on the description and analysis of three campaigns for
the eradication of ageism that were undertaken in Israel during
the pandemic (April 2020–May 2021): “Don't fall into ageism” undertaken
by Vehadarta – The Third Strength; “Active aging” of The
Space for Initiatives of 60+-; and “Age diversity” of the
Ministry for Social Equality.
