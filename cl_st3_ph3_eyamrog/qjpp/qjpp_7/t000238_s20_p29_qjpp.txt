ATV’s eclectic orientation is based on a manual, which prevents
it from being completely tailored to clients’ needs. The clients
are additionally in a position of dependence as they are
in need of help which may result in the relationship
becoming over-differentiated. Overall, this dimension is a mixture of engulfing
elements, which restricts the possibility of increasing trust between the
therapist and the client. Both under- and over-differentiation hamper the
achievement of balance between the parties.
