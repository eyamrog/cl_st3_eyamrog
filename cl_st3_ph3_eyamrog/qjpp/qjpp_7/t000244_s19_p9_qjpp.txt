In a model specifically for NGO members, we added a
latent factor for participatory efficacy and incorporated collective efficacy concerning
activists’ NGO either into already existing latent factors or as
separate scales. Both a latent factor model, χ 2 /
df = 2.35, CFI = .949, SRMR = .038, AIC
= 22,411, and a scale model, χ 2 / df
= 2.23, CFI = .949, SRMR = .039, AIC =
22,418, showed a good fit that did not differ significantly,
χ 2 diff (6) = 5.49, p = .483.
