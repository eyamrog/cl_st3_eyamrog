Within schemes of greening industries, aluminum has global strategic importance.
As a light, flexible and recyclable metal, it is a
component in energy and lower-carbon infrastructures (e.g., power lines, substations)
and can substitute heavier metals, like steel, enabling lighter vehicles
that need less energy input (Brough & Jouhara, 2020 ).
