This list of personality and background traits used to proxy
for heuristics is by no means exhaustive and indeed shows
the difficulties of creating a broad-based, cross-country analysis which may
absorb leadership traits—and the corresponding use of heuristics—across countries. But
based on availability at this moment, and in the absence
of the grand post-COVID history that is yet to be
written, these traits may at least be plausibly correlated with
the response to the pandemic and informed how a leader
would approach the question of a lockdown (summary statistics of
all variables are shown in Appendix B ).
