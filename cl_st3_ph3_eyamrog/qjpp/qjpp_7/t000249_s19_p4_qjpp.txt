To compare whether adolescents differed in their answer patterns regarding
high- and low-SES parents’ expectations, we used an Exact McNemar
test with central confidence intervals, which was significant χ 2
(1) = 180.66, p < .001, odds ratio = .04[CI
95 = .02, .08], Φ = .18. In line with
H3a, 81% of adolescents thought that the low-SES parents would
want the two story characters to be friends, while only
30% of the adolescents reported that the high-SES parents would
approve of that friendship.
