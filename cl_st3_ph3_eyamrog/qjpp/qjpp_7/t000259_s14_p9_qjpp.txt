I test the theoretical expectations outlined earlier (i.e., H1–H5, and
RQ1) by constructing an OLS regression model that regresses respondents’
vaccination intentions on their assignments to each level of each
attribute, for each rating task. These data are structured in
“long” format, such that each row corresponds to one of
six rating tasks completed by each respondent (i.e., the unit
of observation is each rating task).
