Further, a handful of studies have investigated adolescents’ perceptions of
intergroup relations with regard to their own SES, whereby adolescents
from higher SES backgrounds were more likely to consider the
needs of high SES peers (Burkholder et al., 2019 ;
Elenbaas & Killen, 2018 ). The current study extends this
prior work by investigating whether negative perceptions of traits based
on SES are extended to peer contexts and whether adolescents
perceive parents as approving of cross-group friendships determined by SES.
