The AST (Killen et al., 2010 ; McGlothlin et al.,
2006 ) involves displaying pictures to students that depict a
dyadic peer encounter (see Figure 1 ), whereby participants evaluate
whether an individual is helping or hindering in a situation
in which the intentions are ambiguous (e.g., did the target
peer push the other one down, or will the target
peer help the other one?). In this task, the social
group membership of that individual is systematically varied to compare
whether negative intentions are more likely attributed to the perpetrator
based on group membership (for more details, see the supplementary
file, S1 ).
