Correspondingly, the extent to which probation officers prioritized “exposing” disingenuous
remorse displays in their pre-sentencing reports, and their interest in
conveying that evidence to the court and what they believed
it indicated about defendants’ intrinsic character, was striking. Remorse-based narratives
have been often been considered as a minor part or
section of information for officers in their pre-sentencing reports (Berryessa
& Balavender, 2021 ; Everett & Nienstedt, 1999 ; Weisman,
2009 ), but data here indicate that probation officers may
utilize their assessments of remorse displays, as influenced by implicit
social cognitive processes, to shape detailed, often nuanced, descriptions of
defendants’ implicitly violent character and resulting sentencing recommendations in their
pre-sentencing reports.
