In Barcarena and São Luís, the precarious health situation was
salient. Participants mentioned headaches, stomach pains, allergies, and dizziness and
suspected that the toxic environment of many industries caused high
incidences of stomach cancer and Alzheimer's. They brought us to
forests and gardens to observe white dust on leaves, dried-out
fruit trees, and dirty streams. For Ribeirinho communities, rivers are
the heart of their ways of life. We visited one
such community by boat in which the river had a
foul smell and was uninviting. People pulled up their sleeves,
exposing multiple small scars caused, according to them, because the
dirty water infects even trivial scratches, and they described losing
their hair. “We can no longer touch the water,” explained
Teresa.
