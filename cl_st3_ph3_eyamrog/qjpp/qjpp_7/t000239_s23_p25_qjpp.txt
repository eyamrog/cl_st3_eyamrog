In the example of New York (along with other jurisdictions
not examined here), the uncertainty associated with the course of
the crisis and its possible severity in a particular locale
joined with the pre-existing beliefs of the leader in charge
and the political rivalries and factions attached to the personalities
involved to generate a number of decision-making heuristics. However, this
case study, done from the outside and based on secondary
sources, may fall prey to the sin that Vis (2019
:61) noted, namely that we “assume instead of showing empirically
that political elites use heuristics.” While we cannot—at the moment—derive
a survey of all actors involved in the COVID-19 pandemic
response, we can use a simple empirical exercise to further
illuminate the role of a leader’s background in heuristic formation,
and thus also in driving policies in response.
