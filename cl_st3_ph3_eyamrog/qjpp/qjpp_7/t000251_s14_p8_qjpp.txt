Interrater reliability (see Guba, 1981 ) was calculated to validate
the internal consistency of the coding scheme using Dedoose. After
primary coding, a second independent co-coder analyzed a random sample
of excerpts from all 151 interviews and calculated high interrater
reliability of the coding scheme (Cohen's kappa = 0.88). Reporting
standards from the Standards for Reporting Qualitative Research guidelines, including
reflexivity practices during and after the coding process, were adopted
in the writing of this article when appropriate (see O'Brien
et al., 2014 ).
