Discussing the violence's psychological impact, participants had ambiguous points of
view. Linda had received death threats and was living under
protection measures. She held that talking about psychosocial health was
not regarded as important: “..as if it was snobbish [frescura].
In the past, people saw talking about depression as irrelevant.
We see that people try to hide their suffering. They
suffer in silence.” Several participants expressed loneliness and personal vulnerability.
While grassroots-organizations supported them in intense moments of struggle, they
tackled the aftermath, which could mean increased threats or unemployment
resulting from publicly confronting companies individually.
