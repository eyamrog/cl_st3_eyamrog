These research tasks seem highly relevant as matching, if positive
effects could be demonstrated, would be a relatively easy to
implement way of improving data quality and decreasing the amount
of item nonresponse. Finally, it should be admitted that the
focus on item nonresponse in comparison to unit nonresponse seems
arbitrary, which is true to a certain extent. However, as
there are given word limits, it seemed best to focus
on item nonresponse as the ESS provides richer data to
explore this context. Subsequent studies will also take the unit
nonresponse into account.
