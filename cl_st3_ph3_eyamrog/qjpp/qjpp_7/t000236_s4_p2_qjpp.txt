On the basis of the results, it is imperative for
governments in Africa to prioritize COVID-19 education, with particular emphasis
on improving public knowledge on the impact of the pandemic
and the existing effective response strategies. Such an approach, as
argued by PMT, will increase mass response to the pandemic,
thereby minimizing its spread and impact. In addition, social intervention
programs and incentives that address inequality and vulnerability, and trampoline
people from the adverse impact of the pandemic, should be
strengthened, particularly in vulnerable communities.
