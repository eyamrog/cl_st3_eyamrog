The work of mapping led to a deceptively simple set
of requirements: notifications had to be straightforward and quick to
comprehend; be interoperable with many EMRs to reach as many
FPs as possible; be sustainable; and could not increase anyone’s
workload once it was built since, as with most Canadian
hospitals, the hospitals in this community routinely operate over-capacity (e.g.
see Crawley 2020 ; Olsen 2017 ). IMIT created an
automated electronic notification of hospital admissions, deaths in hospital, and
discharges from acute care facilities to any FP in the
province.
