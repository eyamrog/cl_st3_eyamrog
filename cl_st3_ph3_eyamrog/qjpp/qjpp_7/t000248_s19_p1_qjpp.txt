Table 2 shows all models with regression estimates, standard errors,
significance levels, variance components, and model fit statistics. The null
model showed the decomposition of the variance in national identification
at the individual and class level. The variance was significant
at both individual and class levels: 93% of the variance
was at the individual level, while 7% of the variance
was at the class level. This class-level variance corresponds to
the roughly 10% variance usually found at the contextual level
in school-based research (Hox et al., 2010 ).
