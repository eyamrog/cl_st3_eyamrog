When we consider what we accomplished: news media made mention
of our findings on precarity ( LeFleche 2018 ) and
we held presentations and met with provincial politicians, so we
sustained some public discourse about precarious employment in Niagara. We
also produced a study broadly comparable with others carried out
in Ontario. That said, we learned that despite the report
and our attempt to influence public discourse, it ultimately had
limited ability to make an impact given provincial legislative changes
following an election. Grace says this best: I’m not convinced
the project contributed to any major change like would have
ideally happened. In part, I think this is because election
cycles in Canada occur so rapidly that it tends to
conflict with any ability to make real, lasting changes.
