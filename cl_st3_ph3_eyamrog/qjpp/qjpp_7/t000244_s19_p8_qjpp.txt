To replicate our assumption that both efficacy actors and efficacy
goals can be distinguished, we first performed a CFA on
efficacy items that were answered by all participants. Supporting H1,
a model in which self-efficacy, collective efficacy, direct goal efficacy,
and indirect goal efficacy represented distinct latent factors provided a
better fit to the data, χ 2 / df =
5.17, CFI = .973, SRMR = .030, AIC = 34,835
(see Figure 2 ), than a model structured according to
scales, χ 2 / df = 4.87, CFI = .968,
SRMR = .031, AIC = 34,882, χ 2 diff (8)
= 31.53, p < .001, or a one-factor solution, χ
2 / df = 36.87, CFI = .658, SRMR =
.135, AIC = 37,603, χ 2 diff (14) = 1469.50,
p < .001.
