Overall, the therapy is voluntary and without sanction. On a
basic level, it counteracts an engulfing over-differentiation between the parties.
The therapists’ statements, however, suggest that this is not enough
to assert a balanced relationship. For example, complete trust between
the client and the therapist cannot be achieved because the
“secrets” that clients disclose in confidence must be reported when
it comes to serious crimes committed. Furthermore, when the psychodynamic
element in group therapy enters sensitive areas that clients do
not want to share with others, some choose to drop
out.
