This study aims to raise awareness about the specific needs
of a diverse group of postsecondary students during this particular
emergency. The findings and related implications of this study can
be used to inform colleges and universities of the inequities
within higher education, relevant to the pandemic and related economic
crises. By adding student voices and perspectives, this study intends
to help administrators and other decision-makers in higher education institutions,
who may face similar challenges at their campuses, make informed
decisions during similar emergencies to ensure that the students’ basic
needs are met.
