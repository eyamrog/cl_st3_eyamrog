The empirical element of this paper is our own reflections
on the tensions that existed between the academic and NGO
elements in the project. Following the work of Saldaña (2016)
, we recognize that our reflections may be shaped by
our memories, which are fluid and imperfect; nonetheless, we believe
that they accurately capture some of the challenges of working
together with a diverse group. One way to address the
issue of recall would be to capture our reflections during
the research process in a similar future project. An ongoing
reflexive approach might mitigate some of the tensions and pitfalls
that face multiresearcher applied sociology projects.
