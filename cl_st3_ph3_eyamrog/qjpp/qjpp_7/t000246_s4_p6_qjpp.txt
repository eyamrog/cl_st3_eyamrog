Future research should also more strongly consider questions of group
status. According to Ron et al. ( 2017 ) some
predictors might work differently for high- and low-status groups. For
instance, Saguy and Dovidio ( 2013 ) showed that high-
and low-status groups differ with regard to their expectations regarding
the content of intergroup contact. While high-status groups prefer to
discuss commonalties during intergroup encounters, low-status groups strive toward a
discussion of status differences (see also Hässler et al., 2020
). Moreover, results by Shelton and Richeson ( 2005 )
point out that the type of threat-driven rejection of intergroup
contact might differ between groups of different status.
