From the perspective of the enterprise, the effects of training
are as follows. First, it can improve loyalty to the
organization. General-purpose skills training can serve as an incentive for
employee commitment instead of high wages and employment security (
Cappelli 2004 ). This was confirmed at universities in Malaysia
( Hanaysha 2016 ), hotels in Turkey ( Bulut and
Culha 2010 ), and banks in Uganda ( Ocen et
al. 2017 ). A similar trend is seen among part-time
employees ( Jaworski et al. 2018 ).
