In this section, we will first provide an overview of
variables that have been previously suggested to facilitate contact on
an intrapersonal and interpersonal level, that is, factors associated with
individuals’ experiences, personality, cognitive abilities, attitudes, beliefs (see Paolini et
al., 2018 ; Ron et al., 2017 ; Turner et
al., 2020 ). We will then present a more detailed
analysis of emerging research on relational self-expansion motivation, and confidence
in contact.
