the same result—utilizing various institutional metrics such as polity2, level
of democracy, political competition (all from the Polity IV database)
or an overall political risk rating, corruption, and a composite
risk rating (from the International Country Risk Guide, ICRG) in
place of executive constraints also showed no significance for institutions
and in each case the institutional variable was dropped as
part of the GETS process. 2 An additional robustness test,
shown here as column 15 in Table 2 , included
both dummies for European Union (EU) membership and whether a
country was located in Asia (including South, Central, Southeast, and
East Asia); as can be seen, the results remain the
same in terms of leadership attributes but Asian countries also
are marginally correlated with a slightly lower lockdown stringency at
the outbreak of the pandemic, perhaps attributable to cultural precepts
(although this is not tested for explicitly). 3 In sum,
by March 30, the attributes of leaders—which would have been
ignored in a purely institutional analysis—were the main determinants of
the key policy decisions of 2020.
