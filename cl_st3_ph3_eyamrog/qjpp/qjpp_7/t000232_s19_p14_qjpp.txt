The admission notification is triggered when the registration clerk completes
an admission record for a patient. The system does not
require the clerk to alter their work tasks but is
automatically engaged when the admission submission is received by the
EHR. The form auto-populates fields from the EHR including patient
identifiers; date of admission; facility identifiers; and the name of
the most responsible provider (MRP) at admission (often, an ED
physician). Discharge notifications work in a similar fashion but are
triggered by the submission of the discharge form (deaths are
considered a form of discharge in the EHR).
