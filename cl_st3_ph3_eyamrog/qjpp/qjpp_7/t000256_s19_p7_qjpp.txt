Vehadarta chose to name its campaign “Don't fall into ageism,”
and “Stop Ageism.” Use of negatives – “don't,” “fall,” and
“stop” relate to ageism with negative images, such as, a
trash can, a danger, a trap, and/or a phenomenon to
avoid or even stop. In Hebrew, the slogan, “Don't fall
into ageism,” has two forms. In the first sense –
“don't fall” – cautions against sliding down the slippery slope
of ageism. In the second sense – “don't fall into”
cautions against committing an act of discrimination. Perhaps the double
entendre of the slogan reinforced the message.
