The figure demonstrates that the initial lockdown in both countries
took place with slightly different length at the same time.
This is the common ground, which enabled us to compare
both countries in this study. The research question and question
in the interviews focused on the experience of age-related discrimination.
The age discrimination from the participants experience in turn focused
on the new COVID-19 restrictions (shielding, stay at home, not
leave the house), which had been implemented in both countries
in March 2020.
