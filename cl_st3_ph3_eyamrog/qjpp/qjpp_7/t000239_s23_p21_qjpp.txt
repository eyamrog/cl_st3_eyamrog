The dramatic shift from blasé to hyper-stringent measures—ones which had
never before been seen in New York City—likely exacerbated both
the effects of the virus in New York ( Pei,
Kandula, and Shaman 2020 ) and the economic consequences, but
in neither situation were the decisions driven by the institutional
mechanism of the executive nor the checks and balance of
NYC governance. In reality, the decisions emanated directly from Gracie
Mansion, and appeared to shift as the Mayor’s own read
of the situation—again, rather than the institutional levers of government—switched
decision rules.
