The Y variable in Equation 1 is the Oxford University
“Stringency Index,” which measures the extensiveness of lockdown provisions in
a particular country across nine policy dimensions ( Hale et
al. 2020 ). The Stringency Index here is taken at
two separate dates, first at March 15, 2020, the point
at which nearly every country worldwide had begun to institute
some form of lockdown policy, but at a point where
policies still differed wildly across countries (see Figure 1 for
the distribution of the Index on that date). The second
date is March 30, 2020, at which point the extent
of the pandemic had become evident and where, theoretically, the
leader’s own personality would be taking center stage beyond merely
institutional mechanisms.
