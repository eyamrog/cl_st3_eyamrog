We reported two studies that test the specific role of
efficacy actors and goals on diverse PEBs. Although the manipulation
in Study 1 did not affect PEB intentions or goal
efficacy beliefs, both studies showed that efficacy actors and goals
can be differentiated. Indeed, self- rather than collective efficacy predicted
private and indirect PEB. Activist PEB was best predicted by
self-efficacy (non-activist samples) and participatory efficacy (activist sample). Public PEB
revealed diverging findings. In Study 1, it was positively predicted
by collective efficacy (of students), whereas in Study 2 it
was positively predicted by self-efficacy and negatively predicted by collective
efficacy (with all humanity).
