Evaluations of Character . Probation officers described how they utilized
and extended their evaluations of remorse displays to make resultant
character assessments of defendants in their pre-sentencing reports; they often
crafted narratives that detailed their reservations and doubts about the
sincerity of particular defendants’ remorse displays to illustrate that they
suffered from violent “true character” as an explanation for their
past and potential future violence. As detailed here, officers used
these remorse assessments to make implicit judgments about three aspects
of defendants’ character in pre-sentencing reports: their fixed violent thinking,
their inherent dangerousness, and the futility of their attempted moral
reform.
