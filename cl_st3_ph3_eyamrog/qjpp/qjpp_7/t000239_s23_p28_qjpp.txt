For the right-hand side control variables, they can be plausibly
thought to determine why the lockdown was the way it
was in that particular country. Most important would seem to
be the actual extent of impact that COVID-19 had had
on a country by March 15 (or March 30) and
the prospect of how the shape of the curve was
beginning to look; to account for this, I use both
the number of confirmed cases of coronavirus and the number
of deaths attributed to the virus as of March 15
or March 30. The information contained in these variables may
not only capture the extent of COVID’s effect on that
particular country, but also may capture additional institutional and cultural
attributes which could be determining both popular and political responses
( Huynh 2020 ; van Bavel et al. 2020 ).
