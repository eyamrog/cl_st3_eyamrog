Drawing upon the theories of empathy-altruism and planned behavior, this
study investigated beliefs about the factors that motivate prosocial sentiments
among people in the privileged class of Pakistan during the
COVID-19 pandemic. In-depth interviews were conducted with 31 participants who
were deemed to be members of the privileged class within
the class system of Pakistan. The results revealed nine themes
including social interaction, peer influence, role models, collectivism, vicarious emotions,
religiosity, capability, volition, and education.
