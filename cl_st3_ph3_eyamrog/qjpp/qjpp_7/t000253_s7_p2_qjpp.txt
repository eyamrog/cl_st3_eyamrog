Why was such a statement considered necessary? As national vaccination
programmes started to gear up, it became clear that not
all countries were giving older people a high priority. For
example, in Bulgaria, people aged 65 and over were given
a lower status than several other large groups, including teachers
and many categories of civil servants (Kassova, 2021b ). Also,
in late April 2021, it was claimed that most people
aged over 80 were being denied hospital admission in order
to prioritize treatment for younger people (Kassova, 2021a ).
