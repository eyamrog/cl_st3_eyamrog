Having identified the most preferred vaccine characteristics from the conjoint
setup, I next turn to each of the specific hypotheses
outlined earlier. First, Fig. 2 presents strong support for H1a.
On average, respondents report that they are more likely than
not (58%) to intend to receive a US based vaccine.
Vaccination intentions drop to 52% for a vaccine produced in
the UK (β = −0.06), and just 37% for a
vaccine produced in China (β = −0.21); both of which
are statistically significant decreases ( p < 0.05 in both
cases).
