On February 6, 2021, the Director General of WHO stated:
“There is a disturbing narrative in some countries that it's
OK if older people die. It's not OK… It is
important that everywhere older people are prioritized for vaccination. Those
most at risk of severe disease and death from COVID-19,
including health workers and older people, must come first. And
they must come first everywhere.” (Ghebreyesus, 2021 ).
