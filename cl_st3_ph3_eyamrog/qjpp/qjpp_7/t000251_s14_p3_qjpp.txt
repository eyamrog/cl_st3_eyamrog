Over 300 probation officers emailed expressions of interest in the
study. Although I replied to every officer who expressed interest,
only ∼50% of those 300 officers ultimately scheduled an interview.
Thus, 151 probation officers were interviewed (∼2.5% of the total
email list). Interviews were conducted via telephone, recorded, and transcribed
in late 2019 and early 2020. Verbal consent was gathered
from all participants.
