The importance of identifying solutions is the focus of the
commentary by Romero-Canyas and Hiltner ( 2020 ), which concludes
this special issue. They argue that for social psychology to
contribute to developing solutions to the global problems facing us,
it must exploit its existing scientific strengths, embrace a problem-solving
approach, and broaden its scope to include community, social, cultural,
and national processes. All of this requires interdisciplinary collaboration for
us to translate social psychological insights into scalable solutions.
