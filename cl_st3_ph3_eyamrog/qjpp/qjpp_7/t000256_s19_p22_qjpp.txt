No research was undertaken on the Vehadarta campaign, during any
of the stages. According to the head of content and
digital marketing, the lack of research on this campaign was
due to two main reasons: budget problems (“We are a
small not-for-profit”) and mainly because of the urgency to run
a campaign due to the COVID-19 pandemic. “This campaign was
undertaken during an emergency. It was very intuitive. We didn't
have time to undertake a study, because we felt that
we needed to get going.” Nevertheless, she asserted that the
outreach to the audience of older people and the choice
of their message of raising their self-image was influenced by
academic studies concerning the harm caused by self-ageism.
