These sustainability problems are immediate, complex, and wicked (Brown, Harris,
& Russell, 2010 ). No single discipline can provide all
the answers to these problems, but many disciplines have something
unique to contribute. Social psychologists can contribute much to further
our understanding of the issues at the intersection of material
consumption and social justice, especially if they work in interdisciplinary
teams and connect to other disciplines in ways that use
their strengths. We hope that the papers in the present
special issue of the Journal of Social Issues in some
small way further this understanding and encourage these contributions.
