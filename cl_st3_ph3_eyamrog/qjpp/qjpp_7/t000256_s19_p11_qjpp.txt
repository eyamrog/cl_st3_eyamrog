The government ministry chose to name its campaign “age diversity.”
One of the campaign's organizers explained this decision, “Today, it's
very much in fashion to talk about diversity.” The idea
of diversity, presented in the campaign, was the motif in
all the creative materials. There were a variety of pictures,
a variety of slogans, a variety of video clips, a
range of ages, genders, sectors, colors, and a variety of
business/employment organizations. It can be assumed that the choice of
the term, diversity, aimed to harness and recruit the public
for societal change that would be supportive and inclusive. The
campaign was disseminated through integrated advertising, in traditional and new
media, and through the targeting of different audiences.
