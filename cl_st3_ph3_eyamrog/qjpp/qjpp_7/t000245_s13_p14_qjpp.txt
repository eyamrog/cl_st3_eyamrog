By ignoring the ways in which many barriers to action
are cultivated by powerful groups and individuals, the PBE fails
to raise the possibility that in order to respond adequately
to climate change, humans have to change how political and
economic power is distributed. Lowering our expectations at the psychological
level and not raising possibilities for change at the social
level leaves people with little reason to feel hopeful. To
be clear, we are not suggesting that people are mere
pawns of the powerful; in contrast, we hope to illuminate
issues of power and inequality so that people might effectively
resist being such pawns.
