Tracing social processes is especially important in the Canadian health
care system where legislation, regulation, and service provision is undertaken
at multiple levels (federal, provincial, and regional) and where there
are numerous organizations and individuals with different accountabilities who need
to manage patients ( Vedel et al. 2011 ). When
there is a lack of information, there may be an
important role for new “texts” play. However, IE emphasizes the
fact that texts stand in for social relations; they do
not stand on their own. To build solutions that can
coordinate action across jurisdictions, work must begin with the social,
not technical, which is what this project aimed to do.
