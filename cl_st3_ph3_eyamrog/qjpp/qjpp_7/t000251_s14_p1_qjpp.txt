The Rutgers University Institutional Review Board (IRB) approved this study.
Purposeful sampling was used to provide “information-rich cases…from which one
can learn a great deal about issues of central importance
to the purpose of the inquiry” (Patton, 2002 , p.
273). Further, purposeful sampling techniques allow for collecting data that
do not seek a single correct answer, but rather, seek
to examine the complexity of different conceptualizations of the research
objectives (Patton, 2015 ).
