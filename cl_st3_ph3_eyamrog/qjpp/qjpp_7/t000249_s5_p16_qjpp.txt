Moreover, considering that adolescents’ reasoning reflects messages received by parents
and society, the finding is also in line with previous
work from U.S. and European samples, whereby children from high-status
groups reported more negative intergroup attitudes when their parents also
were negative about intergroup attitudes, as compared to low-status children
and their parents (Degner & Dalege, 2013 ). Future research
should clarify the mechanisms by which adolescents learn about power
inequalities through socialization.
