In the context of limited supplies of COVID-19 vaccines and
limited capacity to administer them to large populations, large gaps
between theoretical entitlements in Table 1 and actual coverage are
to be expected. The clearest example of this was Bulgaria,
which in the face of limited uptake in the initial
phase of roll-out, removed all prioritization criteria. As a result,
it was reported that older people were required to compete
with people of all ages in lengthy vaccine queues (France
24, 2021 ).
