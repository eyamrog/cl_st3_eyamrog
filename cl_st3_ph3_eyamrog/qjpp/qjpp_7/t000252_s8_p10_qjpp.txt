A majority of studies on prosocial behavior have attempted to
identify similar reasons for helping others, such as religiosity, empathy,
compassion, and interpersonal altruism. These reasons primarily stem from EAT.
However, other potential predictors of altruistic behavior highlighted within TPB
may also help to explain the dimensions of prosocial behavior
including sharing, helping and comforting. The purpose of the present
study therefore was to integrate the two theories to explore
the motives underlying the prosocial behaviors of the privileged class
in Pakistan during the COVID-19 pandemic.
