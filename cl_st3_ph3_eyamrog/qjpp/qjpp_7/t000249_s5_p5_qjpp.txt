Thus, the concept of limited social mobility may be a
future avenue to investigate and integrate in the multidimensional measurement
of ERS. Similar concepts were previously identified as part of
the construct “racial barrier awareness,” for example, in the adolescent
racial socialization scale (Brown & Krishnakumar, 2007 ). Moreover, research
has yielded inconsistent findings on the associations between racial barrier
socialization and adolescent adjustment (Cooper & McLyod, 2011 ). Thus,
future research on how parental socialization messages vary among different
marginalized groups could shed light, not only the multidimensionality of
this construct, but also on the implications for adolescent development.
