Additionally, to better understand the relevance of this work for
other types of vaccines, scholars ought to consider replicating this
study with respect to other (voluntary) vaccines in development. Although
I was not able to do so in the present
study, researchers might consider conducting qualitative interviews with vaccine specialists
to determine which vaccine attributes are worth further study, and/or
conduct pre-test interviews with survey respondents to ascertain relevant vaccine
preferences. While elements of the conjoint experimental setup would almost
certainly need to be amended on a case-by-case basis, my
hope is that the utility of the paradigm makes this
an ideal approach for future work.
