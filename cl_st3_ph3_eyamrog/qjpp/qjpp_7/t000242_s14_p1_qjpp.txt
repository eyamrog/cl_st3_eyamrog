From August 20 to 28, 2021, we conducted an online
survey of office workers living in Kochi prefecture. A total
of 200 office workers who had taken the Tosa MBA
course were randomly selected to take the survey. They were
all from Kochi prefecture. A total of 200 non-participants, living
in Kochi prefecture, of the Tosa MBA were distributed the
survey from a database owned by Cross Marketing Group Inc,
a Japanese research company. Finally, 103 valid responses were obtained
for participants and 74 valid responses were obtained for non-participants.
