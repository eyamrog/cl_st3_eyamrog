In this campaign, there were no activities undertaken with the
target audiences, and it solely focused on media publication. The
campaign's organizers located three to four heterogeneous employees from well-known
and respected companies. From each company, a pair of employees
was selected and the two were filmed and used in
campaign materials. Perhaps, for the teams of presenters, the campaign
included an “activity” of a joint day of shooting. However,
there was nothing beyond this.
