By discussing various ways to bring individuals from different groups
into contact, we explicitly address a shortcoming of previous work
in the field anecdotally referred to as “the leading-the-horses-to-water problem”
(Pettigrew et al., 2011 , p. 278). Pettigrew et al.
argued that practically relevant intergroup contact research should not solely
focus on the effects of contact, but also on the
ways in which one can bring past adversaries together in
optimal contact situations.
