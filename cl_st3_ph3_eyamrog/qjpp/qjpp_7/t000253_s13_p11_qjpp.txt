It can therefore be argued that the limited inclusion of
older people in drug trials was not intrinsically unjust, according
to our YLLs yardstick. That does not mean that these
decisions were not influenced by ageist mindsets which have been
identified by other studies (Kanik et al., 2022 ). For
example, in justifying excluding older people, Indonesia's Sinovac clinical trial
leader, said, “Why do we target people of a productive
age? Because these people can work hard, so the country
will not have a deficit” (BBC News Indonesia, 2020 ).
