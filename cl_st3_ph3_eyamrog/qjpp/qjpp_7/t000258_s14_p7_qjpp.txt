Self-esteem was assessed using the Rosenberg's 10-item Self-Esteem Scale composed
of five positive and five negative questions about feelings of
self-worth. The scale constitutes an uni-dimensional factor structure which was
easy to use, quick to administer and has been validated
in many cultural contexts including South Africa ( Baranik et
al., 2008 ; Corwyn 2000 ; Makhubela and Mashegoane 2017
; Westaway et al., 2015 ). All items were answered
using a 4-point Likert scale design ranging from “strongly agree”
to “strongly disagree”. For the present study, the internal consistency
was 0.76.
