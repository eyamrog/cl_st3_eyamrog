The World Health Organisation's conceptualization of ageism, distinguishes between stereotyping,
prejudice and discrimination (WHO, 2021a ). Ageism is relevant to
all age groups, including younger people, and intersects with other
forms of prejudice, such as racism and sexism. This paper
focuses on health policy discrimination against older people, reflecting the
specific vulnerabilities of this age group to COVID-19. The paper
also interrogates explanations or justifications provided by policy-makers, establishing links
between discrimination, stereotyping and prejudicial attitudes.
