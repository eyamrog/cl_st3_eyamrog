Regarding its effectiveness – it was found that among those
who were exposed to the campaign, there was an increase
in public awareness of the term “age diversity,” there was
an increase in the perception of the possible contribution of
age diversity to the workplace and there was an increase
in the willingness of employers and recruiters to hire people
over the age of 60. However, the impact was not
examined over time and the study's results were not published.
Moreover, the same people who developed and delivered the social
campaign are the ones who evaluated it.
