Stata 15 was used to conduct the PSM analyses (
Stuart et al., 2009 ). In order to assess the
robustness and validity of our model results, we conducted sensitivity
analysis in which we modified the stratification of the main
socioeconomic characteristics: education in three categories (no schooling, lower than
secondary, secondary schooling and higher), welfare index in three categories
(20% poorest, 60% middle, 20% richest). We also tested whether
our results were sensitive to an alternative specification of the
outcome variable, where stigma, depression and self-esteem were dummy instead
of continuous variables.
