The rapid assessment survey was employed using a telephone-based data
collection approach with a cross-sectional design to target citizens aged
18 and above in nine regional states and two city
administrations in Ethiopia 1 ( Figure 1 ). The sampling
of this survey aimed to construct a nationally representative urban
and semi-urban sample that could help draw inferences about understanding
of the various preventive practices being undertaken in response to
COVID-19 as well the public’s level of risk perception, knowledge,
and attitudes. A telephone-based survey was the preferred option because
it was impossible to conduct face-to-face interviews during the onset
of COVID-19. A stratified random sampling strategy was used in
which the regional states/city administrations constituted the strata. A sample
size of 1,050 respondents was estimated for the eligible population
based on a 3 percent sampling margin of error at
a 95 percent confidence level.
