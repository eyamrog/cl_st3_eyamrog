All four countries in Table 1 included specific occupational categories
in their COVID-19 vaccination prioritization. In some cases, these groups
are given the same priority as older people, and, in
the cases of “front-line workers” in India and mink farm
workers in Bulgaria, a higher one. The precise definitions of
some of these categories lack specificity, especially for those referred
to variously as “front-line”, “key” or “essential workers.” Again, it
can be seen that Brazil applied more specific occupational categories
and placed these below older people.
