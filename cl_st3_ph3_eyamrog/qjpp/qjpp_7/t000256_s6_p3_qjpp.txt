We found that the three organizations that led campaigns for
the eradication of ageism in Israel are aware of the
importance of planning strategically and of the use of online/offline
media. Therefore, they dedicated resources of time, money and thought
to these two principles. However, they failed to incorporate a
strong evaluative component in their campaigns. Such an evaluation should
have included both formative and outcome perspectives. The COVID-19 pandemic
and the ageism pandemic which followed required a quick and
urgent response in the form of social campaigns that tended
to neglect the evaluative aspects of the campaign.
