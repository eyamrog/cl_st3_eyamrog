Items were adapted from van Zomeren et al. ( 2010
, 2013 ) and Hanss and Böhm ( 2010 ).
Four items measured each of the four types of goal
efficacy beliefs: direct goal self-efficacy ( α = .66), indirect
goal self-efficacy ( α = .78), direct goal collective efficacy
( α = .70), and indirect goal collective efficacy (
α = .72). See Table 1 for exemplary items.
