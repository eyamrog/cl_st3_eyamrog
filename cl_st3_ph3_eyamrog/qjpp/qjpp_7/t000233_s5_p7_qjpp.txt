The context of academic research also largely influenced our involvement
in and implementation of the project. Although all authors felt
some form of internal motivation for joining the project (e.g.,
a desire to improve working people’s lives) and all believed
to some degree that the work might result in important
policy change, external motivation in the form of career goals
was also somewhat present. We mentioned hoping the project would
bolster our curriculum vitae or provide new skills. These reflections
demonstrate that even with internal motivation present, the potential pressure
toward publication and academic skill building that exist for all
academics employed in public universities ( Brienza 2016 ; Luka
et al. 2015 ) affected how we designed and used
the research.
