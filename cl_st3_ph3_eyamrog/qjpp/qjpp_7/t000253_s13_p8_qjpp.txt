Several early trials for COVID-19 vaccines included only minimal numbers
of people at older ages or none at all. For
example, Sinovac trials in China were only conducted on adults
aged under 60 (Zhang et al., 2021 ). No initial
COVID-19 vaccination trials included people with pre-existing health conditions, which
further reduced their validity for many older people. This led
to a seemingly absurd outcome that vaccines were not trialed
on the very people who needed them most.
