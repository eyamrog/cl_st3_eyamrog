Health care delivery in Canada involves eHealth components that are
essential to ensuring continuity across a patchwork of services administered
at different jurisdictional levels. It is important that eHealth design
attend to these social relations. The work of Canadian sociologist
Dorothy Smith has great value in providing an approach for
mapping the extra-local connections through which health work is accomplished,
recognizing that the texts that “write the social” are increasingly
electronic ( D. Smith 1999 ).
