Evaluation results are provided in the Findings section with the
caveat that it was not designed as a “mixed-methods” study.
The intention is not to have quantitative data make a
“cameo appearance” to add validity to ethnographic findings ( Hancock,
Sykes, and Verma 2018 :314), but to demonstrate that the
project evaluated whether the solution arrived at through the use
of IE concepts addressed the problem originally identified.
