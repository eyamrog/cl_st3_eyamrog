On the question of whether therapists are loyal to colleagues
or clients, the therapists argue that there are risks to
being allied with the client, the greatest being the loss
of objectivity. T2 states, We therapists sometimes have different perspectives
on how to treat our clients, but we also have
some sort of common reference. In our case, it is
ATV as our working method. However, we balance not relying
too strongly on only one single method and becoming too
eclectic.
