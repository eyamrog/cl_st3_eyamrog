As noted, a large number of social determinants have been
identified and explored in the literature as important factors in
shaping health outcomes. It has further been observed that these
factors are directly influenced by patterns of social stratification and
social hierarchy, including class, income status, gender, and race/ethnicity (
Solar and Irwin 2010 ; Braveman and Gottlieb 2014 ).
Nonetheless, while there exists a wide range of possible SDH
that could be incorporated into epidemiological modelling, we have selected
three of the most commonly discussed with a clear and
explicit bearing on the infection rate and on the CFR.
We briefly discuss these SDs, their global variability, and their
potential impact on the pandemic.
