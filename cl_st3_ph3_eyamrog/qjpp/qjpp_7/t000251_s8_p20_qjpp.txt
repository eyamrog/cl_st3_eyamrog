Ultimately, if displays of true remorse are indeed misread and
disregarded by probation officers during the sentencing of certain defendants
charged with violent offenses, remorse bias is likely to have
significant consequences. Indeed, as judges’ sentencing decisions are most often
in line with probation officer recommendations in their pre-sentencing reports
(Freiburger and Hilinski, 2011 ; Leiber et al., 2018 ),
the potential effects of remorse bias could be both pervasive
and meaningful for the punishment trajectories of defendants who are
subjected to implicit assumptions of violence due to their backgrounds.
