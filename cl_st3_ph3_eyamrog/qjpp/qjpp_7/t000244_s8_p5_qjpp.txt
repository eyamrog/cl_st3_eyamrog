We also draw on qualitative findings that positive affective states
of enthusiasm and confidence accompany PEBs like environmental activism (Drury,
Cocking, Beale, Hanson, & Rapley, 2005 ). Although Jugert and
collegues ( 2016 ) did not find an association of
general positive affect with PEB, more efficacy-associated affects like “hope”
were indeed related to it (Ojala, 2015 ). This finding
reflects Bandura's ( 1997 ) notion that general affective and
physical states are associated with cognitive efficacy beliefs.
