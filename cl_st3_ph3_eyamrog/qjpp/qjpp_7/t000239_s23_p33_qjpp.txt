To test this model econometrically, I have put together a
database of 135 countries for a cross-sectional regression on the
two days, with the estimator used for Equation 1 a
standard ordinary least squares regression with standard errors clustered on
the country variable; given the large number of plausible covariates,
a stepwise general-to-specific (GETS) method in the manner of Hendry
(1995) is used to narrow down the specification to only
significant variables other than those variables of interest. Given the
patchy availability of some institutional data, this yields in reality
a number of observations between 103 and 119 for the
