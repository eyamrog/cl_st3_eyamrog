Whereas Harvey was writing about the time up to 1990,
Davis (2017) describes the multidecade cultural shift in corporate structure
since the 1990s. He notes that larger firms still have
large financial assets but rely on a smaller primary labor
market as outsourcing to the secondary labor market. In turn,
the expansion of a more fragmented secondary labor market makes
regulating employment precarity more difficult ( Davis 2017 ). As
a result of these emerging flexible standards, entire sectors of
the economy have transitioned to using precarious labor ( Lewchuk
et al. 2014 ).
