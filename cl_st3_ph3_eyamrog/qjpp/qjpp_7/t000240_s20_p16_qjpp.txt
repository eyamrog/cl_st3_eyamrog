Further analysis of the above five factors using one-way analysis
of variance (ANOVA; see Table A1 of the appendix )
indicated that residence type was significantly associated with attitude and
perception scores but not with knowledge score. The sex of
the respondent was found to have a significant association (
p < .05) with the knowledge and perception score. There
was a significant mean difference according to the age and
education of the respondents for both knowledge and attitude scores.
The job category variable appeared to be significant across the
three outcomes ( p < .001).
