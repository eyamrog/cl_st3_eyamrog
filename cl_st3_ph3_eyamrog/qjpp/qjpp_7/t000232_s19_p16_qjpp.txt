Pilot testing included technical testing to ensure the messages were
delivered correctly and on time, and “real-world” testing of admissions,
deaths, and discharges during the study period. A total of
943 notifications were sent during the 4 months. At the
end of the pilot, FPs rated the value of the
system: 96 percent agreed/strongly agreed that eNotification was an asset
to their practice; 72 percent reported they scheduled or canceled
an appointment as a result of the notification; 68 percent
found that they were better prepared to discuss a patient’s
care with them or their families; and 83 percent thought
that the notification enabled them to provide better care. Complete
