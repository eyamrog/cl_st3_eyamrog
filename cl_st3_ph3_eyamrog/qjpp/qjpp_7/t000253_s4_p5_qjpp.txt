Additionally, age-specific case fatality for men is significantly higher than
it is for women. Whether prioritizing vaccination for less advantaged
groups, or for men, would save more or fewer overall
YLLs would be balance between their relative risk of dying
and their age-specific life expectancy (which tends to be lower
for men than for women). This is essentially a theoretical
concern: the complexity of making and implementing such a calculus
would be unfeasible in the real world of vaccine roll-out.
