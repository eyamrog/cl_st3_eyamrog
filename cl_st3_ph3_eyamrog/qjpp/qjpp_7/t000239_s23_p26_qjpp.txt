psychological, political, or even economic standpoint on what drive the
particulars of an economic lockdown in response to a pandemic.
However, there is a small literature in political science and
public health which examines the drivers of either health policy
or health efficiency (e.g., Correa and Namkoong 1992 ), acknowledging
the prominent role for politics in driving various health outcomes
( Muntaner et al. 2011 ; Patterson and Veenstra 2016
) or in fashioning crisis management and responses ( Haeffele
