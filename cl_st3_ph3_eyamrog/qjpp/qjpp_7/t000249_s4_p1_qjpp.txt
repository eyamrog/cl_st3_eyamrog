Taking a developmental science approach, this study investigated how adolescents
living in a country with a long history of societal
and structural inequalities, conceptualized obstacles to social equality. While the
awareness of specific social barriers increased with age, most adolescents
were critical, referring to moral concerns about the benefits of
social equality. Adolescents explicitly reflected on their awareness of the
social barriers due to social hierarchies, and the negative consequences
for social inclusion.
