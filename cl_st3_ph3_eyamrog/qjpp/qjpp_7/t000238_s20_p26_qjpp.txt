The therapists agree that clients often anticipate the expectations of
them. T2 argues that this kind of process involves identification:
“It is part of their transformation, and adaption to norms,
but are they lying to us, yes they are!” The
therapists want to see straight through the lies, but do
not believe they can. T2 adds, Yes that is what
you want . . . group processes contribute to this
. . . and in this group, you want to
capture this kind of thing in some kind of way.
They express what we longed for . . . maybe
it is unconscious, but it feels genuine.
