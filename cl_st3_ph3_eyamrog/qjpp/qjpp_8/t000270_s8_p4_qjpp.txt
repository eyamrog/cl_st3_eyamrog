Although we know quite well how small-world properties and homophily
affect disease spread in networks separately, it is less clear
how the two shape epidemics in combination. Furthermore, network models
of disease spread typically neglect adaptive changes in the network
structure, despite their potentially large effect on predictions ( Bansal
et al., 2010 ). We therefore ask: What are the
effects of health behavior homophily on epidemics in adaptive small-world
networks? and How do epidemics differ between adaptive and static
networks?
