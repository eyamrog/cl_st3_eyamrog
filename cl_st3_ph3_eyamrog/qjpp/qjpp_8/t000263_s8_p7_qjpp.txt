Why might past studies’ findings conflict? Being open is essential
for sexual minorities to develop a sense of community with
other sexual minorities, but it also changes how sexual minorities
view the status of their group. As we will outline
below, openness likely activates different social psychological mechanisms that operate
on subjective well-being and mental health – some beneficial and
some adverse. Together, these potentially opposing mediators lead to the
weak association between openness and both subjective well-being and mental
health – an association that, at the aggregate level, masks
the importance of these distinct forces.
