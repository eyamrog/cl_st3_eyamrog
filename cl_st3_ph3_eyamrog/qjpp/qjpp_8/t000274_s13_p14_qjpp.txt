In recent years there has been a move towards greater
archiving of datasets in national or international repositories ( Tilley
and Woodthorpe 2011 ; Irwin 2013 ; Parry and Mauthner
2004 ). Archiving data in this way (i.e. for use
by researchers beyond a project or single institution) is frequently
required by research funding bodies and highly recommended by journals.
In addition to reducing costly repetition of data collection, an
important rationale for archiving datasets is to enable greater access
to datasets, thus encouraging replication of studies and verification of
findings.
