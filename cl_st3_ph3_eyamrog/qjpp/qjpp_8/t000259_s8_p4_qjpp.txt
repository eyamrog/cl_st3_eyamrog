I find that Americans have strong preferences for vaccine candidates
produced in the US, that are highly (at least 90%)
effective at protecting against infection, and that offer a low
chance of experiencing minor side effects (1 in 100). This
finding is potentially problematic, as leading vaccine candidates may miss
some or all of what Americans deem to be optimal
benchmarks. Worryingly, intended vaccine refusal rates exceed 30% for even
a vaccine that meets all of the above specifications.
