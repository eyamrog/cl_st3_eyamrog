reflect an integration of previous insights that, to our knowledge,
have not yet been brought together and simultaneously tested. That
is, instead of identifying both the potential benefits and costs
of openness, previous work has seemed to illustrate one or

Yet, as we can test here, these previous findings are
not necessarily conflicting: instead, they are limited in their ability
