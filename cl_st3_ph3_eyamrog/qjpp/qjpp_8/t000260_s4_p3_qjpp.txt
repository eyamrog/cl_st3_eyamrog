First, using available data regarding relevant SDs and also the
data regarding the contagion dynamics of COVID-19 across countries, it
is possible to more closely quantify the effects of the
various SDs. This would contribute towards: (i) having a better
understanding of the importance of SDs with regards to infections
and deaths (ii) estimating the economic benefits of improving relevant
SDs and (iii) running policy simulations using the SEC model
to analyse the health effects of different policies.
