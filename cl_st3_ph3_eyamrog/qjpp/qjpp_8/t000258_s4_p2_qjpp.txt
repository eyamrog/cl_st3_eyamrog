Nonetheless, South Africa has a unique constitution endorsing equal rights
(civil, political, social and economic) for persons with disabilities (
Government of South Africa 1996 ). If rights are politically
endorsed, their implementation on a day-to-day basis remains elusive in
many areas from access to health care to right to
employment ( Hussey et al., 2017 ; Wiggett-Barnard and Swartz
2012 ). This state of affairs leaves persons with disabilities
vulnerable and at the margins of society reinforcing the observed
vicious circle of disability, unfair treatment, and poor mental health
outcomes.
