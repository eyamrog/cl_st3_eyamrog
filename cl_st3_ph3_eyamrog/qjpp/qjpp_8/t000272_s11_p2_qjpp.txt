The specific policy documents investigated herein are the statutes concerning
opioids chaptered by the California State Senate and Assembly. As
( Lian, 2020 ) states, “applying a corpus-assisted approach to
the language of lawmakers can provide a glimpse into the
ideologies of policymakers and politicians who create legislation” (p. 138).
The language of policies permits the investigation into the current
state of the community that implements it ( Ramanathan and
Morgan, 2007 , Wodak, 2006 ). After all, the importance
of policies relies on the need that calls for it.
