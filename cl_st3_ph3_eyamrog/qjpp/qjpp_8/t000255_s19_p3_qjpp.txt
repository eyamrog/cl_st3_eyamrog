Table 2 shows results from the MLMs. The first model
predicted overall subjective memory trajectories across the study period using
linear and quadratic terms for time with no covariates in
the model. Both the linear and quadratic slopes were statistically
significant. The linear slope indicator was negative, Estimate (SE) =
−.042 (.004), and the quadratic slope indicator was positive, Estimate
(SE) = .003 (.001). These coefficients indicate that subjective memory
followed a curve from April 2020 to January 2021, with
a sharper decline in the initial months of the pandemic
and a subsequent leveling off in rate of decline.
