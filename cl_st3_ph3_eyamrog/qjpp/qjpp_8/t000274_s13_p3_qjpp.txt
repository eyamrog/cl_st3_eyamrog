Since it is generally not possible to include all texts
under investigation, it is necessary to select texts ‘on the
basis of stringent criteria or by random sampling’ (Mautner, 2019:8).
A clear difficulty here is in determining the nature of
the totality of texts since we may only discover that
a corpus is unrepresentative if the results are skewed (cf.
Tognini-Bonelli 2001 ). Many means of sampling rely on all
items within the target population being available for selection (see
discussion in Biber, 1993 ).
