As discussed in section 5, the results from EEBO are
based on the lemmatized search strings COME _v?g*, GO _v?g*,
and RUN _v?g*. This kind of search retrieves both instances
where the oblique complement is explicit (e.g., came running into
the room ) and those where it is latent (e.g.,
came running ), since it was thought that having information
at hand on the latter subtype might prove useful. One
disadvantage of this procedure was that a considerable amount of
manual sorting was needed in order to quantify the number
of actual VV ing OBL examples attested in the six
decades in EEBO (1470s, 1480s, 1490s, 1550s, 1570s, 1640s; see
section 5) that were selected for analysis.
