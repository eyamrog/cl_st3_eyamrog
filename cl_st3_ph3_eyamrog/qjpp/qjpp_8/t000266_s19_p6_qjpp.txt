Results for the question about whether the importance of the
different well-being domains had changed due to COVID-19 were the
following: The domains ‘physical health’, ‘mental health’, ‘independence’, and ‘social
life’ generally appear to have become more important (suppl. material,
Figure A2 ). Depending on the dimension, between 61% and
74% of respondents indicated that each respective domain had remained
equally important for their well-being, with the lowest value observed
for ‘social life’.
