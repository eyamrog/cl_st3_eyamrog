As the worsening US opioid crisis continue to be a
fraught issue ( Torres et al., 2020 ), changes in
the frequency of restrictive and permissive modals across time can
determine whether the general perception of the opioid crisis are
reflected in the framing of policies. Note that each modal
would have appeared in a unique policy clause; therefore, the
frequency of restrictive or permissive modal is synonymous with the
number of restrictive and permissive clauses.
