Earlier we saw how patients with under 1 year of
treatment were more likely than the other groups to praise
aspects of service provision that are evident from a single
(the first) visit to a provider. This is also true
of criticisms, with this group being more likely than the
others to raise the theme of facilities in their complaints
(10% vs. 2% in both of the other groups), including
parking facilities (4% vs. no cases in the other groups).
