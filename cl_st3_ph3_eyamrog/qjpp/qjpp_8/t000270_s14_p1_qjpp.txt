Although numerous studies were concerned with the effects of small-world
properties ( Badham and Stocker, 2010 ; Keeling, 1999 ;
Miller, 2009 ; Watts and Strogatz, 1998 ) or homophily
( d’Andrea et al., 2022 ; Kitchovitch and Lì, 2010
; Koku and Felsher, 2020 ) on epidemics, there is,
to our knowledge, no study that looks into their interplay.
Furthermore, simulation studies typically consider static networks ( Badham and
Stocker, 2010 ; d’Andrea et al., 2022 ; Keeling, 1999
; Kitchovitch and Lì, 2010 ; Miller, 2009 ; Watts
and Strogatz, 1998 ). To study the combined effects of
small-world properties and homophily on epidemics in adaptive social networks,
we created the so-called Small-Worlds Infectious Disease Model ( SWIDM
), a specific model case of the Networking during Infectious
Diseases Model ( NIDM ; Nunner et al., 2021 ).
