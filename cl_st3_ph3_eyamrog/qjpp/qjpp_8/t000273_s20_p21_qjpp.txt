The involvement of multiple staff members, departments and hospitals over
time, which may, as we have discussed, garner a greater
appreciation of the value of treatment in patients in the
over 5 years category, also helps to explain why patients
in this group were most likely to praise treatment that
was well co-ordinated between different individuals, departments and sites (13%).
This was also commented upon, albeit less frequently, by patients
in the 1-5 years group (5%) but did not appear
at all in our sample of comments from patients within
the first year of treatment.
