From the 1992 recession until the financial crisis of 2008,
the UK economy had been on an upward trajectory. When
the financial crisis hit, GDP began to fall and took
five years to recover ( ONS, 2018 ). At the
same time, unemployment reached its highest level since 1995 and
took until 2015 to recover. Paradoxically, despite increasing levels of
employment since 2015, earnings did not equally recover and continued
to lag behind price increases. It took until 2019/2020 for
real pay to return to its pre-2008 recession peak (
ONS, 2021 ).
