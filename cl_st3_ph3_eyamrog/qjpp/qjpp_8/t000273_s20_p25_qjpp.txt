Another area where differences are small, but which is worth
commenting on nonetheless, is in comments where patients mention that
they feel they have been given sufficient time with practitioners.
This was mentioned in 4% of the positive comments from
patients with over 5 years of treatment, in 2% of
comments from patients with 1-5 years of treatment, and 1%
of our sample of comments from patients with under 1
year of treatment.
