to test the model shown in Fig. 3 . We

the Methods and found they constitute a reliable single measure
of mental health ( α = .85). Therefore, we present
results from that composite. Results for each dependent variable can
be found in Table S5-S6 , though the pattern of
results for individual dependent variables mirrors what is presented. A
post-hoc Monte Carlo power analysis determined that we had sufficient
