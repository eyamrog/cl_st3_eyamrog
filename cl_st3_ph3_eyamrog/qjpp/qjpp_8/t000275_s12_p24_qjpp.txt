In this section, I present two ways that online discussions
can be explored with a synergy of corpus analysis and
interactional approach by using the CWB tool. The first analysis
reveals the practice of concessions in online discussions by conducting
a micro-analysis on a thread that contains keywords identified from
a keyword analysis. The second analysis unravels in-depth discussions on
a specific topic among users by extracting threads with the
most mentions of specific terms.
