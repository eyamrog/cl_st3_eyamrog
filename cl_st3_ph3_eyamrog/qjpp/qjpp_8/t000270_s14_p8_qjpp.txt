For each agent j in J , i dissolves an
existing tie, if i 's utility (see section Utility) without
tie ij exceeds the utility with tie ij ; or
i proposes a new tie to j , if i
's utility with tie ij exceeds the utility without ij
. Note that only if j accepts the proposal (
j 's utility with tie ij also exceeds the utility
without ij ), a new tie ij is formed.
