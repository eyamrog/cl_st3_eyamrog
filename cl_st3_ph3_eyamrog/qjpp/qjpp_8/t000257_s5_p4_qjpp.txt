The findings of this study are also consistent with previous
studies that examined demographic characteristics as predictors of resilience and
stress: age ( Masten, 2002 ), gender ( Kimhi and
Eshel, 2016 ), community size ( Koziara et al., 2019
), and economic status ( Martini, 2020 ). In this
respect, it seems that the demographic predictors of different threats
are similar beyond the specific type of threat.
