Given the large descriptive system and the target population of
the WOOP, the optimal elicitation method was selected based on
a preceding study ( Himmler et al., 2020a ). Furthermore,
we undertook several steps to reduce the cognitive burden of
the choice tasks. Based on the responses to the cognitive
debriefing statements presented to respondents after the choice tasks, it
seems that the combination of a stepwise introduction to the
experiment, colour-coding, level overlap and the separation of the trade-offs
between well-being domains and duration was successful in reducing the
cognitive burden to a manageable amount for this sample of
older people.
