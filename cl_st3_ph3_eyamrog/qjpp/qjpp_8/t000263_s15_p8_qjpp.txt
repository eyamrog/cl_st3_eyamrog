Adjustment variables . Person-level adjustment variables included: two dummy codes
for gender (cis-female and gender diverse compared to cis-male); race/ethnicity
(Black, Hispanic, and “Other,” compared to White), age, age squared
( Blanchflower and Oswald, 2008 ), political conservatism (6-point scale),
education (7-point scale), religious community involvement (4-point scale), and income
(12-point scale).
