The campaign began with a meeting of the steering committee
that was comprised of representatives from the field staff of
older people from the community. Older adults were recruited and
worked on different functions, depending on their talents. During the
stage of campaign dissemination, there was full involvement of older
people. As a result, for example, in the exhibit of
the products in the “Global Virtual Museum,” and in the
Cinematheque's hall, the older people themselves walked around, presented and
served as guides.
