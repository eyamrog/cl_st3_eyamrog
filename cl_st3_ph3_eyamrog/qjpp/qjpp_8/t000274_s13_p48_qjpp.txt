The first worked example uses WMatrix semantic tagging software to
uncover similarities and differences between Corpus 1 and Corpus 2,
when compared to a reference corpus (see also Leedham, Lillis
and Twiner, 2020 ). Wmatrix (Rayson, 2008) was used to
extract key lexical items from each corpus in turn using
British English 2006 (BE06), a 1-million-word corpus of published general
written British English (Baker, 2009), as a reference corpus. 5
