ED attendance by children aged between six and 15 also
increased over the study period, with some of this increase
attributable to population changes. However, there may also be a
spill-over effect, with GP capacity constraints arising from this policy
resulting in parents of older children attending the ED to
access same-day healthcare. Attendance by children aged six to eight
did increase post-policy and this increase is concentrated over the
two regional hospitals. The policy impact may therefore be understated.
Capacity constraints due to increased GP attendance by the under
six cohort may also have a knock-on effect on access
to GPs by adults.
