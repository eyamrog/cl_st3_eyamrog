Four different composite measures of community vulnerability were considered here
as alternatives for the independent variable: Area Deprivation Index (ADI),
Social Vulnerability Index (SVI), COVID-19 Community Vulnerability Index (CCVI), and
the COVID-19 Pandemic Vulnerability Index (PVI). The ADI, SVI, and
CCVI measures are spatial only and remain constant over time,
whereas the PVI is dynamic and changes over time.
