Social policy-wise, our findings highlight several principles: First, the importance
of acknowledging diversity related to old age and aging, as
“older persons” represent a heterogeneous group spanning a range of
chronological ages, functional levels, and socio-economic contexts. Second, the need
for evaluation research to accompany social campaigns, even if conducted
during stressful times. We suggest the possibility of adopting action
research to mitigate some of the challenges associated with this
requirement. Third, the possible benefits associated with combining efforts of
different organizations, rather than work carried out separately by the
different organizations should be highlighted. The advantages and disadvantages associated
with the use of digital media also are evident.
