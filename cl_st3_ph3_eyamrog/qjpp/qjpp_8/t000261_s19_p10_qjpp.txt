Table 5 presents the policy effect by diagnosis for the
three Dublin hospitals, and by presenting complaint for the two
regional hospitals. What is striking is that the marginal probability
of a visit being GP referred increased overall for Injury
and most other categories, though there was some variability by
hospital. The only categories to experience a statistically significant decrease
in attendance were Digestive and Surgical at the Dublin hospitals.
