The parameter of decategorialization ( Hopper 1991 :30-31), whereby grammaticalizing
units lose some of their markers of categoriality, including their
morphosyntactic trappings, also applies to the VV ing OBL construction.
Goldberg (2006 :51; see also section 8.2) aptly notes that
the V ing form cannot appear with its own arguments
(as illustrated in 45), unlike the related paraphrase involving a
subordinate clause (i.e., “Bill went down the street whistling a
tune ”).
