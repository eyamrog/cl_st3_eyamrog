Objective: Health economic evaluations using common health-related quality of life
measures may fall short in adequately incorporating all relevant benefits
of health and social care interventions targeted at older people.
The Well-being of Older People measure (WOOP) is a broader
well-being measure that comprises nine well-being domains. The objective of
this study was to estimate a utility tariff for the
WOOP, to facilitate its application in cost-utility analyses.
