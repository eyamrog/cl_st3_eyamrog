Additionally, Fischer (2000 , 2001 ) points to a visible
tendency of “adverbial” adjectives to occur postnominally. These adjectives seem
to refer to time, direction, degree, etc. and it is
clear that, similarly to stage-level adjectives, they do not describe
any enduring properties of their nouns. Consequently, in this study,
