It is not uncommon that a lexical bundle has more
than one function (e.g., Biber et al. 2004 ; Cortes
2015 ). It would be very difficult, if not impossible,
to assign a specific function to each one of the
instances, given their sheer number. We therefore decided to follow
previous studies such as Chen and Baker (2010) and Ädel
and Erman (2012) in assigning one function (the main function
as identified in the concordance) to each bundle type. Some
clausal fragments and incomplete verb phrases without a clear function,
such as that it is, has been the, and do
not, were put under an additional category (‘indeterminate’).
