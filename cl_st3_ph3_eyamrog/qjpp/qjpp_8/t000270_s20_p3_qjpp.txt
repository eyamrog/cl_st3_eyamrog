Fig. 2 shows bivariate effects of network properties on epidemics
in adaptive networks. The data reveal negative effects on final
size for homophily, network clustering, and average path length. Furthermore,
final size is mostly stable at high levels in networks
with low levels of homophily ( < 0.45 ) and
clustering ( < 0.45 ) . In contrast, final size
drops off quickly for low levels of average path length
and remains stable for higher levels of average path length
( > 3.0 ) . Interestingly, multivariate regression analyses show
that the effect of average path length on final size
vanishes when controlled for all other independent variables ( Table
