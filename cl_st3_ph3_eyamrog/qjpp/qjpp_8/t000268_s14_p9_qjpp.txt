Fig. 2 provides a graphical analysis of the distribution of
average suicide rates within the regions in each continent. The
box plots show upper-average levels in Europe. Lesotho , Eswatini,
Botswana and South Africa have particularly high average rates, despite
having a lower average rate than Europe and Oceania .
When the results are broken down by region, notable differences
can be observed within each continent. Southern Africa, Northern America,
Western Asia, Eastern Europe and Micronesia had the highest average
suicide rates in the first two decades of the current
century. As the WHO (2021) noted, the prevalence and characteristics
of suicidal behaviour vary widely between different communities and over
