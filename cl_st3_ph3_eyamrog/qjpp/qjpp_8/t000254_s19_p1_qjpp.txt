It is remarkable that the cross-cultural analysis undertaken independently, and
given the differences both in culture and in the timeline
of the interviews, found similar results. Overall, the pandemic has
exacerbated ageism, and in turn, that ageism has impacted the
lives of older adults. We discuss the three main themes
in turn: benevolent versus hostile ageism; ageing viewed as homogenous;
and lost autonomy. As noted above, we provide literal (rather
than grammatically correct) translations of the Spanish to English to
reflect the context of the quotes.
