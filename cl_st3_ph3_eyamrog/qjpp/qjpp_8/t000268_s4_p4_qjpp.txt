The present study had several limitations. As has been noted,
the findings may have been influenced by several biases derived
from the measurement of suicide and uncertainty. In connection with
this, the aggregate nature of the data did not allow
us to analyse potential discrepancies between different socioeconomic groups. In
addition, given the complex interplay between the very diverse factors
that affect suicidal behaviour (some of which have not, for
various reasons, been considered), additional potential biases may have arisen.
Finally, future researchers might consider applying panel local projections to
control for time-invariant factors and employing alternative techniques to model
potential non-linear relationships between variables.
