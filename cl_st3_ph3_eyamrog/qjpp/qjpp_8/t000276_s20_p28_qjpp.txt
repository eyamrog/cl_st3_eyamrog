Therefore, a possible way of interpreting these findings in a
constructionist framework could assume the existence of formally distinct constructions
falling under one abstract schema: asyndetic, which sanctioned both the
(outgoing) ANA and the (incoming) AAN constructions, and conjoined, which
sanctioned both the (outgoing) ANCA and the (incoming) ACAN constructions.
Traugott and Trousdale (2013 :65-66) and Hilpert (2013 :463-464) notice
that such temporary coexistence is not uncommon and indicates a
change in progress. The mutual relations between both split patterns
and their respective prenominal counterparts are discussed more fully in
