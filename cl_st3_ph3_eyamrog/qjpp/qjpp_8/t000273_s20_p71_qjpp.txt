Compared to patients who have received treatment for a year
or more, feedback provided by patients within their first year
of treatment can be characterised in terms of the telling
of narratives relating to a single episode of treatment or
at least a small number of treatment episodes. These stories
are characteristically located within the past, which explains the keyness
of both the past continuous verb was and its plural
equivalent, were , as well the past tense verbs did
and had .
