As in my earlier research on the development of various
motion patterns in the history of English ( Fanego 2012
, 2017 , 2019 ), the analysis of the VV
ing OBL construction and its precursors since OE draws on
data gathered from a variety of sources. For usage in
Old and Early Middle English, I relied chiefly on Ogura
(2002) and Visser (1963-1973 :III/1 1391-1400, III/2 1906-1912). These sources
were supplemented with the following reference works: the Dictionary of
Old English ( DOE ), the Middle English Dictionary (
MED ), and the Oxford English Dictionary ( OED ).
