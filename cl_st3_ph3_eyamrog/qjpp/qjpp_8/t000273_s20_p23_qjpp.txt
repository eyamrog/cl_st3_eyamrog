Patients in the 1-5 and over 5 years groups also
shared a focus on the quality of aftercare (5% and
9%, respectively) – a theme which was visible in just
1% of the comments in the under 1 year sample.
This feature may reflect the fact that patients who have
received treatment for longer durations are more likely to be
at the stage where they have received aftercare, or at
least have a better sense of what aftercare entails, having
received treatment for longer.
