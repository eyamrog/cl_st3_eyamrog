As well as focusing on individual staff members, the pronoun
keywords me and I indicate a characteristic focus on the
self in this feedback. The keyness of these items partly
reflects the narrative style that is characteristic of comments in
this group, where patients place themselves in their stories, for
example as the recipients of procedures and diagnoses. These pronouns
also reflect a greater emphasis on the disclosure of feelings
and emotions, as reflected in the keyness of felt and
impressed .
