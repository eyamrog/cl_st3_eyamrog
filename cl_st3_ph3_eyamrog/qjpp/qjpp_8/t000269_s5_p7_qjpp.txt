There are few analytical outputs drawing conclusions from the impact
of the 2008 financial recession on children and young people.
As such, there is a lack of knowledge about potential
lessons which could be exploited to support recovery from the
Covid-19 pandemic, particularly in relation to the expected worsening of
socioeconomic inequalities. We argue that there are obvious implications relating
to what impacts can be expected on children's wellbeing -
and how they come about - following an economic crisis.
