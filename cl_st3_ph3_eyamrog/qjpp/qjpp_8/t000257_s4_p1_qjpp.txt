This study focused on the investigation of factors that may
predict distress symptoms and sense of danger, including resilience, wellbeing
and demographic characteristics. The main conclusion of this study is
that the first and foremost predictors of COVID-19 anxiety are
individual resilience and well-being. Community and national resilience were not
found to be significant predictors of both a sense of
danger or distress symptoms. Though both predictors that were found
are complex and may be influenced by many factors, given
the potential return of COVID-19 threat and other future health
pandemic threats to our world, we must rethink and develop
ways to reinforce them.
