Because we were interested in LGBTQ + participants, we excluded
participants if they identified as both heterosexual and cisgender, or
if they were missing data on either their self-reported gender
or sexual orientation ( n = 362), or if they
were from outside one of the 50 US states (
n = 663). Finally, we excluded those who were missing
data on our key variables of interest ( n =
334). This left us with a sample of 3594 participants
( M age = 35.63, SD = 13.00) which was
racially diverse: 22.9 % White; 33.6 % Black; 13.9 %
Hispanic/Latinx; 5.4 % Asian/Pacific Islander; 2.2 % Native American; 22.0
% multiracial, other, or missing.
