Moreover, it is possible that a vaccine for COVID-19 will
need to be administered in multiple doses to achieve full
effectiveness. For example, early clinical trial data suggest that Moderna's
mRNA vaccine candidate must be administered in two doses to
stimulate a strong immune response ( Jackson et al., 2020
). Past research on other multi-dose vaccines suggests that Americans
are less likely to pursue vaccination -- and may have
more difficulty doing so -- when multiple doses are required
to achieve full effectiveness (e.g., Nelson et al., 2009 ).
As a result, I hypothesize that Americans will be less
likely to prefer vaccines that require just one dose to
achieve its projected effectiveness ( H5 ).
