This study used a latent variable approach to derive the
main covariates of interest. This approach confers an element of
uncertainty, which can be seen as a limitation; however, it
yielded satisfactory, robust results. Further, it allows a parsimonious way
to address the complexity of change over time. Another limitation
is that all measures of children's wellbeing are parent-reported, which
can carry measurement error due to over- or under-reporting. Future
waves of GUS incorporate children's self-reported wellbeing measures, which can
help alleviate this issue.
