In the commenting space, a user can create a new
post or reply to others’ posts or replies. I use
the term comments to refer to posts and replies collectively.
Each new post has the potential to elicit replies from
other users and initiate a thread of discussion, which is
where explicit user-user conversations occur ( Ksiazek and Lessard, 2016
). I call the posts that receive replies initiating posts
( n = 32,333). However, most posts are left unresponded,
which I call independent posts ( n = 118,606). The
number of initiating posts, independent posts and replies, and corresponding
