Utility tariffs for the WOOP are currently lacking, which clearly
hampers its application in (economic) evaluations of health and social
care services for older people ( Neumann et al., 2016
). Therefore, the objective of this study is to estimate
a Dutch WOOP utility tariff. The structure of this paper
is as follows: the next paragraph specifies the methods, with
an emphasis on the design of the choice experiment and
the data collection; subsequently, the results are presented, including the
WOOP utility tariff; finally, we discuss our findings and their
implications.
