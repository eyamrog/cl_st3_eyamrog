Micro-analysis is another interactional approach widely used to examine discourse
practices in online discussions. The analysis adopts digital CA's focus
on how users respond to each other but does not
focus solely on adjacent pairs. For example, Ziegler et al.
(2014) examined an online discussion thread through micro-analysis and revealed
discourse practices employed by users to explore new ideas and
engage in negotiation, such as making underlying assumption explicit and
raising new questions.
