Recent years have seen great advances in the approximation of
economic uncertainty. Nevertheless, the question of what exactly is meant
by the term, and how it can be measured, are
still the subject of debate ( Dibiasi and Iselin, 2021
; Glas, 2020 ). Kozeniauskas et al. (2018) differentiated three
types of uncertainty: micro uncertainty (a cross-sectional variance in firm-level
outcomes); macro uncertainty (aggregate shocks); and higher-order uncertainty (disagreement). Castelnuovo
(2019) has provided an overview of recent developments in this
area.
