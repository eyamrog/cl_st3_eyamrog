One feedback theme – facilities – featured only in the
sample of comments from the under 1 year group (8%).
One explanation for this could be that patients in the
other groups are more familiar with the facilities by the
time they wrote their comments, having likely made multiple visits
to the care site in question. On the other hand,
many of the patients within the first year of treatment
category seem to be providing feedback based on their very
first visit to a provider, and so may be encountering
the facilities for the very first time.
