provided by patients who have received treatment for cancer for
differing lengths of time. The proportions of patients giving each
score, between 0 and 10, is shown in Table 2

a comprehensive picture of all feedback provided during the period
under study, but specifically those scores given by patients who
also provided free-text comments in addition to their quantitative ratings.
