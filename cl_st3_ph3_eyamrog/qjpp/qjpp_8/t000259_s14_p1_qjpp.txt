Data for this study come from a demographically representative survey
( N = 990) of US adults conducted on August
24–25, 2020. Respondents were recruited via Lucid Theorem's online opt-in
sampling service, which uses quota sampling to ensure representativeness on
nationally representative benchmarks for respondents' age, gender, race, educational attainment,
household income, four-category Census region, and political party (see: Callaghan
et al., 2019 for an example of U.S. vaccine hesitancy
research using this platform). Data from Lucid have been shown
to closely mirror known demographic benchmarks, and replicate well-studied experimental
findings ( Coppock and McClellan 2019 ). Please refer to
Supplemental Table S1 for a comparison of data from this
study to nationally representative demographic benchmarks.
