Direct effects among focal variables. Summarized in Table 2 ,

day-to-day life. Furthermore, in Study 2 we found a positive
direct effect of openness on the perception of both group-based
and personal discrimination, suggesting respondents who were more (vs. less)

effect of identity importance on perception of personal discrimination, but
