While existing research estimates that this policy increased attendance by
children aged under six at general practice by over 25%,
this policy did not lead to a reduction in ED
attendance. Hospital level effects on attendance varied from no impact
to increased attendance by children aged under six of 28.9%.
While increased GP referrals, particularly for injury and medical reasons,
indicated more patients presented to their GP prior to ED
attendance, walk-ins without referral did not decrease.
