This section explores the possible epistemological implications arising from the
existence of two corpora using the WiSP corpora as an
illustrative case study, focusing on three worked examples. The first
employs the whole of each corpus, comparing findings from Corpus
1 with Corpus 2; Example 2 slices the corpora by
text category and Example 3 returns to the whole corpora
for analysis of one lexical item. Extracts included in this
section from ‘NFS’, following our agreement with participants, have undergone
further minor wording changes and additionally have been confirmed by
participants as sufficiently anonymised. In each worked example, the wider
co-text is examined to explore the context of each concordance
line or keyword.
