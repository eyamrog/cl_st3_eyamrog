In this research, we seek to understand which of the
four composite measures perform best in explaining disease spread and
mortality, and we explore the extent to which mobility (or
the lack thereof) account for variance in the outcomes of
interest. First, we detail our data, variable operationalization, and methodological
approach. Next, we present results from the spatial and temporal
analysis and offer a discussion of the relevant findings. We
conclude with implications of our research for scholars, public health
practitioners, and policymakers.
