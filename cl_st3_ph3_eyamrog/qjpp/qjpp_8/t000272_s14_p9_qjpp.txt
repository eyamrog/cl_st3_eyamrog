Adhering to the calls made by The American Pain Society
(1999) and ( Department of Veterans Affairs 2000 ), the
California Board of Registered Nursing released a policy in 2000
requiring nurses to include pain along with temperature, blood pressure,
pulse rate , and respiration rate as vital signs gathered
during clinic intake. Therefore, nurses in the state ask patients
to rate their pain from one to ten. Nurses were
also tasked with taking appropriate action when the patient's pain
is not managed according to the agreed comfort level (
California Board of Registered Nursing, 2000 ).
