In the following, we discuss the results of our simulations
starting with a comparison of epidemics in adaptive and static
networks. Thereafter, we discuss how health behavior homophily affects epidemics
in adaptive and small-world networks and conclude with how these
dynamics differ in static networks. Additional analyses can be found
in Online appendix B – Additional analyses , where we
show, among others, that the effect of degree on final
size is negligible ( Effect of degree on final size
) and can therefore be omitted for further analyses.
