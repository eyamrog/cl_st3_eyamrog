More serious illness is likely to lead to a child
being admitted as an inpatient. A priori, one might expect
that the policy would lead to a higher proportion of
urgent cases, with less urgent cases being diverted to general
practice. However, there was no significant increase in the marginal
probability of a visit for a child aged under six
resulting in admission following the introduction of the policy (Appendix:
Table A2 ). There was an increase in the marginal
probability of visits being triaged as urgent and a decrease
in the marginal probability of the visit being triaged as
low urgency, though effects were small (<1pp).
