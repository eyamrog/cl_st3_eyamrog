Of the various co-events identified by Talmy (2000) , three
are directly relevant to the present study: manner, concurrent result,
and concomitance. A manner, in addition to a motion, is
expressed by walked in (8). In (9) and (10) the
forms clinked and crashing express a concurrent result: the people
make a clinking sound as a result of, and concurrently
with, their motion, and the rhinoceros produces a crashing sound
while moving through the jungle.
