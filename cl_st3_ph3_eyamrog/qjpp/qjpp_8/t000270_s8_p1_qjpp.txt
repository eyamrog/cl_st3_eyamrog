The COVID-19 pandemic has revealed the vulnerability of our globalized
world to the spread of infectious diseases. As a reaction
to this, many countries implemented physical distancing measures to lower
the number of infections for a disease mainly transmitted through
human-to-human contact in social networks ( Li et al., 2020
; Shen et al., 2020 ; WHO, 2021 ). In
other words, reducing social contacts should reduce the transmission of
the virus.
