While this campaign was not based on formal research of
an external body, internal questionnaires and surveys were conducted during
the campaign. The campaign was based on many years of
professional experience of the staff. At the end of the
campaign, data about exposure and involvement in publicizing materials on
the social networks were collected (percentages of watching, likes, shares,
etc.). Furthermore, an on-line and off-line questionnaire on attitudes was
distributed to learn about the impact of the campaign. The
organization is now awaiting its results. The CEO of the
not-for-profit summarized it in this way: “Using the internal resources
that we had, and within the limited time frame, we
couldn't undertake a thorough evaluation study. However, we are definitely
relating to this as a future aspiration.”
