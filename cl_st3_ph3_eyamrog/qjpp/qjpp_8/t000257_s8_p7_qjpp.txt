According to Bonanno et al. (2015) , community resilience (CR)
expresses the interaction between individuals and their community and refers
to the success of the community to provide for the
needs of its members and the extent to which individuals
are helped by their community. A recent literature review claims
that CR is associated with increased local capacity, social support,
and resources, and with decreased risks, miscommunication, and traumas (
Patel et al., 2017 ).
