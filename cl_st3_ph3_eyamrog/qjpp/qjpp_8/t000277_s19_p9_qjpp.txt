While it is not possible to extract all the tokens
of singular they in the corpora—as doing so would require
manual analysis of all tokens of they —it is significant
to note that in an earlier study I found 180
tokens of singular they in a subset of the BE06
corpus ( Paterson 2014 :51)—almost seven times the number of
combined pronouns ( N = 26) found here in the
whole corpus. Thus, there is clear evidence that writers of
BrE, at least, showed a preference for singular they in
the 2000s. Taking the corpora diachronically, the spike in combined
pronoun use in Figure 2 and the fact that combined
pronouns are independent of more general trends in third-person pronoun
use ( Figure 3 ), it is possible to argue
for the increased visibility of women in discourse. But is
the occurrence of combined pronouns enough to claim democratization?
