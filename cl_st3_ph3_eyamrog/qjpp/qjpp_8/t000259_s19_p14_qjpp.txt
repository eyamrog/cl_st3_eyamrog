Colum 3, presents the results of models which adds control

dichotomous indicator of whether or not respondents completed college), race
(indicators denoting whether respondents self-identify as Black [non-Hispanic] or Hispanic),

ranging from <$14,999 to $250,000 or more; and intervalized to
