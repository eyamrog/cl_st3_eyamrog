The sensitivity analyses in Appendix G confirm the robustness of
our estimates, both in terms of direction and size. We
find consistent results when using larger age bandwidths, with estimates
for males becoming more precise, and small and imprecise placebo
discontinuities at age 16 ( eTable 3. ). Estimates obtained
from a logistic model lead to similar conclusions, as well
as those allowing for postcode-year variation or not accounting for
any of the controls ( eTable 4. ).
