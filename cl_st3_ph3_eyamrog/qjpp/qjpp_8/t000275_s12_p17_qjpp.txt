More importantly, CWB allows a data structure that can authentically
encode the online discussions. As mentioned earlier, the comments in
the online discussions are nested within five units of analysis:
MOOCs, Weeks, Learning Pages, Threads, Comments. They were annotated in
XML according to TEI standard (TEI Consortium, 2020 ), as
shown in Fig. 3 , then subjected to treetagger (Schmid,
1994) for tokenization, lemmatization and part-of-speech tagging before being encoded
in the CWB. More details about the encoding procedures can
be found in the CWB manual ( Evert, 2021 ).
