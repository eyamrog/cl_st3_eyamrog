The DCE was embedded in an online questionnaire and administered
to citizens in the Netherlands aged 65 years and above
recruited from the panel of the market research company Dynata.
We aimed to sample around 2,000 respondents, representative in terms
of age and gender, using stratified sampling. After completion, respondents
could make a small donation to a charity of their
choice. Data collection took place between December 2020 and March
2021.
