We observed that countries in certain regions (e.g., Eastern Europe)
had much higher average rates of suicide during the period
under study (i.e., the first two decades of this century).
This was not the case in Africa − with the
exception of Southern Africa (i.e., Lesotho , Botswana, and South
Africa). North America, Western Asia, and Micronesia were the other
regions with the highest average suicide rates.
