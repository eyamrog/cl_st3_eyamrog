Participants had been involved in conducting interventions in 23 different
countries (7 high-income countries and 16 low- and middle-income countries,
see Fig. 1 for more precise breakdown). Multiple interventions were
covered including Problem Management Plus, Common Elements Treatment Approach, Self-Help
Plus, Step-by-Step, Community-Based Sociotherapy, Narrative Exposure Therapy, Interpersonal Therapy, Early
Adolescent Skills for Emotions, and Integrative Adaptive Therapy, among others.
