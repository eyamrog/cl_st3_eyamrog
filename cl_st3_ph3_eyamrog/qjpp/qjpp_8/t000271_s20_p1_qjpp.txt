This section is divided into three parts. Sections 4.1 and
4.2 present the quantitative results regarding the frequencies of lexical
bundles in the two corpora as well as the distribution
of different forms and functions. Section 4.3 complements the quantitative
results with an in-depth analysis of some of the most
frequent bundles in each corpus. Section 4.4 discusses in-text variation
