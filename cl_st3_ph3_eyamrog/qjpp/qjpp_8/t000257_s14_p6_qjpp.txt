A short version of 10 items of the community resilience
scale has been employed (CCRAM10; Leykin et al., 2013 ).
Items pertaining to the ‘security crisis' were modified into the
‘Coronavirus crisis'. This tool encompassed five main issues: social trust,
social support, leadership, emergency preparedness, and attachment to place (e.g.,
‘‘I trust the decision-makers in my community’‘). Items of this
scale were rated by a five-point scale ranging from 1
(does not agree at all) to 5 (totally agree). Reliability
of this scale was high ( α = 0.92).
