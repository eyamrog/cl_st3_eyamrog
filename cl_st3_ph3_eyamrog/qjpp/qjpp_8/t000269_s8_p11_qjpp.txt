An international review found that the impact of the financial
crisis on national economies resulted in a decline in children's
well-being after 2008 ( Fanjul, 2014 ). The impacts, such
as parental unemployment or income loss, were reported to make
children feel anxious and stressed ( Fanjul, 2014 ). A
further recent review similarly found that the 2008 financial crisis
negatively affected children's mental health and that children in low-income
families were more likely to experience a decline in mental
health compared to their wealthier counterparts ( Hiilamo et al.,
2021 ).
