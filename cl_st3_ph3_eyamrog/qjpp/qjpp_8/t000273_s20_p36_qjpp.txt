Patients with under 1 year of treatment were also most
likely, as they were with their positive comments, to base
negative feedback on a single episode of treatment. This gives
rise to complaints around the theme of treatment effectiveness (8%,
compared to 2% of the 1-5 years group and no
cases at all in the over 5 years group), with
particular focus on medication, including comments about the wrong medication
being given, requested medication being denied, and pain relief medication
being ineffective which leads to treatment being painful.
