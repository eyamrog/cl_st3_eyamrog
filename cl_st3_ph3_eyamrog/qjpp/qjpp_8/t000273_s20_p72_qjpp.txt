The narratives typically focus on a single operation or procedure,
indicated in uses of the keywords operation, radiotherapy , surgery,
biopsy and mammogram , as well as details of where
the operations took place ( theatre ), and events surrounding
the procedure ( discharge, recovery ). Other keywords tend to
be used to locate the events in the narratives within
time and to give a sense of the duration of
a stay in hospital ( weeks, night, days ), or
to otherwise sequentially arrange the events being recounted ( after,
from ).
