For reasons that fall beyond the scope of this paper,
the type cuman + infinitive becomes rarer towards the end
of ME and eventually disappears, the place of the infinitive
being taken by the present participle ( Mustanoja 1960 :536;
Los 1999 :222-224). The MED ’s comprehensive entry for comen
reflects this development well: sense 1c, ‘to approach or come
(in a certain manner),’ is illustrated with twenty-seven different quotations.
Of these, seventeen contain a form of comen + present
participle (now ending in - ing ), as in (30),
and the remaining eleven contain a form of comen +
infinitive.
