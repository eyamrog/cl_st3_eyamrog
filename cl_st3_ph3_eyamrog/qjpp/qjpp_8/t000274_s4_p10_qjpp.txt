The WiSP corpus is to date the only available collection
of social worker writing and represents a significant first step
in corpus creation for social work writing, extending the range
of texts available to researchers and enabling more systematic text-based
research into this professional discourse than has previously been possible.
We envisage that Corpus 2 will lead the way for
future researchers of social work writing practices and will also
be useful to social work educators: the texts have already
been useful in the creation of a site for trainers
and students (WiSP). We anticipate that the discussions and potential
solutions around anonymisation issues presented in this paper will be
beneficial for future corpus builders, particularly in the case of
sensitive datasets.
