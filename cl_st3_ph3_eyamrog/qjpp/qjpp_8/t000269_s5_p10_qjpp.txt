The main strength of this research is that it uses
a large, nationally representative, longitudinal sample of children. The data
allowed us to examine long-term trajectories of children's wellbeing along
with family circumstances, which enabled a nuanced and dynamic view
of how changing parental socioeconomic status impacts children's health. Further
research can build upon this and explore how parents' and
children's mental health are interrelated over time and how socioeconomic
circumstances can inhibit or induce poorer outcomes.
