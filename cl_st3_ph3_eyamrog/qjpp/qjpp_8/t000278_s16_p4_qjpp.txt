In order to identify the verbs occurring most frequently in
the VV ing OBL construction in both earlier and contemporary
English, I conducted searches in EEBO and the BNC, using
the string _vv* _v?g* _i*, which retrieves any verb followed
by V ing and a preposition. The search options were
set to 1000 hits, grouped by lemmas; this very high
figure (100 hits is the option selected for automatic searches
in many corpus-based studies) ensured the retrieval of a large
number of relevant strings, in their various forms (e.g., come
running , comes running , came running , go wand(e)ring
, goes wand(e)ring , goeth wandring , went wand(e)ring ,
etc.).
