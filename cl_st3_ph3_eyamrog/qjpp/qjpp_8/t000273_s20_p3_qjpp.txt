Overall, then, the proportions of patients giving each score are
similar across all categories, which suggests that duration of treatment
does not strongly influence satisfaction rates, and that the vast
majority of patients across all categories gave very positive feedback,
with scores of at least 8: under 1 year (87.69%),
1-5 years (86.27%), over 5 years (86.36%). However, we should
note that the largest differences between the categories also came
in the proportions of patients who gave the very highest
score of 10, where the 39.71% of patients with under
1 year of treatment who gave this score is approximately
4% higher than the proportions giving this score in both
of the other categories. Therefore, while the feedback is overwhelmingly
positive for patients regardless of the duration of their treatment,
patients who have received treatment for less than one year
are the most enthusiastic, being most likely of all the
groups to give the highest score of 10.
