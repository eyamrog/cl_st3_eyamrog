The primary outcome variable in this study is a measure
of whether or not respondents intend to receive each of
the vaccine candidates (labeled A-C in the first rating task,
and D-F in the second) to which they were randomly
assigned (more on this procedure below). I asked respondents, in
a matrix grid placed directly below the conjoint experiment to
“Please tell us how likely you would be to choose
to receive any of the following vaccines.” Respondents could then
indicate their intentions on a ten-point numeric scale with verbal
labels at 1 “not likely at all” and 10 “very
likely.”
