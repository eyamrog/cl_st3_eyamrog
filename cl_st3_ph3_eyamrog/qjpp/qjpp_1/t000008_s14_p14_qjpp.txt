Vector genome ITR fusions were measured using ddPCR technology, as
described above. The primer and probe sets used for ddPCR
and drop-phase ddPCR are presented in Supplementary Fig. 4b and
Supplementary Table 1 . To specifically detect ITR fusions, two
directional PCR primers were designed: fusions of the 5′ and
3′ (H–T) ITRs were detected using forward and reverse primers
located on the 5′ and 3′ ends of the linear
genome that extend toward the ITR and only produce an
amplicon when 5′ and 3′ ITRs are fused.
