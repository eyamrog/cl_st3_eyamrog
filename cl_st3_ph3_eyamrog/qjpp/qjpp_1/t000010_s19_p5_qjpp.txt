The results of the comparisons among the treatment groups for
the MACE-3 outcomes demonstrated an HR of 0.83 (95% CI,
0.58–1.18) for MACE-3; an HR of 0.78 (95% CI, 0.56–1.08)
for composite outcome of MACE-3 or HHF; and an HR
of 0.67 (95% CI, 0.26–1.70) for HHF. All-cause death had
an HR of 0.80 (95% CI, 0.51–1.25) (Fig. 1 ).
