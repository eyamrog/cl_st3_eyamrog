CRISPR-based diagnostics offer an alternative approach to detecting multiple viruses
and variants . The CRISPR effector proteins Cas12 (refs. )
or Cas13 (refs. ) activate upon CRISPR RNA (crRNA) target
binding, which unleashes their collateral cleavage activity on a fluorescent
reporter for detection of viral nucleic acids . The crRNA
target binding events are highly specific and altered by the
presence of sequence variation. Maximally active crRNA design has been
accelerated by machine learning and other computational methods . Nonetheless,
most CRISPR diagnostics detect one to three targets per sample
, which is not sufficient for differential diagnosis via comprehensive
microbe or variant identification.
