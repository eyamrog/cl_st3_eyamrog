Finally, the FDA recommends testing a minimum of 30 known-positive
clinical specimens for each pathogen in an assay, as well
as 30 negative specimens. Where positive specimens are not available,
the FDA allows the creation of contrived samples by spiking
viral genomic material at clinically relevant concentrations into a negative
specimen. Each virus evaluated must have a minimum of 95%
agreement performance, both positive percentage agreement (PPA) and negative percentage
agreement (NPA), to clinically approved comparator assays.
