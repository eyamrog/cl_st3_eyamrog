For each phenotype, we computed the theoretical OR we were
powered to detect (given in Supplementary Tables 5 and 8
) as follows: let m be the proportion of individuals
used in the association study of that phenotype who are
LRRK2 pLoF carriers and let n 0 and n 1
be the number of controls and cases, respectively. For each
OR in the interval (1, 10) at steps of 0.02,
we computed the power of the Cochran–Armitage trend test to
detect an association between a variant with minor allele frequency
m and OR at α = 0.05, with n 0
controls and n 1 cases . We reported the smallest
OR such that the power was ≥0.8.
