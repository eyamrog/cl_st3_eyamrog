Given concerted and cell-type-specific IFN responses during hyperacute HIV infection,
we next explored whether other modules exhibited shared expression dynamics.
We applied fuzzy c-means clustering to the median module scores
at each timepoint across all cell types on a participant-by-participant
basis, generating clusters of modules which we refer to as
meta-modules (MMs) ( Methods ). MMs represent gene programming across
distinct cell types with coordinated temporal dynamics—here, synchronized responses to
infection—enabling us to link cellularly discrete but contemporaneous behaviors to
both common and unique propagators.
