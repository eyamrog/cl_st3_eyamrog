Bastagli and associates ( 10 ) examined 41 studies reporting
the effects of cash-transfer programs on health and nutrition indicators.
Conducted from 2000 to 2015, the reviewed studies covered Latin
America, sub-Saharan Africa, South Asia, and the Asia-Pacific region. The
reviewers concluded that cash-transfer programs in LMICs improve dietary diversity
and increase utilization of health care services, especially when conditionalities
are attached to the program.
