ION363 was manufactured by ChemGenes Corporation according to Good Manufacturing
Practice standards. For the animal experiments, drug powder was reconstituted
in sterile PBS to a total concentration of 4 mg
ml . For the administration to the patient, ION363 was
reconstituted in a USP compendial grade artificial cerebrospinal fluid to
a total concentration of 11 mg ml . The appropriate
drug volume for 20-mg, 40-mg and 60-mg doses was subsequently
sterilized through a 0.2-µm filter into a sterile pyrogen-free syringe.
