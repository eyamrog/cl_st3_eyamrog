All ICD10 codes relating to diseases of the liver (K70–K77),
diseases of the respiratory system not specific to the upper
respiratory tract (J20–J22, J40–J47, J80–J99) or kidney diseases (N00–N29) were

cause (Supplementary Table 12 ). Asthma was excluded from all
analyses to avoid any issues caused by the deliberate ascertainment
of the exome-sequenced portion of the cohort on the basis
of asthma status.
