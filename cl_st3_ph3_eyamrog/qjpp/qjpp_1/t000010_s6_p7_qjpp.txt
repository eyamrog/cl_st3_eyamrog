The present cardiovascular meta-analyses include a broad clinical population at
different stages of their T2D course. The inclusion of a
large number of participants with a history of multiple cardiovascular
risk factors, prior events and prevalent cardiovascular disease, and participants
with a trial treatment duration of up to 24 months,
supported the assessment of cardiovascular safety . Relatively few MACE
were reported in participants with lower cardiovascular risk up to
56 weeks of follow-up despite high total investigational product exposures.
Thus, cardiovascular safety assessment of tirzepatide was less robust in
this subgroup.
