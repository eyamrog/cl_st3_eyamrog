Formalin-fixed paraffin-embedded (FFPE) and hematoxylin-and-eosin-stained tissue sections of IBD patients
were scored according to the Nancy index, based on criteria
reported in ref. . The extent of histologic ulceration was
scored on a semiquantitative scale (0–25–50–75–100% of section area). All
scorings were carried out by blinded, consultant gastrointestinal histopathologists.
