Immunostaining of spinal cords with an antibody to the modified
backbone of the ION363 and NTC ASOs showed robust predominantly
nuclear signal in all cells, including MNs (Fig. 4e and
Extended Data Fig. 6a,b ) at 1 and 4 months
of age. However, by 6 months of age, analysis of
anti-ASO immunostaining showed near background levels of signal for both
ION363 and the NTC ASO (Extended Data Fig. 6a,b ).
The observed decrease in ION363 levels at 6 months of
age was associated with a corresponding increase in FUS levels
(Extended Data Fig. 6c,d ).
