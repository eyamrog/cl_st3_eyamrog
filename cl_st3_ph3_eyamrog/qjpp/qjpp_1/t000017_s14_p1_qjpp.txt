Patients aged ≥18 years with locally diagnosed AdvSM or relapsed
or refractory myeloid malignancies with evidence of aberrant KIT or
PDGFR signaling were enrolled in the dose-escalation phase (part 1),
and only patients with locally diagnosed AdvSM according to WHO
criteria were enrolled in the dose-expansion phase (part 2). Patients
with ECOG performance status of 0–3 were eligible. Patients with
platelets <25 × 10 /l were excluded initially; the threshold
was subsequently raised to <50 × 10 /l to mitigate
the risk of ICB. The complete inclusion and exclusion criteria
are listed in Supplementary Table 3 . Diagnoses were retrospectively
adjudicated by central pathology and by an Adjudication Committee using
WHO criteria. In patients with a centrally confirmed diagnosis of
AdvSM, evaluable baseline C-findings and responses were defined using mIWG-MRT-ECNM
consensus criteria (Supplementary Tables 4 and 5 ).
