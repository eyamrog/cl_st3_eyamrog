Throughout history, the global food system has experienced periods of
overproduction, poor harvests, distribution breakdowns, and other disruptions, sometimes fomenting
social unrest and political change. The COVID-19 pandemic is only
the latest crisis to affect both global and local food
systems. The COVID-19 pandemic, which dramatically increased food insecurity among
Black and Latinx populations, further showed how precarious and unequal
the food system remains despite the last several decades of
food policy making.
