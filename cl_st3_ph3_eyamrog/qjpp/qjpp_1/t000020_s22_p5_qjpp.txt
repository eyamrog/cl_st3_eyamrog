We manually curated the 150 identified variants to remove those
of low quality or with annotation errors suggesting that they
are unlikely to cause true LoF (Fig. 1a and Supplementary
Tables 1 and 2 ). We removed 16 variants identified
as low confidence by the LoF transcript effect estimator ((LOFTEE);
6 variants in 409 individuals) or manually curated as low
quality or unlikely to cause LoF (10 variants in 129
individuals). One additional individual was excluded from the UK Biobank
cohort as they carried both a pLoF variant and the
G2019S risk allele.
