Correlations between clinical and metadata measures and module eigengenes were
assessed using Pearson correlations, with P values estimated using the
corPvalueStudent function and adjusted for multiple testing. Benjamini–Hochberg correction using
the p.adjust function was used for all analyses with adjusted
P values. This was carried out on inflamed IBD tissue
samples and CRC tissue samples combined, and also on inflamed
IBD tissue samples alone. Eigengenes were also compared between paired
inflamed and noninflamed tissues sections using a t -test and
adjusting for multiple testing across modules.
