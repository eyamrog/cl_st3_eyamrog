Since 2019, the French Health Authorities have required extensive data
collection for each patient with a theoretical indication of CAR
T treatment. Reimbursement is conditional on data comprehensive completion by
the local investigator. The DESCAR-T registry has been set up
by the Lymphoma Study Association (LYSA) and the Lymphoma Academic
Research Organization (LYSARC) to fulfil this regulatory request and to
allow for comprehensive RWE studies.
