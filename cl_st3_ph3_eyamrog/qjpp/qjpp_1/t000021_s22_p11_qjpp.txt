Given the diverse, participant-specific GMs in MMgp (Extended Data Fig.
4 ), we next looked whether any acute infection responses
were present in multiple participants. In CD4 T cells, monocytes,
NK cells, CTLs and proliferating T cells, we found GMs
in MMgp that shared genes in two or more participants
(Fig. 3e–i ; see Supplementary Table 8 for overlapping genes).
While DCs and B cells also expressed multiple GMs within
MMgp, some did not share any genes across participants (Extended
Data Fig. 6a ) or had low membership scores and
were thus excluded (membership <0.25, labeled with † in Extended
Data Fig. 4 ; Methods ).
