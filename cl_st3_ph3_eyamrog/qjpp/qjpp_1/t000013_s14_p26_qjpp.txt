All or a combination of the following thermal cycling conditions
were tested to shorten assay run time: reverse transcription at
50 °C for 15–30 min, PCR activation at 95 °C
for 5–15 min, denaturation step at 94 °C for 10–30
s and extension step at 72 °C for 10 s
to 1 min. The final extension at 72 °C for
10 min was omitted in all runs. The following primer
pool conditions were also tested to optimize the assay: 150,
300, 500 and 600 nM of virus-specific primer and 100
and 150 nM of RNase P primers, with 5 μM
of each virus-specific primer and 1.7 μM of RNase P
primers. The reaction volumes tested include: 20 μl with 10%
RNA template input, 30 μl with 20% RNA template input
and 50 μl with 20% RNA template input. The final
amplification conditions used for the RVP panel are described under
‘General mCARMEN Procedures-Amplification’.
