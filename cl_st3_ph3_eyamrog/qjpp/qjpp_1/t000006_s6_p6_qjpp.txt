In summary, CHRONOS demonstrated (to our knowledge for the first
time prospectively) that genotyping tumor DNA in the blood can
be safely, effectively and conveniently incorporated in the management of
patients with mCRC. We conclude that ctDNA analysis is an
effective strategy to select patients with mCRC for panitumumab rechallenge
by allowing the maximization of treatment efficacy and concomitantly sparing
iatrogenic toxicities.
