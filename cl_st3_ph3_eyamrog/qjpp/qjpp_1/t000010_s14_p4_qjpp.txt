MACE were prospectively captured and centrally adjudicated using similar event
definitions across the trials’ program by personnel blinded to randomized
assignment by the Duke Clinical Research Institute Clinical Endpoint Committee
(CEC) (for the phase 2 trial) and the Cleveland Clinic
Coordinating Center for Clinical Research CEC (for the phase 3

and potential cardiovascular outcome events, including acute coronary syndromes (MI
and HUA), coronary revascularization procedures (coronary artery bypass grafting and
percutaneous coronary interventions), HHF and cerebrovascular events (stroke and transient
ischemic attack).
