To capture between-study heterogeneity—that is, disparities across the estimates obtained
from the input sources for the meta-analysis—we entered the mean
risk–outcome function and selected bias covariates obtained from our previous
steps into a linear mixed-effects model that scaled the RR
yielded by each study using study-specific random slopes. We estimated
the variance of between-study random effects using the Fisher information
matrix, which is robust to both data sparsity and the
presence of within-study correlation. We report our main estimates of
mean RR using uncertainty intervals that include the effect of
between-study heterogeneity, from which we derived BPRF and associated ROS
and star rating.
