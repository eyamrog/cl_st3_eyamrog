For droplet pooling, a total droplet pool volume of 150
µl of droplets was used to load each standard chip;
a total of 800 µl of droplets was used to
load each mChip. To maximize the probability of forming productive
droplet pairings (amplified sample droplet + detection reagent droplet), half
the total droplet pool volume was devoted to target droplets
and half to detection reagent droplets. For pooling, individual droplet
mixes were arrayed in 96-well plates. A multichannel pipette was
used to transfer the requisite volumes of each droplet type
into a single row of eight droplet pools, which were
further combined to make a single droplet pool. The final
droplet pool was pipetted up and down gently to fully
randomize the arrangement of the droplets in the pool. The
pooling step was rapid (<10 min) and the small-molecule exchange
between droplets during this period did not substantially alter the
color codes.
