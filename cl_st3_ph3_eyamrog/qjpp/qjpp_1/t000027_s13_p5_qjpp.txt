Commercial actors use market and nonmarket strategies to encourage unhealthy
but profitable consumption patterns. Market strategies are actions that businesses
take to maximize returns on investment, revenues, profits, shareholder value,
and market share. Nonmarket strategies are actions that businesses take
to exert political and economic influence in ways that create
and sustain favorable operating environments. Regulatory science approaches to mitigate
health harms from CDoH have generally targeted one of these
strategies.
