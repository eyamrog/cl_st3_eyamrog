The AdvSM-SAF comprises a ten-item questionnaire completed by the patient
daily, specifically designed to assess symptoms related to AdvSM and
measure the impact of treatments on patient symptom improvement .
It has been validated and demonstrated to be reliable and
sensitive to clinical changes. The AdvSM-SAF assesses the severity of
eight symptoms of AdvSM, including abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea,
spots, itching, flushing and fatigue over a 24-h period on
a numerical rating scale, and also measures the frequency of
vomiting and diarrhea by asking patients to enter a discrete
numerical value. Data were analyzed at the item level, domain
