In the cohort studies, the lowest SBP category reported for
a reference group was <115 mm Hg while the highest
for a comparison group was >175 mm Hg. In RCTs,
the observed average SBP ranged from 119.3 ± 10.1 to
166 ± 9.6 mm Hg in the reference/treatment groups and
from 129 ± 10.3 to 192 ± 10.2 mm Hg
in the comparison groups. Across all studies, only five studies
included groups with SBP values <120 mm Hg at baseline,
although the average of mean SBP exposure (defined as the
midpoint of the lower and upper bounds of the exposure
range/category associated with the reported risk measurement for the group
in question) in the reference group was 121 mm Hg
(100–166 mm Hg). The average mean SBP exposure in the
alternate group was 146 mm Hg (115–193 mm Hg). The
average mean SBP exposure among alternate and control groups was
135 mm Hg. We calculated the 15th percentile of exposure
in the cohorts and trials to be an SBP of
107.5 mm Hg, and the 85th percentile to be 165
mm Hg (Fig. 1 ).
