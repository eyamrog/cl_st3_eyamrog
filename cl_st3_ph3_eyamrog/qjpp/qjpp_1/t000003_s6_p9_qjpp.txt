In conclusion, although only a randomized study could allow for
an undisputable comparison between the two CAR T products, our
study is in favor of a higher efficacy but also
a higher toxicity of axi-cel compared to tisa-cel in ≥3rd
line of treatment for R/R DLBCL. These results need to
be confirmed by other large RWE studies with similar statistical
methods to account for imbalance between patient characteristics. Our findings
could help in refining the choice of CAR T product
for a specific patient based on the tradeoff between safety
and efficacy.
