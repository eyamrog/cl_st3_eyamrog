Spleen response was determined for patients with baseline splenomegaly (spleen
≥ 5 cm by palpation) and was assessed by palpation
or spleen volume by imaging (that is, ≥35% reduction from
baseline in spleen volume based on central imaging); presence of
either criterion was considered a response. DOR started at the
first evidence of a response in any evaluable C-finding (per
mIWG-MRT-ECNM criteria) and ended at the time a response was
lost (that is, loss of response per mIWG-MRT-ECNM criteria). For
patients who were still responding at the time of analysis,
DOR was censored at the latest time point that the
patient is assessed as having clinical improvement or better. OS
was analyzed in the safety population and was defined as
the time from first dose to the time of death
due to any cause. PK data were summarized for patients
with sufficient plasma concentration–time data. Descriptive statistics were used to
summarize PK parameters for each dose level, as appropriate.
