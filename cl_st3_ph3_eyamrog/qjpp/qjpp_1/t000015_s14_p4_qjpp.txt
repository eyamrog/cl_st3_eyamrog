All experiments involving live animals were approved by the Institutional
Animal Care and Use Committee at Columbia University Irving Medical
Center. C57Bl/6J (000664), ChAT-Cre (006410) , ChAT-CreΔneo (031661), protamine-Cre (003328)
and Pgk1-flpo (011065) mouse lines were obtained from Jackson Laboratory.
All strains were backcrossed to C57Bl/6J background for at least
five generations. All animals were housed in a specific pathogen-free
facility with ambient temperature of 18–23 °C and 40–60% humidity
and a 12-h light/dark cycle.
