The GoTaq Probe qPCR Kit was used to amplify nucleic
acid via RT–PCR according to the manufacturer’s instructions, using custom
primer pools in 20-μl reactions. Primer pools of 200, 300
and 500 nM were tested and compared to determine optimal
conditions. Each target in the panel was amplified with its
corresponding primer pool. The following thermal cycling conditions were used:
(1) reverse transcription at 45 °C for 15 min and
95 °C for 2 min; and (2) 40 cycles at
95 °C for 15 s, 60 °C for 1 min.
Amplified samples were directly added into the detection reaction or
stored at −20 °C until use.
