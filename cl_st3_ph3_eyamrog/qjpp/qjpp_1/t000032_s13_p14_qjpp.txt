One could summarize the goal of reproducible research as providing
a means to answer the question, “Do I understand and
trust this data analysis?" With the computational nature of today's
research, we cannot hope to answer that question without being
able to look at the data and the code. In
addition, we may wish to know things about the experimental
or study design as well as the hypothesis being examined
( 22 ). Given the claimed results, the data, and
code, one can theoretically determine the reproducibility status of a
data analysis. Reproducibility gives us the means by which we
can assess our confidence and trust in an analysis, but
it is important to reiterate that reproducing an analysis is
not a check on the validity of the analysis.
