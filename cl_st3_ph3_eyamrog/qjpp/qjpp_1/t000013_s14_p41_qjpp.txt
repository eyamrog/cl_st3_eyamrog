Sample and assay mixtures can be prepared in advance for
multiple 96-sample batches according to similar methods, with the following
changes: the batch sample mix contained all reagents described above
excluding 9 mM MgCl 2 , and the batch assay
mix contained all reagents described above excluding the 2x Assay
Loading Reagent. Both mixtures were calculated with 10% coverage. Both
mixtures were stored at −80 °C until use; 9 mM
MgCl 2 was added to the sample mix and 2x
Assay Loading Reagent was added to each assay mix before
use.
