Like race, SEP is a strong determinant of health (
1 , 13 , 20 , 21 , 29 ,
75 , 93 , 124 ). SEP involves an array
of resources that all protect health, regardless of which health
mechanisms are relevant at a given time ( 75 ).
Individuals with substantial income and assets work in roles with
autonomy, job security, and fringe benefits, including paid time off.
They have resources to navigate unexpected costs, as well as
access to healthy foods and safe green spaces for exercise
( 61 , 73 ). Their children attend good schools
and grow into well-paid jobs of their own ( 125
). These relationships are structural, replicating, and determinative.
