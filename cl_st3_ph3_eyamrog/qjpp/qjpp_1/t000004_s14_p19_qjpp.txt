Statistical analysis (Minitab Data and Statistical Toolbox) was performed using
two-sided Student’s t -test to compare bandpower means during clinical
tasks. One-way analysis of variance (ANOVA) followed by Student’s t-
test was performed comparing mean power during ambulatory recordings under
three conditions (control, craving, hunger). The χ test was performed
on the rDBS detection count for each subject. A statistical
significance criterion of α = 0.05 was used for all
tests. Results are shown as a mean ± s.e.m. unless
otherwise noted.
