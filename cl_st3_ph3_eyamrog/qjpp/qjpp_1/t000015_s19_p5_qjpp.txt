Our studies of heterozygous ALS mutant FUS knock-in mice show
that a single copy of P517L or Δ14 mutant allele
is sufficient to cause gliosis and NMJ denervation, followed by
selective degeneration of the same MN subtypes that are preferentially
involved in ALS-FUS. This pathology is not restricted to the
lumbar spinal cord, as similar trends in denervation, MN degeneration
and gliosis were observed in the diaphragm muscle and cervical
spinal cord (Extended Data Fig. 1k–n ). Together, these observations
show that the knock-in mice closely replicate ALS-FUS pathology, providing
a model in which to study disease-relevant mechanisms of neurodegeneration.
