Because our previous experiments suggested that mutant P517L is more
pathogenic than Δ14, we suspected that co-expression of these two
mutant alleles in MNs would further accelerate disease. We, therefore,
generated P517L/c14; ChAT-Cre (‘MN-P517L/Δ14’) conditional compound heterozygote mutants that express
both P517L and Δ14 mutant FUS only in MNs, on
an otherwise P517L- and WT-expressing background. Immunofluorescent staining of spinal
cords confirmed that, in MN-P517L/Δ14 mice, MNs express P517L and
Δ14 (but not WT) FUS, whereas non-cholinergic cells express P517L
and WT (but not Δ14) FUS (Fig. 3d ). As
predicted, addition of the more pathogenic P517L allele led to
NMJ denervation, microgliosis and astrogliosis at 3 months of age
and MN degeneration at 4 months in MN-P517L/Δ14 mice (Fig.
3e,f and Extended Data Fig. 5f,g )—earlier than the corresponding
changes in MN-Δ14/Δ14 animals (Extended Data Fig. 5b,d ).
