We also observed an expansion of ACKR1 endothelial cells alongside
activated fibroblasts in M4/M5-high tissues/deep ulcers. Module M2 also strongly
correlated with endothelial cells in our in silico predictions, and
was the third strongest association with therapy nonresponse. Since endothelial
cells did not express high levels of genes coding for
neutrophil-chemoattractant CXCLs, we can speculate that inflammatory fibroblasts produce these
chemokines, which are then presented by ACKR1 endothelial cells to
circulating neutrophils to initiate extravasation .
