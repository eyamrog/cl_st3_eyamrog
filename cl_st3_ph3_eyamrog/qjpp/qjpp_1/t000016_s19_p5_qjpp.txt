The study design of the PANDA challenge was preregistered and
consisted of a competition and a validation phase. The competition
was open to participants from 21 April until 23 July
2020 and was hosted on the Kaggle platform (Supplementary Methods
5 ). During the competition phase, 1,010 teams, consisting of
1,290 developers from 65 countries, participated and submitted at least
one algorithm (Fig. 1 ). Throughout the competition, teams could
request evaluations of their algorithm on the tuning set (Supplementary
Methods 2 ). The algorithms were then simultaneously blindly validated
on the internal validation set (Fig. 2 ). All teams
combined submitted 34,262 versions of their algorithms, resulting in a
total of 32,137,756 predictions made by the algorithms.
