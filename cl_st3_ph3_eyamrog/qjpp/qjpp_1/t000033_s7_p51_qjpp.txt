Overall, research on the connection between place attachment and adaptation
impacts and success is rapidly growing, and several significant research
areas are emerging. For example, the interplay between institutional practices
and responsibility and residents’ adaptive practices has significant influence on
how adaptation responses affect communities ( 138 ). Property rights,
access to resources, risk perception, social capital, institutionalized racism, and
income inequalities are important mediating factors in the uneven distributional
impacts of adaptation plans ( 58 , 75 ). Another
emerging research area is residential displacement via adaptation responses, which
can drive competition for limited resources in urban environments where
a limited amount of land is available and costs for
resettlement land are high ( 14 , 97 ).
