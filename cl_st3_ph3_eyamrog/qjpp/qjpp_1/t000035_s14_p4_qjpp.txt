Study designs were evaluated for rigor and the risk of
bias. The risk of bias in this context is largely
dependent on study designs that enable causal inference, so our
evaluation reflected that emphasis. We classified each study's risk of
bias into one of three broad categories: low risk of
bias (randomized study or rigorous quasi-experimental design), medium risk of
bias (regression analysis with a rich set of controls, propensity
scores, or hierarchical modeling), or high risk of bias (descriptive
comparisons with no or limited regression adjustment). We did not
exclude any studies on the basis of weak study designs,
as descriptive studies can be informative; however, we placed more
weight on studies with more rigorous designs when drawing conclusions
across studies.
