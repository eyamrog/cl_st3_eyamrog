Systemic mastocytosis (SM) is a rare hematologic neoplasm characterized by
accumulation of neoplastic mast cells in the bone marrow (BM),
skin and other organs . Mast cell mediator release and
SM-related organ damage (known as C-findings) lead to severe, debilitating
symptoms . AdvSM comprises three subtypes: aggressive SM (ASM), SM
with an associated hematologic neoplasm (SM-AHN), which accounts for ~60–70%
of advanced disease , and mast cell leukemia (MCL) .
Median overall survival (OS) in patients with AdvSM is ≤3.5
years, usually due to organ damage and/or progression of the
associated hematologic neoplasm (AHN) .
