Influenza surveillance is a common public health activity done around
the world. The WHO coordinates global influenza surveillance and provides
recommendations for the conduct of influenza surveillance in Member States
( 86 ). Having learned from the 1918–1919 pandemic, the
WHO established the Global Influenza Programme in 1947 and the
Global Influenza Surveillance Network (GISN) in 1952. The latter was
renamed as the Global Influenza Surveillance and Response System (GISRS)
in 2011 and has expanded to include 143 institutions in
113 Member States ( 88 ).
