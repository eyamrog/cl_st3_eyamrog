The phecode definitions used to map the diseases that were
used as an outcome for Cox model analyses can be
found in Supplementary Table 1 . Steps per day (averaged
monthly) was examined as a repeated measure and time-varying variable
to account for fluctuations in activity over an individual’s monitoring
period. Only daily steps data before incident diagnoses were used
in the Cox models. The time components for Cox models
were chosen in terms of months. We also performed similar
Cox model analyses restricted to individuals who were at high
risk of incident obesity by virtue of a baseline BMI
of 25–29.9 kg m . To examine whether the relationship
between steps per day with the hazard of incident outcomes
was linear or nonlinear, restricted cubic spline functions using 3,
4 and 5 knots of steps was fitted with separate
Cox models. The model with lowest Akaike information criterion (AIC)
value was chosen to then interpret the relation of steps
per day with risk of developing a condition. Months for
which participants had fewer than 15 days of observations were
excluded from Cox models. We also examined the percent months
and days that were excluded based on this additional criterion
implemented in the Cox models.
