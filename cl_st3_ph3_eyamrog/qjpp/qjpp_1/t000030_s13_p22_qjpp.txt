Broadly speaking, two general approaches have been used to make
forecasts in these studies. One approach uses statistical and phenomenological
methods, such as time series models, generalized linear models, classification
algorithms, regression models, and Bayesian networks. The second approach uses
mechanistic models such as compartmental models and agent-based models. While
mechanistic approaches can sometimes better account for nonlinear infection dynamics,
they also require a much larger number of parameters and
assumptions.
