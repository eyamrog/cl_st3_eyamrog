Forty-one of 69 patients with AdvSM (59%) had received prior
antineoplastic therapy, including 23 (33%) previously treated with midostaurin and
10 (14%) with cladribine (Supplementary Table 2 ). Thirty-six patients
with AdvSM (52%) had at least one mutation in the
SRSF2 , ASXL1 or RUNX1 ( S/A/R ) genes, which
are associated with poor disease prognosis .
