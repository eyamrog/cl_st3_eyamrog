Over 30% of patients with mCRC are eligible for a
third or further line of treatment throughout their continuum of
care . In this context, retreatment with drugs to which
patients had been previously exposed is often adopted in clinical
practice despite the lack of biomarkers and of conclusive clinical
evidence . In keeping with such empirical practice, rechallenge with
anti-EGFR agents is deployed according to the described decay of
resistance mechanisms , its manageable toxicity profile and potential induction
of tumor shrinkage . This strategy is commonly used in
clinical practice and is increasingly guided by extended and real-time
molecular profiling, despite the lack of prospective data . We
and others previously proposed that ctDNA analysis of anti-EGFR resistance-conferring
mutations could be used to direct the selection of patients
in this context .
