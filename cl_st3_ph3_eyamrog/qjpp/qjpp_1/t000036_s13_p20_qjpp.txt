Particularly poignant cases relate to pregnant child rape survivors who
have petitioned courts seeking permission to terminate their pregnancies beyond
the gestational limit of 20 weeks. The courts, usually based
on the recommendations of expert committees of doctors, have ruled
against terminations in many instances, forcing children as young as
12 years old to undergo high-risk pregnancies, childbirth, and motherhood
as a child. Between 2016 and 2019, various high courts
of India rejected 12 of 78 petitions, as did the
Supreme Court in 2 of 5 petitions, for terminations of
late pregnancies in child rape survivors ( 56 ).
