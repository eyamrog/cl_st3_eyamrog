Data on patterns of hepatic transduction of AAV vectors in
humans have been lacking . In animals, there is evidence
that transduction patterns may be species and capsid dependent and
coincide with lobular distribution of AAV uptake receptors (Supplementary Fig.
2a ). We demonstrated unbiased distribution of AAV5-hFVIII-SQ vector genomes
in human liver biopsy samples, similar to that observed in
NHPs (Supplementary Fig. 7 ) and consistent with an even
distribution of AAV5 uptake receptors in normal human liver (Supplementary
Fig. 2b ).
