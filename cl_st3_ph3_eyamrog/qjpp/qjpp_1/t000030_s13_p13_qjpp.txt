Influenza is an acute contagious infection of the airways and
lungs caused by an influenza virus infection. Influenza transmission dynamics
are characterized by several epidemiological parameters estimated from available surveillance
data. These dynamics include the incubation period, infectious period, serial
interval, generation time, exponential growth rate, and reproduction number, among
others. Reliable information on these parameters is important for influenza
prediction and forecasting studies, as discussed below.
