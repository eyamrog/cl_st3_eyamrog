Several matching-adjusted indirect comparisons (MAICs) have been attempted to compare
different CAR T products . MAIC uses individual patient data
(IPD) from one study and trial-level data from another to
form a population-adjusted indirect comparison between treatments. One of these
recently reported MAICs suggests that axi-cel is superior to tisa-cel
for disease control but is associated with significantly more toxicity
. In addition, despite increasing popularity, many biases remain with
such statistical methods .
