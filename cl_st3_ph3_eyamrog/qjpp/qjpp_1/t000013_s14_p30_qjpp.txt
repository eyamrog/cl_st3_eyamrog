We designed this panel according to the methods described above
under ‘Respiratory Panel-Design’ for these nine viruses: SARS-CoV-2, HCoV-HKU1, HCoV-OC43,
HCoV-NL63, FLUAV/FLUAV-g4, FLUBV, HPIV-3, HRSV and HMPV, with the addition
of an RNase P primer pair and crRNA. RNase P
primers and crRNAs were designed within the same region of
the gene as the CDC RT–qPCR assay (Supplementary Table 4
).
