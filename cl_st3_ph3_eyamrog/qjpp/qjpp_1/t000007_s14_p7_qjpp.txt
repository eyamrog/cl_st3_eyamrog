A summary of all substitution events was compiled into three
tables. Supplementary Table 1 lists every substitution and the patient(s)
where it was found. Data on the proportion of a
given substitution in the general global viral samples were taken
from http://cov-glue-viz.cvr.gla.ac.uk/ , and counts of substitutions along a sampled
phylogeny of approximately 3,500 sequences were taken from NextStrain (
https://nextstrain.org/ ) (Supplementary Table 9 ). Each of the S
amino acid replacements was queried to see if information exists
on antibody evasion for a given mutation and was marked
as enhancing antibody evasion if a publication was available showing
direct experimental evidence supporting this (Supplementary Table 1 ), which
does not necessarily capture the full in vivo repertoire of
antibody evasion. Moreover, we note that that could not directly
test for antibody evasion in each patient but, rather, could
infer it indirectly.
