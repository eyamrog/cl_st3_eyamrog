Finally, we have not explored the major threat of variants
that escape vaccine and/or naturally induced immunity, owing to their
lack of substantial impact during 2021 (ref. ). The emergence
of Omicron in November 2021 has posed just such a
threat , with the potential for large waves of infection
and the need to re-vaccinate some vulnerable populations. This emergence
strengthens the arguments for vaccine sharing as a means of
reducing the global levels of infection and, hence, retarding the
accumulation of new variants.
