Hematoxylin and eosin sections were cut from FFPE tumor blocks
corresponding to a tumoral lesion of the 27 patients subjected
to panitumumab rechallenge. These were archival samples collected before trial
enrollment. In nine patients, the available lesion was collected after
a prior anti-EGFR-containing regimen because samples used for eligibility to
anti-EGFR prescription, as detailed in inclusion criterion 9, were not
available due to tissue exhaustion for diagnostic procedures. For samples
with adequate tumor cell content (26/27), four 8-µm-thick sections were
microdissected, and DNA was extracted with the GeneRead DNA FFPE
Kit (QIAGEN) and quantified with a fluorometric assay (Qubit, Thermo
Fisher Scientific).
