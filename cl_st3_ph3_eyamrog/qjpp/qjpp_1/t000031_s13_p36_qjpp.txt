Metformin is seldom used in daily practice, despite the ADA's
recommendation to use it for prediabetes treatment. In 2010–2012, only
3.7% of prediabetes patients with UnitedHealthcare insurance (one the largest
private insurers in the United States) were prescribed the medication
( 72 ). In the 2013–2014 NHANES, metformin use among
those with self-reported prediabetes was reported in only 8% of
US adults ( 65 ).
