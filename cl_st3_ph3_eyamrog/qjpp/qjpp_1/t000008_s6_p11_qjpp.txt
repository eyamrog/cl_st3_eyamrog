In this study, long-term expression of hFVIII following a single
valoctocogene roxaparvovec (AAV5-hFVIII-SQ) infusion was associated with the presence of
circularized full-length genomes, with observable dose-dependent trends in the number
of hepatocytes containing vector genomes, quantities of circular full-length/ITR-fused vector
genomes and hFVIII-SQ gene expression in the liver. Interindividual variability
in transgene expression following effective vector genome transduction may result
from differences in expression of regulatory molecules involved in transcription
and protein folding/secretion. Further investigation into these complex mechanisms is
warranted.
