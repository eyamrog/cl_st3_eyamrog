In the present study, 809 patients for whom a CAR
T order was obtained outside of a trial setting for
DLBCL in second or subsequent relapse were analyzed. Median OS
from CAR T order and CAR T infusion was 17
months and 19 months, respectively, for the whole cohort of
patients. Strikingly, in the 1:1 matched population of 418 patients
considered after the stringent PSM statistical approach, ORR/CRR were 66%/42%
for tisa-cel and 80%/60% for axi-cel, which mirror response rates
in the two pivotal clinical trials: JULIET and ZUMA-1 (52%/40%
and 82%/58%, respectively) . Similarly, median OS was 11.2 months
with tisa-cel, whereas median OS was not reached with axi-cel,
echoing the 11.1 months and 25.8 months of median OS
in the recent updates of the JULIET and ZUMA-1 trials,
respectively.
