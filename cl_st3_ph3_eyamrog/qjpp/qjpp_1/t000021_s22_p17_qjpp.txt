As we have previously demonstrated that natural control of HIV
is associated with diverse cellular phenotypes in CTLs and DCs
, we looked to see whether the presence of polyfunctional
monocytes in P3 might link to disease progression in chronic
infection. We observed that both P3 and P4 maintained low
levels of viremia (<1,000 viral copies ml ) at 2.75
years after infection in the absence of antiretroviral therapy (ART)
(Fig. 4a ). HIV infected persons who naturally maintain low
levels of viremia in chronic infection (HIV controllers) demonstrate enhanced
immune responses systemically . However, whether early events in acute
HIV infection reflect or contribute to control of chronic viremia
is unknown.
