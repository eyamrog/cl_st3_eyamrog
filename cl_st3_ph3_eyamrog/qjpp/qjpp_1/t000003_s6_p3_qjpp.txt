After stringent matching to control for slightly more aggressive disease
features in the patient population treated with tisa-cel (more frequent
stage IV disease, older age and poorer performance status), ORR,
CRR, PFS and OS were all significantly higher or longer
after axi-cel infusion than after tisa-cel infusion. All sensitivity analyses,
by considering time from CAR T order instead of time
from infusion, by performing complete case analysis, by adjusting for
residual imbalance in aaIPI or by using multiple imputation, led
to the same exact conclusions.
