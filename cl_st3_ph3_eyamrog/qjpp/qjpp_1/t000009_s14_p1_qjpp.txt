Primary care-linked EHR databases were used. Information governance approval was
obtained from the Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency (19_222).
Primary care EHRs were linked to the secondary care Hospital
Episode Statistics, Office for National Statistics death registry, patient-level IMD
(an area-based proxy for socioeconomic deprivation) and the National Cancer
Registration and Analysis Service (NCRAS). Detailed cancer registration data (cancer
site, behavior, morphology and treatment information) were available in NCRAS.
Chemotherapy drug details were obtained from the Systemic Anti-Cancer Treatment
dataset within NCRAS, whereas radiotherapy details were obtained from the
Radiotherapy dataset.
