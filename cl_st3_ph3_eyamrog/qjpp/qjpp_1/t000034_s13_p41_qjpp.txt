Designed to improve household consumption and material well-being, UBI has
garnered attention from policy makers in high- and low-income countries
( 38 , 54 ). Additional applications of the UBI
concept continue to emerge, and the idea gained a foothold
in US public discourse during the 2020 presidential primary campaigns
( 38 , 129 ). The federal passage of widespread
stimulus payments and increased unemployment benefits in response to the
COVID-19 crisis might be a precursor to some form of
UBI ( 117 ).
