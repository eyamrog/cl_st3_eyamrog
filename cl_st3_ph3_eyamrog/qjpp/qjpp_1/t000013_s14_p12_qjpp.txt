We plotted reference-normalized, background-subtracted fluorescence for guide-target pairs. For a
guide-target pair (at a given time point, t, and target
concentration), we first computed the reference-normalized value as (median(P t
− P 0 ) / (R t − R 0
)) where P t is the guide signal (FAM) at
the time point, P 0 is its background measurement before
the reaction, R t is the reference signal (ROX) at
the time point, R 0 is its background measurement and
the median is taken across replicates. We performed the same
calculation for the NTC of the guide, providing a background
fluorescence value for the guide at t. (When there were
multiple technical replicates of such controls, we took the mean
value across them.) The reference-normalized, background-subtracted fluorescence for a guide-target
pair is the difference between these two values.
