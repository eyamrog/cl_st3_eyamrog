The very specific localization of IL-1β in the areas of
epithelial cell damage in ulcers suggests that disruption of the
epithelial barrier may be a primary event. Early responders to
this damage may also include resident MNP, which can produce
excessive IL-1β and IL-23 particularly in the context of IL-10
pathway deficiency . Alarmin IL-1α may similarly contribute to the
activation of fibroblasts and initiation of colitis , and can
be released by necrotic epithelial cells in IBD . Further
studies are required to establish whether the IL-1R-driven activation of
inflammatory fibroblasts identified here is dominated by IL-1α or IL-1β
signaling or both.
