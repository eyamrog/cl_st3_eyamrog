Analyses of NHANES and also of the Atherosclerosis Risk in
Communities (ARIC) study demonstrate that demographic profiles differ, depending on
the definition of prediabetes used. For example, individuals with prediabetes
defined by HbA 1C tended to be older, more likely
to be female, more likely to be black (compared with
white), and more likely to be obese than individuals meeting
ADA-IFG or ADA/WHO-IGT definitions of prediabetes ( 6 , 89
, 110 ).
