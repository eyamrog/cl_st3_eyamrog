The conditional expression of Δ14 mutant FUS in MNs showed
that the dosage of mutant protein determines the age of
onset of MN degeneration in vivo. These experiments also revealed
the cell-autonomous nature of this dose-dependent toxicity. The timing of
microgliosis and subsequent MN loss in Δ14/WT animals was identical
to that of c14/WT; ChAT-Cre mutants. The same was true
in Δ14/Δ14, Δ14/c14; ChAT-Cre and c14/c14; ChAT-Cre mice, indicating that
the MN degenerative phenotype is determined by the level of
mutant Δ14 FUS expression specifically in MNs, without measurable contribution
of mutant FUS expression in other cell types. Furthermore, these
data suggest that microgliosis—and other possible, cell-non-autonomous contributors to MN
degeneration—is a pathological response to mutant FUS-dependent MN pathology, as
it occurs independently of mutant FUS expression in the responding
microglia (Extended Data Fig. 5a–d ).
