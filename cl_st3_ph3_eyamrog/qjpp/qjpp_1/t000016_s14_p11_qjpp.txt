We defined the main metric as the agreement on ISUP
GG with the reference standard of each particular validation set,
measured using quadratic Cohen’s κ. To compare the performance of
the algorithms with that of the general pathologists, we performed
a two-sided permutation test per pathologist panel. The average agreement
was calculated as the mean of the κ values across
the algorithms and the pathologists, respectively. The test statistic was
defined as the difference between the average algorithm agreement and
the average pathologist agreement.
