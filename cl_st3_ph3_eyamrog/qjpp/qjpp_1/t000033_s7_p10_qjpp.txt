The rate at which coastal cities are growing because of
urbanization is increasing cities’ overall exposure to flood risk (
106 ). For instance, the growing density of communities in
low-elevation, flood-prone areas is being compounded by increasing risk from
changes in frequency and intensity of intense precipitation events (i.e.,
heavy downpours) ( 53 ). The dynamism of urbanization means
that urban environments’ risks and hazards are constantly changing, which
in turn requires community responses that are flexible and constantly
evolving ( 128 , 167 ).
