Improvements in measures of mast cell burden in patients with
AdvSM are presented in Fig. 2a–d and Extended Data Fig.
7 . BM mast cells decreased by ≥50% in 92%
of patients and mast cell aggregates were eliminated in 77%
of patients. Serum tryptase was reduced by ≥50% in 99%
of patients and reduced to <20 ng ml in 74%
of patients. Spleen volume was reduced by ≥35% in 82%
of 66 patients with a baseline spleen volume assessment. The
KIT D816V variant allele fraction (VAF) in BM was reduced
from baseline by ≥50% in 80% of patients and became
undetectable in 30% of patients. Clinical improvements in C-findings per
mIWG-MRT-ECNM criteria observed in response-evaluable patients are presented in Supplementary
Table 1 .
