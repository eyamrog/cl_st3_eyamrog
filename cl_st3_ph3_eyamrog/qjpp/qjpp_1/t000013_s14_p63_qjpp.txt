We generally followed the methods outlined under ‘General mCARMEN procedures-Analysis’,
this time taking into account the VIC signal separate from
the FAM signal. We used a custom Python script to
determine whether the FAM signal of a reaction was significantly
above background by comparing it to the NTC. If the
background-subtracted and normalized fluorescence intensity was 1.8 higher than the
normalized and background-subtracted NTC, the assay was considered positive.
