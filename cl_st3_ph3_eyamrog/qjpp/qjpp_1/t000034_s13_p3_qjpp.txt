In the United States (as elsewhere), race/ethnicity has a troubled
history with ongoing effects. Race is primary. Racial disparities have
been documented in overall mortality and multiple disease outcomes (e.g.,
8 , 65 , 71 ). It is no coincidence
that the weight of the coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19) pandemic
has fallen disproportionately on African Americans, Native Americans, and Latinos
( 123 ). In our view, it is impossible to
understand social determinants of health without “putting race at the
forefront” ( 59 ), and we take that approach in
this article.
