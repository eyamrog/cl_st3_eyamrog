In addition to being highly expressed in tissues from therapy
nonresponders, modules M4 and M5 also demonstrated the strongest correlation
with histologic inflammation (Nancy index) in the discovery cohort (Fig.
1a ). Using an additional clinical cohort of Oxford UC
patients (Extended Data Fig. 3 ), we confirmed that the
Nancy index (Fig. 2a ), but not other clinical or
endoscopic measures (Extended Data Fig. 4a ), is higher in
nonresponders to anti-TNF therapy before the start of treatment.
