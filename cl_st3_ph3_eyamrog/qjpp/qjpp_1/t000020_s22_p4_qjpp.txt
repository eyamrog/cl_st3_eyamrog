We identified LRRK2 pLoF variants and assessed associated phenotypic changes
in three large cohorts of genetically characterized individuals. First, we
annotated LRRK2 pLoF variants in two large sequencing cohorts: the
gnomAD v.2.1.1 dataset, which contains 125,748 exomes and 15,708 genomes
from unrelated individuals and 46,062 exome-sequenced unrelated European individuals from
the UK Biobank . We identified 633 individuals in gnomAD
and 258 individuals in the UK Biobank with 150 unique
candidate LRRK2 loss-of-function (LoF) variants, a combined carrier frequency of
0.48%. All variants were observed only in the heterozygous state.
Compared to the spectrum observed across all genes, LRRK2 is
not significantly depleted for pLoF variants in gnomAD (LoF observed/expected
upper bound fraction = 0.64).
