We performed differential expression to associate genes with inflammation status
within each single-cell cluster. We used DESeq2 on the pseudobulk
samples described above, this time analyzing each cluster separately with
the design y ~ 1 + InflamStatus . We treated
InflamStatus as a random effect (DESeq2 parameter βPrior=TRUE) and recovered
a mean multiplicative offset for each of the three inflammatory
status categories.
