At the same time, the number of residents in the

Urban expansion is rapidly moving into flood-prone areas of many
coastal US metropolitan regions such as Houston, Miami, New York
City, Los Angeles, and Seattle, with Miami having the largest
amount of urban extent at low, flood-prone elevations ( 84
). Many disadvantaged communities that are located in these low-elevation
coastal zones are at risk from environmental and climate hazards
such as SLR and flooding ( 109 , 114 ).
