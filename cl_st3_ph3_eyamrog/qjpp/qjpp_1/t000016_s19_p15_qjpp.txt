To compare algorithms’ performances with those of general pathologists, we
obtained reviews from two panels of pathologists on subsets of
the internal and US external validation sets. For the Dutch
part of the internal validation set, 13 pathologists from 8
countries (7 from Europe and 6 outside of Europe) reviewed
70 cases. For the US external validation set, 20 US
board-certified pathologists reviewed 237 cases. For details on the pathologist
review protocol, see Supplementary Methods 3 .
