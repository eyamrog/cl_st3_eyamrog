Cardiomyocyte differentiation of the control and engineered H1 hESC lines
was performed according to the protocol by Lian et al.
. Briefly, 500,000 cells were plated on hESC-qualified Matrigel (VWR,
BD354277), grown in mTeSR1 medium for 4 d (StemCell Technologies,
85850) and switched to RPMI medium (Life Technologies) with B27
supplement (Life Technologies), switching to B27 with insulin at day
7 for the remainder of the protocol. On day 0
of differentiation, 12 µM CHIR99021 (Tocris) was applied for 24
h. At day 3, cultures were treated with 5 µM
IWP2 (Tocris) for 24 h. Bright-field images and movies were
acquired at day 17 and cells were collected for protein/RNA
extraction at day 19.
