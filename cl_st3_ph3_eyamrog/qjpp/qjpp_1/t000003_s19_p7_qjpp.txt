Hematological toxicity was also significantly more frequent and more severe
with axi-cel than with tisa-cel (Table 3 ). Any grade
cytopenia at 1 month after CAR T infusion was observed
in 64.6% of patients compared to 39.2% and grade ≥3
cytopenia in 34.0% compared to 12.4% with axi-cel and tisa-cel,
respectively (Table 3 ). Significantly higher hematological toxicity after axi-cel
infusion compared to after tisa-cel infusion was consistent across all
hematological lineages (that is, neutropenia, anemia and thrombocytopenia; Table 3
). The same held true for prolonged cytopenias observed at
3 months after CAR T infusion (Table 3 ). Notably,
no significant difference in cytopenias was observed before lymphodepletion between
patients treated with axi-cel or with tisa-cel, meaning that the
observed higher hematological toxicity with axi-cel was not attributable to
significant baseline differences.
