For each individual, we extracted all ICD10 codes from the
fields ‘41270 Diagnoses: ICD10’ (recorded from episodes in hospital), ‘40001
Underlying (primary) cause of death: ICD10’ and ‘40002 Contributory (secondary)
causes of death: ICD10’. The number of carriers and noncarriers
with any ICD10 code relating to lung (5 pLoF carriers;
2,378 noncarriers), liver (0 pLoF carriers; 652 noncarriers) or kidney
disease (3 pLoF carriers; 2,272 noncarriers) were counted. For J43
(emphysema), J44 (other chronic obstructive pulmonary disease) and J47 (bronchiectasis),
ICD10 codes were not counted if they were reported alongside
exposure to or history of tobacco use (Z77.22, P96.81, Z87.891,
Z57.31, F17 or Z72.0).
