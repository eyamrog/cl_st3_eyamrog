Similar to widely used multiplexed approaches, such as BioFire ,
the original design of CARMEN did not provide a quantitative
assessment of viral genome copies present in a sample. Establishing
the total viral quantity in a patient is important for
assessing the stage of infection, transmission risk and most effective
treatment plan . The criterion standard assay for sample quantification,
RT–qPCR, leverages the standard curve—serial dilutions of a given target
at a known concentration—as a means of using Ct values
to approximate viral quantity . We wanted to determine if
a similar approach could be applied to mCARMEN.
