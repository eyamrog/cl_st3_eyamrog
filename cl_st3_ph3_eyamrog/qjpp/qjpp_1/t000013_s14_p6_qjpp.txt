RNA was manually extracted from input material using the QIAamp
Viral RNA Mini Kit (QIAGEN) according to the manufacturer’s instructions.
RNA was extracted from 140 μl of input material with
carrier RNA and samples were eluted in 60 μl of
nuclease-free water and stored at −80 °C until use. RNA
was automatically extracted using the MagMAX DNA Multi-Sample Ultra 2.0
Kit on a KingFisher Flex Magnetic Particle Processor with 96
Deep Well Head (Thermo Fisher Scientific). RNA was extracted from
200 µl of input material and was run according to
the ‘Extract RNA-automated method (200-μl sample input volume)’ protocol in
the TaqPath COVID-19 Combo Kit Protocol, on pages 21–24. The
MVP_2Wash_200_Flex protocol was used. Samples were eluted in 50 μl
of elution solution and either directly added to the amplification
reaction or stored at −80 °C until use.
