Most of the studies in this category were cross-sectional in
design, using regression models with varying degrees of sophistication in
the methods. However, most did not account for the endogeneity
risk; thus, confounding by health status and other factors is
likely. A small proportion, just over 12% ( 24 ,
64 , 70 , 92 ), used longitudinal data primarily
to measure outcomes over a longer time period, not to
