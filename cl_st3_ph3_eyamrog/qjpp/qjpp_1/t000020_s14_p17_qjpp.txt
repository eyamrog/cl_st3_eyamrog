The Estonian Biobank cohort is composed of volunteers from the
general Estonian resident adult population . The current number of
participants of close to 165,000 (representing 15% of the Estonian
adult population) makes it ideally suited to population-based studies. Participants
were recruited throughout Estonia by medical personnel and participants receive
a standardized health examination, donate blood and fill out a
16-module questionnaire on health-related topics such as lifestyle, diet and
clinical diagnoses. A detailed phenotype summary from a health survey
and linked data including ICD10 codes, clinical laboratory values and
treatment and medication information is annually updated through linkage with
national electronic health databases and registries.
