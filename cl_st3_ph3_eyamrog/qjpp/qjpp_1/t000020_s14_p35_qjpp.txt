Given that the carrier count for rs429358 is much higher
than for LRRK2 pLoF, we assessed the power of the
23andMe dataset to detect an age effect associated with LRRK2
pLoF variants that is of the same effect size as
the known effect of the APOE allele rs429358 by sampling
carriers of this variant. We randomly selected N carriers of
rs429358 from the 23andMe dataset, performed a Kolmogorov–Smirnov test on
the age distribution of those carriers versus 4,000,000 noncarriers and
considered the resulting P value. We repeated this process 100
times and then computed the proportion of these simulations with
P < 0.05. This tells us our power to reject
the null hypothesis that APOE does not have an effect
on age at α = 0.05, if we had N
carriers in the dataset. We repeated this for different values
of N between 1,000 and 20,000 (Supplementary Table 6 ).
