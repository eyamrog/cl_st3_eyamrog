In the effort to identify such a craving biomarker, previous
work in mice found that anticipation of a high-fat food
reward was associated with increased low-frequency oscillatory power in the
NAc . This work supported a growing body of evidence
across species reporting electrophysiological, neurochemical and functional neuroimaging activities within
circuits involving the NAc that correlate with reward anticipation and
predict consequential behavioral outcomes . Using low-frequency, delta-bandpower as a
biomarker to trigger delivery of a brief train of high-frequency
electrical stimulation to the NAc (hereafter referred to as rDBS)
resulted in substantial and lasting attenuation of binge-like eating in
mice sensitized to high-fat food , whereas conventional, continuous DBS
appeared to lose efficacy over time .
