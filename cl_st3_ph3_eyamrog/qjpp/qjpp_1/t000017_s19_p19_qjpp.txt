The AdvSM-symptom assessment form (AdvSM-SAF) patient diary evaluated symptoms across
two domains—gastrointestinal and skin. Patients provided information about the severity
of abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, spots, itching, flushing and
fatigue, and the frequency of vomiting and diarrhea. Reductions from
baseline were seen in both the gastrointestinal and skin domains.
Treatment with avapritinib also yielded consistent reductions in the patient-reported
AdvSM-SAF total symptom score (TSS), which encompassed gastrointestinal and skin
symptoms and fatigue. The mean TSS at baseline was 19.1
points ( n = 40). Statistically significant improvements in symptoms
occurred rapidly and were sustained through C11 (mean change from
baseline of −10.9 points, P = 0.002, n = 20;
Fig. 2e ).
