This review of relevant evidence on five approaches to mitigating
health-harming CDoH (i.e., behavioral change, regulation of market and nonmarket
business practices, fiscal policy strategies, citizen and consumer activism, and
litigation and other legal remedies), as well as the different
strategies that are used to advance these approaches, shows a
substantial and wide-ranging yet heterogeneous body of evidence to inform
public health practice and policy. Several lessons can be drawn
from this body of knowledge.
