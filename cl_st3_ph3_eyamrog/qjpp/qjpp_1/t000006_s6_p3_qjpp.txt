CHRONOS was initially conceived to longitudinally monitor the drop of
resistant mutations in blood, as originally reported by us and
subsequently confirmed by others . However, in clinical practice, implementation
of multiple blood tests to confirm decay of resistant mutations
to prompt a rechallenge therapy would be overcostly and impractical.
When the approach was indeed attempted in our clinics, the
availability of sequential samples, often widely spaced in time between
each other, and the time needed to wait for two
longitudinal ctDNA measurements (to assess mutation decay) clashed with the
need to rapidly initiate the next round of treatment, as
clinically required in patients with advanced-line mCRC.
