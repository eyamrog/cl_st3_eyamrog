We grouped self-reported disease diagnoses in UK Biobank individuals into
categories corresponding to the organ system and/or mechanism (Supplementary Table
10 ). We observed no enrichment for any of these
phenotype groups in LRRK2 pLoF carriers when compared to noncarriers
(Supplementary Table 11 ). We also mined ICD10 codes from
hospital admissions and death records for any episodes relating to
lung, liver and kidney phenotypes, removing any with a likely
infectious or other external cause (Supplementary Table 12 and Methods
) and identified six pLoF carriers with ICD10 codes relating
to these organ systems (6.19%), compared to 4,536 noncarriers (9.87%;
Supplementary Tables 13 and 14 ).
