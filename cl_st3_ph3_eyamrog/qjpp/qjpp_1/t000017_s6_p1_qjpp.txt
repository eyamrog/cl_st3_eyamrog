Patients with AdvSM have life-threatening organ damage and poor survival,
caused by the increased proliferation of neoplastic mast cells driven
by the KIT D816V mutation in ~95% of patients .
Midostaurin, until recently the only approved therapy for all subtypes
of AdvSM, does not selectively target the KIT D816V mutant
and only rarely do patients achieve a complete remission .
In a post hoc exploratory analysis of the pivotal midostaurin
phase 2 study using IWG-MRT-ECNM criteria, responses occurred in 28%
of patients with a 2% CR rate .
