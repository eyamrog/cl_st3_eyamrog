The median time to achieve PR or better (CR/CRh/PR) was
2 months (range, 2–27 months), and 9 months to achieve
CR/CRh (Supplementary Fig. 1 ). There was a shorter median
time to PR or better at starting doses of 200,
300 or 400 mg (each 2 months) compared with the
median time to PR or better at starting doses of
<200 mg (9 months; Extended Data Fig. 6 ). Median
duration of response (DOR) was 38 months (95% CI, 22
months–not estimable). Estimated 12-month and 24-month DOR rates were 84%
(95% CI, 72–96%) and 67% (95% CI, 49–‍84%), respectively.
