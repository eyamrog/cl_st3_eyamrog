In 2015, we and others reported that mutant KRAS clones,
which emerge in blood during EGFR blockade, decline upon withdrawal
of anti-EGFR antibodies, indicating that clonal evolution continues beyond clinical
progression . Interestingly, RAS and EGFR ECD clones have been
reported to decay in ctDNA with a half-life of 3.7
months and 4.7 months, respectively . The decay of KRAS
mutations in blood upon anti-EGFR antibody withdrawal is an indication
of clonal evolution during therapy .
