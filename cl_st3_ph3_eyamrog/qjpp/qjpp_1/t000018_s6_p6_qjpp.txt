In addition to the M4/M5-high pathotype, we also identified patients
with high M6 tissue expression associated with lymphoid aggregates. The
high tissue expression of CCL19 / CCL21 / CXCL13 suggests
that this pathotype reflects the presence of fibroblastic reticular-like cells
. The expression of M6 was very low in pediatric-onset
UC and CD; this may reflect the different nature of
the samples analyzed in the two studies, the latter using
endoscopic punch biopsies limited to the mucosa rather than full-thickness
surgical specimens. This requires consideration when interpreting lymphoid signals from
endoscopic biopsies.
