To determine whether the associations with nonresponse are uniform across
the genes in modules M4 and M5 or are driven
by a small number of them, we compared the contribution
of genes by ranking their individual power to predict nonresponse
to anti-TNF and corticosteroid therapies. Again, the majority of genes
from modules M4 and M5 were among the top predictors
of nonresponse to both anti-TNF and corticosteroid therapy relative to
genes in other modules (Fig. 1e,f and Supplementary Table 4
). Thus, M4 and M5 reflect a coordinated shift in
the expression of all their constituent genes in relation to
therapy nonresponse across multiple IBD medications.
