A total of 10 μl of extracted RNA was converted
into single-stranded cDNA in a 40-μl reaction. First, Random Hexamer
Primers (Thermo Fisher Scientific) were annealed to sample RNA at
70 °C for 7 min, followed by reverse transcription using
SuperScript IV for 20 min at 55 °C. cDNA was
stored at −20 °C until use. DNase treatment was not
performed at any point during sample preparation.
