To fulfill the public health need for a clinically relevant
surveillance technology that detects multiple viruses and variants quickly, we
developed mCARMEN. mCARMEN builds on CARMEN v.1 and uses commercially
available Fluidigm microfluidics and instrumentation. To our knowledge, mCARMEN is
the only diagnostic method that combines surveillance capabilities into a
single technology platform with the ability to test hundreds of
samples in a day for multiple respiratory viruses and variants,
while also being able to quantify viral genomic copies.
