The reference standard for the Dutch part of the training
set was determined based on the pathology reports from routine
clinical practice. For the Swedish part of the training set,
the reference standard was set by one uropathologist (L.E.) following
routine clinical workflow. The reference standard for the Dutch part
of the internal validation set was determined through consensus of
three uropathologists (C.H.v.d.K., R.V. and H.v.B.) from two institutions with
18–28 years of clinical experience after residency (mean of 22
years). For the Swedish subset, four uropathologists (L.E., B.D., H.S.
and T.T.) from four institutions, all with >25 years of
clinical experience after residency, set the reference standard.
