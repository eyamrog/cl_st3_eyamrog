Primary stromal cell lines were expanded by plating the single-cell
suspension of tissue digests onto plastic cell culture vessels and
expanding the adherent fraction in RPMI 1640 (with 20% fetal
calf serum (FCS), antibiotics, 5 mM HEPES) (Sigma). Primary cell
lines were used for assays between passage numbers 7 and
15. For the production of conditioned medium, sorted cell populations
were plated at 1,000,000 cells ml in cell culture dishes
with RPMI 1640 containing 5% FCS (Life Technologies), antibiotics and
5 mM HEPES for 16 h. Next, supernatants were aspirated,
spun down to remove cells and frozen at −80 °C
until further use.
