The 23andMe dataset includes self-reported data for thousands of phenotypes
across a diverse range of categories. We performed a phenome-wide
association study comparing LRRK2 pLoF carriers to noncarriers for 366
health-related traits and found no significant association between any individual
phenotype and carrier status (Fig. 3b ). In particular, we
found no significant associations with any lung, liver or kidney
phenotypes (Supplementary Tables 5 and 8 ).
