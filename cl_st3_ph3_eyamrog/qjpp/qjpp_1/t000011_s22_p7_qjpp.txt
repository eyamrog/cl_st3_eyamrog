Fractional dosing of vaccines in the context of global vaccine
shortages has a great potential to mitigate the healthcare and
economic burdens. Our cost-effectiveness analysis provides an indication of potential
benefits for the population vaccinated with fractional doses in India.
We provide a data-driven modeling approach to tailoring fractional-dosing strategies
to local epidemiological conditions, using the ChAdOx1 vaccine as an
example. Compared to the alternatives of either full-dose vaccination or
no vaccination, fractional doses of ChAdOx1 vaccines would be an
economically optimal vaccination strategy.
