In the matched population ( n = 418), 180 (86.1%)
out of 209 patients treated with axi-cel experienced a cytokine
release syndrome (CRS) of any grade compared to 158 (75.6%)
patients out of 209 with tisa-cel (Table 3 ). Most
CRSs were of grade 1 or 2 whatever the CAR
T product. Grade 1–2 CRS was significantly more frequent with
axi-cel than with tisa-cel (80.9% versus 66.5%; P < 0.001),
but no significant difference was observed for grade ≥3 CRS
(9.1% versus 5.3% for tisa-cel and axi-cel, respectively; P =
0.130).
