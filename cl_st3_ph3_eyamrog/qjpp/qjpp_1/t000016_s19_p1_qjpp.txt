In total, 12,625 whole-slide images (WSIs) of prostate biopsies were
retrospectively collected from 6 different sites for algorithm development, tuning
and independent validation (Table 1 , Extended Data Fig. 1
and Supplementary Tables 7 and 8 ). Of these, 10,616
biopsies were available for model development (the development set), 393
for performance evaluation during the competition phase (the tuning set),
545 as the internal validation set in the postcompetition phase
and 1,071 for external validation.
