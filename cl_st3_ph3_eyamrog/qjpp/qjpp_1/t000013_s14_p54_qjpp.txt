A subset of patient specimens, all SARS-CoV-2 and negative patient
specimens, from the MGH Clinical Microbiology Laboratory were verified using
the TaqPath COVID‑19 Combo Kit. These samples were initially reported
to be positive for SARS-CoV-2 via Xpert Xpress SARS-CoV-2/Flu/RSV or
reported to be negative for all targets (excluding RNase P)
on the RVP panel via BioFire FilmArray Respiratory Panel (RP2).
The TaqPath COVID‑19 Combo Kit was performed according to the
manufacturer’s instructions. The assay was performed using the Applied Biosystems
7500 Real-Time PCR System.
