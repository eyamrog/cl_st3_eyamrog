Further work will be required to bring mCARMEN fully to
the clinic, such as obtaining FDA approval, integrating RVP and
VIP into a single panel, decreasing the amount of manual
labor and easing Fluidigm equipment constraints. Nonetheless, we have taken
substantial steps to streamline the assay workflow while enhancing sensitivity
without sacrificing specificity. By combining high-throughput, multiplexed pathogen testing with
variant tracking, the mCARMEN platform is highly scalable and amenable
to clinical laboratory settings for the detection of respiratory pathogens
and variants. This technology also has the potential to test
for other types of infectious diseases and can be used
on other sample types to achieve even more comprehensive diagnostic
and surveillance capabilities.
