These pre-clinical data show that a single perinatal injection of
ION363 results in a robust and persistent decrease in FUS
protein levels for more than 4 months, and that, while
the ASO perdures in the spinal cord, the onset of
gliosis is delayed and MN loss is prevented, showing the
therapeutic potential of FUS silencing—and of this specific FUS-ASO—in the
treatment of patients with ALS-FUS.
