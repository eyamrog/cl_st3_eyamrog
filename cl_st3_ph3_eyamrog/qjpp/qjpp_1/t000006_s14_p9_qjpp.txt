QIAmp MinElute ccfDNA Mini Kit (QIAGEN) was used for cfDNA
extraction from plasma samples following the manufacturer’s protocol. Quantity and
quality of cfDNA were evaluated by the Qubit dsDNA HS
Assay Kit (Thermo Fisher Scientific) and 2100 Bioanalyzer with a
High-Sensitivity DNA Assay Kit (Agilent Technologies), respectively.
