Endoscopic punch biopsies or dissected tissue pieces from surgical resections
were stored in RNAlater (Qiagen) following collection, until further processing.
Tissue was homogenized using the soft tissue homogenizing CK14 kit
(Precellys, Stretton Scientific, no. 03961) in 300 µl of RLT
lysis buffer (Qiagen) and 20 µM DTT (Sigma). RNA was
isolated using the Qiagen Mini kit with a DNA digestion
step (Qiagen). Bulk-sorted cell populations and cultured cells were directly
lysed in RNA lysis buffer, followed by RNA isolation with
the appropriate kits and on-column DNase treatment.
