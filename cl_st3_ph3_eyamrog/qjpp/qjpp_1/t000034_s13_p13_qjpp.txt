The global social welfare discussion on cash transfers generally excludes
analysis of programs in which eligibility is contingent on participant
contributions. Despite contributory social insurance programs such as Social Security
and unemployment insurance, typical social-assistance cash transfers in social safety
nets are noncontributory. A broad view of cash transfers combines
both social assistance (noncontributory programs) and social insurance (contributory programs)
in a social-protection system ( 10 , 38 ).
