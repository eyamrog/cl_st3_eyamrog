We next sought to determine whether lifelong lowering of LRRK2
protein levels through LoF results in an apparent reduction in
lifespan. We found no significant difference between the age distribution
of LRRK2 pLoF variant carriers and noncarriers in either the
gnomAD or 23andMe datasets (two-sided Kolmogorov–Smirnov P = 0.085 and
0.46 respectively; Fig. 3a ), suggesting no major impact on
longevity, though we note that this analysis is based on
age at sample collection, which is not equivalent to longevity
and at current sample sizes we are only powered to
detect a strong effect (Supplementary Table 6 ).
