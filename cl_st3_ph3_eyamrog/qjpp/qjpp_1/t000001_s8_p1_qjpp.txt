High SBP is a common modifiable risk factor for IHD
. IHD is the leading cause of death and disability
worldwide, accounting for an estimated 9.1 million (95% uncertainty interval
(UI) = 8.4–9.7 million) deaths, 197 million (178–219) prevalent cases
and 182 million (170–194) disability-adjusted life years in 2019 (ref.
