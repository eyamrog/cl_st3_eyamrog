At the outset of this study, we searched for evidence
of onwards transmission from a chronically infected patient in our
studied cohort, yet we found no such evidence in the
publications or from epidemiological investigations of our TASMC patients. This
is not necessarily surprising, as often such patients are bedridden
and in strict isolation both due to COVID-19 and to
their background conditions. However, this does raise the possibility that
(1) chronic infections harbor variants that are non-infectious and (2)
chronic infections harbor non-transmissible virus—options that we discuss below.
