23andMe participants have been genotyped on a variety of platforms
and imputed against a reference panel comprising 56.5 million variants
from the 1000 Genomes Project phase 3 (ref. ) and
UK10K . Putative LRRK2 LoF variants were defined as those
classified as high confidence by LOFTEE. Variants were manually assessed
for call rate, genotyping and imputation quality and manually curated
to ensure they were expected to cause true LoF.
