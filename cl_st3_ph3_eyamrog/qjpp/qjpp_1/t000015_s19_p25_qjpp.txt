Immunohistochemical staining of the spinal cord and brain sections with
an antibody specific to the oligonucleotide backbone of ION363 revealed
diffuse predominantly cytoplasmic puncta within neurons (Fig. 5b and Extended
Data Fig. 7a ). This pattern suggests broad distribution of
ION363 throughout the patient’s central nervous system (CNS), and that,
at least 2 months after the last therapeutic infusion, the
ASO perdures in the treated brain and spinal cord.
