Single-cell suspensions obtained from tissue digests were blocked in PBS
5% BSA containing human anti-Fc block (Miltenyi), except when CD16
was in the panel, stained for FACS analysis or sorting
with Fixable Viability Dye eFluor 780 (eBioscience, 1:1,000), DAPI (Invitrogen,
1:50,000) and antibodies (all from Biolegend, except anti-Pdpn: clone NZ-1.3
from eBioscience and anti-FAP:sheep from Biotechne) in PBS with 5%
BSA and 5 mM EDTA for 20 min on ice.
After washing in the same buffer, cells were either analyzed
(LSRII or Fortessa X20) or sorted (Aria III, 100-µm nozzle).
All antibodies used for FACS analysis and sorting can be
found in Supplementary Table 10 .
