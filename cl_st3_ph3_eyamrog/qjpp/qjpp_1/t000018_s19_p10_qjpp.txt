In silico cell type deconvolution showed that M4 and M5
were predominantly associated with stromal cells including fibroblasts and granulocytes,
such as neutrophils (Extended Data Fig. 2b ). Projection of
modules M4 and M5 onto single-cell transcriptomic datasets derived from
inflamed and noninflamed CD and UC patient tissue suggested that
module M4 reflects the presence of ‘activated/inflammatory fibroblasts’ whereas module
M5 reflects ‘myeloid cells/inflammatory monocytes’ (Fig. 3a,b ).
