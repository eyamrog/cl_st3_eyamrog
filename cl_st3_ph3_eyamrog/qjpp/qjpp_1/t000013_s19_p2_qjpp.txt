In our first implementation of the mCARMEN platform, we designed
a panel to detect 21 clinically relevant human respiratory viruses
(Supplementary Table 4 ). This included all viruses covered by
BioFire RP2.1-SARS-CoV-2, four other human-associated coronaviruses and both influenza strains-
as well as a few additional illness-inducing viruses . To
generate maximally active virus-specific crRNAs and PCR primers to detect
the 21 viruses, we applied the assay design method ADAPT
(Activity-informed Design with All-inclusive Patrolling of Targets; described in the
Methods ) . We were able to encompass the full
genomic diversity of these viral families by including multiple primers,
if needed.
