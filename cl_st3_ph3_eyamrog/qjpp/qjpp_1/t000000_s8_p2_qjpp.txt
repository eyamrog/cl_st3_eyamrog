By January 2022, approximately 49% of the global population had
received a full two doses of a COVID-19 vaccine, although
delivery varied greatly between (and within) countries . Many high-income
countries enjoyed very successful vaccination campaigns, with several exceeding 90%
coverage of adults (aged 16 years and older). However, among
many low-income and lower-middle-income countries, vaccine availability continues to be
considerably more limited, with low-income countries counting for as little
as 0.9% of the overall total vaccine deployed .
