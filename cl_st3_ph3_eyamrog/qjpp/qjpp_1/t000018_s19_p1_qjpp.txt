Inflamed tissue from patients with IBD is commonly resected when
either medical therapies have failed, when patients opt for elective
surgery or when acute complications require emergency surgery. We used
such tissues (referred to as difficult-to-treat IBD) as our discovery
cohort (Extended Data Fig. 1 ). Amongst IBD tissue samples
( n = 41) from the 31 patients in this
cohort, 15 were macroscopically uninflamed, including seven samples for which
paired uninflamed/inflamed tissue was available. We additionally profiled unaffected, nontumour
tissue ( n = 39) collected from 39 patients with
colorectal cancer (CRC) and undergoing surgery as non-IBD controls. Bulk
RNA-sequencing (RNA-seq) was used to generate whole-tissue gene expression profiles
across all samples ( n = 41 IBD and n
= 39 non-IBD; the‘discovery cohort’).
