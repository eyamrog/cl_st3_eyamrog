Focusing on the results for the individual mutations themselves, we
found only one mutation, E484K, had more than five specimens
that differed in their results between NGS and VIP (Fig.
5e ). The 7 E484K discrepancies are attributed to the
cross-reactive signals between E484K and T478K; thus, new crRNA designs
are likely needed to optimally differentiate these signals (Supplementary Figs.
6b and 7b,c ). Altogether, we found that VIP had
97.7% concordance to NGS at allelic discrimination.
