Much interest has arisen in cases of chronic infection since
the first emergence of VOCs. One major hypothesis that was
proposed when the Alpha VOC was first discovered, and, more
recently, with the rise of the Omicron variant, was that
these variants evolved due to selection pressure created during chronic
infections . More specifically, the hypothesis posited that strong selection
for antibody evasion mutations may occur in immunocompromised individuals with
chronic infection who are treated with convalescent plasma (CP) or
monoclonal antibodies (mAbs), collectively denoted here as antibody-based treatments (ABTs).
