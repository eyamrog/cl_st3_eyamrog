RNA from brain or spinal cord samples was isolated with
TRIzol reagent (Invitrogen); its concentration was measured using NanoDrop 2000
(Thermo Fisher Scientific); and cDNA was generated with SuperScript IV
reverse transcriptase (Invitrogen) according to the manufacturer’s protocol. GoTaq G2
Hot Start Master Mix (Promega) and PowerUp SYBR Green Master
Mix (Thermo Fisher Scientific) were used to set up PCR
or qPCR reactions, respectively. All primers used in this study
are listed in Supplementary Table 1 .
