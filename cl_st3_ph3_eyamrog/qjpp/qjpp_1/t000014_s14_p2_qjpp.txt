Community doctors recruited participants from one clinic site in Lianshui
County, Jiangsu Province. Healthy participants, male or female, between 18
and 59 years of age, who had completed one-dose priming
of CoronaVac in the past 1–3 months or two-dose priming
of CoronaVac in the past 3–6 months were recruited for
eligibility screening. Investigators verified the vaccination record and checked the
medical history of each participant.
