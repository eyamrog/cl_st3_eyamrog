Unfortunately, funding models for scientific research are aligned with the
mechanism of paper publication, where one can definitively mark the
end of a project (and also the end of the
funding). However, with data and code, there is often no
specific end point because other investigators may reuse the data
or code for years into the future. Term-based project funding,
which is the structure of almost all research funding, is
simply not designed to provide support for maintaining materials on
an uncertain timeline.
