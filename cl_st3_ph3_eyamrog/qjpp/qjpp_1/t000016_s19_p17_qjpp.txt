On the subset of the US external validation set with
pathologist reviews, the algorithms exhibited a similar level of agreement
with the uropathologists as the US general pathologists did (0.828,
95% CI, 0.781–0.869 versus 0.820, 95% CI, 0.760–0.865; P =
0.53). The representative algorithm had higher sensitivity for tumor (96.4%,
95% CI of all algorithms, 96.6–99.5) than the representative pathologist
(91.9%, 95% CI of all pathologists, 89.3–95.5) but lower specificity
(75.0%, 95% CI of all algorithms, 61.2–82.7 versus 95.0%, 95%
CI of all pathologists, 87.4–98.1). On average, the algorithms missed
1.9% of cancers, whereas the pathologists missed 7.3%.
