Negative detection control is a negative control for the Fluidigm
detection step and is prepared by adding nuclease-free water (instead
of amplified RNA) to the sample mix without MgCl 2
. This should yield a negative result for all targets
on the panel. No crRNA control is a negative control
for the Fluidigm detection step and is prepared by adding
nuclease-free water (instead of 1 µM crRNA) to the assay
mix. This should yield a negative result for all targets
on the panel.
