Finally, we come to the most critical legal challenge of
all. Women who resort to abortions outside the purview of
the MTP Act are criminals (and so are their providers,
if the abortion was not self-induced) under Section 312 of
the Indian Penal Code ( 22 ). Abortion has not
been decriminalized in India, and the MTP Act merely outlines
the circumstances under which abortion will not be considered criminal.
Thus, women in India face the double burden of limited
access to safe and legal abortions and being criminalized if
other safe means are used.
