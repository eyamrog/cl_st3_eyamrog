Cash-transfer programs have one or two purposes: consumption support or
asset building. Most programs aim to alleviate liquidity constraints and
provide resources for consumption needs. Other programs are designed to
support asset accumulation for long-term investments in education, homeownership, entrepreneurship,
and retirement. Programs that pursue asset building as a purpose
undertake social investment to promote and strengthen individuals’ financial and
human capabilities and opportunities for inclusive growth ( 102 ,
103 ).
