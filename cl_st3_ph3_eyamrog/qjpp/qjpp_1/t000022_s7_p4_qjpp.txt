In this review, we ( a ) summarize concerns about
big data and health equity, ( b ) present a
series of analytic approaches to explore and address inequities using
big data, and ( c ) discuss data augmentation methods
to embed a health equity lens into big data research.
We use the five Vs framing ( 6 ), encompassing
volume, velocity, veracity, variety, and value ( Figure 1 ),
and add a sixth V, virtuosity, to reconceptualize big data
research to explicitly focus on equity (see the sidebar titled
The Six Vs).
