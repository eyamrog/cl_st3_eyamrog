For the most part, the policy environment is intended to
ensure the sexual and reproductive health and rights of women
in LMICs, including access to abortion services should they be
required. The critical message, however, is that abortion rights need
to be part of an overall package of sexual and
reproductive health and rights. The need to terminate a pregnancy
may not arise if the pregnancy is planned and does
not jeopardize the life, health, and well-being of the mother.
Furthermore, the policy environment needs to be coherent and supported
by enabling regulatory environments.
