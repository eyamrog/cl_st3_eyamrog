150-bp paired-end READS were generated. The READS were processed using
the described bioinformatic pipeline, which can be divided into five
steps: (1) READS pre-processing; (2) tag parsing and initial alignment;
(3) single-strand consensus sequence (SSCS) assembly; (4) duplex consensus sequencing
(DCS) assembly; and (5) mutational calling. Points 2–4 were performed
