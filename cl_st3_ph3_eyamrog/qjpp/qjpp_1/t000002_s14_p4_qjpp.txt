The primary outcomes were identified using any incident billing code
in EHR. We excluded any new diagnoses coded during the
first 6 months of monitoring, assuming that such conditions were
likely prevalent but not yet recognized clinically. The EHR data
from different participating sites were mapped and harmonized using the
Observational Medical Outcomes Partnership common data model . We used
the ICD to phecode map developed by Zheng et al.
to map the EHR data to create phecodes (Supplementary Tables
1 and 2 ). We mapped ICD9CM and ICD10CM ‘source’
codes found in the AoURP Curated Data Repository to phecodes,
which were used as outcomes.
