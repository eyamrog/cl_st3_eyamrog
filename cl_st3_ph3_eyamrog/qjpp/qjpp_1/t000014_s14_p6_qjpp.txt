One of the study vaccines, CoronaVac (Sinovac), is an inactivated
whole-virion vaccine with aluminum hydroxide as the adjuvant and was
developed in China. Each dose of COVID-19 vaccine contains 3
μg SARS-CoV-2 virion in a 0.5-ml aqueous suspension for injection
with 0.45 mg ml aluminum. The other study vaccine is
Convidecia (CanSino), a recombinant AD5-vectored COVID-19 vaccine, which contains 5
× 10 viral particles per dose.
