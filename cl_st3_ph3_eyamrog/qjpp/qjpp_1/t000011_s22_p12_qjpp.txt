To summarize, fractionation of vaccine doses for SARS-CoV-2 is expected
to be a cost-effective strategy for mitigating the lingering threat
of the COVID-19 pandemic in India. If COVID-19 remains a
persistent threat, especially with multiple SARS-CoV-2 variants escaping immunity, fractional
dosing of vaccines might provide additional public health and economic
benefits, especially when the global supply of vaccines is limited
or in the early period of a new vaccine that
is developed to target new variants in the future.
