Safety assessments included determination of ECOG performance status, clinical laboratory
testing, vital signs, electrocardiograms, brain imaging (MRI or CT scan)
and physical examinations. AEs were graded according to National Cancer
Institute Common Terminology Criteria (v4.03) . Treatment-emergent AEs were defined
as any AE occurring between the first dose of avapritinib
through 30 d after the last dose of avapritinib, any
event that was considered related to the study drug regardless
of the start date of the event, or any event
that was present at baseline but worsened intensity or was
subsequently considered related to the study drug by the investigator.
