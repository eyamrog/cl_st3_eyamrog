The total number of patients enrolled in part 1 was
dependent on the observed safety profile, which determined the number
of patients per dose cohort, as well as the number
of dose escalations required to identify the MTD and RP2D.
It was expected that approximately 25 patients who met the
criteria for the dose-determining population (described below) would be enrolled
in part 1. In part 2, to adequately assess the
safety of avapritinib, approximately 45 patients were planned for enrollment
in cohort 1 at a starting dose of 300 mg
QD, and approximately 10 patients were planned to be enrolled
in cohort 2 at a starting dose of 200 mg
QD. With 55 patients, there is approximately 94% probability of
observing an AE that occurs at a frequency of ≥5%.
