For clinical relevance, we developed an automated variant calling procedure
that evaluates the mutation-specific signal in SARS-CoV-2-positive patient specimens and
returns a variant lineage result (Supplementary Fig. 6a ; described
in the Methods ). For some mutations at the same
or similar genomic position, we observed cross-reactive signals that we
overcame by comparing the maximum fluorescence ratios between those mutations
and assigning the positive call to the higher of the
two (Supplementary Fig. 6b ).
