The 23andMe dataset includes self-reported phenotype data for thousands of
phenotypes across a diverse range of categories. These phenotypes have
different sample sizes and prevalence, so the power to detect
associations varies widely. We began with a curated set of
748 disease phenotypes. We then applied a liberal filter based
on our power to detect an association with carrier status.
More specifically, assuming a minor allele frequency of 2 ×
10 , we restricted to phenotypes where we had power
0.1 to detect an association effect with odds ratio (OR)
> 1.3 (for binary traits) or β > 0.2 (for
quantitative traits) at α = 0.0001 significance. This left us
with 460 binary and 14 quantitative phenotypes.
