IPTW was used as another statistical approach to allow for
outcome comparison between patients treated with axi-cel and patients treated
with tisa-cel. In the IPTW method, the weight for each
patient is calculated by inverting the probability of receiving the
treatment the patient actually receives. PSM and IPTW rely on
different statistical matching approaches, provide different information and should be
interpreted differently. The first one (PSM) allows for assessing average
treatment effect for the treated (ATT), whereas the other (IPTW
with the weighting technique used here) provides estimation of the
average treatment effect (ATE). The first gives the average effect
of treatment on those patients who ultimately received one CAR
T versus the other, whereas the second provides the average
effect of theoretically moving the entire population from receiving one
CAR T to the other. For IPTW, the exact same
covariates as for PSM were used for the logistic regression
model to calculate the propensity of receiving one of the
CAR T products versus the other. Methodology underlying propensity-score-based matched
comparisons and differences with adjustment approaches have been reviewed elsewhere
