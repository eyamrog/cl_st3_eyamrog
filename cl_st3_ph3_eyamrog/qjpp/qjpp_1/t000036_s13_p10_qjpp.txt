The years since the International Conference on Population and Development
(ICPD) in 1994 have seen some progress in terms of
laws governing abortion access. As of 2019, 47 countries, 40
of these in developing regions, have expanded the grounds for
legal abortion ( Table 1 ). Unfortunately, in at least
