SM is driven by the KIT p.Asp816Val (D816V) mutation in
approximately 95% of cases , yet until recently, therapies designed
to specifically target KIT D816V were unavailable . Imatinib lacks
activity against KIT D816V and is approved in the United
States for the rare patients who have ASM without KIT
D816V mutation or unknown KIT mutation status . The multikinase
inhibitor midostaurin is approved for AdvSM; however, few patients achieve
complete remission, and gastrointestinal adverse events (AEs) are common .
Safe and effective therapies for patients with AdvSM remain an
area of unmet need.
