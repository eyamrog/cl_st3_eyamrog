The basic mobile phone enables phone calls and text (SMS)
messages; both functions remain vital to the user experience (
14 , 58 , 59 , 77 ). However, the
advent of the smartphone in 2001, with access to Internet
data, scales up access to new functionalities, with video streaming,
social media, and the ability to send email among the
most popular ( 20 , 24 , 27 ). Research
on mobile data subscribership estimates approximately 73% penetration of the
global market; smartphones are overtaking the classic phone and account
for approximately 63% of all phones in use ( 48
).
