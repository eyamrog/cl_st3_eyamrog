To benchmark mCARMEN RVP performance to comparator assay results, we
analyzed 385 SARS-CoV-2-positive and 140 SARS-CoV-2-negative patient specimens and compared
these results to both prior and concurrent RT–qPCR evaluation (Fig.
2b ). By the time of comparative evaluation, the number
of RT–qPCR-positive specimens dropped from 385 to 316, suggesting substantial
viral degradation either from extended sample storage or multiple freeze–thaw
cycles. Nonetheless, mCARMEN identified all 316 (100% sensitivity) of the
concurrent RT–qPCR-positive specimens. We noted that mCARMEN further detected SARS-CoV-2
in 42 specimens that tested positive by prior RT–qPCR but
were missed by concurrent RT–qPCR testing, suggesting that mCARMEN is
more robust to low viral quantity.
