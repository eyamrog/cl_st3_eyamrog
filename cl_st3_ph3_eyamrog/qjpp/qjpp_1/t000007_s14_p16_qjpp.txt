After validating that the model is accurate enough (weighted F1
score of 0.78, weighted accuracy of 0.82), we used two
approaches for assessing feature effect on the outcome of antibody
evasion: the feature importance as calculated by the trees and
SHAP values . SHAP values were obtained for each mutation
in each test set (that is, for each patient separately)
(Fig. 1 ) and were aggregated to obtain the average
effect that each feature has on the model’s outcome (Fig.
1 and Supplementary Figs. 2 and 3 ).
