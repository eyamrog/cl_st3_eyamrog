At least 10 ml of whole blood was collected by
blood draw using EDTA as anticoagulant. Plasma was separated within
5 hours through two different centrifugation steps (the first at
room temperature for 10 minutes at 1,600 g and the
second at 3,000 g for the same time and temperature),
obtaining up to 3 ml of plasma. Plasma was stored
at −80 °C until ctDNA extraction.
