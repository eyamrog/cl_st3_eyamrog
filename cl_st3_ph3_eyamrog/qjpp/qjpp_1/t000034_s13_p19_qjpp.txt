Asset-building tax subsidies benefit the wealthy but do little to
help the poor in building wealth ( 113 ). Nearly
80% of the benefits for the four largest asset-building tax
incentives (for homeownership, financial investment, retirement saving, and education) have
gone to households in the top income quintile ( 41
). In other words, this form of cash transfer is
very large (more than means-tested social assistance) and is highly
regressive (most of the benefits go to the top).
