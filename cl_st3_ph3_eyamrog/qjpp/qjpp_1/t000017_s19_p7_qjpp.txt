In patients with AdvSM ( n = 69), the most
frequent non-hematologic AEs of any grade were periorbital edema (65%),
peripheral edema (45%), diarrhea (43%), nausea (42%), fatigue (35%) and
vomiting (32%). The most frequent non-hematologic AEs of grade 3
or 4 were fatigue (10%), nausea and vomiting (both 4%),
and arthralgia and hypokalemia (both 3%). The incidence of non-hematologic
AEs was comparable between all patients and those with confirmed
AdvSM.
