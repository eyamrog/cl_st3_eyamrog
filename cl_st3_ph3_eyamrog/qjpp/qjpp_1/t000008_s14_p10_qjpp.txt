DNA was extracted using AllPrep DNA/RNA micro kits (Qiagen) following
the manufacturer’s instructions from participant liver biopsy samples and naïve,
control human liver samples (iSpecimen). Samples were digested with the
restriction enzyme KpnI, which cuts the vector genome at nucleotide
positions 2,207 and 3,596, to separate out vector genome units
within concatemeric forms (Supplementary Fig. 4c ). Before the droplet
generation step of ddPCR, 5 µl of DNA at a
concentration of 2 ng µl was incubated with 4 units
of high-fidelity KpnI (KpnI-HF) enzyme (New England Biolabs) in the
ddPCR reaction mixture at 37 °C for 30 min.
