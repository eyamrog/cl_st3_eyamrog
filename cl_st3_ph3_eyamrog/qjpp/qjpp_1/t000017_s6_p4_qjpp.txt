Notably, KIT D816V became undetectable in the BM of 30%
of patients with AdvSM, using a highly sensitive ddPCR assay.
This demonstrated that avapritinib induced deep molecular responses, an outcome
that represents a new response benchmark in AdvSM. In an
earlier multivariate analysis of 38 midostaurin-treated patients with AdvSM, a
mere 25% reduction in KIT D816V RNA-expressed allele burden was
statistically associated with prolonged survival .
