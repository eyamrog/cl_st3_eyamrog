For ICV injections, newborn animals were first anesthetized on ice,
and then 20 μg of ION363 or NTC oligonucleotide in
a total volume of 5 μl was injected into the
right lateral ventricle (2 mm frontal and 1 mm lateral
to bregma) using a Hamilton Neuros syringe. After the injection,
animals were placed on a heating pad and monitored until
recovery.
