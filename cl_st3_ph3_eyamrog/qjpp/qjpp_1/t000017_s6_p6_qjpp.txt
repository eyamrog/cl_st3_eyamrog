Although patients with and without S/A/R mutations at baseline had
similar response rates, the presence of S/A/R mutations or a
MARS score of ≥2 was associated with shorter OS. These
findings suggest that although avapritinib reduced mast cell burden and
C-findings regardless of co-mutation status, patients with adverse myeloid co-mutations
such as S/A/R were more likely to experience disease progression.
In contrast, patients with AdvSM and a MARS score of
0–1 had excellent outcomes on avapritinib monotherapy in this study,
as demonstrated by a 100% 24-month OS rate.
