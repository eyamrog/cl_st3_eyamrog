The foundation of contemporary representations of women rests on the
distinction between the private and public sphere ( Parisi, 2010
). Apic is no exception to this trend. The dichotomy
is not as important, however, in the representation of women's
political, economic and social empowerment in gkis . The collocations
‘private-sphere’ and ‘domestic sphere’ are not used. The ‘private sector’
features prominently, but refers to arenas of the capitalist enterprise,
