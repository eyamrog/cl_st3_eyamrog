The esl writers used personal pronouns more frequently than bawe
writers: we was used 6.7 times per 1,000 words (1.7/1,000
for bawe ) and I 1.5 times per 1,000 words
(0.1/1,000 words for bawe ). This greater use by the
esl learners often indicated lack of caution (‘[my results] are
reliable, because I ’ve proved it myself’). The learners also
used I and we to assert their own opinions. Some
inappropriate uses appeared to stem from using textbooks as a
discourse model (‘ I will answer some questions that are
going to make it easier for anyone to understand pulse
rate’). Also inappropriate was using we to include a general
audience (‘ we must take good care of the ozone
layer’).
