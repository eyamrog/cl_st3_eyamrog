According to Zhang (2006), when an rc and a dp
modify the head np together, two types of nominal constructions
can be classified by the order of the rc and
dp : the outer modifier nominal ( omn ) and
the inner modifier nominal ( imn ). The former has
an rc as an outer modifier, namely, the structure of
the [ rc - dp -head np ]. The latter
is the inverse ordering of two modifiers: [ dp -
rc -head np ] with an rc as an inner
modifier. The two constructions can be used interchangeably in some
contexts such as Examples 1a and 1b. However, according to
Zhang (2006), in Examples 2a and 2b, both are acceptable,
and they are more inclined to use the former. This
alternation may be affected by semantic, morphosyntactic, and producer-related factors.
