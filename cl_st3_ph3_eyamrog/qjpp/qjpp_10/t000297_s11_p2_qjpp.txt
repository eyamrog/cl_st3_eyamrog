and booster constructions than their EL1 counterparts by adopting a
more limited range of expressions and showing greater problems in
conveying a precise degree of certainty. Also using the same
learner corpus, Flowerdew (1998) compared the underuse and overuse of
cause/effect markers between Hong Kong learners and L1 expert users,
finding that writing by the former was characterised by an
