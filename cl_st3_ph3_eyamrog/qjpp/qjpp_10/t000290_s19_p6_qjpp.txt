0.001. Considering first the results pertaining to vowel type, the
finding of significant F1 differences for each vowel in the
pre-/ɡ/ context (i.e., bag ‐ beg ‐ bagel ) would
suggest the lack of a full and complete merger of
the vowels, which would entail no significant differences in any
aspect of their acoustic properties. However, comparing these differences to
the other contexts indicates that there is much more cross-vowel
proximity within this context than any other (except /ŋ/, but
see below). Before velar /k/, F1 differences between vowels are
at least twice as large than they are before /ɡ/,
and F2 differences are robust as well. The results for
the other coda class are similar to those for /k/.
Moreover, significant F2 differences before /ɡ/ occur only between /e/
versus /æ/, with /ɛ/ not being significantly retracted in relation
to /e/; minimally, this suggests the potential for a fair
degree of overlap between beg and bagel , mostly in
the front-back dimension. For vowel formants before /ŋ/, which pertain
only to /æ/ versus /ɛ/ (i.e., gang , mango ,
plank , etc. versus length , Jenga ), the low
token count for the /ɛ/ group limits our ability to
rely on these results, which is unfortunate because this context
exhibits the least amount of difference between vowels and thus
the greatest potential for merger in F1/F2 space.
