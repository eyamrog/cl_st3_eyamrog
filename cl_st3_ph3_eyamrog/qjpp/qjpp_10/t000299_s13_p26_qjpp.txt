Learners used that -clauses more than twice as frequently as
the research articles. They used controlling verbs ( say ,
think and believe ) that are common in conversation, but
they also used controlling words common in academic writing (
find , show and fact ). The study views this
as evidence of learners beginning to share academic values and
language. Other evidence of drawing on conversational norms was students’
emphasis on their own beliefs and a tendency to draw
on common knowledge.
