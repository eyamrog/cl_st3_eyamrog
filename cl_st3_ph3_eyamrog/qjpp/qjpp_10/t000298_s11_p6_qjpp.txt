The last meaning is the ‘emphatic’ just . Different from
the other meanings which originated from the core meaning of
just – ‘restriction’, the ‘emphatic’ just manifests an opposite reading,
functioning as a particle to ‘emphasize the expression with which
it enters into construction’ ( Lee, 1987 : 394). An
example of the ‘emphatic’ just is presented in Example 4.
( 4 ) ‘Emphatic’ just I just can’t keep going.
( Lee, 1987 : 394)
