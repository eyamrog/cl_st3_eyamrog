Although this study closely examined L1 influence on learners’ use
of collocations from corpus data, completely eliminating subjectivity in L1
influence analysis remained a challenge. Since the study did not
observe learners’ cognitive activities when they used collocations, it cannot
conclude that learners must have used their L1 lexicon in
the way it presumed. Consequently, one may find it effective
to integrate the results from corpus-based data with psycholinguistic experiments
that analyse learners’ cognitive process.
