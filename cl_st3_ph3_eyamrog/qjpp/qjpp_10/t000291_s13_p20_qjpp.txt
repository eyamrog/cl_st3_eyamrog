In compiling apic , I sought to parallel to some
degree the selection criteria of gkis , which avoids including
news items (first-order discourses) in its corpus, preferring texts in
which (mainly left-leaning) authors make explicit their political viewpoints and
engage in debate about political principles. I included texts in
apic on the basis of the aforementioned distinction between first-
and second-order political discourses in a handful of Arabic-language political
websites. Consequently, apic represents a wider range of viewpoints than
gkis , including left-leaning as well as neoliberal outlets (see
Table 3 ).
