Moreover, more studies should be conducted in interlanguage, 2 with
subjects from different language backgrounds. The transfer of classifier use
from native languages has already been tested ( Wu and
Sheng, 2014 ; Wu and Lü, 2016 ; and Lü
and Wu, 2017 ). However, the native language types are
limited, and the classification is unclear. Gil’s (2013) typological investigation
of classifiers in languages divided them into three categories: obligatory,
optional, and absent. These can be a reference for native
language classification.
