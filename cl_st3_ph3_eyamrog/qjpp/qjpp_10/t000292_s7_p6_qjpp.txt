This annotated corpus is based on the Series I of
the ‘Third Republic’, which stores the debate transcripts that took
place in the Portuguese National Assembly since 1976, when the
first democratically elected Parliament took seat, covering the full story
of the Portuguese democracy. Thus, this corpus builds on the
growing collection of parliamentary corpora at the European level (
Marx and Schuth, 2010 ; Ogrodniczuk, 2012 ; Pražák and
Šmídl, 2012 ; Abercrombie and Batista-Navarro, 2018 ; Nanni et
al ., 2018 ; Hansen, 2018 ; and Wissik and
Pirkir, 2018 ) and offers tools for discourse analysis and
comparative studies at the national and international levels.
