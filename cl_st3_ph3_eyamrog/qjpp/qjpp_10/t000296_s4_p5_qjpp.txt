In addition, future researchers should conduct larger-scale replication studies. This
study focussed exclusively on make + noun collocations, so the
results might not apply to other high-frequency English verb +
noun collocations such as have , take and give .
Also, over half of the learners’ proficiency in this study
was B1 or higher; hence, A2 learner data were relatively
limited. Therefore, future research needs to shed more light on
lower-level learners’ collocation acquisition.
