Our findings suggested that the ‘depreciatory’ and ‘restrictive’ just were,
overall, the most frequently used meanings in all corpora, while
the ‘emphatic’ and ‘specificatory’ just were less commonly adopted. By
adding up the two high-frequency meanings in the learner corpora,
we found that learners used the adverb just with a
smaller variety of meanings. The two major meanings made up
a great proportion of the sub-corpus, while the other meanings
each accounted for less than 10 percent.
