Despite its history of diverse in-migration and settlement, ethnolinguistic variation
is little-documented in Manitoba. Certain distinctive production patterns of ethnically
Filipino-Winnipeggers ( Tran 2018 ; Onosson, Rosen & Li 2019
) and Mennonite-Manitobans ( Hadei 2020 ; Onosson 2020 )
have only recently been documented. Being of particular social and
linguistic importance within Manitoba, the Mennonite and Filipino communities were
therefore selected to form major components of the corpus utilized
for the present study (see section 3.1). The following subsections
provide a short synopsis of each community.
