The semantic broadness of tsukuru appears to affect learners at
all proficiency levels – that is, they tend to apply
the meanings of tsukuru to English make + noun collocations
beyond the acceptable semantic fields of make . This indicates
that they are unaware of the semantic differences between tsukuru
and make , which was also true for the learners
in Mochizuki (2007) and Fukutomi (2012) .
