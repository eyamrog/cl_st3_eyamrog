The quality of the corpus in terms of various elements
is also very important. In particular, Granger (2003 : 465)
emphasises that learner corpora should be supplemented with proper annotation,
such as error tagging. These annotations will allow for more
systematic searches of corpus data, more accurate error statistics, and
in-depth analyses of specific error types. However, despite the possibility
of automated error tagging, in the case of learner data,
manual annotations of learner errors are still needed.
