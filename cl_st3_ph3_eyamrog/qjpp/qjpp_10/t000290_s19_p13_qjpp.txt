To summarize the main findings regarding vowel durations: (i) /æ/
is the longest vowel with the least cross-coda variation (ii)
/ɛ/ is the shortest vowel with the greatest cross-coda variation
(iii) Coda /k/ correlates with abbreviated duration of /ɛ, e/
(iv) Coda /ɡ/ correlates with increased duration of /ɛ/, and
abbreviated duration of /e/ (v) Coda /ŋ/ correlates with abbreviated
duration of /æ/ (vi) Women produce consistently longer vowels than
men (vii) Filipino /ɛ/ is shorter than Mennonite /ɛ/
