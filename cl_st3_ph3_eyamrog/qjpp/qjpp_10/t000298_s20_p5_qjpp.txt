In the learner corpora, adult Taiwanese efl learners showed preferences
for the pattern [X + be + ‘depreciatory’ just +
verb (phrase)] (51.6 percent), [X + be + ‘depreciatory’ just
+ noun (phrase)] (16.1 percent) and [‘depreciatory’ just + imperative]
(16.1 percent). As for the younger learners, [X + be
+ ‘depreciatory’ just + verb (phrase)] (43 percent) was also
the most common, followed by [X + be + ‘depreciatory’
just + noun (phrase)] (26.4 percent) and [‘depreciatory’ just +
imperative] (25 percent).
