To answer RQ1, we calculated the proportion of L1 influence
on each proficiency level and performed a chi-square test with
R version 4.0.3 ( R Core Team, 2020 ). Table
3 summarises the proportions of L1 influence on learners’ make
+ noun collocations. A2 and B1 learners closely share proportions
of L1 influence, with only a 1.4 percent difference. B2
learners produced the least unusual collocations caused by L1 influence
(15.6 percent). However, the result did not show any statistically
significant differences in the frequency of L1 influence among the
learners. This result suggests that L1 influence persists during the
developmental process of learners’ collocational knowledge.
