It is noteworthy that the two older corpora match the
chronology of B-Brown/B-LOB and Brown/LOB, as well as newspaper corpora
of other postcolonial Englishes (for the 1930s and 1960s) that
are currently being compiled. In the absence of much balanced
historical IndE data, the newspaper corpora therefore allow for at
least tentative comparisons with some findings from Leech, Hundt, Mair,
and Smith (2009) , Leech (2011 , 2013) , and
Mair (2015) for the Brown family.
