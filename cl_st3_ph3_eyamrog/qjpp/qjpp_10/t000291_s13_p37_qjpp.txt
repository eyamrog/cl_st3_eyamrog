While the collocation ‘public sphere’ is prominent, there is only
a single instance in which the public sphere is represented
as a gendered arena, which is constructed as one from
which women are absent. The problem now is that this
notion of personal lives mattering within the political realm has
also become a way to demarcate and restrict women's participation
in the public sphere as well. ( Salvage Zone ,
25 July 2016)
