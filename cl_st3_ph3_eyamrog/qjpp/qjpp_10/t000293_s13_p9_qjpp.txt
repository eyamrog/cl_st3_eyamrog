Some general observations can be made about the aforementioned learner
corpora. First of all, most of the data used in
the compilation of these corpora are written data, rather than
spoken data. Second, the data contained in each corpus are
collected from a fairly homogeneous group of participants (e.g., first-year
students). Third, the data are mostly derived from timed output
(e.g., classroom assignments and placement tests). Another shared feature of
these corpora is that the corpus data are not annotated.
