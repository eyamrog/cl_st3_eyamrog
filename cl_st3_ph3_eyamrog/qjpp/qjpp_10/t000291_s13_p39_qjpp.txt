Encouraging women's public participation is a pillar of the UN's
political policy for the Middle East ( Moghadem, 2005 ;
Braunstein, 2012 ; and Pfeifer, 2016 ). This policy also
aims to reduce the abuse and discrimination that the women
in the Middle East experience in the private sphere. The
status of women in the Middle East is a popular
liberal cause. Consequently, the division of the world into public
and private spheres is a bedrock of apic 's political
discourse. However, while women previously were thought to naturally occupy
the private sphere, women in apic 's cosmos now occupy
the public sphere with much greater frequency, though after much
