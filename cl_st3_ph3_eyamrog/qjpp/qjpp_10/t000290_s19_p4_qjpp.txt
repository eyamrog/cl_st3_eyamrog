Significant differences between coda contexts were identified for formants of
the three vowels. For /æ/, coda /k/ exhibits only a
small increase in F1 (lower articulation) from the intercept (non-velar
codas) and no significant difference in F2. The voiced velars
/ɡ/ and /ŋ/ both correlate with lower F1 (higher articulation)
and higher F2 (advanced articulation) values for /æ/, with /ŋ/
producing the largest differences. /ɛ/ exhibits a similar pattern. Again,
coda /k/ correlates with higher F1 (lower articulation). As for
/æ/, codas /ɡ/ and /ŋ/ produce lower F1 (higher articulation)
and higher F2 (advanced articulation) values for /ɛ/; however, these
do not reach the level of significance for /ŋ/, most
likely due to low quantity of tokens, only eight, which
occur in that condition. For /e/, significant differences only occur
in F1, where codas /k/ and /ɡ/ correlate with slightly
lower values (a higher articulation); this is the only condition
where /k/ and /ɡ/ produce a similar direction of effect.

( Hammond 1999 :113) and so cannot be compared in
