H ave to , need to , and want to
show constant increases from the 1930s on, whereas be to
declines. Be to never occurs in its non-finite forms in
the corpora. This is perhaps unsurprising since non-finite be to
has been extremely restricted, if not obsolete, since the early
1800s in BrE ( Hundt 2014 ). No tendency either
way can be discerned for be able to. Finally, be
supposed to rises in frequency over the period, despite small
token numbers.
