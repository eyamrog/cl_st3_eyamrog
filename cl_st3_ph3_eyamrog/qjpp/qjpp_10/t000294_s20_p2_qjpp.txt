The percentages of the three major structures of lexical bundles
in each sub-corpus are presented in Figure 1 . This
shows that vp -based bundles, which are considered to be
characteristic of conversational spoken registers ( Biber et al .,
2004 , 2021 ; and Chen and Baker, 2010 ,
2016 ), comprise the majority in all three corpora. The
proportion of these bundles, however, decreases as proficiency increases (Low:
79.1 percent, Mid: 73.1 percent, High: 53.4 percent). Characteristic of
academic written registers, phrasal bundles (i.e., np -based and pp
-based bundles) amount to only 21 percent and 27 percent
of the bundles in the Low and Mid corpora, respectively,
but to over 45 percent of those in the High
corpus.
