The outermost tag is the <debate>. It contains data and
other sub-structures that pertain to a single, specific meeting of
the parliament. Thus, the tag includes the following fixed metadata:
Period . Corresponds to the historic data source, in this
case always ‘r3’, the third Republic. Legislature . Indicates the
government cycle in which the debate took place. Legislative_session .
Typically refers to a year period within each four-year legislature.
Number . Ordinal, per legislature session index of the debate.
Date . Date when the parliamentary session took place (not
the publication date of the document, which is usually one
or more days later).
