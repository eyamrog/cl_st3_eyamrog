Learner corpus research now serves as a popular research method
for studying issues in foreign/second language acquisition and L2 learning
and teaching. This section will examine English learner corpus-related studies
published in two Korean academic journals, et and kjal .
The year 2002 saw the publication of the first learner
corpus-based article in et . Since its initiation two decades
ago, there has been an exponential rise in the publication
of learner corpus studies in various Korean academic journals as
well as in et and kjal .
