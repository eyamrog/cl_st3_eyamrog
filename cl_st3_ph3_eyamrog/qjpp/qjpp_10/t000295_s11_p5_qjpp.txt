In addition, some potential factors require further study. Accessibility is
related to language processing, which affects imn / omn alternation.
Zhang (2018) used accessibility theory to explain the DPs’ distribution.
Moreover, other factors affecting accessibility include: the length of head
np s and rc s, number of head np s’
modifiers, distance between the head np s and the relativised
np s, and whether the head np s are necessary
arguments. The head np s’ lengths and rc s are
studied in this paper.
