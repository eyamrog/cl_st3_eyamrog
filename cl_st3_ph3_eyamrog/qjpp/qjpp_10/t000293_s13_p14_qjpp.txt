Beginning in 2002, learner corpus-based articles started to be published
in et and, later, in kjal in 2005. During the
period from 2006 to 2010, with the availability of skelc
and nickle the number of articles expanded rapidly: five articles
in et and eight articles in kjal . The 2010s
saw a notable increase in the number of articles to
twenty-two in each journal. Considering the beginning of the compilation
of the learner corpora in the late 2000s and early
2010s, the increase in interest and use of learner corpora
in subsequent years is a natural progression, and is clearly
reflected in the growing number of learner corpus-related articles. In
the following sections, all pf these articles will be reviewed
and analysed in terms of their research topics and areas,
data types, analysis methods and corpus compilation practices.
