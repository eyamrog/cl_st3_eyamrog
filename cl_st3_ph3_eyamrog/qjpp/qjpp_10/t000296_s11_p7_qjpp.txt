The same pattern has been reported in studies on learners’
productive collocational knowledge (e.g., Koya, 2003 ; Laufer and Waldman,

is, although some differences among studies do exist in terms
of learners’ collocational accuracy and degree of L1 influence based

significant effect of L1 on foreign-language learners’ collocational knowledge.
