Returning to the US, prevelar raising in the Upper Midwest
was first documented by Thomas (1947 ; cited in Bauer
& Parker 2008 ). Analysis of this as a change
in progress began under Zeller (1997) . The ANAE (
Labov, Ash & Boberg 2006 ) subsequently identified a prevelar
merger of Wisconsinite bag - bagel. Other studies of Wisconsin
describe a number of phenomena: raising of bag but absence
of a merger with either beg or bagel ( Bauer
& Parker 2008 ); differentiation between the voiceless (i.e., /k/)
and voiced (i.e., /ɡ/) velar coda environments, with pre-/ɡ/ context
being more raised ( Purnell 2008 ); and the complicated
interplay between prevelar raising and other vowel processes in the
region such as the Low Back Merger and Northern Cities
Shift ( Benson, Fox & Balkman 2011 ). To the
west, prevelar raising patterns have also been documented in Minnesota
( Labov, Ash & Boberg 2006 ; Benson, Fox &
Balkman 2011 ; Koffi 2014 ; Stanley forthcoming ), although
that state has received less attention in terms of detailed
phonetic analysis.
