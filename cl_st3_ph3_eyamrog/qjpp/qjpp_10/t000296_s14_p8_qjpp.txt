The first objective here was to observe L1 influence on
learners’ make + noun collocations. In this study, the term
‘L1 influence’ refers to negative L1 influence, which leads to
erroneous or unusual uses of the target language (English). Therefore,
all unusual collocations needed to be extracted first to check
whether or not they resulted from L1 influence. This study
hereafter defines the term ‘unusual’ as less frequent collocation uses
compared with those of native English speakers.
