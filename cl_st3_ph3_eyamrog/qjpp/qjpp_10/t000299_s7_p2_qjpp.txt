Since its inception in the late 1980s, lcr has remained
an active area of research and has been receiving increasing
attention from around the world (to which this Special Issue
of Corpora is partly a testament). In this paper, we
aim to highlight the state of lcr conducted in New
Zealand. In so doing, we provide a representative, rather than
an exhaustive, review of learner corpus studies undertaken by researchers
in this part of the world. All the studies reviewed
here have been conducted by New Zealand-based researchers on learner
corpus data collected in the country or overseas. The review
is organised based on the primary aims of the studies
into two broad categories: lexical studies and syntactic studies. It
should be noted that findings from the studies described in
this review often cut across the boundary of lexis and
syntax, shedding light on both lexical and syntactic aspects of
learner language. Thus, the categories mentioned in this review are
considered to be overlapping and we do not intend to
suggest a divide between lexis and syntax.
