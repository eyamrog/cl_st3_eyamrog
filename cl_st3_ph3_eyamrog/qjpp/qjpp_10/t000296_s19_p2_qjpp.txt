RQ2 was addressed by investigating the proportions of learners’ uses
of the de-lexical make . First, learners’ de-lexical make collocations
were compared based on their proficiency. Table 4 indicates that
learners continue to acquire the de-lexical make as their proficiency
levels increase. B2 learners, the most proficient group, use the
de-lexical make more than A2 and B1 learners. There is
almost an 11 percent surge from A2 to B2, and
this seems to indicate the development of learners’ collocational knowledge.
Nevertheless, Table 4 shows no statistically significant differences in frequency
amongst the learners.
