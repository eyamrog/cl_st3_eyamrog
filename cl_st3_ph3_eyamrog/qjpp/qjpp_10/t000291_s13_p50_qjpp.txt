Strong, fearless, organised, politically astute, involved, liberated, unified, knowledgeable, autonomous,
radical, anti-establishment and armed with assault rifles – this is
the ideal female political actor constructed in gkis , and
it is contrary to a purportedly watered-down version of ‘corporate’
‘Western feminism’: As young Kurdish women , we are very
lucky to inherit this legacy. The knowledge that women in
Kurdistan have led village uprisings, prison hunger strikes and rebellions,
founded guerrilla armies, and risked their lives for political causes
of course enriches our identity and breaks social chains and
taboos. ( Salvage Zone , 8 March 2017)
