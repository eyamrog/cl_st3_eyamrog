Considering that imperatives ‘stand[] out as clear examples of bald-on-record
usage’ ( Brown and Levinson, 1978 : 100), we speculated
that the higher frequency of the pattern [‘depreciatory’ just +
imperative] in the younger Taiwanese efl learner corpus might have
resulted from a limited grasp of politeness strategies. For elder
learners and native speakers, they might not risk damaging the
hearers’ face and turn to other means of stating their
opinions instead of adopting imperatives in their essays.
