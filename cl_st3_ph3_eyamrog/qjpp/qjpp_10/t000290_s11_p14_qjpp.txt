Although Filipino is a considered a distinct ethnicity within Canada,
it is important to mention that the Philippines is a
multi-ethnic society within which Filipino is simultaneously a nationality and
an ethnicity, akin to some usages of “Canadian” in that
respect (see the earlier reference to Canadian as the second-largest
ethnic group in Manitoba) . While a range of distinct
Philippine ethnic groups and languages are represented in Manitoba, including
Ilocano, Visayan, Kapampangan, Cebuano, and others, it is the Tagalog
language, associated with the eponymous ethnicity (the second-largest ethnic group
in the Philippines) which is by far the most widely-spoken,
to the extent that mother-tongue Tagalog speakers outnumber French as
well as all of Manitoba’s Indigenous languages, and are only
exceeded numerically by English and German (typically Mennonite) speakers.
