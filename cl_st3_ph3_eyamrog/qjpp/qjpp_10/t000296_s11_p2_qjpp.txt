Collocations are distinguished from ‘free combinations’ and ‘idioms’ ( Cowie,
1994 : 3169). In ‘free combinations’, verbs can be combined
with virtually any kind of noun (e.g., want a car
), while, in ‘idioms’, both the verb and the noun
are fixed and used in a restricted sense (e.g., kick
the bucket ). For this reason, substitution of both the
verb and the noun is not possible in idiomatic expressions
( Chen, 2016 ).
