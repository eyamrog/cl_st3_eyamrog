The rarity of extended would may be affected by the
large amount of backshift in the corpora, which makes extended
would indistinguishable from its use as the preterite of will
(hence the comparatively high amount of indeterminate tokens). Another factor
influencing the frequency of extended would is, once more, the
amount of direct quotes present in the corpora. Of the
thirty-two instances found in the three corpora, thirteen are in
direct quotes. Of those, eleven are to be found in
the SAVE mini corpus, which contains the most recent texts.
The apparent increase of extended would might simply be due
to changes in journalistic style, which, as we have seen,
appears to rely more on direct quotes in the twenty-first
century than in 1939. Ignoring the quoted material, frequencies of
extended would fall to four (1939), seven (1968) and eight
(2000s). None of these differences is significant.
