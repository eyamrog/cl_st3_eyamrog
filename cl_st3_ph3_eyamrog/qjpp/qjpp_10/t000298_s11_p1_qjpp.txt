The adverb just has its root in the Latin adjective
iust (‘righteous, legitimate’). Just entered English in the early-fifteenth century
as an adjective borrowed from the medieval French adjective juste
( Molina and Romano, 2012 ). Over time, the adverb
has undergone a semantic expansion, growing from its core meaning
‘restriction’ ( Brown and Levinson, 1978 ; and Lee, 1987
) into a rich variety of senses that it carries
today.
