To examine the effect of pH in the MD simulations,
we prepared three conditions: all histidine and sTyr residues of
the RGI3/RGFR1 and RGF1 were protonated to simulate an aqueous
solution at pH 6.0, all histidine residues were protonated but
sTyr was deprotonated (referred to as dp-sTyr pH 6.0), and
ε-protonated histidine and deprotonated sTyr were used for pH 8.0.
