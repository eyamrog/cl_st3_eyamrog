E. coli DH5α was cultured at 37°C in LB medium
for plasmid DNA extraction. E. coli BL21 (DE3) was cultured
at 37°C in LB medium for protein expression. E. coli
DH10Bac was cultured at 37°C in LB medium for bacmid
production. A. tumefaciens GV3101 was cultured at 28°C in LB
medium for plant transformation. Pseudomonas was cultured at 25°C in
TSB medium for plant infection.
