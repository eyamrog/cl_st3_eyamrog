and 72 h. Viral supernatants were then cleared of cellular
debris by 10 min centrifugation at 3,500 rpm and subsequent
filtration through a 0.45 μm 2 low protein-binding PES syringe
filter. Viral supernatants were finally purified using the Lent-X concentrator
as per manufacturer's instructions and resuspended in Optimem. If not
used immediately, aliquots were frozen at -80°C.
