To better understand the origin of EYS wt genotypes, we
analyzed events in the first cell cycle after MII injection
of sperm and Cas9 RNP in either androgenetic zygotes with
only a paternal genome, or zygotes with two pronuclei (2PN).
These approaches also lead to a more conclusive determination of
mosaicism, which is more reliably achieved when there is just
a single cell and genome.
