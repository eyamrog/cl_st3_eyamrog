To determine relative abundance of taxa (where applicable) in each
profile we sub-sampled each sample to 100,000 classified reads, computed
the proportion of reads assigned to each taxon, and took
the distribution of values from all samples. This was the
minimum number of reads sufficient to maintain taxonomic richness (
Figure S2 D). We chose sub-sampling (sometimes referred to as
rarefaction in the literature) based on the study by Weiss
