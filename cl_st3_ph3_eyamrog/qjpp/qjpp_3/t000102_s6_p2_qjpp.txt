directly into the DNA. This enabled us to study mixing
of chromatin itself rather than the proteins that transiently bind
to it. We found the physical state of chromatin in
vivo to be a solid on the timescale of minutes
to hours, which is a relevant timescale for functional regulation
of the genome in a dividing cell. We established this
by assessing the physical state of packaged chromatin in living
cells labeled using fluorescent nucleotides and found no evidence of
mixing of surrounding chromatin. This applied to the euchromatin (
Figures 2 D and 4 B) and heterochromatin ( Figures
