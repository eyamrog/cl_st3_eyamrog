To monitor the neural activity of NTS neurons receiving inputs
from aIC (the NTS Recipient ) using fiber photometry, we
injected the aIC with ∼0.3 μl of the anterograde trans
-synaptic AAV1-hSyn-Cre-WPRE-hGh, and injected the NTS with ∼0.3 μl of
AAV1-Syn-Flex-GCaMP6f-WPRE-SV40 at the coordinates described above. An optic fiber (core
diameter, 200 μm; length, 4.5 mm; NA, 0.37; Newdoon, Hangzhou,
China; or Inper, Hangzhou, China) was inserted 0.2 mm above
the injection site in the NTS.
