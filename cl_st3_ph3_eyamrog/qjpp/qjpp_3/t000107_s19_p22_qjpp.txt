Loss of Fli1 conferred a similar advantage following LM GP33
infection. Although both sgCtrl + and sgFli1 + C9P14 cells
improved survival following a high dose LM GP33 challenge (
Figure 6 E), sgFli1 + C9P14 cells resulted in substantially
better control of bacterial replication compared to the sgCtrl +
C9P14 cells at day 7 p.i. ( Figure 6 F).
Consistent with the influenza virus model, this improved protective immunity
was associated with greater numerical expansion of sgFli1 + C9P14
cells compared to the sgCtrl + group ( Figure S6
E). Thus, deficiency in Fli1 confers a substantial benefit on
T EFF cell expansion and protective immunity during chronic Cl13
infection, respiratory influenza virus infection, and systemic infection with an
intracellular bacterium.
