Here, we present a draft genome of D. gliroides and
detailed phylogenomic comparisons with five other marsupial species. Our analyses
confirm that exceptionally high frequencies of ILS must have contributed
to the controversy over the early geographic speciation among ancestral
marsupials. After identifying a series of genes with strong signatures
of ILS, we used transgenic techniques to demonstrate that ILS
can induce morphological similarity across non-sister lineages. Our results underline
the likely pervasiveness of ILS and the urgency of quantifying
its general impact on phylogenetic reconstruction and trait evolution.
