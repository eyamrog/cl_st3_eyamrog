We also examined the pH dependency of the RGF1-RGI1/RGFR4 interaction
in Nicotiana benthamiana . We transiently co-expressed KD-truncated and GFP-tagged
RGI1/RGFR4 ▵KD and HA-tagged SERK1 ▵KD in leaves of N.
benthamiana and analyzed their interaction at different pH levels using
co-immunoprecipitation (coIP). Consistently, RGF1 induced a strong interaction between RGI1/RGFR4
▵KD -GFP and SERK1 ▵KD -HA at pH 5.0; however,
this interaction progressively decreased when the pH increased to 6.0,
7.0, and 8.0 ( Figure 4 B). Altogether, these results
demonstrate the acidic pH-dependent manner of the RGF1-receptor complex formation.
