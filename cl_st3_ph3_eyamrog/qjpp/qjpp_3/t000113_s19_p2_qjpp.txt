We examined the activity of single neurons recorded from the
anterior portion of the primate hippocampus (CA3 and CA1) (
Figure S1 ) during the performance of this task. Many
neurons fired bursts of action potentials sporadically over the course
of the session. We examined whether this sparse activity encoded
task relevant information. We focused on the fixation epoch of
the task because our previous results showed that hippocampal activity
during the fixation epoch is critical to task performance (
