Known AMR genes can come from gene families with homologous
regions of sequence. To reduce spurious mapping from gene homology
we used BLASTn to align all MegaRes AMR genes against
themselves. We considered any connected group of genes with an
average nucleotide identity of 80% across 50% of the gene
length as a set of potentially confounded genes. We collapsed
all such groups into a single pseudo-gene with the mean
abundance of all constituent genes. Before clustering genes we removed
all genes which were annotated as requiring SNP verification to
predict resistance.
