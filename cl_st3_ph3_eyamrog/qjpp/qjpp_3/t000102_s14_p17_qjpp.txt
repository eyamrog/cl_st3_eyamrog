Condensate volumes of 2mL were added to MatTek glass-bottom 35
mm Petri dishes with a 14 mm Microwell No. 1.5
cover glass slip and spun at 1,000xg for 3 minutes.
Chromatin condensates were imaged and subject to photobleaching using an
Olympus IX81 spinning disk confocal microscope, a 100x/1.40 numerical aperture
objective, and a Photometrics Cascade II camera. Time-lapse images were
collected every 10 s. Imaging software Slidebook6 (3I, Denver, CO)
was used for image capture and analysis of fluorescence intensity
and recovery after photobleaching. Representative images in Figures 1 ,
S1, and S2 were obtained from the time-lapse series.
