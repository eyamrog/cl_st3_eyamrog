Plasmid DNA was extracted from each library and amplified via
PCR (NEB M0492L) with primers containing homology to the binding
region of the indexing primers. PCR reactions were monitored via
qPCR (SigmaMillipore S4438) on a Roche LightCycler 96. Indexing of
the library member DNA proceeded with a global primer (Pi5)
and a unique primer (Pi7) for sample barcoding. Sequencing was
performed at the Biopolymers Facility at Harvard Medical School on
an Illumina NextSeq 500 sequencer, yielding 300bp forward and reverse
reads containing both coils when combined.
