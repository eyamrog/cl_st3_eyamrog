In the present study, we refer to the three most
prevalent variants of the 501Y.V2 lineages as 501Y.V2-1, 501Y.V2-2, and
501Y.V2-3. In the early stages of the second wave, 501Y.V2-1
was prevalent; it is identifiable by five amino acid mutations
in the S protein (in addition to D614G), including D80A,
D215G, E484K, N501Y, and A701V. Subsequently, two further mutations arose
in the S protein, L18F, and K417N, resulting in the
emergence of variant 501Y.V2-2. The third variant (501Y.V2-3) appeared based
on deletion of S protein residues (Del242-244) from 501Y.V2-2. Compared
against the S protein of the 614G virus shows that
501Y.V2-3’s S protein contains 8 mutations: four are located at
the NTD (L18F, D80A, D215G, and Del242-244), three are in
the viral RBD (K417N, E484K, and N501Y), and one is
