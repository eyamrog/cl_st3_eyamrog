There is an urgent need to better understand the molecular
determinants of protective and pathogenic immune responses in COVID-19. Given
the importance of CD4 + T cells in anti-viral immunity,
studying this adaptive immune cell population is likely to provide
insights into the nature of host responses observed in patients
with COVID-19. Current studies on antigen-specific CD4 + T cells
are limited to flow-cytometry-based phenotyping of SARS-CoV-2-responding cells using limited
