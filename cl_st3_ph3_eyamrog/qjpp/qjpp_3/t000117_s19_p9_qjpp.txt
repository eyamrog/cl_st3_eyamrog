These results show that aIC Fezf2 neuron response is potently
modulated by action vigor, homeostatic demand, and the values of
expected outcomes. In particular, these neurons represent the anticipation of
outcomes that meet the changing needs of animals, even if
the outcome—such as the high-sodium—is expected to be unpleasant. Thus,
aIC Fezf2 neurons integrate the learned, predictive signals with the
information about homeostatic needs to represent anticipation and motivational vigor.
