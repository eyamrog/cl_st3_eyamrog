To prevent the use of multiple hits from the WGAs,
we used the reciprocal best matches and filtered out other
multiple hits. Using the MULTIZ v11.2 program, we initially obtained
∼2.77 Gb WGAs. To meet the objectives of the subsequent
analyses, only blocks longer than 100bp and containing the gray
short-tailed opossum, monito del monte, at least one diprotodontian species,
and at least one dasyuromorphian species were retained. These filtering
steps removed about 1.14 Gb from the initial alignment.
