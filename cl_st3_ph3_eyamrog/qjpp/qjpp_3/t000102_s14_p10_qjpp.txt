For the hyperacetylation experiments, the cells were incubated for 24h
in 100 nM TSA before they were used in live-cell
experiments once they reached 60%–70% confluency. To inhibit the BRD-acetylated
lysine interactions, JQ1 inhibitors was added at a concentration of
2 mM to the cells 24 h before they were
used in live-cell experiments.
