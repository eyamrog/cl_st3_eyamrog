5E5 barcoded MDA-MB-231 cells were seeded on the 10-cm dish
and the next day treated with 100 μg/ml doxycycline for
2 hours. Cells were then rinsed by PBS three times
to completely remove doxycycline and then allowed to grow in
vitro for 4 days. 1 million cells were collected for
barcode sequencing and 0.5 million cells were re-cultured and received
next round of doxycycline treatment 24 hours later.
