Expression of osteomorph markers were assessed on viable bone marrow
cells by multicolor flow cytometry (FACSymphony; BD Biosciences, North Ryde,
Australia). Cells collected from the bone and marrow fractions were
incubated in (1:200) Fc-block for 10mins and subsequently stained for
30mins on ice in the dark with the indicated antibodies.
Cells were washed in FACS buffer (2% FCS in PBS)
and stained with DAPI before analysis.
