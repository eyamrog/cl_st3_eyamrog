monogenic skeletal disorders ( IARS2 , DNMT3A , MMP14 ,
ARSB , TBXAS1 , and CSF1R ) in humans (
Table S3 ). Despite these associations, competitive gene set analysis
suggested that the set of osteomorph genes, as a whole,
was not enriched for eBMD-associated genes. In summary, our analysis
shows that osteomorph genes cause monogenic skeletal diseases and that
some of these genes are also strongly associated with eBMD,
indicating that osteomorph genes play a role in the pathogenesis
of both rare monogenic and common polygenic skeletal diseases like
osteoporosis.
