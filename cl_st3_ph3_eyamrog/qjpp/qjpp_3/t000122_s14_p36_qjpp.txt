Titers of EBOV were determined in sera using an Avicel-based
crystal violet stain plaque assay on Vero E6 cell culture
monolayers (ATCC CRL-1586) with a limit of detection (LOD) of
100 PFU. Briefly, 10-fold dilutions of sera were adsorbed to
Vero E6 cell monolayers in triplicate and covered with 2.5%
Avicel overlay. After 7 days of incubation at 37°C and
5% CO 2 , a 0.2% crystal violet stain was
added, and plaques were counted after 24 h of incubation.
Sera were inactivated in TRIzol LS according to the manufacturer’s
instructions (Thermo Fisher Scientific, Waltham, MA, USA), removed from the
BSL-4 and nucleic acid isolated using QIAamp Viral RNA Mini
Kit (QIAGEN, Germantown, MD, USA). Standard curve quantitation of EBOV
nucleic acid was conducted using the BEI Resources Critical Reagents
Program (CRP) EZ1 RT-PCR kit assay in accordance with manufacturer’s
instructions ( Trombley et al., 2010 51. Trombley, A.R. ∙
