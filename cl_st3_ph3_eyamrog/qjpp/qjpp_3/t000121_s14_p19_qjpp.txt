The initial sub-volumes used for generation of the asymmetric map
were the same as that used for the C3 symmetrized
map described above. Refinement strategies were also nearly identical between
the two structures except the asymmetric map was generated with
C1 symmetry. However, the refinement and processing steps were carried
out completely independent of each other. The number of sub-volumes
in the final C1 hVLP-Env structure was 29074 with a
global resolution of 10.67 Å at 0.143 FSC cut-off.
