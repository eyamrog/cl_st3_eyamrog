Total RNA was isolated using the RNeasy Plus Mini Kit
(QIAGEN no. 74134). cDNA was synthesized using qScript cDNA SuperMix
(Quantabio no. 95048) following the manufacturer’s instructions. qPCR reactions were
performed using SYBR Select Master Mix (Thermo Fisher Scientific no.
4472908) and a Stratagene Mx3000P instrument (Agilent Technologies). Thermal cycling
conditions included an initial denaturation step of 95°C for 10
min and 40 cycles at 95°C for 15 s and
60°C for 60 s. Analyses were carried out in triplicate
for each data point. The qPCR primers used for gene
