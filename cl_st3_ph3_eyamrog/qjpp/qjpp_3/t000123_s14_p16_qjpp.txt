Orthologs were identified among all six species based on the
sequence similarity and the synteny evidence. We first aligned protein
sequences of the gene sets of the gray short-tailed opossum
and another species to each other by BLASTP (BLASTall v2.2.23)
with an e-value cut-off of 1e-5, and combined local alignments
with the SOLAR v0.9.6 program ( Almasy and Blangero, 1998
