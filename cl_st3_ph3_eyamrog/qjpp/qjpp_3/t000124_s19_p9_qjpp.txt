in Tyba arrays located near telomeres like those at both
ends of R. pubera chromosome 2 ( Rp Chr2) (
Figures 4 F and 4G), confirming the telomere-to-telomere centromere activity
of holocentric chromosomes. Notably, we observed an enrichment for H3K4me3

4I). We identified 313 genes that showed at least a
