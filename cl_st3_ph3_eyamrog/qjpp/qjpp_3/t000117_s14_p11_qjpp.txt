For the in vivo opto-photometry experiment, Fezf2-CreER mice were injected
with ∼0.3 μl AAV5-Ef1a-DIO-hChR2(H134R)-eYFP into the aIC and ∼0.3 μl
AAV9-hSyn-DA2m into the NAc core (coordinates: 1.3 mm anterior to
Bregma, 0.8 mm lateral from midline, 3.8 mm ventral from
cortical surface). Optical fibers for photoactivation were implanted in the
NTS at coordinates described above. Optical fibers for fiber photometry
were implanted 0.2 mm above the injection site in the
NAc.
