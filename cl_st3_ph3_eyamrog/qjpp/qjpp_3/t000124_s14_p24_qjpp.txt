To represent the intra- and interchromosomal interactions in an intuitive
manner, the cis Hi-C contact of a chromosome was quantified
as the sum of all Hi-C counts within the chromosome
per se , while trans Hi-C contacts referred to the
sum of Hi-C counts between the designated chromosome and all
other chromosomes. The final intra- and interchromosomal contacts for each
single chromosome were normalized through the percentages of Hi-C counts
over the sum of all Hi-C signals in the corresponding
Hi-C map. It is also noteworthy that the infinite extracted
Hi-C counts through Straw were replaced by the mean of
all other finite counts within the extracted chromosomal pair.
