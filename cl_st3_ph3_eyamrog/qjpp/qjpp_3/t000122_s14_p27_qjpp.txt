Groups of 5 animals were exposed IP with 10,000 PFU
of GPA-EBOV or 4,000 PFU of GPA-SUDV in 0.1 mL
of PBS. The single 1C3 or 1C11 antibodies (10 mg)
or in combination (5 mg for each mAb) were delivered
by the IP route 1 or 3 days post-exposure in
1 mL of PBS. The control groups were treated with
PBS. Blood collection was performed 3, 6, 9, 12, and
28 days after exposure and from animals found dead or
moribund to analyze viremia titer in the serum samples. Animals
were monitored for disease signs, survival, and weight loss as
