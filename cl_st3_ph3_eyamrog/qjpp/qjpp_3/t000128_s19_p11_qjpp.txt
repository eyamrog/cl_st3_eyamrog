To test the library-derived CC pairs as helixCAMs, we built
four K562 cell lines that inducibly expressed each and repeated
the pairwise aggregation experiment ( Figures 3 F and G;
S4 ). We found that both engineered helixCAM pairs robustly
generated patterned cell aggregates by quantifying aggregate size change (
Figure S5 ). In particular, the sg30/sg61 pairing consistently yielded
aggregates containing thousands of cells, and some aggregates spanned millimeters
in length ( Figure S12 ) and were visible by
eye ( Figure S13 ).
