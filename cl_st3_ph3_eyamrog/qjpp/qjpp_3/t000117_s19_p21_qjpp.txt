We then probed aIC Fezf2 → NTS function with optogenetics.
To this end, we bilaterally infected aIC Fezf2 neurons with
an AAV expressing the light-gated cation channel ChR2, or GFP,
and implanted optical fibers in the NTS above the axons
originating from the infected neurons ( Figures 3 E and
3F). These mice underwent training in the go/no-go task (
Figure 3 G). At the late stage of training, we
delivered blue light pulses into the NTS during the window
between CS onset and US onset in randomly selected trials
( Figures 3 E–3H).
