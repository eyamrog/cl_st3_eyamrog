For fixation, cells were washed once with 1x PBS pH
7.2 and then fixed in 1 x PBS pH 7.2
with 4% paraformaldehyde for 10 minutes. For electron microscopy, cells
were fixed in a 2% glutaraldehyde and 4% paraformaldehyde fixative
in PBS pH 7.2. For fixing cells with hypercondensed chromatin
cells were incubated in a 560 mOsm PBS solution and
then fixed at 560 mOsm in a fixative containing 2%
glutaraldehyde and 4% paraformaldehyde.
