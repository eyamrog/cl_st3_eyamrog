The second method used an individually wrapped Isohelix Buccal Mini
Swab (MS Mini DNA/RNA Swab, Isohelix, Cat.: MS-02) paired with
a barcoded storage tubes (2D Matrix V-Bottom ScrewTop Tubes, Thermo
Scientific, Cat.: 3741-WP1D-BR/1.0mL), hereafter referred to as ‘matrix tubes’, prefilled
with 400 μ l of a transport and storage medium
suitable for both DNA and RNA (DNA/RNA Shield, Zymo Research,
Cat.: R1100), hereafter referred to as ’Zymo Shield’. Once the
surface was sampled, the swab was immediately placed into a
matrix tube containing Zymo Shield and stored in a −80C
freezer until DNA extraction.
