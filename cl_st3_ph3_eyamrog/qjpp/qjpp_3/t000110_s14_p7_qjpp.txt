The sera were obtained by immunizing nine SPF BALB/c mice
with the SARS-CoV-2 RBD protein. RBD protein (20 μg) was
mixed with an equal amount of aluminum adjuvant and injected
subcutaneously through the head and neck. Immunization was performed once
every other week (a total of three times). Blood samples
were collected 14 days after the third immunization. Sera of
three mice were pooled and labeled as RBD1, RBD2, and
RBD3. The protocol of the animal study was approved by
the Ethical Review Committee for Animal Welfare of The National
Institutes for Food and Drug Control.
