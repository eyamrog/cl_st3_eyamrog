The kinase domain truncated SERK1 (1-825 bp) was amplified using
the primers Sac I-SERK1-F and SERK1dK-HA-Kpn I-R from Arabidopsis Col-0
cDNA, and inserted into Sac I and Kpn I sites
of vector pCAMBIA1307 (a derivative of pCAMBIA1300 by inserting CaMV
35S promoter sequence prior to multiple cloning site) to generate
construct of pCAMBIA1307-SERK1▵KD-HA by restriction enzyme digestion.
