of homologous regions of R. pubera , we identified the
two nearest paralogous genes on both sides of each CENH3
domain. Subsequently, the groups of four genes surrounding CENH3 domains
were used to identify corresponding regions on the other homologous
blocks where we checked for the presence of the CENH3
domain. Resulting groups of four homologous regions were manually inspected
