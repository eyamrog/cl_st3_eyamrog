All of the LRR proteins were expressed in High Five
cells at 28 °C. One liter of insect cells (2
× 10 6 cells per mL) was infected with 30
mL baculovirus and the media were harvested after 60 h
of infection. All of the proteins were purified using Ni-NTA
(Novagen). After elution and concentration, the proteins were further purified
by size-exclusion chromatography (Hiload 200, GE Healthcare) in buffer containing
10 mM Bis-Tris, pH 6.0, and 100 mM NaCl.
