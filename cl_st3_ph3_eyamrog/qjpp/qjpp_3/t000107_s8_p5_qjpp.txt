Here, we discovered a central role for Fli1 in T
EFF responses where this TF specifically antagonized the genome-wide function
of ETS:RUNX activity and prevented Runx3-driven T EFF biology. Indeed,
genetic loss of Fli1 resulted in robustly improved T EFF
responses whereas enforced Fli1 expression restrained differentiation. Fli1 prevented chromatin
accessibility specifically at ETS:RUNX motifs and loss of Fli1 enabled
transcriptional induction of the T EFF program in a Runx3-driven
manner. Moreover, loss of Fli1 improved T EFF biology and
protective immunity not only during LCMV infection, but also following
infection with influenza virus or Listeria monocytogenes . In addition,
deletion of Fli1 potently improved anti-tumor immunity. Thus, Fli1 safeguards
the developing activated CD8 + T cell epigenome from excessive
ETS:RUNX-driven T EFF differentiation and disruption of Fli1 activity improved
T EFF activity and protective immunity to infections and cancer.
