Raw reads were subjected to adapter and quality trimming using
cutadapt (version 2.4; parameters: --quality-cutoff 20 --overlap 5 --minimum-length 25;
Illumina TruSeq adapter clipped from both reads), followed by trimming
of 10 nucleotides from the 5’ end of the first
read, 15 nucleotides from the 5’ end of the second
read and 5 nucleotides from the 3’ end of both
reads ( Kechin et al., 2017 66. Kechin, A. ∙

-v 0.1 -s 16 -q 20 -w 100 -S 1
-u -R) ( Xi and Li, 2009 146. Xi, Y.
