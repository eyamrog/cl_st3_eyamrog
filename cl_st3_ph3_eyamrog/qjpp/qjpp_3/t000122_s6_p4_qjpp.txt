In this study, we analyzed a pool of human mAbs
from EVD survivors and identified two antibodies, 1C11 and 1C3,
that bound the desired core and head epitopes, respectively, were
specific for GP, did not cross-react with sGP, offered potent
neutralizing activity in vitro against SUDV as well as EBOV,
and were efficacious in both laboratory mouse and domesticated guinea
pig models of EVD. We next determined that a combination
formed by the 1C11–1C3 pair could protect NHPs against EBOV-
and SUDV-induced disease, in doses as low as 25 mg/kg
(12.5 mg/kg of each mAb).
