GCaMP6 fluorescence signals were captured with a monochrome CCD camera
(pco.pixelfly, digital 14 bit CCD camera, image sensor ICX285AL) mounted
onto the Olympus BX51. A custom Imaging Acquisition software written
in LabVIEW (National Instruments) was used to interface the camera
with a dedicated desktop computer and record the GCaMP6 signals
at a frame rate of 10 frames/s. To synchronize imaging
with behavioral events, Imaging Acquisition was triggered with a TTL
(transistor-transistor logic) signal from the Bpod State Machine (Sanworks) used
for behavioral control. During imaging, the timestamps of different events,
including the trigger signals sent to Imaging Acquisition, CS onset,
US onset and licking events, were all recorded with Bpod.
