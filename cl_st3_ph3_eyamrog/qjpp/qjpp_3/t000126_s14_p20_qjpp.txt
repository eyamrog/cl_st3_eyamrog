Cells were washed in PBS and gently lysed by resuspension
in a freshly prepared and chilled sucrose lysis buffer (320
mM sucrose, 10 mM Tris pH 8.0, 3 mM CaCl
2 , 2mM magnesium acetate, 0.1 mM EDTA, 0.5% NP-40,
1% protease/phosphatase inhibitor cocktail). Cells were incubated in this buffer
for 15 minutes on ice followed by centrifugation at 500
g for 15 minutes at 4 o C to pellet
isolated nuclei. Supernatant containing the cytosolic fraction was removed and
the remaining nuclear pellet was mixed in a nuclei sonication
buffer (500 mM NaCl, 50 mM Tris pH 8.0, 10%
glycerol, 1% NP-40, 1% protease/phosphatase inhibitor cocktail) followed by incubation
on ice for 5 minutes. Next, the nuclei were lysed
in this buffer by sonication for 10 seconds. Insoluble debris
from this lysate was removed by centrifugation at 21000 g
for 20 minutes at 4 o C and remaining supernatant
was taken as nuclear fraction protein.
