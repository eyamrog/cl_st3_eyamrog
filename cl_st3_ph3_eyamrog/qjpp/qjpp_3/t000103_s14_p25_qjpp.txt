Antibody preparations were diluted serially (using 2-fold dilutions) and incubated
with ~100 pfu of EEEV FL93-939 for 1 h at
37°C. Anti-EEEV ascites serum (ATCC) was used as a positive
control. After incubation, Vero cell monolayer cultures in 6-well plates
were inoculated and incubated for 1 h at 37°C. After
overlay with agarose immunodiffusion grade (MP Biomedicals), plates were incubated
for 2 days followed by overlay with neutral red for
at least 6 h to count plaques. Percent neutralization was
calculated based on the number of plaques in each mAb
dilution compared to the number of plaques in untreated control
wells inoculated with virus.
