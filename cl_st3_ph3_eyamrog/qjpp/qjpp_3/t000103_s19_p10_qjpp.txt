Next, to assess whether EEEV-33 or EEEV-143 block virus attachment
to cells, a post-attachment neutralization assay was performed ( Figures
4 B and 5 B). In this assay, virus was
incubated with cells at 4°C followed by addition of the
mAb at 4°C after attachment and removal of free virus.
A reduction in neutralization potency of EEEV-33 and EEEV-143 was
observed, which could indicate that EEEV-33 and EEEV-143 block virus
attachment to cells to some degree or cannot reach full
occupancy of virion binding sites for optimal neutralization due to
epitopes blocked by the initial virus attachment to host cells.
However, substantial inhibition still occurred post-attachment, indicating that EEEV-33 and
EEEV-143 can inhibit virus entry into cells after adsorption to
the cell surface.
