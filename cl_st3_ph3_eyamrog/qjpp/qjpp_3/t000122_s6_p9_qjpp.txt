In summary, we show that mAbs 1C3 and 1C11 are
highly protective against severe disease and death due to EBOV
and SUDV infections in NHPs and confer protection through a
unique recognition of quaternary epitopes of the ebolavirus GP. Effective
EBOV-specific mAb-based therapeutics are now standard of care after the
PALM clinical trial; however, the PALM results sounded a note
of caution: in patients with high viral loads, case-fatality rates
remained >60% even with these effective agents ( Mulangu et
