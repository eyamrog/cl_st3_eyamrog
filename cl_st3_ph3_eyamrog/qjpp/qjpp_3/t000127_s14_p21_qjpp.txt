ITC200 was used to quantify the binding affinity of RGF1
with RGI3/RGFR1 LRR . The protein samples for ITC were
prepared in buffers with different pH (10 mM Bis-Tris, pH
6.0, 100 mM NaCl; and 10 mM Tris, pH 8.0,
100 mM NaCl). The titrations were performed at 25 °C.
The stirring speed was 750 rpm. 1mM RGF peptide was
injected into the stirred calorimeter cells containing 0.05 mM RGI3/RGFR1
LRR . All of the ITC data were analyzed using
MicroCal Origin 7.0.
