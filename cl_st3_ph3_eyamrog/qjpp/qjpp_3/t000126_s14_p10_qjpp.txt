Human induced pluripotent stem cell lines (hiPSC), derived from three
patients with Crohn’s Disease (CD) homozygous for the CD-associated minor
allele of the rs28445040 variant in SP140 (SP140 mut )
and from three healthy controls (HC) homozygous for the major
allele (SP140 wt ) were produced through reprogramming of lymphoblastoid
cell lines (LCL). HiPSC lines were then differentiated into CD14+
monocytes and validated via FACS analysis. Harvested monocytes were further
cultured and differentiated into M0 macrophages by adding M-CSF for
7 days, and then polarized to M1 macrophages with IFNγ.
