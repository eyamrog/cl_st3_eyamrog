Competitive gene set analysis was used to determine whether human
orthologs of the 520 osteomorph defining genes was on average
more strongly associated with eBMD than the all other protein
coding genes in the genome. The analysis was conducted using
default settings, and accounted for several confounding factors including: gene
size, gene density (i.e., representing the relative level of Linkage
Disequilibrium between SNPs in the gene) and the inverse of
the mean minor allele count in the gene (i.e., to
correct for potential power loss in very low minor allele
count SNPs), as well the log value of these three
factors.
