HEK293T cells were maintained in DMEM media (Life Technologies) with
1% penicillin-streptomycin (Gibco) and 10% heat-inactivated Hyclone fetal bovine serum
(FBS, GE Healthcare). THP-1 human monocytes were maintained in RPMI
media (Life Technologies) with 1% penicillin-streptomycin (Gibco) and 10% heat-inactivated
Hyclone fetal bovine serum (FBS, GE Healthcare).
