Imaris was used to track the replication foci in time
after laser micro-irradiation to show the expansion of the chromatin
after DNA-damage ( Figure S4 ). Replication foci were manually
identified, and “Spots” were placed at their intensity maximum in
each frame of the observation. Spots representing one replication focus
over time were joined into a “Track” and the xy
coordinates of the spots of a track were exported into
a CSV, which was further processed in Microsoft Excel (v16.39).
