Like aIC Fezf2 neurons, aIC NTS neurons showed excitatory or
inhibitory response to sucrose and quinine that scaled up or
down with the licking rates of the mice ( Figures
S5 A–S5C). Moreover, the response amplitudes of many neurons showed
positive or negative correlations with the licking rates during liquid
consumption ( Figures S5 D and S5E) but not during
baseline periods ( Figure S5 F). Interestingly, compared with aIC
Fezf2 neurons, a much larger fraction of aIC NTS neurons
showed excitatory response to sucrose (aIC NTS , 34.5%; aIC
Fezf2 , 19.5%) or to both sucrose and quinine (aIC
NTS , 14%; aIC Fezf2 , 8.4%) ( Figures S5
G and S5H).
