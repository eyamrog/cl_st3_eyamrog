Briefly, viruses were incubated with 2-fold serial dilutions of mAbs
and added to Vero E6 cells (ATCC) in 6-well plates.
The endpoint titer was determined to be the highest dilution
with plaque reduction neutralization test (PRNT) producing a ≥50 or
≥80% reduction (PRNT 50 , PRNT 80 ) in the
number of plaques observed in control wells. EBOV Zaire 1995
(Kikwit strain) was used for neutralization testing. For SUDV, the
Boneface strain was used. For BDBV, the Uganda 2007 strain
was used.
