In addition to cancer cells that are manually extracted from
various organs, we examined naturally occurred circulating tumor cells (CTCs)
emitted from bone lesions versus mammary tumors. Not surprisingly, bone
lesions generated a higher number of CTCs, probably due to
the more permeable vascular structures or survival advantage conferred by
the bone. However, on top of the higher number, CTCs
from bone lesions also express higher quantity of CD44 and
ALDH1 ( Figures 6 L and 6M), suggesting increased stemness.
