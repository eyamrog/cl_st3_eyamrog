Apoptotic cells were detected using the FITC Annexin V Apoptosis
Detection Kit I (BD Biosciences no. 556547) according to the
manufacturer’s protocol. Cells were analyzed on an LSR Fortessa instrument
(BD PharMingen). For breast CSC quantifications, cells were stained with
anti-CD24-APC and anti-CD44-PE antibodies using the Breast Cancer Stem Cell
Isolation Kit (MagCellect no. MAGH111). Cells were analyzed on a
NovoCyte 3000 flow cytometer (ACEA Biosciences). For cell surface expression
of ULBP2 and SSX1, MDA-MB-231 cells were transfected with non-targeting
or FBXO44 siRNAs and stained with anti-ULBP2 antibody (GeneTex no.
GTX53048) at 1:50 dilution or anti-SSX1 antibody (Novus Biologicals no.
NBP2-00614) at 1:50 dilution and analyzed on a LSR Fortessa
instrument (BD PharMingen). For 4T1 experiments, tumors were dissociated into
single cells using the gentleMACS Octo Dissociator with Heaters (Miltenyi
Biotec) as described in the protocol for the Tumor Dissociation
Kit (Miltenyi Biotec). Cells were passed through a 70 μm
filter to remove clumps and maintain single cell suspensions. Cell
surface staining was performed with the indicated antibodies before fixation
and permeabilization of cells for intracellular staining. For IFNγ detection,
Cell Activation Cocktail (BioLegend) was used for stimulation (5 hr)
prior to cell surface staining. All antibodies (anti-CD45.2, anti-CD3ε, anti-CD4,
anti-CD8, anti-CD25, anti-CD335, anti-FOXP3, anti-IFN-γ, anti-H-2Kd, anti-PD-L1) were purchased from
BioLegend. Samples were analyzed using an LSR Fortessa instrument (BD
PharMingen) and data analyzed with FlowJo Software (Treestar).
