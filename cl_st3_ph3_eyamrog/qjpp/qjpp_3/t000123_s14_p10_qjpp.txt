We extracted DNA from a male monito del monte for
genome sequencing. Paired-end and mate-pair DNA libraries with seven different
insert sizes (250 bp, 500 bp, 800 bp, 2 kb,
5 kb, 10 kb and 20 kb) were constructed and
sequenced on the Illumina HiSeq2000 platform. In total, 370 Gb
of raw reads were produced. To facilitate the assembly work,
a series of strict filtering steps were conducted to remove
artificial duplications, adapter contaminations, and low-quality reads ( Li et
al., 2010 53. Li, R. ∙ Fan, W. ∙ Tian,
G. ... The sequence and de novo assembly of the
giant panda genome Nature. 2010; 463 :311-317 Crossref Scopus (815)
PubMed Google Scholar ). Before starting the assembly, we estimated
the monito del monte genome size to be 3.2 Gb
using K-mer analysis.
