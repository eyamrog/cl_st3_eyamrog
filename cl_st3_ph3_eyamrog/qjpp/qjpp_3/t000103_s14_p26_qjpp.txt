Anti-penta his (HIS1K) biosensor tips (FortéBio) on an Octet Red96
or HTX biolayer interferometry instrument (FortéBio) were soaked for 10
minutes in 1x kinetics buffer (FortéBio), followed by a baseline
signal measurement for 60 s. Recombinant EEEV E2 glycoprotein (5
μg/mL; IBT BioServices) was immobilized onto the biosensor tips for
60 s. After a wash step in 1x kinetics buffer
for 30 to 60 s, the first antibody (50 μg/mL)
was incubated with the antigen-containing biosensor for 600 s. After
a wash step in 1x kinetics buffer for 30 to
60 s, the biosensor tips then were immersed into the
second antibody (50 μg/mL) for 180 s. Comparison between the
maximal signal of each antibody compared to a buffer-only control
was used to determine the percent binding of each antibody.
A reduction in maximum signal to < 33% of un-competed
signal was considered full competition of binding for the second
antibody in the presence of the first antibody. A reduction
in maximum signal to between 33 to 67% of un-competed
was considered intermediate competition of binding for the second antibody
in the presence of the first antibody. Percent binding of
the maximum signal > 67% was considered absence of competition
of binding for the second antibody in the presence of
the first antibody.
