To explore the effect of helixCAM expression on cell viability,
each of the ten single-helixCAM lines was individually seeded and
sampled over four days to measure cell count and viability.
For each line, 4x10 5 cells were seeded into wells
in 24-well plates along with 750uL of culture media either
with or without 500 ng/mL of doxycycline, in triplicates. At
five separate time points (0, 1, 2, 3, and 4
days after seeding), 10uL of cells from each well was
mixed with 10uL of 0.4% Trypan Blue Stain (NanoEntek EBT-001),
from which 10uL was taken out and loaded into a
disposable cell counting slide (NanoEntek E1020). The cell count and
viability are then measured with a ThermoFisher Countess II automated
cell counter.
