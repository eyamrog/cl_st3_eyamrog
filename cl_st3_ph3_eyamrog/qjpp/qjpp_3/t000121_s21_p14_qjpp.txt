To ascertain whether the observed HR1-C density was affected by
applied symmetry to the map, we calculated an asymmetric structure
of hVLP-Env at 10.7 Å resolution ( Figure S3 ;
Table 1 ). The asymmetric structure ( Figure 5 B)
appears overall similar to the 3-fold symmetrized map ( Figure
5 A). Partial density for the HR1-C helices is consistently
present across all 3 protomers in the asymmetric structure, with
near-complete density for HR1-C observed in 1 protomer ( Figure
5 B).
