To generate osteoclast reporter mice, we lethally irradiated 7-12 weeks-old
C57BL/6J recipient mice with 950-990 rads in split doses 6
h apart. Irradiated mice were rescued with 3-10 × 10
6 bone marrow cells from Lysm Cre/+ . Tdtomato LSL/LSL
and Blimp-1 Egfp/+ . Rag1 −/− or Csf1r Egfp/+ mice
in a ratio of 1:4 for Blimp-1 Egfp/+ . Rag1
−/− and 1:1 for Csf1r Egfp/+ donors. Chimeric mice were
analyzed 8-14 weeks later following bone marrow reconstitution.
