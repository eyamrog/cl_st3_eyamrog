5-day-old seedlings were collected and treated with 100 nM Pep1
in liquid MS medium at different pH for 6 h.
After treatment, the seedlings were washed with staining buffer without
X-Gluc, stained with GUS staining buffer (50 mM sodium phosphate
buffer, pH 7.0, 10 mM Na 2 EDTA, 0.5 mM
K 4 [Fe(CN) 6 ]·3H 2 O, 0.5 mM K
3 [Fe(CN) 6 ], 0.1% Triton X-100, and 1 mg/ml
X-Gluc), incubated at 37 °C for 12 h, and then
ethanol (70%) was used to terminate the staining reaction. The
seedlings were cleared in chloral hydrate solution (chloral hydrate:water:glycerol =
8:2:1; w/v/v) for 5 min before observation. Pictures were taken
using a Zeiss AxioImage A2 microscope. The experiments were repeated
at least three times with similar results.
