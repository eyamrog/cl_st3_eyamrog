brain constructs internal representations of the world to flexibly guide
behavior. In the Euclidean world, hippocampal place cells form one
component of such a representation. However, whether the problem at
hand is navigating to the coffee shop or navigating your
social network when you arrive, cognitive maps, spatial, sensory, and
conceptual alike, appear to share a common computational substrate for
