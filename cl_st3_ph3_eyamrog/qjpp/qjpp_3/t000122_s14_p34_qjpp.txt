Antibodies 1C3 and 1C11 were stored at −20°C until thawed
on the day of administration. A fresh aliquot of the
same lot was used for all NHPs of the same
group and for both administrations. Antibodies were diluted in sterile
PBS (GIBCO, USA), aseptically drawn up in 20-mL syringes and
capped until use. On Day 4 and Day 7, the
NHPs received their respective antibody dose intravenously (IV) via a
temporary 22-gauge catheter (Introcan Safety catheter, Braun, USA), aseptically placed
in the right (Day 4) or left (Day 7) great
saphenous vein and delivered slowly as a bolus using a
syringe pump (Medex Medfusion, Medex, Inc., USA) at a rate
of 3 mL per min. The dosages for administration on
Day 4 or Day 7 were calculated based on individual
body weights obtained on Day 0 or Day 4, respectively.
The total volumes of antibodies administered ranged from 5.9–18.4 mL.
An equivalent, averaged volume of 11.2 mL of sterile PBS
(GIBCO, USA) was administered to the mock-treated control NHP. The
administration time ranged from 3–6 min after which the IV
catheter was removed and NHPs returned to their cage for
recovery.
