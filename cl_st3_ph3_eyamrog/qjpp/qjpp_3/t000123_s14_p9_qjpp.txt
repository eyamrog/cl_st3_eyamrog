F0 male mice were mated for one generation to select
the individuals with the reproductive capacity. Then, IVF (in vitro
fertilization) was performed on these candidates to obtain F1 female
mice (heterozygous). 3-4 weeks of F1 female mice were used
in the IVF with F0 or F1 male mice to
produce the homozygous mice for the morphological analyses. The genotypes
of homozygous mice were confirmed by PCR experiment.
