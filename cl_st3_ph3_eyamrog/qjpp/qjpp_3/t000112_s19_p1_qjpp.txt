We collected 4,728 samples from the mass transit systems of
60 cities around the world ( Table 1 ; Table
S1 ). These samples were collected from at least three
common surfaces in each mass transit system (railings, benches, and
ticket kiosks), with additional optional surfaces also collected in each
city, and all were subjected to shotgun metagenomic sequencing (125
× 125 PE reads, see STAR Methods ). We use
the microbiome of mass transit systems as a proxy for
the urban microbiome as a whole and present our key
findings here.
