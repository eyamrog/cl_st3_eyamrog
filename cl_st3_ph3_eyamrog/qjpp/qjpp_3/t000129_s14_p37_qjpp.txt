Single-end, 100 bp reads from Illumina sequencing were mapped to
the reference genome (mm10) using the STAR mapper (splice junctions
based on RefSeq; options: --alignIntronMin20 --alignIntronMax500000 --outFilterMismatchNmax 10). Differential gene
expression was ascertained using the DESeq2 package ( Love et
al., 2014 88. Love, M.I. ∙ Huber, W. ∙ Anders,
S. Moderated estimation of fold change and dispersion for RNA-seq
data with DESeq2 Genome Biol. 2014; 15 :550 Crossref Scopus
(29293) PubMed Google Scholar ). The cut-off for significantly altered
gene expression was an adjusted P value of 0.05.
