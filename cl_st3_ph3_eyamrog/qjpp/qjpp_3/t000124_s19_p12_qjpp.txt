The TCR2 element was found to be a miniature inverted-repeat
TE (MITE) and ranged from 672 to 1,235 bp, likely
originated from the DNA transposon MuDR with shared similarity (up
to 97%) in the terminal inverted repeats. All 158 full-length
TCR2 elements identified in the R. pubera genome were in
Tyba arrays, but none were characterized by Tyba insertions. Thus,
TCR2 elements did not contribute to the dispersal of Tyba
in the R. pubera genome.
