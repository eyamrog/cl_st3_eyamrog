FC (Santa cruz) and DCCD (Sigma-Aldrich) were dissolved in DMSO
as 5 mM stocks, frozen at -20 °C, and RGF1,
dRGF1, Pep1, flg22 and elf18 peptides were dissolved in double
distilled water as 100 mM stocks. These chemicals were dissolved
to final concentration (5 μM FC and DCCD, 10 nM
Pep1, 100 nM RGF1, flg22 and elf18, 5 μM dRGF1
unless specified labeled) in MS medium before solidification.
