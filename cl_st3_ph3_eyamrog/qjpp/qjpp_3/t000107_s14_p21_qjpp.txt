C): For each gene, mean of the log10 fold changes
from multiple sgRNA was computed. In the matrix of genes
by conditions, quantile normalization was performed across conditions such that
each condition has the same distribution of values. Genes were
ordered by the mean value of each row. Histogram (
Figure 1 D): For each condition, the background (gray bars
and the histogram) was plotted for sgRNAs of all the
genes. The 5% and 95% intervals were extracted by using
