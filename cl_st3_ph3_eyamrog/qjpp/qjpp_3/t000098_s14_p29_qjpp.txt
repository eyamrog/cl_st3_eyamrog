diffusion coefficient D refers to the free cAMP diffusion rate.

scales of the order 30 nm, which is well below

the cAMP reaches the surfaces of the sphere, a degradation
reaction takes place, making the PDE effectively a spherical sink

time, in units of mol/s (or nmol/s or μmol/s). The
requirement that the total flux of free cAMP toward the
