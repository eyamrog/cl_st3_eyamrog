The urgency of bone metastasis research is somewhat controversial. It
has long been noticed that, at the terminal stage, breast
cancer patients usually die of metastases in multiple organs. In
fact, compared to metastases in other organs, bone metastases are
relatively easier to manage. Patients with the skeleton as the
only site of metastasis usually have better prognosis than those
with visceral organs affected ( Coleman and Rubens, 1987 15.
