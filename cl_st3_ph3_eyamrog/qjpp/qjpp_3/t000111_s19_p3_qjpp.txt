As an independent approach to introduce bone lesions, we used
intra-femoral (IF) injection that delivers cancer cells directly to bone
marrow, bypassing the artery circulation involved in IIA injection. This
approach also resulted in multi-organ metastases at late time points
in both MDA-MB-231 and AT-3 models ( Figures 1 I
and S1 G). The frequency and distribution patterns of metastases
were similar between intra-femoral and IIA injection models ( Figures
1 J and 1K). Thus, we hypothesize cancer cells in
the bone microenvironment may gain capacity to further metastasize.
