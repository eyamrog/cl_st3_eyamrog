Human lymphoblastoid cell lines (LBL) collected by the NIDDK IBD
Genetics Consortium from three patients with Crohn’s Disease (CD) homozygous
for the CD-associated minor allele of the rs28445040 variant in
SP140 (SP140 mut ) and from three healthy controls (HC)
homozygous for the major allele (SP140 wt ) were obtained
from the NIDDK Central Repository ( https://repository.niddk.nih.gov ).
