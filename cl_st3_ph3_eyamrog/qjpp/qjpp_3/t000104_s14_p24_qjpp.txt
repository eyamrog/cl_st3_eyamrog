extraction buffer. Biotin-azide click reactions were performed on a rotator
for 1 hr at 4°C, and 50 μL of streptavidin
beads (Cell Signaling Technology no. 3419) were used for streptavidin
capture for each sample. Beads were washed 3 × 10
min and boiled in 1x SDS gel loading buffer prior
to electrophoresis. For aniPOND analysis of FBXO44 binding to H3K9me3-modified
nucleosomes, MDA-MB-231-Flag-FBXO44 cells (∼1.7 × 10 8 ) were incubated
with 10 μM EdU for 30 min, harvested, subjected to
biotin-azide click reactions, and chromatin sonicated prior to IP with
IgG or anti-Flag antibody (Sigma-Aldrich no. F3165). Chromatin was eluted
3x with Flag peptide solution (150 ng/μL) and subjected to
affinity purification with streptavidin beads.
