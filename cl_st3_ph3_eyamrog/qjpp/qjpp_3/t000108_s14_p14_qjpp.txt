Dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DXA) (Faxitron Ultrafocus DXA, Hologic, USA) was
performed weekly or fortnightly on anaesthetised mice under 3%–5% inhaled
isoflurane. Analysis of the hind limb was performed using Vision
DXA (Hologic, USA) using a manually drawn region of interest
encompassing the left hind limb to quantify Bone mineral density
(BMD).
