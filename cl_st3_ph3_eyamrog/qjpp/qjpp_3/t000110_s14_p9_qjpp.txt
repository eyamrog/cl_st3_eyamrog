A total of 14 ACE2 expressing plasmids were constructed, including
human (BAB40370.1), mink (QNC68911.1), dog (MT663955), cat (MT663959), pangolin (XP_017505746.1),
pig (NP_001116542.1), mouse (ABN80106.1), bat (KC881004.1), cow (NP_001019673.2), rabbit (MT663961),
ferret (MT663957), sheep (XP_011961657.1), civet (AY881174.1), and monkey (MT663960). Each
gene of the 14 species was mammalian codon-optimized. The codon-optimized
ACE2 fused with a FLAG tag (GACTACAAGGACGATGACGATAAG) at the 3′-terminal
end was synthesized by General Biol. Inc, (Anhui, China). Each
synthesized sequence was inserted into the eukaryotic expression vector pRP[Exp]-EGFP-CMV
using the BamHI and Xho I sites to get ACE2
expression plasmids from the different species.
