Glycopeptide data were visualized and processed by Byonic (Version 3.8,
Protein Metrics Inc.) using a 10 ppm precursor and 10
ppm fragment mass tolerance. Glycopeptides were searched using the N-glycan
309 mammalian database in Protein Metrics PMI-Suite and scored based
on the assignment of correct c- and z- fragment ions.
The true-positive entities were further validated by the presence of
glycan oxonium ions m/z at 204 (HexNAc ions) and 366
(HexNAcHex ions).
