Arabidopsis seeds were plated on Murashige and Skoog medium (4.4
g/L MS salts, 1 % [w/v] sucrose, pH 5.7, and
0.8% [w/v] agar) in petri dishes after surface-sterilization in 70%
ethanol and 0.1% Triton X-100. The dishes were stored at
4 °C for 3 days, and then vertically placed in
an incubator with a 16/8 h light/dark cycle at 22
°C in 70% humidity to grow.
