A long continuous ILS fragment such as WFIKKN1 might, thus,
have been favored by selection over sufficient evolutionary time to
fix phenotypic hemiplasy in multiple marsupials. Whether such positive selection
would have continued until the present day is unknown but,
adaptive or not, phylogenetic reconstructions need to take ILS into
account as a possible mechanism for explaining mismatches between genomic
variation used to construct phylogenies and phenotypic variation mapped onto
such trees.
