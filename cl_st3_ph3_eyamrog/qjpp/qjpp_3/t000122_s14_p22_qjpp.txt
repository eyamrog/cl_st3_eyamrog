EBOV GPΔmucin was expressed in Drosophila S2 cells using a
single plasmid encoding a C-terminally Strep-tagged construct. EBOV GPΔmucin was
purified using a pre-packed StrepTrap HP column (Cytiva Life Sciences),
followed by cleavage of the Strep-tag at an enterokinase cleavage
site, using EKMax (Thermo Fisher Scientific). The tagless protein was
further purified using a Superose 6 Increase 10/300 GL SEC
column (Cytiva Life Sciences).
