HEK293T cells (ATCC) were transfected to produce pseudovirus used for
neutralization assays and virus ELISAs. TZM-bl cells (NIH ARRRP) were
used as the target cells for HIV infection in neutralization
assays. Both cell lines were cultured in DMEM (ThermoFisher Scientific)
supplemented with 10% fetal bovine serum, 20 mM L-glutamine, 100
U/mL penicillin, and 100 μg/mL streptomycin (all additives from ThermoFisher
Scientific) and incubated at 37þC with 5% CO 2 .
