Since 2017, MetaSUB has partnered with the Critical Assessment of
Massive Data Analysis (CAMDA) camda.info, a whole conference track at
the Intelligent Systems for Molecular Biology (ISMB) Conference. At this
venue, a subset of the MetaSUB data was released to
the CAMDA community in the form of an annual challenge
addressing the issue of geographically locating samples: ‘The MetaSUB Inter-City
Challenge’ in 2017 and ‘The MetaSUB Forensics Challenge’ in 2018
and 2019. In the latter challenge the MetaSUB data has
been complemented by data from EMP ( Thompson et al.,
