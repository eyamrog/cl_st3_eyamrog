In the first method of sample collection used a Copan
Liquid Amies Elution Swab (ESwab, Copan Diagnostics, Cat.: 480C) paired
with a 1mL of Liquid Amies in a plastic, screw
cap tube, hereafter referred to as a ‘Copan swab’. The
Amies transport medium maintains the sample at pH 7.0 0.5
and contains sodium thioglycolate as well as calcium, magnesium, sodium,
and potassium salts to control the permeability of bacterial cells.
Once the surface was sampled, the swab was immediately placed
into the collection tube and stored in a −80C freezer
once returned to the laboratory.
