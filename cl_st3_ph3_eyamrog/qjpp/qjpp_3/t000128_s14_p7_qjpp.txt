The initial constructs for testing Z17-ehaA and Z18-ehaA in E.
coli were gifted from the Tirrell Lab then modified by
removing the His-tag and GS linker between the PelB and
CC sequences. For the remaining E. coli helixCAM construction, coiled-coil
sequences were found via literature search and private communications and
synthesized (Twist). The synthesized CC-encoding DNA fragments were then used
to replace the Z17 sequence region via Gibson Assembly (NEB
E2611L). Plasmids were transformed in T7 Express Competent E. coli
cells (NEB C2566), miniprepped (Qiagen 27,104), and verified via Sanger
sequencing (Genewiz).
