To determine the sizes and positions of centromere units, we
merged with bedtools CENH3 peaks that were separated by less
than 50 kb to eliminate the gaps that arise because
of fragmented Tyba arrays or due to insertion of TEs.
Small CENH3 domains of less than 1 kb were discarded.
Length and distance between Tyba arrays and between CENH3 domains
were then calculated using bedtools. Bar plots of the average
distance and unit length used to compare CENH3 domains and
Tyba arrays were made in RStudio using the ggplot library.
