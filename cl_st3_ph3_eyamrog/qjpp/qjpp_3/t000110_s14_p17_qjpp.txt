GraphPad Prism 8 was used for plotting and statistical analysis;
the values were expressed as means ± SEM. One-way ANOVA
and Holm-Sidak’s multiple comparison tests were used to analyze differences
between groups. A p value of less than 0.05 was
considered to be significant. ∗ p < 0.05, ∗∗ p
< 0.01, ∗∗∗ p < 0.005, ∗∗∗∗ p < 0.001.
