Both treatments have been approved by the U.S. F.D.A. These
treatments target the EBOV GP, which is the prominent antigen
on the virion surface. GP is a trimer of heterodimers,
consisting of GP1 and GP2. The GP1 subunit contains a
mucin-like-domain (MLD) and the receptor-binding site (RBS); GP2 is a
classical class I fusion protein and mediates virion-cell fusion. Distinct
ebolaviruses vary significantly in genomic sequence and, as a result,
are also antigenically distinct ( Kuhn et al., 2020 31.

with natural infection or vaccination eliciting single ebolavirus-specific responses at

ebolaviruses, though rare, are of heightened interest for development of
broadly applicable therapeutics and as design primers for the development
of vaccines that induce pan-ebolavirus protection.
