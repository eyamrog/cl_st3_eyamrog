For FRET measurements in cytosolic preparations, HEK293 cells were plated
on 100 mm dishes (SARSTEDT, Nümbrecht, Germany) to give a
density of approximately 50%–60%. 8 h later cells were transfected
with a total amount of 20 μg cDNA (10 μg
cDNA encoding the gene of interest and 10 μg pcDNA3)
using calcium phosphate precipitation. 48 h after transfection, cells were
used for experiments.
