The LIAISON SARS-CoV-2 S1/S2 IgG (DiaSorin S.p.A., Saluggia, Italy) was
utilized as per the manufacturer’s instructions to obtain quantitative antibody
results from plasma samples via an indirect chemiluminescence immunoassay (CLIA)
in a United Kingdom Accreditation Service (UKAS) diagnostic laboratory at
University Hospital Southampton. Sample results were interpreted as positive (≥
15 AU/mL), Equivocal (≥ 12.0 and < 15.0 AU/mL) and
negative (< 12 AU/mL).
