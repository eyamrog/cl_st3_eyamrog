The affinity of the dual helixCAM-expressing “layer” lines to the
previously made single helixCAM-expressing lines, as well as pairwise to
each other, was tested by HCSRA ( Figure S18 )
and confirmed to have the intended affinities. All five cell
types were first induced to express helixCAM for 48 h.
Core sg30 mCherry cells were then incubated with sg61 +
sg88 eBFP2 cells in a 1:8 ratio for 12 h,
followed by addition of subsequent layers of cells at a
1:2 ratio (total prior cells:new cells), each followed by a
12 h incubation ( Figure 5 A). The dense cell
mixture was then allowed to settle briefly, and the supernatant
was removed along with unbound cells. The precipitated cellular aggregates
were then imaged using widefield and confocal microscopy.
