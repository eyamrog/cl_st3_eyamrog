There are three key limitations to this study. First, this
study exclusively measured DNA, meaning RNA viruses would be excluded,
as would evidence of transcriptional activity from Bacteria and Archaea.
Second, this study is unable to identify a large proportion
of DNA collected. This is at least partly due to
the highly novel nature of urban microbiomes, and as more
data are generated, this proportion could be improved. Third, AMR
genes are often difficult to distinguish from similar genes that
do not confer resistance (though we have removed genes that
require SNP-level verification), so our results likely have a degree
of noise.
