binding affinities of RGF1 to RGI3/RGFR1 LRR at different pH
conditions using isothermal titration calorimetry (ITC). Although they exhibited a
strong interaction at pH 6.0, the interaction was completely disrupted
at pH 8.0 ( Figure 5 A). We also found

(RGF 2, 3, 4, 5, and 8) ( Figures S5
