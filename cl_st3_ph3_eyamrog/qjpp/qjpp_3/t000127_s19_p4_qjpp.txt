We next investigated the pH response of RGF1 signaling using
different medium pH conditions. Consistent with the FC and DCCD
treatments, the medium pH conditions markedly induced apoplastic pH change
( Figures S3 D and S3E). Not considering the potential
buffering capacity of the root apoplast, we conducted a HPTS
calibration using Col-0 seedlings grown on different medium pH conditions
and approximately estimated that the pH value near the QC
region was less than 4.7 ( Figures S3 D and
S3F). To maximize the effect of exogenous RGF1 peptide application
in different pH conditions, the aqc1-1 mutant that possesses little
basal RGF1 bioactivity was used ( Zhou et al., 2010
