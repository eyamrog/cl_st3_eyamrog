We elected to measure the effect of helixCAM expression using
individual helixCAM lines over co-cultured helixCAM lines for multiple reasons.
First, the formation of large, tightly-bound cell aggregates reduces the
accuracy of cell viability measurements. Secondly, we wanted to avoid
conflating the effect of expressing helixCAM protein on growth and
viability with the added effect of nutrient and oxygen diffusion
in large cell aggregates. As other have previously established, aggregates
greater than 100μm in diameter start to observe viability changes
due to limitations in nutrient and oxygen diffusion ( Griffith
