For each species, a single chromatin-capture library was prepared from
0.5 g fresh weight material input. All treatments were according
to the recommendations of the kit vendor for plants (Omni-C,
Dovetail). As a final step, an Illumina-compatible library was prepared
(Dovetail) and paired-end 2 x 150 bp deep-sequenced on a
HiSeq 3000 (Illumina) device for R. breviuscula , R. tenuis
, and J. effusus . Alternatively, the R. pubera library
was paired-end 2 x 150 bp deep-sequenced using DNBseq technology
(BGI Genomics, Hong Kong).
