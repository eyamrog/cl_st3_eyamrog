helixCAM interactions in E. coli were tested as follows. A
single colony was picked into 3mL of LB + carbenicillin,
and grown overnight. 150 μL of culture was diluted into
30mL LB + carbenicillin and grown for 75 min. 120
μL of 100 mM IPTG was added to induce (0.4
mM final concentration) and the culture was grown for an
additional 3 h. 2mL from two separate cultures, each expressing
a distinct helixCAM, were mixed, and after 4 h, a
pipette is placed a quarter of the depth into the
culture, and 100uL of cells are taken. This was either
measured as an OD600 or plated onto a #1.5 glass
coverslip for imaging.
