We performed CoalHMM analysis on four sets of the multiple
alignment data, which were made up of different species (more
details in “ CoalHMM analysis ” section). Based on the
gray short-tailed opossum’s coordinates, we picked out the overlapped type
0 loci in four combinations and concatenated these loci into
a multiple alignment that supported the Dipr_Dasy tree (∼43 Mb).
Although type1 loci also supported the Dipr_Dasy tree, we exclude
these loci when estimating the divergence time to eliminate interference
from the loci with a deeper coalescence between diprotodontian and
dasyuromorphian species. In the same way, we generated the multiple
alignment supporting the Dipr_Micr tree by concatenating the overlapped type2
loci in four combinations (∼100 Mb). We also generated the
multiple alignment supporting the Dasy_Micr tree by concatenating the overlapped
type3 loci in four combinations (∼67 Mb). The MCMCTree program
was then used to analyze three types of alignments and
the outputs are presented in Figure 3 A.
