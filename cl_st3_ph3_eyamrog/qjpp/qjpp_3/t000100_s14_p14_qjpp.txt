Briefly, single-cell RNA-sequencing library preparation was performed as per the
manufacturer’s recommendations for the 10x Genomics 5' TAG v1.0 chemistry
with immune profiling and cell surface protein technology. Both initial
amplification of cDNA and library preparation were carried out with
13 cycles of amplification; V(D)J and cell surface protein libraries
were generated corresponding to each 5'' TAG gene expression library
using 9 cycles and 8 cycles of amplification, respectively. Libraries
were quantified and pooled according to equivalent molar concentrations and
sequenced on Illumina NovaSeq6000 sequencing platform with the following read
lengths: read 1 – 101 cycles; read 2 – 101
cycles; and i7 index - 8 cycles.
