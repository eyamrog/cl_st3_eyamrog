We isolated a panel of 48 human anti-EEEV mAbs from
the B cells of a peripheral blood sample of a
donor who had a prior documented, naturally acquired EEEV infection.
To down-select mAbs from this panel, we focused on those
that could efficiently neutralize Sindbis virus (SINV)/EEEV, a chimeric virus
for use in BSL2 conditions containing the nonstructural proteins of
SINV and the structural proteins of EEEV strain FL93-939 (
