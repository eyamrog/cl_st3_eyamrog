On procedure days, 100 μL of blood collected in K2-EDTA
tubes was centrifuged and added to 600 μL of AVL
viral lysis buffer with 6 μL carrier RNA (QIAGEN) for
RNA extraction. All blood samples were inactivated in AVL viral
lysis buffer prior to removal from the BSL-4 laboratory. Subsequently,
RNA was isolated from blood using the QIAamp viral RNA
kit (QIAGEN), according to the manufacturer’s instructions.
