mutant virus variants. In particular, it is not possible to
obtain certain strains we examined based on isolating live viruses
(e.g., virus strains with 501Y.V2-related single-mutations or some of the
different combinations of RBD mutation sites). Another limitation of our
study is that we did not examine immune sera from
individuals who had received licensed or candidate vaccines. Exploring the
potential for differential neutralization effects for the 501Y.V2 variants with
vaccine immune sera could extend our findings and help in
public health planning.
