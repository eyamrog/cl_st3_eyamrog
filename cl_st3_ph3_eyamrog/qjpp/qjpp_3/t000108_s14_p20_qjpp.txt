Bone marrow chimeric mice were injected with 3 doses of
AF647-Zol (BioVinc, Pasadena, CA, USA) over 7 days prior to
analysis. Mice were sacrificed, long bones were harvested and soft
tissue was removed. Cells in the bone marrow were flushed
from the long bones with PBS. Cells adherent to the
bone were isolated from the remaining marrow-depleted bone fraction through
two rounds of enzymatic digestion with collagenase A (2mg/mL) and
dispase (2mg/mL) for 15 min at 37°C. Red blood cell
lysis was performed using ammonium chloride prior to isolation of
individual cells for flow cytometric analysis and FACS sorting.
