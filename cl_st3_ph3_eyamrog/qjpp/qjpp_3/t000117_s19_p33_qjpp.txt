To determine if the anticipatory activity of NTS Recipient neurons
is required for need-seeking behavior, we specifically inhibited these neurons
during anticipation in mice performing the go/no-go task ( Figures
6 A–6E). Strikingly, this manipulation caused a dramatic decrease in
the anticipatory licking in go trials, resulting in impaired performance
( Figures 6 C–6E). In contrast, the inhibition had no
effect on the on-going licking behavior in the continuous licking
task ( Figures 6 F and 6G).
