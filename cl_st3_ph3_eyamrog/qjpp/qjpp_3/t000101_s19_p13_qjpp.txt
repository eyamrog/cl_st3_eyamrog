To more comprehensively analyze chromosome content, we performed genome-wide SNP
array analysis. We first analyzed genomic DNA from sperm and
oocyte donors to identify homozygous donor-specific alleles. Genomic DNA of
an egg donor ( Figure S1 A) and genomic DNA
of the semen donor ( Figure S1 B) showed homozygous
alleles of maternal and paternal origin along chromosome 6. Only
SNPs that were homozygous within the donors but different between
donors were subsequently used for analysis.
