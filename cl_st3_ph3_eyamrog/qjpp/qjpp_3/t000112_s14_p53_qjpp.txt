Intra-sample (alpha) diversity was measured by using Species Richness and
Shannon’s Entropy. We took species richness as the total number
of detected species in a sample after rarefaction to 1
million reads. Shannon’s entropy is defined as H = a
i log 2 a i where a i is the
relative abundance of taxon i in the sample. This formulation
is robust to sample read depth and accounts for the
relative size of each group in diversity estimation. For alpha
diversity based on k -mers or pathways, we simply substitute
the relative abundance of a species for the relative abundance
of the relevant type of object.
