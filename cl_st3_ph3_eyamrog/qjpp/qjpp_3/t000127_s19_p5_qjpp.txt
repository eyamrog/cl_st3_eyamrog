We further monitored the pH effects on the RGF1 receptor
quadruple mutant rgi1,2,3,4 . The FC or DCCD treatment and
different pH conditions were unable to induce inhibition of RAM
size in rgi1,2,3,4 ( Figures 3 I–3L). Together with the
finding that DCCD had no effect on RAM size in
aqc1-1 , these results suggest that alkalinization-induced RAM growth inhibition
is mainly achieved through RGF1 signaling and that RAM growth
becomes largely independent of pH without an intact RGF1 signaling
pathway.
