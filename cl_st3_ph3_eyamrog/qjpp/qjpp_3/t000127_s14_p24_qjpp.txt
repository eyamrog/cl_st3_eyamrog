Calculation of binding free energy using the MM-PB/SA method: The
molecular mechanics–Poisson–Boltzmann/surface area (MM-PB/SA) method was employed to calculate the
binding free energies for the complexes between RGFR1 and RGF1
in protonated and deprotonated conditions. The solvation free energy is
the sum of polar (ΔGPB) and nonpolar (ΔGNP) parts. ΔGPB
was calculated by solving Poisson–Boltzmann equation with interior and exterior
dielectric constants of 2 and 80, respectively, with a salt
concentration of 0.2 M. ΔGNP was determined on the basis
of solvent accessible surface area (SASA): ΔGNP = 0.0378×ΔSASA −
0.5692 + ΔG dispersion , where SASA was the solvent
accessible surface area and ΔG dispersion was the dispersion term(
