For imaging data processing and analysis, we first used Inscopix
Data Processing software (v.1.2.0., Inscopix) to spatially down-sample all the
raw images by a factor of 4 to reduce file
size, and to correct the image stack for motion artifacts.
The motion-corrected images were cropped to remove post-registration borders and
margin areas. The pre-processed image stack was exported as a
.tif file. Next, we used the extended constrained non-negative matrix
factorization optimized for one-photon imaging (CNMF-E) ( Pnevmatikakis et al.,
