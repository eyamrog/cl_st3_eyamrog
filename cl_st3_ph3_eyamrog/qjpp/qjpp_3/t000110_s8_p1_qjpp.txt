As of early February 2021, SARS-CoV-2 had infected more than
100 million people worldwide and killed more than 2 million
people ( https://covid19.who.int ). SARS-CoV-2 is a member of the
coronavirus family, which carries the largest genome among single-stranded RNA
viruses. Although the replication-dependent RNA polymerase in most RNA viruses
has no proofreading activity, the coronavirus genome encodes a 3′–5′
exonuclease (ExoN, nsp14) with proofreading activity that can partially correct
mutations introduced during virus replication ( Smith and Denison, 2013
