To investigate the methylome space in R. pubera and J.
effusus , the relatively non-destructive NEBNext® Enzymatic Methyl-seq Kit was
employed to prepare an Illumina-compatible library, followed by paired-end sequencing
(2 x 150 bp) on a HiSeq 3000 (Illumina) device.
For each library, 10 Gb of reads were generated.
