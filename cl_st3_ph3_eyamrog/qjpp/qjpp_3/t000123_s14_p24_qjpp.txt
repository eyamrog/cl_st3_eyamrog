Considering that the assembly procedure could result in a small
number of Ns in some LASTZ synteny blocks, we found
61 informative markers with Ns in both upstream and downstream
in the tammar wallaby and/or monito del monte genomes. By
excluding these TEs in the analyses, the above conclusion was
not affected. The monito del monte is still placed outside
the Australasian group, while polytomy remains rejected (p value =
1.1477e-14), and hybridization is not accepted either (p value =
1).
