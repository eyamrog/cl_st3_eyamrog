To determine whether EYS wt blastomeres were caused by paternal
chromosome loss, we tested blastomeres of two embryos after a
single mitosis post-Cas9/RNP injection for heterozygosity by using SNP arrays
( Figures 4 E–4G and S6 ). In one embryo,
two sister blastomeres with an EYS wt genotype showed loss
of chromosome 6q and its sister cell showed monosomy 6
because of loss of the paternal chromosome ( Figures 4
F and 4G).
