Female BALB/c mice, ages 6 to 8 weeks, were purchased
from Charles River Laboratory. Five- to seven-week-old STAT1 KO female
mice were acquired from Taconic Biosciences. The animal experiments were
performed at Galveston National Laboratory animal biosafety level 4 (ABSL-4)
facility in accordance with National Institutes of Health (NIH) guidelines,
the Animal Welfare Act, and U.S. federal law and approved
by the Institutional Animal Care and Use Committee (IACUC).
