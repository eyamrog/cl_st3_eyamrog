We recorded the bulk activities of NTS Recipient neurons in
these mice with fiber photometry, at different stages of training
in the go/no-go task ( Figures S5 K–S5M). At the
early stage of training, NTS Recipient neurons showed little response
following CS presentations in either the go or no-go trials
( Figures S5 N, S5O, and S5R). However, at the
late stage, these neurons displayed ramping-up activities following CS presentations
until sucrose was delivered in the go trials ( Figures
S5 P–S5S). In contrast, such anticipatory response was absent in
the no-go trials. Thus, the anticipation response of NTS Recipient
neurons, which bears characteristics of that of aIC Fezf2 neurons,
emerges during learning, paralleling the establishment of need-seeking behavior.
