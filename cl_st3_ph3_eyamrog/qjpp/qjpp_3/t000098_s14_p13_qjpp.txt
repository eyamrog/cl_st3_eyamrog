For assembly of the PCR products the Gibson Assembly® Master
Mix (New England Biolabs GmbH, Frankfurt, Germany) was used according

were transformed and amplified in NEB® 5-alpha Competent E.coli (New
England Biolabs GmbH, Frankfurt, Germany).
