We assessed the absorption strength of both the Copan and
Isohelix swabs for various biological and surface materials encountered when
sampling metro stations. A single surface was selected for a
designated sampling area to test the absorption strength. Both swabs
were moistened by submerging the swab for a few seconds
in their preservative media. The area was then swabbed for
3 min, covering the selected surface. By moistening the swab
prior to sampling, the swab matrix would take up more
microflora already saturated with the transport medium.
