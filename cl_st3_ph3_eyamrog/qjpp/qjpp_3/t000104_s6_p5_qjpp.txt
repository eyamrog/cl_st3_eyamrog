In summary, our study demonstrates that FBXO44 promotes DNA replication-coupled
transcriptional silencing of RE in cancer cells. Moreover, FBXO44/SUV39H1 are
crucial suppressors of DNA replication stress and IFN signaling in
cancer cells. FBXO44/SUV39H1 inhibitors could have widespread application in cancer
treatment, as stand-alone anti-cancer therapies and enhancers of immunotherapy response.
