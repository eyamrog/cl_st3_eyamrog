HEK293T cells were transfected with FLAG-Empty Vector (FLAG-EV) and FLAG-SP140
plasmids using Lipofectamine 2000 (Invitrogen) for 48 hours. Lysates were
incubated with FLAG M2 (Sigma, F3165) antibody and immunoprecipitated protein
was run on a 4-12% Tris-Bis. Gel and stained with
Silver Stain (Thermo Fisher) and gel sections were cut and
sent for in-gel digestion, micro-capillary LC/MS/MS, analysis, protein database searching
and data analysis at the Taplin Mass Spectrometry Facility (Harvard
Medical School). Mass Spec of lymphoblastoid cells (LBLs) carrying SP140
wildtype or SP140 loss-of-function variants (homozygous for rs7423615, Coriell Institute
of Medical Research) was performed on endogenous SP140 immunoprecipitated from
1mg of nuclear lysates with SP140 antibody (Sigma).
