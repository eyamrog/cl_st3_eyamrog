To investigate whether FBXO44 associated with human tumorigenesis and therapeutic
responses, we interrogated public cancer transcriptomic data, revealing FBXO44 overexpression
in many human cancer types compared to normal adjacent tissues
( Figure 7 A). FBXO44 was expressed at low levels
in normal breast tissues and increased with tumor stage (
Figure 7 B). High FBXO44 expression correlated with poor patient
outcome in several major cancer types ( Figure 7 C).
