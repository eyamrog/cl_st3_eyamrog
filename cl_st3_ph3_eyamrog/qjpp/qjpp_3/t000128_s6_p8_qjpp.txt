The helixCAM platform offers a simple but powerful way to
control cell-cell interactions, a ubiquitous feature found from prokaryotes to
eukaryotes. Beyond the set of five helixCAM pairs characterized in
this work, there are likely many more orthogonal helixCAMs to
be discovered that further expand potential applications. We believe that
the toolkit of helixCAMs presented here will assist biologists across
multiple fields to better understand the roles cell interactions play
in various contexts, as well as enable engineers to better
control these interactions for therapeutic applications.
