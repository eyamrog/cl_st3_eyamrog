After using NGS to examine the barcodes of yeasts that
survive selection, we assigned an “enrichment” and an “orthogonality” score
for each helixCAM pair and, using these metrics, selected two
top candidate CC pairs (sg30/sg61 and sg83/sg88) that were likely
to be capable of directing strong and specific cell aggregation
