We measured sequencing quality based on 5 metrics: number of
reads obtained from a sample, GC content, Shannon’s entropy of
k -mers, post PCR Qubit score, and recorded DNA concentration
before PCR. The number of reads in each sample was
counted both before and after quality control, we used the
number of reads after quality control for our results though
the difference was slight. GC content was estimated from 100,000
reads in each sample after low quality DNA and human
reads had been removed. Shannon’s entropy of k -mers was
estimated from 10,000 reads taken from each samples. PCR Qubit
score and DNA concentration are described in the wet lab
methods.
