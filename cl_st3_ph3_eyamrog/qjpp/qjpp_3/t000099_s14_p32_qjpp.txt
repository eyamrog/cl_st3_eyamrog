Influenza A virus strain A/PR8/34 (H1N1) stock suspension was diluted,
and 60 PFU were administered intra-nasally to isoflurane-anesthetized 8-12 week-old
male mice in 50 μl of PBS. Mouse weight was
monitored daily after infection and mice that presented weight loss
< 20% of their initial body weight were sacrificed and
considered deceased.
