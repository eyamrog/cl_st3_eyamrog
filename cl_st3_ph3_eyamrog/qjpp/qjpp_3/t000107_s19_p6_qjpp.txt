We next interrogated the differentiation state of Fli1 -sgRNA (sgFli1)
or Ctrl-sgRNA (sgCtrl) transduced C9P14 cells during acutely resolved infection.
On Day 8 p.i., Fli1 deletion reduced the proportion of
the CD127 Hi memory precursors (T MP ), whereas the
frequency KLRG1 Hi terminal effector (T EFF ) population remained
unchanged and the CD127 Lo KLRG1 Lo population slightly increased
( Figures 2 A and S3 A). These effects were
more dramatic at day 15 p.i. when the frequency of
the CD127 Hi T MP population was reduced and the
KLRG1 Hi T EFF cell population increased ( Figure 2
A). However, at both time points the absolute number of
both T MP and T EFF was increased ~2- to
10-fold due to the proliferative expansion of Fli1 -deficient CD8
+ T cells ( Figure 2 A). This skewing of
T cell differentiation toward T EFF -like populations was also
observed using CX3CR1 and CXCR3 ( Gerlach et al., 2016
