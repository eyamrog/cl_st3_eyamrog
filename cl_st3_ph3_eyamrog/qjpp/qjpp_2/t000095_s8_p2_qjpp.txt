Coronaviruses are enveloped non-segmented positive-sense RNA viruses. There are four
coronavirus genera, Alphacoronavirus , Betacoronavirus , Deltacoronavirus , and Gammacoronavirus
, in the subfamily Coronavirinae belonging to the family Coronaviridae
. Human coronaviruses belong to the genera Alphacoronavirus and Betacoronavirus
and usually cause mild respiratory illness except for the two
highly pathogenic viruses, SARS-CoV and MERS-CoV, which cause a severe
respiratory syndrome in humans ( Cui et al., 2019 6.
