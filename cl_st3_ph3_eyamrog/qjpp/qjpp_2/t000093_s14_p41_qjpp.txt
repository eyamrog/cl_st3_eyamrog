The average fluorescence intensity for mCherry and mGFP in an
individual cell was used to approximate the concentration of associated
fusion proteins. This was determined by using manual image segmentation
(ImageJ) to draw 4.5 × 4.5 μm square ROIs in
cytoplasmic regions featuring homogeneous distribution of fluorescence (i.e., regions with
low density of membrane-bound organelles like the Golgi). The aforementioned
FCS calibration curves were then used to determine the protein’s
concentration. Presence or absence of stress granules was manually annotated.
