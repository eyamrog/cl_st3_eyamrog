The surviving mice generated low titers of neutralization antibodies (1:10
to 1:40). The reinfection with much higher titers of virus
(7 × 10 5 TCID 50 SARS-CoV-2) only caused mild
pneumonia in the surviving mice. In contrast, the naive-infected mice
had severe disease with much higher viral RNA copies in
the lung and brain tissues. It is unclear that the
neutralizing antibody or humoral immunity caused the observed protection. Future
studies are needed to determine whether cellular immune arms also
contribute to the protection from reinfection.
