In 2018, Waymo, the Google subsidiary developing AVs, introduced the
first shared AV fleet to the market (Waymo-One) ( https://waymo.com/
). More than 1,400 self-driving cars, trucks, and other vehicles
are currently being tested by more than 80 companies across
36 US states and the District of Columbia ( 25
). Recent estimates suggest that by 2020, 5% of car
sales will be AVs, representing 2% of the vehicle fleet
and 4% of the miles traveled in the United States
( 42 ). The same estimates predict that by 2030,
AVs will cover 40% of the car market sales, representing
20% of the vehicle fleet and 30% of the miles
traveled in the United States ( 42 ).
