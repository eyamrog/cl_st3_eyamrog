In conclusion, among patients at high bleeding risk, a strategy
of PCI with a polymer-based zotarolimus-eluting stent followed by 1
month of dual antiplatelet therapy was noninferior to a polymer-free
drug-coated stent with regard to a composite outcome of death
from cardiac causes, myocardial infarction, or stent thrombosis, as well
as with regard to target-lesion failure.
