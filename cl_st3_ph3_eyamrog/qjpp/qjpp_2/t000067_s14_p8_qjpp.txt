We quantified the generation of reactive oxygen species and cellular
bioenergetics of human kidney proximal tubular (HK-2) cells that were
exposed to recombinant suPAR at a concentration of 10 ng
per milliliter, with or without anti-uPAR antibody, using the MitoSOX
(Invitrogen) and Seahorse Extracellular Flux Analyzer (Agilent), respectively. Details of
the experiments are provided in the Supplementary Appendix .
