Fluency was assessed using a semantic fluency test based on
an animal naming task. In ELSA, participants had 1 min
to say as many animals as possible aloud to an
interviewer. In Whitehall II, participants were given 1 min to
write as many animals as possible. Fluency was available at
all waves of the Whitehall II study and waves one
to five (2002–10) and wave seven (2014) of the ELSA
study.
