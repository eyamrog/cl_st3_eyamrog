Our database searches identified 463 potentially eligible studies. After removal
of duplicates, 260 study titles and abstracts were screened (
figure 1 ). We assessed 51 full text articles for
eligibility, of which 13 were excluded from the systematic review
( appendix p 1 ) and an additional 16 were
excluded from the meta-analysis. There was a high inter-rater reliability
for screening titles and abstracts (κ=0·95) and full texts (κ=0·96).
