Insulin sensitivity in the liver (suppression of glucose production during
stages 1 and 2 of the clamp procedure), skeletal muscle
(stimulation of glucose disposal during stage 3 of the clamp
procedure), and adipose tissue (suppression of lipolysis during stages 1
and 2 of the clamp procedure) increased after weight loss
in both the diet and surgery groups, with no significant
differences between the groups ( Table 3 ). Weight loss
increased beta-cell function in both groups, which appeared to be
caused by an increase in beta-cell glucose sensitivity and whole-body
insulin sensitivity ( Table 3 ).
