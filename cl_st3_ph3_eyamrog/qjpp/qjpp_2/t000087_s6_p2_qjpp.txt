The 3-year event-free survival of 92% in the brentuximab vedotin
group was attained with tailored radiation therapy in 53% of
the patients. This finding contrasts with the results of the
recent EuroNet-PHL-C1 trial , in which 5-year event-free survival was
88% among patients with an early response and 86% among
those with a slow response; 65% of the patients received
radiation therapy, and a boost to 30 Gy was necessitated
in 29%. Our data are also consistent with the results
of a single-group, multicenter, phase 2 trial that incorporated brentuximab
vedotin in the treatment of patients with high-risk disease, with
a lower cumulative anthracycline dose but with 66% of the
patients receiving radiation therapy and with a substantially higher incidence
of prednisone-associated toxic effects in bone.
