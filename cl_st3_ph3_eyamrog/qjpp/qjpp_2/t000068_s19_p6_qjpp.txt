At 1 year, the principal secondary outcome of target-lesion failure
had occurred in 174 patients (17.6%) in the zotarolimus-eluting stent
group and in 169 patients (17.4%) in the polymer-free drug-coated
stent group (risk difference, 0.2 percentage points; upper boundary of
the one-sided 95% CI, 3.0; upper boundary of the one-sided
97.5% CI, 3.5; P=0.007 for noninferiority) ( Table 3 ).
