To establish a reliable on-treatment baseline, patients entered a 4-week
run-in period to receive either ivacaftor at a dose of
150 mg every 12 hours (ivacaftor comparator cohort; Phe508del –gating
genotypes, including Phe508del–Arg117His ) or tezacaftor at a dose of
100 mg once daily combined with ivacaftor (tezacaftor–ivacaftor comparator cohort;
Phe508del –residual function genotypes) (Fig. S1). These genotype-defined comparator cohorts
were based on approved indications for CFTR modulators in each
country where the trial was conducted.
