The strongest evidence for prion-based inheritance is transformation. We thus
examined whether Snt1 expressed in E. coli and aggregated in
vitro could transform naive cells to become [ ESI +
]. We expressed Snt1 as a recombinant His-tagged fusion protein
and then lysed the bacteria, digested all nucleic acids, and
isolated Snt1 by using nickel affinity chromatography. Snt1 eluted over
an increasing gradient of imidazole and, after elution, proceeded to
form visible aggregates within 1–2 h ( Figure 1 F).
After washes with 4M guanidine hydrochloride, Snt1 remained aggregated (
Figure 1 G).
