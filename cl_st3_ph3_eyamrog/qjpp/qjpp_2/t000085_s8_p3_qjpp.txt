Circulating tumor DNA (ctDNA) analysis is a promising alternative strategy
in which peripheral blood (a “liquid biopsy”) is directly evaluated
for evidence of minimal residual disease that could ultimately be
the source of a later clinical recurrence. Several observational studies
involving patients with solid tumors, including those with stage II
colon cancer, have confirmed a very high risk of recurrence
(>80%) when ctDNA is detected after curative-intent therapy without further
adjuvant treatment. Nevertheless, uncertainty remains as to whether adjuvant treatment
is beneficial for these ctDNA-positive patients who are at high
risk for recurrence.
