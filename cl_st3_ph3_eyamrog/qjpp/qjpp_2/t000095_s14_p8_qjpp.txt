Blood was collected retro-orbitally and transferred to a blood collection
tube containing EDTA to prevent clotting. Cell classifications were analyzed
using a ProCyte Dx Hematology Analyzer (IDEXX) followed with manufacturer’s
instructions. Plasma was separated by centrifugation at 3,000 × g
for 10 min at room temperature and stored at −80°C
until use. Plasma was collected, diluted 1:6 with phosphate-buffered saline
(PBS), and tested using Catalyst One (IDEXX) followed with manufacturer’s
instructions.
