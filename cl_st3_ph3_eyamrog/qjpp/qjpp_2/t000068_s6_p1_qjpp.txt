Among patients at high bleeding risk who were treated with
1 month of dual antiplatelet therapy after PCI, polymer-based zotarolimus-eluting
stents were found to be noninferior to polymer-free drug-coated stents
at 1 year with regard to both safety (as shown
by the composite outcome of death from cardiac causes, myocardial
infarction, or stent thrombosis) and effectiveness (as shown by the
composite outcome of death from cardiac causes, target-vessel myocardial infarction,
or clinically indicated target-lesion revascularization).
