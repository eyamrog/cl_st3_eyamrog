In conclusion, among adults in China in 2018–19, the estimated
overall prevalence of past 30-day e-cigarette use was 1·6%, which
represented an increase of 0·3% from 2015–16. The factors associated
with e-cigarette use were male sex, young age, high-level education
and high income status, conventional smoking, and obesity. Future studies
and surveys on e-cigarette use in China will be crucial
to improve understanding of the trends and potential effects of
regulations.
