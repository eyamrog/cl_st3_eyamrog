We used the 2008–09 New Zealand Nutrition Survey (acquired from
the University of Otago's Life in New Zealand Research Group
who conducted the survey) to inform our specification of the
business-as-usual (BAU) diet, by sex, age, and ethnicity (Māori and
non-Māori) for 340 food categories. Percentage changes in purchasing were
assumed to be the same as percentage changes in food
consumption. BMI change was derived from the change in energy
intake.
