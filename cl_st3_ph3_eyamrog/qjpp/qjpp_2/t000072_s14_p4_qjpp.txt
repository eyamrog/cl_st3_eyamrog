Glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonists were discontinued 2 weeks before each
metabolic study, oral diabetes medications were discontinued 3 days before,
and insulin was discontinued 1 day before to reduce their
effects on metabolic outcomes. Diabetes medications were adjusted on the
basis of twice-daily blood glucose measurements. A diabetes medication score
was calculated on the basis of the number and dose
of medications (scores ranged from 0 to 3.57, with a
higher score indicating a greater number of medications, a higher
dosage of medications, or both; details are provided in the
Calculations section in the Supplementary Appendix , available at NEJM.org).
