Hodgkin’s lymphoma can serve as a model for balancing curative-intent
therapy with long-term effects in populations who have decades of
life ahead. The refining of contemporary approaches to treatment in
children with advanced-stage and high-risk Hodgkin’s lymphoma requires improving initial
disease control to reduce the risk of late disease and
premature death that have been associated with high-dose salvage chemotherapy
and stem-cell transplantation.
