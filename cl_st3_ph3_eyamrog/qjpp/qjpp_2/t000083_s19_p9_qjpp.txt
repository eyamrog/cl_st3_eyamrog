SARS-CoV-2 spike-specific Th1 (interferon-γ, interleukin-2, or both) CD4+ T cells
were detected in 69% of participants at baseline, with higher
response rates and amounts among the mRNA-primed participants ( Figure
3 ). At day 15, an increase in the spike-specific
Th1 responses occurred after boosting in all groups except those
receiving the homologous Ad26.COV2.S regimen. Spike-specific Th2 (interleukin-4, interleukin-5, or
interleukin-13) CD4+ T cells were infrequent (and at low levels)
or absent in most subgroups. Th1-type CD4+ T-cell responses were
predominant before and after homologous or heterologous boosting.
