We used an adapted version of the Cochrane Risk of
Bias Tool for cohort studies to assess study quality. Comprising
six domains of appraisal, including the exposure, confounding variables, outcome,
and adequacy of the follow-up ( appendix p 2 ),
we regarded the quality of the study as high if
the total score was at least 21 (maximum 24). One
author (GDB) evaluated the quality of each study included in
the review and scores were independently corroborated for a random
50% sample by another (PF).
