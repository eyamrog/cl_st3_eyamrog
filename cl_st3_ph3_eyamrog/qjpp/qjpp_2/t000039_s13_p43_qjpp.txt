While research supports the effectiveness of play therapy with children
experiencing many problems, few programs have been designed specifically for
families experiencing homelessness ( 1 , 13 , 81 ).
One exception is the Horizons for the Homeless Children's Playspace
program. Horizons has installed play spaces in more than 90
shelters across Massachusetts ( 35 ). Their teams carefully select
play resources for children ages 0–6 who are experiencing trauma
( 35 ).
