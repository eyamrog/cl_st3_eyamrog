Eligible patients had coronary artery disease and a clinical indication
for PCI. In addition, patients were classified as being at
high bleeding risk or were otherwise a candidate for short-term
prophylaxis for stent thrombosis (1 month of either dual antiplatelet
therapy or single antiplatelet therapy plus an anticoagulant drug). Complete
inclusion and exclusion criteria, including the criteria defining high bleeding
risk, are listed in Table S2.
