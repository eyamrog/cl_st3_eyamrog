Life expectancy at birth was inversely associated with the extent
of unemployment, poverty, and low education in MSOA in 2002
and 2019 ( figure 6 ). There was substantial variation
in life expectancy across MSOAs at any level of poverty
or unemployment seen in the vertical spread of points in
figure 6 . From 2002 to 2019, there were, on
average, smaller gains in life expectancy in the MSOAs with
the highest levels of unemployment, poverty, and low education than
in those in the lowest levels, especially for women.
