Conclusions: Among patients at high bleeding risk who received 1
month of dual antiplatelet therapy after PCI, use of polymer-based
zotarolimus-eluting stents was noninferior to use of polymer-free drug-coated stents
with regard to safety and effectiveness composite outcomes. (Funded by
Medtronic; ONYX ONE ClinicalTrials.gov number, NCT03344653 .)
