The mean absolute change in the percentage of predicted FEV
1 from baseline (measured at the end of the run-in
period) through week 8 with elexacaftor–tezacaftor–ivacaftor treatment (the primary end
point) was 3.7 percentage points (95% confidence interval [CI], 2.8
to 4.6; P<0.001) ( Table 2 and Figure 1A ).
In contrast, the mean absolute change with active control (ivacaftor
or tezacaftor–ivacaftor) was 0.2 percentage points (95% CI, −0.7 to
1.1), reflecting a between-group difference of 3.5 percentage points (95%
CI, 2.2 to 4.7; P<0.001). The mean absolute change in
sweat chloride concentration from baseline through week 8 with elexacaftor–tezacaftor–ivacaftor
was −22.3 mmol per liter (95% CI, −24.5 to −20.2;
P<0.001), as compared with a mean absolute change with active
control of 0.7 mmol per liter (95% CI, −1.4 to
2.8), reflecting a between-group difference of −23.1 mmol per liter
(95% CI, −26.1 to −20.1; P<0.001) ( Table 2 and
Figure 1B ). The mean absolute change in the CFQ-R
respiratory domain score from baseline through week 8 with elexacaftor–tezacaftor–ivacaftor
was 10.3 points (95% CI, 8.0 to 12.7) and with
active control was 1.6 points (95% CI, −0.8 to 4.1),
reflecting a between-group difference of 8.7 points (95% CI, 5.3
to 12.1) ( Table 2 and Figure 1C ).
