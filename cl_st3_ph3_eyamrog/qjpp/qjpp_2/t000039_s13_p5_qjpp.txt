Furthermore, the HUD PIT count misses families who are unseen,
namely, those who live doubled-up, those who couch surf, or
those who live in motels, hotels, or campgrounds ( 48
). While PIT sporadically counts those living in motels or
hotels, they are included only if considered to be shelter
residents with no access to other spaces and the shelter
or a government agency is paying the cost or if
they live in an area where emergency shelters are unavailable
( 21 ).
