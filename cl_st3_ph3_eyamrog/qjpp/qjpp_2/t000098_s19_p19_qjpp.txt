To describe our findings in quantitative terms, we use a
biophysical model for formation of cAMP gradients by PDEs at
the nanoscale ( Methods S2 ). Based on our experimental
observations ( Figures 1 , 2 , 3 , and
S4 ), this model analyzes the effects of binding sites
on free cAMP concentrations in cells and on the spatial
profile of cAMP gradients generated by PDE-mediated degradation.
