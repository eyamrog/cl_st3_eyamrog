We next analyzed the immune infiltrate 14 days after tumor
injection to define T cell responses. Trem2 –/– mice showed
a trend in the increase of intratumoral αβ T cells,
which was significant in anti-PD-1 treated mice ( Figure 4
F). CD8 + T cells were increased in Trem2 –/–
mice with or without anti-PD-1, as were PD-1 + CD8
T cells, corroborating our initial results (see Figure 1 C).
PD-1 + CD4 T cells were only increased in Trem2
–/– mice treated with anti-PD-1 ( Figure 4 F). Treg,
B, NK, and Lag3 + T cell proportions did not
change in the absence of TREM2 (data not shown). Altogether,
these results demonstrate that TREM2 deficiency augments the efficacy of
ICT in two transplantable tumor models responsive to anti-PD-1. Enhancement
of ICT was associated with augmented T cell infiltration and
activation, at least in the MCA model.
