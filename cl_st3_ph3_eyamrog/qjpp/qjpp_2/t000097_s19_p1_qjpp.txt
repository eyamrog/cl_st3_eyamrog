first analyzed an MCA-induced sarcoma cell line (MCA/1956) in WT
and Trem2 –/– mice. MCA/1956 belongs to a panel of
MCA-induced sarcomas known as progressors. Since these tumors were developed
in immunocompetent WT mice, their immunogenic profiles were edited by
