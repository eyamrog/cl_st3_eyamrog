Mice were of mixed sexes. Mice within experiments were age
and sex matched. Mice were housed under specific pathogen free
conditions. Mice from different genotypes were cohoused from birth and
separated during the experiment (the day of tumor injection). Mice
did not undergo any procedures prior to their stated use.
Mice used in this study include WT C57BL/6J and Trem2
–/– animals bred at Washington University School of Medicine animal
facility. For experiments with wild-type groups only (e.g., experiments with
anti-TREM2 treatment), wild-type mice were purchased from Jackson. All animals
were backcrossed until at least > 98% C57BL/6J confirmed by
genotype wide microsatellite typing. For tumor models, animals were injected
at 8 weeks of age for MCA/1956 and MC38 cell
lines. For the PyMT model, female mice were injected at
10 weeks of age. All studies performed on mice were
done in accordance with the Institutional Animal Care and Use
Committee at Washington University in St. Louis approved all protocols
used in this study.
