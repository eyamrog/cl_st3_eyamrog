SAV fleets could have positive impacts on urban land use.
Urban parking space may be reduced as much as 90%
if AVs are implemented in ridesharing mode ( 73 ).
Moreover, AVs could also permit the relocation of public space
from automobile infrastructure to other activities, such as green and
blue spaces that support physical activity and social interaction (
52 , 83 ). However, modeling results on parking space
related to AVs are very sensitive to model assumptions, which
are still very uncertain (e.g., the perception of time in
AVs or operational costs) ( 73 ).
