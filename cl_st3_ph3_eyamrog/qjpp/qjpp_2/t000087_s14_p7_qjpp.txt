FDG-PET-CT was required at baseline and after the receipt of
two cycles of chemotherapy (interim PET assessment). FDG-PET-CT was also
required at the completion of chemotherapy in patients with slow-responding
lesions and after radiation therapy in patients with an incomplete
metabolic response at the completion of five cycles of chemotherapy.
Response and progression were specified with the use of the
modified Lugano criteria (Table S2A).
