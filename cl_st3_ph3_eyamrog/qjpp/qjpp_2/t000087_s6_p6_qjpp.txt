The superior event-free survival that we found with brentuximab vedotin
plus dose-intensive chemotherapy with doxorubicin, vincristine, etoposide, prednisone, and cyclophosphamide
in this randomized, phase 3 trial involving children and adolescents
provides support for its use as first-line therapy in children
and adolescents with high-risk Hodgkin’s lymphoma. Long-term follow-up will be
important to more thoroughly assess the occurrence of late events
and overall survival.
