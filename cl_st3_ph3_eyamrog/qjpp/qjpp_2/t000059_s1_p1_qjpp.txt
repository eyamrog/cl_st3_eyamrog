Background: Falls in older people have become a major public
health concern worldwide, but a comprehensive assessment of the burden
of falls for older people in mainland China has not
been done. We aimed to investigate the burden of falls
among older people at the national and subnational level in
mainland China, and explore the trends from 1990 to 2019,
using data from the Global Burden of Diseases, Injuries, and
Risk Factors Study (GBD) 2019.
