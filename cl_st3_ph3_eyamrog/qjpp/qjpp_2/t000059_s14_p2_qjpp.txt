The measurement framework and estimates from GBD 2019 covering 204
countries and territories from 1990 to 2019 are described in
the GBD 2019 capstone publications. The methods and data used
for estimation of the burden of injury morbidity and mortality
are provided in detail in the GBD 2017 literature. The
key features and new updates for injury-specific data processing in
GBD 2019 are summarised here.
