To investigate the potential relationship between Rap1 and [ ESI
+ ], we performed ChIP-seq against Rap1. In naive cells,
Rap1 pervasively bound both telomeric repeats and sub-telomeres ( Figures
6 A and 6D), consistent with its role in controlling
transcription in the region. In [ ESI + ] cells,
by contrast, Rap1 binding strongly decreased in the sub-telomeric regions
where we observed gene activation and increased Set3C binding (
Figures 6 A and 6D). However, its essential function in
binding to telomeric repeats was unaffected ( Figures 6 A
and S5 E–S5I). This implies that [ ESI + ]
separates the function of these two binding contexts, preserving Rap1’s
essential role in maintaining genome stability ( Palladino et al.,
