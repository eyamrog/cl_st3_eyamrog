The proportion of participants missing data was 14·9% for MET,
3·8% for the number of cigarettes smoked, and less than
2% for all other risk factors ( appendix 2 p
8 ). We applied a multiple imputation method based on
Markov Chain Monte Carlo by PROC multiple imputation procedure to
impute the missing values, and the average of ten imputations
was used to do the factor analysis ( appendix 2
p 7 ). We repeated the factor analysis among participants
without any missing value as a sensitive analysis.
