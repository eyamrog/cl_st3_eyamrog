Both Victoria and other (control) states had a downward trend
in MHI-5 scores in the years leading up to the
pandemic (2011–19) and saw a disproportionately large and statistically significant
drop in 2020 ( figure 1 ). In Victoria, from
2019 to 2020, the mean MHI-5 score declined from 71·6
(95% CI 70·8–72·3) to 69·4 (68·6–70·1), a decrease of 2·2
points (p<0·0001), whereas the corresponding change in the other Australian
states was from 72·0 (71·5–72·5) to 70·9 (70·5–71·4), a decrease
of 1·1 points (p=0·0025).
