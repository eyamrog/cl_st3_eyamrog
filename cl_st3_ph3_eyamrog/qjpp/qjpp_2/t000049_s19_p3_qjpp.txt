Women had better memory scores than men (mean difference [male
scores minus female scores] –0·10 SDs [95% CI –0·15 to
–0·05] at age 50 years, –0·14 SDs [–0·18 to –0·11]
at 60 years, and –0·19 SDs [–0·22 to –0·16] at
70 years) in analyses adjusted for age, ethnicity, birth cohort,
and practice effect. After adjustment for level of education, these
differences were even larger (–0·17 SDs [–0·21 to –0·13] at
age 50 years, –0·22 SDs [–0·25 to –0·20] at 60
years, and –0·28 SDs [–0·31 to –0·25] at 70 years).
