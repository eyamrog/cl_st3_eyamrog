To better understand the magnitude of these health gains, it
is useful to quantify them as the percentage gain in
HALYs beyond that expected for the population under BAU (173
million HALYs for the remainder of the lifespan). The fruit
and vegetable subsidy brought a gain of 935 000 HALYs
(95% UI 448 000–1 310 000), which is a 0·54%
increase on 173 million HALYs beyond BAU, and the combination
of the fruit and vegetable subsidy with a sugar tax
brought a gain of 3·31 million HALYs, which is an
increase of 1·91% beyond BAU ( table 3 ).
