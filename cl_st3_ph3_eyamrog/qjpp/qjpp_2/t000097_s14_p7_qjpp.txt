Mice were treated intraperitoneally (i.p.) with anti-PD1 antibody (200μg/mouse) every
3 days, starting at day 3 or day 8 after
tumor injection, as specified. Mice were treated i.p. with anti-TREM2
antibody (clone 178; 200μg/mouse) every 5 days, starting at day
2 after tumor injection. Anti-hILT1 (clone 135.5) was used as
a control. Anti-CD4 and anti-CD8 treatments were started one day
before tumor injection (200μg/mouse) and then administrated every 3 days
for the entire duration of the experiment.
