The quality of eligible studies was independently appraised by two
reviewers (GB and SDL). We applied a questionnaire that has
been previously used for the assessment of bias in prison
prevalence studies, with original items largely based on the Joanna
Briggs Institute Critical Appraisal Tool for prevalence studies. Using the
modified tool, we assessed the external and internal validity of
included studies across ten questions with prespecified response options (
appendix p 6 ). Final scores range between 0 and
10.
