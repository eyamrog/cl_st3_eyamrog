The increase in mandatory statewide managed-care initiatives has also generated
questions about how different plans achieve cost savings. An evaluation
of Pennsylvania's Geisinger health plan showed that the plan administrators’
focus on proactive and data-driven case management processes generated inpatient
savings, whereas their advanced medical home partnerships between managed-care organizations
and clinical providers significantly reduced outpatient costs ( 29 ).
This type of research starts to provide clues to pathways
to achieve cost reductions but may be specific to the
health plans studied.
