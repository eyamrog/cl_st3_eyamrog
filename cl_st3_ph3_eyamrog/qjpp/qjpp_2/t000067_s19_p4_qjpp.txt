Last, we examined the incremental value of adding suPAR to
the NCDR Simplified Integer Risk Score in predicting the risk
of acute kidney injury after coronary angiography. The AUC for
the NCDR risk score was 0.579 (95% CI, 0.560 to
0.597). The addition of suPAR to the NCDR score modestly
improved the AUC to 0.628 (95% CI, 0.610 to 0.647),
with a change in the AUC of 0.050 (95% CI,
0.013 to 0.087).
