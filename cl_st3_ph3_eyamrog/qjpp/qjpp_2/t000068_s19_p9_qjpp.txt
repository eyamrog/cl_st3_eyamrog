Post hoc landmark analyses set at 30 days for the
primary outcome and the principal secondary outcome are shown in
Table S16. Time-to-event curves with landmark analysis that were analyzed
according to the Kaplan–Meier method are shown in Figures S4
through S7. Between 0 and 30 days, the primary outcome
