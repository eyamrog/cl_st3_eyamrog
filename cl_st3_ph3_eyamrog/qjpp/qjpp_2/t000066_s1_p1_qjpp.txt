Background: Patients with anemia and lower-risk myelodysplastic syndromes in whom
erythropoiesis-stimulating agent therapy is not effective generally become dependent on
red-cell transfusions. Luspatercept, a recombinant fusion protein that binds transforming
growth factor β superfamily ligands to reduce SMAD2 and SMAD3
signaling, showed promising results in a phase 2 study.
