Statistical analyses were performed using PRISM 8.0.2 for Windows (GraphPad).
Significant differences between groups were determined using a two-way analysis
of variance (ANOVA). For contrasting two experimental groups, two-tailed Student’s
t tests were performed to determine significant differences. Statistical significant:
∗ p < 0.05, ∗∗ p < 0.01, ∗∗∗ p
< 0.001, ∗∗∗∗ p < 0.0001.
