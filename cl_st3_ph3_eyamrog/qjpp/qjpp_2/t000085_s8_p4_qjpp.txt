The Circulating Tumour DNA Analysis Informing Adjuvant Chemotherapy in Stage
II Colon Cancer (DYNAMIC) trial was a randomized trial designed
to investigate whether a ctDNA-guided approach as compared with a
standard approach in stage II colon cancer could reduce the
use of adjuvant treatment without compromising the risk of recurrence.
We further examined outcomes among ctDNA-positive patients who received adjuvant
chemotherapy, to assess the benefit of treating this high-risk group
of patients, as well as outcomes among ctDNA-negative patients whose
disease was managed by surveillance alone.
