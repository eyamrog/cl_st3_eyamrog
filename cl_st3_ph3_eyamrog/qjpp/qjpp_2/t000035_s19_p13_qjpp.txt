Health care use outcomes were focused primarily on intensive or
expensive outcomes: institutionalization (8 studies), hospitalization (3 studies), rehospitalization (2
studies), preventative hospitalizations (1 study), emergency room services (2 studies),
health care use postoperatively (1 study), and formal care after
hospitalization (1 study). Only a handful of studies examined less-intensive
outcomes of health care use: medicine adherence (1 study), physician
consults (1 study), and formal care use after hospitalization (1
study). Two studies looked at total health care costs across
all settings of care.
