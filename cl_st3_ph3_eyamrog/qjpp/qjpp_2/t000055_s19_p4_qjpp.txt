Visual inspection of the total number of symptoms reported, asymptomatic
infections, self-reported hospital visits, and instances of long symptom duration
over time suggested no change in any of these outcomes
with an increasing proportion of infections with the B.1.1.7 variant
( appendix pp 3–4 ). When correcting for mean age,
sex, and ambient temperature and humidity, there was no evidence
of an association between the proportion of infections with the
B.1.1.7 variant and the number of symptoms reported over a
4-week period, the number of admissions to hospital, long symptom
duration, or the proportion of asymptomatic cases ( appendix p
8 ).
