The overall incidence of clinically significant adverse events was similar
in the two groups (73.5% with the brentuximab vedotin–based regimen
and 68.2% with the standard regimen) ( Table 2 ).
Febrile neutropenia was reported in 30.9% of the patients who
received the brentuximab vedotin–based regimen and in 32.5% of those
who received the standard regimen, and sepsis occurred in 2.7%
and 4.2%, respectively. No pneumonitis was reported in the brentuximab
vedotin group. No deaths occurred during treatment.
