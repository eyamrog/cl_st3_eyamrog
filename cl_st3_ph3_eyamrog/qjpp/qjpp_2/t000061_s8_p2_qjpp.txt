Children in state care—also known as out-of-home care, public care,
being looked after, or substitute care—have been temporarily transferred to
alternative accommodation owing to problematic circumstances at home, the inability
of the parents to provide a safe environment, or their
own anti-social behaviour, among other reasons. In recent years, there
has been a rise in the prevalence of looked-after children
in several countries, such as the USA and the UK;
although estimates vary by country and ethnicity, the prevalence might
be as high as 13%.
