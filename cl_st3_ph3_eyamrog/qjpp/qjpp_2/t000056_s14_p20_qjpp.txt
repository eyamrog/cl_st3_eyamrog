A statistical analysis plan was finalised before the unblinding of
data for analysis ( appendix pp 2–9 ). There were
no interim analyses. In a post-hoc analysis, we excluded participants
who had been vaccinated from the outcome analyses, to assess
whether their inclusion biased the results. For all analyses, participants
were analysed in the groups they were allocated. Results were
conducted in R (version 4.0.0) and then validated by a
second statistician (L-MY) in Stata SE (version 16.1). This trial
was prospectively registered with ISRCTN, ISRCTN37254291.
