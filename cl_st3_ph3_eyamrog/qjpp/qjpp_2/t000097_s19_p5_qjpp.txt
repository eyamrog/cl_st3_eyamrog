Macrophage subsets were grouped in clusters 0, 1, 2, 3,
4, 6, and 8 ( Figures S2 A–S2D). Some clusters
were designated based on the expression of a characteristic marker
such as CX 3 CR1-Macs-g, Mrc1-Macs-g, Nos2-Macs-g, and Cycling-Macs-g (Mki67
+ ). Other clusters were simply identified as Macs-1-3-g, as
they expressed a constellation of markers rather than an archetypal
macrophage marker. All clusters were further labeled with a “g”
to indicate that they were identified in a comparison between
tumor-bearing mice with different genotypes (WT and Trem2 –/– ).
We noticed that CX 3 CR1-Macs-g, Mrc1-Macs-g, Cycling-Macs-g, and Macs3-g
were relatively meagerly represented in Trem2 –/– tumors, whereas the
Macs1-g cluster predominated ( Figures S2 B–S2D). Moreover, T cells
(cluster 9) and NK cells (cluster 5) were enriched in
Trem2 –/– mice ( Figures S2 B and S2C). This
initial evaluation established that lack of TREM2 impacts both myeloid
and lymphoid tumor infiltrates. Moreover, TREM2 was expressed in all
macrophage clusters, although at different levels, while it was absent
in DCs and lymphoid cells ( Figure S2 E).
