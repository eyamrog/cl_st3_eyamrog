We did ecological studies to assess the symptoms, disease course,
rates of reinfection, and transmissibility associated with increasing proportions of
infections with the B.1.1.7 variant in the UK population. We
used data from the COVID Symptom Study, a longitudinal dataset
providing symptom reports and test results from a population of
more than 4 million adults living in the UK, in
combination with surveillance data from the COVID-19 Genomics UK (COG-UK)
Consortium and a spike-gene target failure (SGTF) correlate in community
testing data.
