The expanded composite outcome of cardiovascular death, nonfatal myocardial infarction,
nonfatal stroke, transient ischemic attack, or major procedure-related complications occurred
in 50 patients (2.8%) in the CT group and in
80 patients (4.6%) in the ICA group (hazard ratio, 0.60;
95% CI, 0.42 to 0.85) (Fig. S4D). The incidences of
other definitions of major adverse cardiovascular events were similar in
the two groups (Fig. S4E, S4F, and S4G); the detection
of silent myocardial infarction in this pragmatic trial was uncommon.
