Our database search identified 210 published studies; 14 met our
inclusion criteria, two of which were unpublished ( figure 1
). There was a sufficiently large number of studies reporting
on the association between care and total mortality (11 published
reports and two unpublished datasets ) and the association between
care and suicide mortality (four published reports ) to facilitate
meta-analyses, whereas the frequency of the presentation of other outcomes,
such as cancer and vascular disease, was too low for
a meta-analysis to be viable.
