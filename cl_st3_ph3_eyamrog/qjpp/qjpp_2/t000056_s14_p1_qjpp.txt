This study was a single-blind, parallel-group, randomised controlled trial, with
planned mediation and moderation tests. The intervention and data collection
were carried out online. Participants completed an item for stratification
by vaccine hesitancy level, provided sociodemographic information, read the vaccine
information in their randomly allotted condition, and then completed measures
of COVID-19 vaccine hesitancy and COVID-19 complacency and confidence beliefs.
The study received ethical approval from the University of Oxford
Central University Research Ethics Committee (reference R74001/RE001).
