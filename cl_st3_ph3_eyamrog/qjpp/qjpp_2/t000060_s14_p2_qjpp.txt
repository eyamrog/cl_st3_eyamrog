Data collection occurred between August and February, with over 90%
of interviews in wave 20 (n=13 758) completed before the
end of October. Before wave 20, more than 90% of
interviews were done face-to-face. In wave 20, because of the
pandemic, the fieldwork shifted to telephone. Respondents also completed a
separate self-completion questionnaire (SCQ). Over the period covered by this
analysis, the return rate for the SCQ averaged 90%.
