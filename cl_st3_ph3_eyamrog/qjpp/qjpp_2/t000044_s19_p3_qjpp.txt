The risk of bias assessment for all studies included in
the systematic review is shown in the appendix (pp 2,
3) . In general, studies had a clear research objective,
recruited participants from similar populations, and clearly described dependent variables.
However, 17 (45%) of 38 studies did not provide a
clear definition of homelessness or marginal housing, and 23 (61%)
did not report whether the participation rate of eligible persons
was more than 50%. Of note, 20 (53%) of 38
studies did not clearly describe the specific definition used to
categorise participants as having TBI. Studies were generally comprised of
predominantly male samples, and eight studies were comprised of exclusively
male samples.
