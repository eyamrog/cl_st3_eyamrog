Results: Among 3561 patients (56.2% of whom were women), follow-up
was complete for 3523 (98.9%). Major adverse cardiovascular events occurred
in 38 of 1808 patients (2.1%) in the CT group
and in 52 of 1753 (3.0%) in the ICA group
(hazard ratio, 0.70; 95% confidence interval [CI], 0.46 to 1.07;
P=0.10). Major procedure-related complications occurred in 9 patients (0.5%) in
the CT group and in 33 (1.9%) in the ICA
group (hazard ratio, 0.26; 95% CI, 0.13 to 0.55). Angina
during the final 4 weeks of follow-up was reported in
8.8% of the patients in the CT group and in
7.5% of those in the ICA group (odds ratio, 1.17;
95% CI, 0.92 to 1.48).
