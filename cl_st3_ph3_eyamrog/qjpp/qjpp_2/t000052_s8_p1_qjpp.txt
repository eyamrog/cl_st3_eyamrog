Tuberculosis is responsible for an ongoing global public health crisis,
causing 1·5 million deaths in 2018. Modelling studies estimate that
24% of the world's population is infected with Mycobacterium tuberculosis
and thus might be at risk of developing the disease,
especially soon after infection. Important reductions in global tuberculosis rates
will require very substantial increases in the number of people
treated for latent tuberculosis infection (LTBI).
