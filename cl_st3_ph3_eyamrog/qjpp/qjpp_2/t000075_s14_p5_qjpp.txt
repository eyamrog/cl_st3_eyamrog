The primary end point was an overall response (partial response
or better), defined according to IMWG Uniform Response Criteria for
Multiple Myeloma as assessed by an independent review committee. The
key secondary end point was a complete response or better
(comprising complete and stringent complete responses). Additional secondary end points
included time to response and duration of response, progression-free and
overall survival, minimal residual disease (MRD), safety, pharmacokinetics, and immunogenicity.
