Using medical and mental health data from Kaiser, the researchers
found a high correlation between multiple ACEs and long-term medical
and behavioral disorders that occurred over a person's lifetime. In
the original study by Felitti et al. ( 23 ),
64% of participants (primarily middle income and above) reported one
ACE and 22% reported three or more. As the number
of ACEs increased, the likelihood of individuals developing various health
conditions also increased substantially, indicating a graded dose response.
