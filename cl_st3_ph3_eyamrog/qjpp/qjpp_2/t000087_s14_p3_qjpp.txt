Patients were randomly assigned in a 1:1 ratio to receive
five cycles of chemotherapy in one of the two trial
groups. Brentuximab vedotin (at a dose of 1.8 mg per
kilogram) plus doxorubicin, vincristine, etoposide, prednisone, and cyclophosphamide or the
standard bleomycin-containing chemotherapy regimen (Table S1 in the Supplementary Appendix
, available at NEJM.org) was administered every 21 days, with
granulocyte colony–stimulating factor support in both trial groups.
