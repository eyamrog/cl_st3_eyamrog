FXR1 rescues SG defects in G3BP KO cells ( Figures
6 A, 6B, and S6 A–S6C), implying that they can
act as G3BP-independent SG nodes. We hypothesized that, in each
of these cases, a self-associating domain would confer the requisite
valence for node identity ( v ≥3). Although previous studies
have indicated that such a domain (dimerization) exists for FXR1
