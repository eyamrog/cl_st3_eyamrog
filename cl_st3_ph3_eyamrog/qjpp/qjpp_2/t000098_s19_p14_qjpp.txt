Combined, our data suggest that, under basal conditions, cells are
able to buffer most of their cAMP. This, in turn,
allows PDEs to generate low-cAMP nanodomains. Only upon stimulation of
adenylyl cyclases (ACs) and inhibition of PDEs is the level
of cAMP raised sufficiently to overcome the capacity of the
endogenous buffers and to progressively “flood” the small domains and,
ultimately, entire cells.
