Melanoma is projected to remain the second most commonly diagnosed
cancer for males, followed by colorectal cancer and lung cancer.
For females, colorectal cancer is projected to remain the second
most commonly diagnosed cancer after breast cancer, followed by melanoma
and lung cancer ( appendix p 32 ). In 2040–44,
cancers of the prostate, lung, colorectum, and female breast and
melanoma are projected to account for 564 089 (54·0%) of
all 1 044 271 cancer cases (298 006 [53·1%] of
560 744 for males and 266 083 [55·0%] of 483
527 for females) in Australia ( appendix pp 27–28, 31
).
