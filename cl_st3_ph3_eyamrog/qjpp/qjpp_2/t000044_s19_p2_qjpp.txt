38 studies were included in the systematic review ( table
1 ). The included studies were published between 1995 and
2018, and recruited participants from Australia, Canada, Japan, South Korea,
the UK, and the USA. The predominant recruitment settings were
through homeless shelters or hostels (18 studies) and services or
clinics that serve homeless populations (16 studies). Six (16%) of
38 studies were conducted in populations of military service members
who were homeless or seeking services for homeless people, and
the remainder of studies recruited participants from civilian populations. Of
the 38 studies included in the systematic review, we included
22 in the meta-analysis ( figure 1 ). 18 (82%)
of 22 studies (n=9702) assessed the lifetime prevalence of TBI,
and nine (41%, n=5787) assessed the lifetime prevalence of moderate
or severe TBI.
