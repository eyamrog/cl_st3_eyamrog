development, cell type specification, and response to environmental inputs. Whereas
silent heterochromatin can be transmitted across cellular division, it has
been argued that activated chromatin states cannot ( Reinberg and

active marks such as histone acetylation do not have readers

suggest that active chromatin can be inherited, but by an
