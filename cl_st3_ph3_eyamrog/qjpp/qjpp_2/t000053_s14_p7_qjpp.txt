For studies that reported effects of active case-finding on tuberculosis
prevalence we extracted the size of intervention population, number of
people screened for tuberculosis during active case-finding, method of tuberculosis
screening, number of people in the prevalence survey or surveys,
definition of a tuberculosis case, and numbers of people with
tuberculosis disease. We reported summary measures of the effect of
active case-finding on tuberculosis prevalence and uncertainty intervals as reported
within the studies.
