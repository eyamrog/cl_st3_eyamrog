There were four specific secondary outcome questions: (1) whether emphasising
collective benefit is better (ie, leads to lower hesitancy) than
emphasising personal benefit; (2) whether it is better to address
why speed of development is not a problem either directly
or indirectly; (3) whether combining information about personal and collective
benefits is better than emphasising either personal or collective benefits
alone; and (4) whether combining information about collective and personal
benefits with the seriousness of the virus and indirectly why
the speed of development is not a problem is better
than just combining information about collective and personal benefits.
