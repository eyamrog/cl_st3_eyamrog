Strikingly, upon spatial separation of Epac1-camps and PDE4A1 by 10
nm (Epac1-camps-SAH10-PDE4A1), isoproterenol stimulation induced a FRET response of almost
the same amplitude as that seen with stoichiometric overexpression of
both proteins individually ( Figures 3 F and 3G). As
expected, inhibition of PDE4 activity in all constructs eliminated cAMP
