CT and ICA were performed at certified clinical centers (Section
S9). CT with at least 64-slice technology was performed according
to a 10-step guide and scanner-specific recommendations. CT scans were
interpreted by board-certified radiologists who had at least a level
2 qualification, according to the Society of Cardiovascular Computed Tomography
or similar certification, and at least one reader had level
3 certification for cardiac CT laboratory leadership. ICA was performed
according to contemporary guidelines by board-certified cardiologists.
