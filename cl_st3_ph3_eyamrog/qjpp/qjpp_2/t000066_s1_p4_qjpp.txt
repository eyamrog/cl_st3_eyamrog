Conclusions: Luspatercept reduced the severity of anemia in patients with
lower-risk myelodysplastic syndromes with ring sideroblasts who had been receiving
regular red-cell transfusions and who had disease that was refractory
to or unlikely to respond to erythropoiesis-stimulating agents or who
had discontinued such agents owing to an adverse event. (Funded
by Celgene and Acceleron Pharma; MEDALIST ClinicalTrials.gov number, NCT02631070 ;
EudraCT number, 2015-003454-41 .)
