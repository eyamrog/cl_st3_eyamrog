Third, the current study has identified an array of clusters

Previous studies were focusing on whether there was a tendency
for risk factors to aggregate, by comparing the rate of
populations having multiple risk factors with the product of each
risk factor's prevalence; here we obtain better insights on which
risk factors often coexist in the population and which risk
