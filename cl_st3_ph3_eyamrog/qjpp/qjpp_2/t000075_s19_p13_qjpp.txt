At baseline, 110 of 112 tumor samples (98%) expressed BCMA,
with 109 having at least 50% BCMA-positive plasma cells, and
all the patients had detectable levels of sBCMA. At disease
progression, 69 of 71 patients (97%) had rising sBCMA levels
(>40 ng per milliliter) consistent with progression of BCMA-expressing myeloma
(Table S17), and 15 of 16 evaluated patients who had
a response (94%) retained BCMA-expressing tumor cells in bone marrow.
Loss of tumor BCMA expression was suspected in 3 of
71 patients (4%) who could be evaluated at progression (Table
S18). In one of these patients, biallelic genomic loss of
BCMA on chromosome 16p was subsequently confirmed.
