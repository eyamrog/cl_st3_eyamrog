We conducted the DISCHARGE (Diagnostic Imaging Strategies for Patients with
Stable Chest Pain and Intermediate Risk of Coronary Artery Disease)
trial to compare CT with ICA as an initial diagnostic
imaging strategy for guiding the treatment of patients with stable
chest pain who were clinically referred for ICA. Here, we
report the comparative effectiveness of CT and ICA in preventing
the primary outcome of major adverse cardiovascular events, defined as
cardiovascular death, nonfatal myocardial infarction, or nonfatal stroke.
