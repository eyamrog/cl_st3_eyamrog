The DiD estimation showed a significant overall treatment effect of
the lockdown on MHI-5 scores of –1·4 points (95% CI
–1·7 to –1·2; table 2 ). The DiD model explained
two-thirds of the variation in MHI-5 scores (R 2 =0·67).
The treatment effect was significantly larger for females (−2·2 [–2·6
to –1·7]) than for males (−0·6 [–0·8 to –0·5]; difference
1·5 points, p<0·0001).
