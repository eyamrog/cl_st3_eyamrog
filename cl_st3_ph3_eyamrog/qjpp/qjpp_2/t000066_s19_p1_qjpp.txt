Overall, 229 patients were enrolled (from March 2016 through June
2017); 153 patients were randomly assigned to receive luspatercept, and
76 to receive placebo. The baseline characteristics of the patients
were well balanced between the groups ( Table 1 and
Table S3). The median age of the patients was 71
years (range, 26 to 95), and 63% of the patients
were men. With regard to the IPSS-R categories, 10%, 72%,
and 17% of the patients had a myelodysplastic syndrome defined
as being of very low risk, low risk, and intermediate
risk, respectively.
