Family homelessness has emerged as one of the most pressing
public health problems in the United States. Families and their
children now account for more than one-third of the overall
homeless population, with half of these families residing in shelters
( 31 ). The families are largely composed of single
mothers in their 20s with vulnerable young children, half of
whom are under the age of six ( 6 ,
10 , 30 ).
