To evaluate the role of necroptosis in IAV-triggered inflammatory responses
in vivo , we compared disease and survival outcomes in
Mlkl −/− mice to their wild-type counterparts, as well as
to Zbp1 −/− and Ripk3 −/− animals. At a modestly
lethal dose (EID 2500 , ∼LD 20 ) of IAV
(strain PR8), we found that Mlkl −/− mice were not
any more susceptible to lethality than wild-type (C57BL/6J) mice, whereas
both Zbp1 −/− and Ripk3 −/− mice displayed significantly increased
rates of mortality ( Figure 7 A). Because Zbp1 −/−
and Ripk3 −/− animals are deficient in both apoptosis and
necroptosis signaling, their increased susceptibility to IAV likely arises from
a failure to eliminate infected cells and limit virus spread
