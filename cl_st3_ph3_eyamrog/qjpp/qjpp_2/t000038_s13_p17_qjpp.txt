Several recent systematic reviews have highlighted interventions aimed at school-aged
refugee children ( 26 , 27 , 57 , 88
, 93 ). The majority of intervention research with this
age group has taken place in HICs, with a few
focused on refugee youth in transit or living in LMICs.
These interventions include individual psychotherapy, family-oriented programs, school-based work, and
multimodal/multilevel approaches.
