We also carried out analyses according to age at entry
to the care system. In comparison with the no-care group,
the magnitude of the association with death was lowest among
adults who, as children, had been placed in care in
early childhood (1·75 [1·25–2·45]) relative to those placed in care
in middle (2·75 [1·52–4·96]) or late childhood (2·47 [0·98–6·52]). Study-specific
results according to each of the 11 characteristics depicted in
figure 3 are presented in the appendix (pp 3–13) .
