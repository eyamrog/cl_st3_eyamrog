One patient in each group had progression to higher-risk myelodysplastic
syndromes, and acute myeloid leukemia developed in 4 patients (3
patients [2%] receiving luspatercept and 1 [1%] receiving placebo). A
total of 21 patients died during the trial: 12 patients
(8%) in the luspatercept group and 9 (12%) in the
placebo group (data not shown). Overall, 5 patients (3%) receiving
luspatercept and 4 (5%) receiving placebo died owing to adverse
events during the double-blind period or within 6 weeks thereafter;
investigators judged the deaths to be unrelated to luspatercept or
placebo.
