FAM-labeled RNAs (1 μM) and antibodies (10 μM) were incubated
in EMSA binding buffer (10 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.0, 100
mM NaCl, 5 mM dithiothreitol, 10 μg/mL BSA, and 10%
(v/v) glycerol) for 1 hr at room temperature. Samples were
then separated by non-denaturing 4% polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis in 1xTBE
(Tris/borate/EDTA) buffer containing 20 mM NaCl at 80 V and
4°C for 2 hr. The gel was imaged on a
phosphorimager (LAS-3000, Fujifilm).
