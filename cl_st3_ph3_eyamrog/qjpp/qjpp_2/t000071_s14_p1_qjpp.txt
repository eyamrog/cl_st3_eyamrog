We conducted a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial to evaluate postexposure
prophylaxis with hydroxychloroquine after exposure to Covid-19. We randomly assigned
participants in a 1:1 ratio to receive either hydroxychloroquine or
placebo. Participants had known exposure (by participant report) to a
person with laboratory-confirmed Covid-19, whether as a household contact, a
health care worker, or a person with other occupational exposures.
