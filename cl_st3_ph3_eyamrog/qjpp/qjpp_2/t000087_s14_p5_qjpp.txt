Children and adolescents 2 to 21 years of age with
newly diagnosed classic Hodgkin’s lymphoma of Ann Arbor stage IIB
with bulk tumor or stage IIIB, IVA, or IVB were
eligible for enrollment. Bulk tumor was defined as either a
large mediastinal mass (see above) or an extramediastinal mass (continuous
nodal aggregate, >6 cm). Patients were ineligible if they had
histologic findings indicating nodular lymphocyte–predominant disease, were pregnant, had a
known immunodeficiency, or had received antineoplastic therapy within the 28
days before enrollment.
