We next explored The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA) database for
correlations between TREM2 expression and clinical outcome in tumor types
related to our experimental models. With a threshold of 75%
quantile, TREM2 expression correlated with worse overall survival in the
CRC cohort ( Figure 7 C). In the TNBC cohort,
TREM2 expression correlated with worse overall and relapse-free survival using
the 75% quantile as cutoff ( Figure 7 D). Similar
results were obtained using the TREM2 median expression. Analysis of
the TCGA database also revealed that TREM2 expression correlated with
that of myeloid signature genes in both CRC and TNBC
cohorts. In CRC patients, the top genes correlating with TREM2
included the scavenger receptor MSR1, the Fc receptor FCGR1A, the
TREM2 adaptor protein TYROBP, the exhaustion marker HAVCR2, and several
lipid metabolism genes (APOC1, APOE, and OLR1) previously associated with
lipid-associated macrophage signature ( Figure S7 A) ( Biswas and
