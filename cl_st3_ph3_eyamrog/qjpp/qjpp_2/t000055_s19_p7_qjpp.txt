On Dec 19, 2020, London and much of the South
East and East of England were placed under Tier 4
restrictions, enforcing stricter rules for physical distancing and decreased human-to-human
contact that stopped short of nationwide measures. On Jan 5,
2021, the whole of England was placed in national lockdown.
In January, the proportion of infections with the B.1.1.7 variant
in London, the South East, and the East of England
(these three regions had the largest proportion of infections with
the B.1.1.7 variant in England) was at least 80%, assuming
the proportion had not decreased from the end of December.
R t fell to around 0·8 in all three of
these regions during the national lockdown ( figure 5 ).
