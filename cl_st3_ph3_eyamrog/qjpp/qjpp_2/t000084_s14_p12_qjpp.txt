In the total population and in subgroups, we calculated odds
ratios to indicate effect sizes for patient-reported angina and hazard
ratios to indicate effect sizes for time-to-event data. Sample-size estimation
was performed with the use of nQuery, version 7.0, and
the R package gsDesign for the group sequential design. Statistical
analyses were performed with the use of SAS software, version
9.4 (SAS Institute); SPSS for Windows, version 26 (IBM); and
the statistical programming language R, version 4.0.3.
