For many years, although advocates have urged the federal government
to adopt the HEARTH definition across all agencies and services,
HUD applies its definition of homelessness to conduct its yearly
PIT count of who is homeless. This count is conducted
on a single night in January by staff and volunteers
from HUD's regional Continuum of Care (CoC) programs located in
cities and counties nationally. Using the HUD definition, the PIT
count excludes families who are precariously housed or doubled-up as
well as regions where there are no CoCs.
