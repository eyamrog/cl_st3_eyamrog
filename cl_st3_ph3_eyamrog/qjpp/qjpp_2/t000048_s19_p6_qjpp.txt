Analyses looking at annual average ambient temperature, altitude, and per
capita GDP also showed differences between regions ( figure 3
; appendix 2 p 21 ). Higher per capita GDP
was associated with lower risk in non-staple food factor in
urban areas and higher risk in metabolic and physical activities
factor in rural areas (p<0·05 for both). Notably, the correlation
between per capita GDP and the smoking and alcohol use
factor differed significantly between rural and urban regions (p=0·042; figure
3 ).
