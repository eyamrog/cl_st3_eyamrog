During weeks 1 through 24, an erythroid response occurred in
81 patients (53%) in the luspatercept group, as compared with
9 (12%) in the placebo group. During weeks 1 through
48, a total of 90 patients (59%) in the luspatercept
group had an erythroid response, as compared with 13 (17%)
in the placebo group ( Table 2 ). No significant
changes in neutrophil or platelet counts were observed during the
