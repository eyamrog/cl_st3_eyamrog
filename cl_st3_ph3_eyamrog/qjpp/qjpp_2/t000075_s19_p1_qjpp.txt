Between December 13, 2017, and November 13, 2018, a total
of 140 patients were enrolled and underwent leukapheresis, of whom
128 received ide-cel infusions. Twelve patients discontinued the study before
ide-cel infusion (including 1 patient who discontinued after unsuccessful manufacture
of the CAR T-cell product) (Fig. S1). At the time
of data cutoff for this report (January 14, 2020), 62
patients remained in the primary study. Among the 128 treated
patients, the median age was 61 years (range, 33 to
78), and the median time since diagnosis was 6 years
(range, 1 to 18) ( Table 1 ). A total
of 65 patients (51%) had a high tumor burden (≥50%
bone marrow plasma cells), 50 (39%) had extramedullary disease, 21
(16%) had stage III disease at screening according to the
revised International Staging System, and 45 (35%) had a high-risk
cytogenetic abnormality, defined as del(17p), t(4;14), or t(14;16).
