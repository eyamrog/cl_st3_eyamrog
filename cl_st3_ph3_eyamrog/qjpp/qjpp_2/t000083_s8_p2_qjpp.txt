Although the vaccines that are available under EUA in the
United States provide high levels of protection against severe illness
and death, the enhanced transmission of the B.1.617.2 (delta) variant
starting in the spring of 2021 resulted in increasing numbers
of breakthrough infections in fully vaccinated persons. The delta wave
was quickly followed by the B.1.1.529 (omicron) wave in November
2021. The detection of such infections coincided with evidence of
waning immunity.
