As part of our primary analysis, we also investigated whether
the effect of information provision on COVID-19 vaccine hesitancy was
moderated by the three levels of hesitancy groupings (ie, willing
to be vaccinated, doubtful, strongly hesitant). We were most interested
in the effects on those in the general population who
were doubtful (approximately 17% in OCEANS-II) or strongly hesitant (approximately
12%) about a COVID-19 vaccination.
