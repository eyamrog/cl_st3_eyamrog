Contemporary drug-eluting stents are the standard of care for patients
undergoing percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI). Nearly one third of patients
treated with PCI are considered to be at high bleeding
risk and are frequently excluded from stent trials. Short durations
of dual antiplatelet therapy were used during the bare-metal stent
era and have been evaluated in a few trials comparing
bare-metal stents with drug-eluting stents in patients at high bleeding
risk.
