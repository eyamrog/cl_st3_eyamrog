Public transportation has been associated with a lower risk of
traffic incidents, compared with other modes of transportation ( 41
, 66 , 67 ). Public transportation has also been
suggested as a promoter of physical activity, owing to the
inclusion of walking trips to and from public transport stations
( 63 , 66 , 70 , 90 ). Furthermore,
if public transportation substituted car trips, the shift could lead
to a reduction in air pollution emissions, specifically if the
public transportation fleet relies on electricity (e.g., light transit rail),
hybrid energy, or natural gas (buses) ( 7 , 33
, 67 ).
