Between October 3, 2015, and April 12, 2019, a total
of 3667 patients were enrolled at 26 centers (Table S1).
Of these patients, 1833 were randomly assigned to the CT
group and 1834 to the ICA group. A total of
3561 patients were included in the modified intention-to-treat analysis; adherence
to the randomized assignments was 98.6% in the CT group
and 97.3% in the ICA group (Fig. S1). The median
follow-up was 3.5 years (interquartile range, 2.9 to 4.2), and
complete follow-up for the primary outcome was obtained for 3523
patients (98.9%).
