When evaluated according to the baseline transfusion burden, transfusion independence
for 8 weeks or longer in the luspatercept group occurred
in 80% of the patients (37 of 46) who had
been receiving less than 4 units per 8 weeks, in
37% of those (15 of 41) who had been receiving
4 to less than 6 units per 8 weeks, and
in 9% of those (6 of 66) who had been
receiving at least 6 units per 8 weeks; the corresponding
values in the placebo group were 40% (8 of 20
patients), 4% (1 of 23), and 3% (1 of 33).
Among patients with a baseline serum erythropoietin level of 200
to 500 U per liter, 40% of patients in the
luspatercept group had a response (Fig. S3).
