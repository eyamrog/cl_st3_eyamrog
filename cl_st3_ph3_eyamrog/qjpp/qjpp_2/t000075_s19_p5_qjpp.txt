The Kaplan–Meier estimated median progression-free survival was 8.8 months overall
(95% CI, 5.6 to 11.6), 12.1 months (95% CI, 8.8
to 12.3) at the 450×10 dose, and 20.2 months (95%
CI, 12.3 to could not be estimated) in patients having
a complete or stringent complete response ( Figure 2C and
Fig. S3). The Kaplan–Meier estimated median overall survival was 19.4
months (95% CI, 18.2 to could not be estimated), with
an overall survival of 78% at 12 months ( Figure
2D ). Data on overall survival are immature, with data
for 84 patients (66%) censored, including 39 of 54 patients
(72%) at the 450×10 dose.
