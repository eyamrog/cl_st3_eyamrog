A treatment effect was also evident in models examining the
binary measure of probable mental disorder. During the lockdown, the
prevalence of poor mental health increased by 2·6 percentage points
( table 2 ). This effect, however, was driven by
females, with the percentage in poor mental health increasing by
4·3 percentage points (95% CI 3·0 to 5·5). Among males,
the effect was small and non-significant (0·7 percentage points [–0·8
to 2·3]).
