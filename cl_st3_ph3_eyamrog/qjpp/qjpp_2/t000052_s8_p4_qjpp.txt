To maximise the impact on tuberculosis prevention, LTBI programmes in
low-income and middle-income countries (LMICs) must be strengthened so that
individuals will at least reach the point of treatment initiation.
The aim of this cluster-randomised trial was to test the
effectiveness and cost-effectiveness of a health systems strengthening intervention, delivered
at the level of the health facility, to increase initiation
of tuberculosis preventive treatment among household contacts.
