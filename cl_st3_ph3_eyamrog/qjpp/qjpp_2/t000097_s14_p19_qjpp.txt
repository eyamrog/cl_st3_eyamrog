The phenotype dataset (with survival outcomes) and the RNAseq dataset
of each cancer cohort (the gene-level transcription estimates, as in
log 2 (x+1) transformed RSEM normalized count) were downloaded from
UCSC Xena ( https://xenabrowser.net/ ) where genes are mapped onto
the human genome coordinates using UCSC Xena HUGO probeMap in
the RNAseq dataset. Spearman correlation coefficient was calculated between TREM2
and each of the genes in each cancer cohort, accompanied
with sample size and p values. The individual scatterplot of
each of the top genes with TREM2 (with fitted linear
lines) was generated with Spearman correlation in the plot.
