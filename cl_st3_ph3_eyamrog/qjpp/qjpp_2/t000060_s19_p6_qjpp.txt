The estimates of mean MHI-5 scores were found to be
insensitive to considering wider treatment windows allowing for a more
persistent lockdown effect ( appendix p 7 ), to excluding
the 253 individuals from Victoria who were interviewed in wave
20 outside the lockdown period ( appendix p 8 ),
or to omitting the 81 persons reporting having tested positive
for COVID-19 ( appendix p 8 ). Allowing for state-specific
year fixed effects ( appendix p 8 ) resulted in
a similar, but slightly larger, negative treatment effect (−2·2 points
[–2·3 to –2·1]).
