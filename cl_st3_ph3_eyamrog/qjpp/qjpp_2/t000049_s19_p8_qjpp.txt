The mean 13-year change in memory scores was –0·76 SDs
(95% CI –0·85 to –0·71) in men and –0·69 SDs
(–0·75 to –0·63) in women (p=0·0001; adjusted for age, ethnicity,
birth cohort, education, and practice effect). 13-year memory decline was
slower in women than in men (sex difference in the
1939–45 birth cohort –0·12 SDs [–0·18 to –0·05] and in
the 1946–55 birth cohort –0·15 SDs [–0·20 to –0·09], with
no difference in the 1930–38 birth cohort; table 3 ).
Adjustment for education did not affect these estimates.
