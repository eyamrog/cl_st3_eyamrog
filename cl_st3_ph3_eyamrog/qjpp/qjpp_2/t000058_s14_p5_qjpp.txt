The number of deaths in each age group, MSOA, and
year is small, which means that death rates calculated from
observed data have an apparent variability from year to year,
or from MSOA to MSOA, which is larger than the
true differences in the risk of death. We used a
Bayesian hierarchical model to obtain stable estimates of death rates
by sharing information across age groups, MSOAs, and years. We
conducted all analyses separately by female and male sexes because
mortality and trends differ by sex.
