We used individual participant data from two sources in the
UK: the 1958 birth cohort study (also known as the
National Child Development Study) and the 1970 birth cohort study.
Described in detail elsewhere, these are well established, ongoing, closed,
geographically representative, prospective birth cohort studies, in which the investigators
sampled all livebirths occurring during a single week in 1958
(17 634 babies) and 1970 (17 287) in the contiguous
countries of the UK. Data collection was approved by the
National Health Service Research Ethics Committee for the 1958 study,
and the London Central Research Ethics Committee for the 1970
study. With the present data being anonymised, permissions for analyses
were not required.
