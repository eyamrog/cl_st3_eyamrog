Methods: We evaluated metabolic regulators of glucose homeostasis before and
after matched (approximately 18%) weight loss induced by gastric bypass
(surgery group) or diet alone (diet group) in 22 patients
with obesity and diabetes. The primary outcome was the change
in hepatic insulin sensitivity, assessed by infusion of insulin at
low rates (stages 1 and 2 of a 3-stage hyperinsulinemic
euglycemic pancreatic clamp). Secondary outcomes were changes in muscle insulin
sensitivity, beta-cell function, and 24-hour plasma glucose and insulin profiles.
