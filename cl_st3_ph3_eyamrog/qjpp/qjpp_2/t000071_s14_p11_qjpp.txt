Because the estimates for both incident symptomatic Covid-19 after an
exposure and loss to follow-up were relatively unknown in early
March 2020, the protocol prespecified a sample-size reestimation at the
second interim analysis. This reestimation, which used the incidence of
new infections in the placebo group and the observed percentage
of participants lost to follow-up, was aimed at maintaining the
ability to detect an effect size of a 50% relative
reduction in new symptomatic infections.
