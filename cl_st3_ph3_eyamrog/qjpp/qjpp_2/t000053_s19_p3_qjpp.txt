Of the 28 studies, five were done in general populations,
seven were done in high-density, low-income urban areas, two were
done in camps for internally displaced people, four were done
in remote rural populations, four were done among indigenous populations
(two of which were also in high-density, low-income urban areas),
four were done in prisons, one was done in gold
mines, and two were done among people experiencing homelessness.
