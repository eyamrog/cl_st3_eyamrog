At a median follow-up of 42.1 months (range, 0.1 to
80.9), the 3-year event-free survival in the intention-to-treat analysis was
92.1% (95% CI, 88.4 to 94.7) in the brentuximab vedotin
group and 82.5% (95% CI, 77.4 to 86.5) in the
standard-care group ( Figure 1A ), indicating that the risk
of an event or death was 9.6 percentage points lower
in the brentuximab vedotin group than in the standard-care group
(P<0.001 by a two-sided log-rank test). The assumptions of a
Cox proportional-hazards model were assessed and considered to be adequate.
The hazard ratio for an event or death was 0.41
(95% CI, 0.25 to 0.67) in favor of therapy with
brentuximab vedotin.
