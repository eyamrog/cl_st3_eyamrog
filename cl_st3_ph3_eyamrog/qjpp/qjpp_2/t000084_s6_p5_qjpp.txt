Freedom from angina and improvement in quality of life are
key objectives in the treatment of patients with stable chest
pain. An initial invasive management strategy, as compared with a
conservative strategy, had similar event rates but larger reductions in
angina symptoms and improvements in quality of life, which persisted
for 2 years in the COURAGE trial and 3 years
in the ISCHEMIA trial. In our trial, we did not
find evidence of differences in angina relief and quality of
life between the CT strategy and the ICA strategy.
