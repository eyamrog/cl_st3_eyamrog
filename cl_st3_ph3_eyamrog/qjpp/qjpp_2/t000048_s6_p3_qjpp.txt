Second, the current study took a step forward to elucidate
the geographical discrepancies in major risk factors contributing to cardiovascular
disease. A study on 195 countries, published in 2019, indicated
that China had the highest age-standardised rates of deaths due
to diet-related cardiovascular disease. We further reported that the unhealthy
dietary habits were associated with such risk differently according to
regions of China. For example, northeast China had the highest
non-staple food factor but the lowest staple food factor in
the country.
