Our findings have relevant implications for tuberculosis prevention globally as
programmes aim to address the UN targets. For LTBI scale-up
to be successful, an in-depth understanding of the local health
system facilitators and barriers, as well as key motivators for
health-care workers and health managers is essential. Rather than a
one-size-fits-all approach understanding and resolving local barriers with ongoing support
and training is needed for LTBI programmes to expand successfully.
