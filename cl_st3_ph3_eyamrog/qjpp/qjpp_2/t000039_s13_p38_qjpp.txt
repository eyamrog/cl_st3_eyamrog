Maternal, Infant, and Early Childhood Home Visiting (MIECHV), supported by
the Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA) in conjunction with
the Administration of Children and Families (ACF), is an evidence-based
approach that builds parenting skills, strengthens relationships between parents and
children, and promotes healthy childhood development ( 74 ). Currently,
18 models are approved as MIECHV programs ( 74 ).
While home visiting has not been widely implemented in shelter
settings, the approach is beginning to reach some of the
most vulnerable families.
