Analysis by age revealed that the effects of lockdown on
MHI-5 scores were largest in women aged 20–29 years (−3·7
points [–4·5 to –2·9]) and 30–54 years (−3·0 points [–4·0
to –2·0]; figure 2A ; appendix p 9 ). No
significant lockdown effects were observed for adolescents (aged 15–19 years)
of either gender, or for young men (aged 20–29 years).
Older males (ie, those aged ≥70 years) were the only
group that showed evidence of significantly better mental health during
lockdown (1·5 points [0·9 to 2·2]).
