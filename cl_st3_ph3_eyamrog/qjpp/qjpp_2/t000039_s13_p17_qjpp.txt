Even in the short term, young children with high ACE
scores (e.g., 4 or greater) are more likely to be
anxious and depressed; have specialized health needs as well as
cognitive, socioemotional, and behavioral health issues such as attention-deficit hyperactivity
disorder; and experience developmental delays ( 14 , 37 ,
44 ). Even young people who never engage in high-risk
behaviors but have high ACE scores are more likely to
develop adverse outcomes.
