In conclusion, although the effects of lockdowns on overall population
mental health were small, there were substantial and clinically relevant
effects for some groups. The adverse mental health effects were
largely seen in women with dependent children, who are likely
to have borne the burden of the additional workload associated
with working from home, as well as caring for and
educating children. As such, the lockdown exaggerated existing inequalities in
the responsibility for household and caring duties. These mental health
effects should be accounted for when evaluating the merits and
costs of the Australian COVID-19 policy approach.
