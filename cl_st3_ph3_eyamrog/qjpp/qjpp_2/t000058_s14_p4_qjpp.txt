The above measures are the three largest contributors to the
Index of Multiple Deprivation, excluding a domain on health that
also uses mortality data. We used data for these measures
for 2004, as data for 2002 were not available, and
2019 to analyse how estimated (posterior) life expectancy in 2002
and 2019 was associated with socioeconomic deprivation at the beginning
and end of the analysis period. We used the rank
of each socioeconomic measure in each year (with values from
one to 6791) because their absolute values are not comparable
in different years. We did not stratify by ethnicity because
data are only available for the 2011 census year. We
used classification of MSOAs into rural or urban from the
Office for National Statistics.
