AVs could have either positive or negative impacts on social
interactions. On the positive side, AVs could facilitate increased access
to venues for social interactions and social support that help
promote good mental health ( 73 ), and shared AVs
could offer opportunities for social interactions among passengers during rides.
On the negative side, AVs could increase commuting time (if
commuting by AV is seen as more painless than driving,
and people, therefore, choose to live farther from work), and
longer commute times are associated with reduced community involvement, reduced
time with friends and family, and reduced levels of social
capital ( 8 , 13 , 46 ).
