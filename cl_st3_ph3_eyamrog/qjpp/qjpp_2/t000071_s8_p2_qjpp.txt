Both chloroquine and the derivative molecule hydroxychloroquine have in vitro
activity against SARS-CoV and SARS-CoV-2. Hydroxychloroquine is thought to impair
the terminal glycosylation of the angiotensin-converting–enzyme 2 (ACE2) receptor, which
is the binding site for the envelope spike glycoprotein and
has been shown to inhibit endolysosome function. In addition, hydroxychloroquine
may have greater in vitro activity against SARS-CoV-2 than chloroquine.
