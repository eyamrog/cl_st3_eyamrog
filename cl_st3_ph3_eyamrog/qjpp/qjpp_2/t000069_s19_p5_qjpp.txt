A multiplexed analysis of inflammation-related gene expression examining 249 genes
from the nCounter Inflammation Panel (NanoString Technologies) revealed similarities and
differences between the specimens in the Covid-19 group and those
in the influenza group. A total of 79 inflammation-related genes
were differentially regulated only in specimens from patients with Covid-19,
whereas 2 genes were differentially regulated only in specimens from
patients with influenza; a shared expression pattern was found for
7 genes (Fig. S1).
