Since 2018, China implemented a strict national regulation policy to
protect youth from e-cigarette use, and banned online selling of
e-cigarettes on Nov 1, 2019. Cities in mainland China—for instance,
Shenzhen and Chengdu—have also revised tobacco control regulations to include
e-cigarettes. However, knowledge remains limited regarding the characteristics of e-cigarette
users and trend in e-cigarette use in China before the
regulations were implemented.
