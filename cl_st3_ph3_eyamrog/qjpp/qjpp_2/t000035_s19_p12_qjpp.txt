Informal care was associated with better health outcomes for cholesterol
control ( 56 ) and unmet needs ( 12 )
compared with formal care. Formal care was associated with better
outcomes for unmet needs ( 74 ) and adverse consequences
due to unmet needs ( 43 ) compared with informal
care. Potter ( 90 ) found that within informal care,
nonspousal caregiving was associated with more unmet needs than spousal
caregiving or formal caregiving.
