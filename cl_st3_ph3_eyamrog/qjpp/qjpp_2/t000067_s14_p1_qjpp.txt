We evaluated the association between suPAR levels and postprocedural acute
kidney injury in two prospective cohorts of patients undergoing coronary
angiography for suspected coronary artery disease: the Emory Cardiovascular Biobank
(EmCAB) and the Catheter Sampled Blood Archive in Cardiovascular Diseases
(CASABLANCA). To determine whether suPAR was associated with acute kidney
injury unrelated to the use of contrast material we sought
to replicate our findings in patients at high risk for
acute kidney injury who were undergoing cardiac surgery and in
critically ill patients who had been admitted to the intensive
care unit (ICU).
