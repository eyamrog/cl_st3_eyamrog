Conclusions: Ide-cel induced responses in a majority of heavily pretreated
patients with refractory and relapsed myeloma; MRD-negative status was achieved
in 26% of treated patients. Almost all patients had grade
3 or 4 toxic effects, most commonly hematologic toxic effects
and cytokine release syndrome. (Funded by bluebird bio and Celgene,
a Bristol-Myers Squibb company; KarMMa ClinicalTrials.gov number, NCT03361748 .)
