Men had higher fluency scores than women (mean difference 0·07
SDs [95% CI 0·00 to 0·13] at age 50 years,
0·07 SDs [0·04 to 0·11] at 60 years, and 0·06
SDs [0·03 to 0·09] at 70 years) in analyses accounting
for age, ethnicity, birth cohort, practice effect, and practice effect
by sex interaction. This difference was attenuated after adjustment for
education (–0·04 SDs [–0·11 to 0·02] at age 50 years,
–0·04 SDs [–0·07 to –0·01] at 60 years, and –0·04
SDs [–0·07 to –0·01] at 70 years).
