Background: Long-term projections of cancer incidence and mortality estimate the
future burden of cancer in a population, and can be
of great use in informing the planning of health services
and the management of resources. We aimed to estimate incidence
and mortality rates and numbers of new cases and deaths
up until 2044 for all cancers combined and for 21
individual cancer types in Australia. We also illustrate the potential
effect of treatment delays due to the COVID-19 pandemic on
future colorectal cancer mortality rates.
