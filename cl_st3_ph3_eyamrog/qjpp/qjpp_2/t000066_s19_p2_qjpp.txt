The baseline serum erythropoietin levels were less than 100 U
per liter, 100 to less than 200 U per liter,
200 to 500 U per liter, and more than 500
U per liter in 36%, 24%, 25%, and 14% of
the patients, respectively. The baseline transfusion burden was at least
6 units per 8 weeks in 43% of the patients
and less than 6 units per 8 weeks in 57%;
29% had a baseline burden of less than 4 units
per 8 weeks. SF3B1 mutations were detected in 93% of
the patients in the luspatercept group (138 of 148 patients)
and in 86% of those in the placebo group (64
of 74) (assessed on the basis of mutation profiles from
bone marrow mononuclear cells obtained at baseline). Somatic mutations were
distributed similarly in the two groups (Fig. S2 and Table
S4).
