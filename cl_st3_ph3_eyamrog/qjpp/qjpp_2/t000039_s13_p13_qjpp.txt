At the same time, homeless children are not a homogeneous
group. A subgroup of children demonstrates resilience in the face
of adversity and weathers the vicissitudes of growing up in
chaotic circumstances remarkably well. Huntington et al. ( 38 )
conducted a cluster analysis and found that some children function
at a high level despite the adversities they face; however,
this number dwindles as children get older and the stresses
accumulate, indicating the necessity of prevention and early intervention.
