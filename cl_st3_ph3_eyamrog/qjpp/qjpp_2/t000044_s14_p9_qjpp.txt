Heterogeneity between studies was quantified with the I 2 statistic.
For studies that did not report age of the sample,
and for which these data could not be obtained from
the corresponding author (n=2), we imputed the weighted mean age
of participants from all other studies that were included in
the analysis. We conducted sensitivity analyses using the leave-one-out method.
We evaluated small-study effects visually with a funnel plot and
statistically with Egger's test.
