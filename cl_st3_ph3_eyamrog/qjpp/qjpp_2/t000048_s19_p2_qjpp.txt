Among all participants, 16·6% (95% CI 16·6–16·7) had a high
risk of cardiovascular disease, ranging from 2·8% to 34·2% across
the counties or districts included (data not shown). After standardising
age and sex using the 2010 national census data, the
overall rate of high risk was 10·3% (10·2–10·3), with a
range of 3·1–24·9% among the counties or districts ( figure
1 ). The median odds ratio of the rates of
high cardiovascular disease risk at the county level or district
level was 1·52 (95% CI 1·46–1·58).
