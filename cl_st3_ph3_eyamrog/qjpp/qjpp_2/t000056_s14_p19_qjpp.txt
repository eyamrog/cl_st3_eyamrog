Mediation analyses were carried out using structural equation modelling. The
factor scores of the Oxford COVID-19 Vaccine Hesitancy Scale were
the response variable with randomised group as the exposure and
a higher-order latent variable (beliefs) derived from the four subscales
of the Oxford Vaccine Confidence and Complacency Scale as the
mediator variable. Age, gender, ethnicity, income, region, and level of
COVID-19 health risk were included in the model as controls.
We employed the Monte Carlo method for a formal inferential
test of the effects in the mediation model.
