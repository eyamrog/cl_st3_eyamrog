Plasma specimens were obtained for ctDNA analysis from all patients
at week 4 and week 7 after surgery. Patients underwent
randomization after confirmation of adequate tumor tissue by central pathological
review and confirmation of an adequate week 4 blood specimen.
For patients assigned to ctDNA-guided management, week 4 and week
7 specimens were analyzed concurrently, and ctDNA results were made
available to the treating clinician 8 to 10 weeks after
surgery. Patients with a positive ctDNA result at either week
4 or week 7 received adjuvant single-agent fluoropyrimidine or oxaliplatin-based
chemotherapy, with the treatment regimen chosen at the clinician’s discretion.
Patients with negative ctDNA results at both week 4 and
week 7 were not treated with adjuvant chemotherapy.
