Infected lung and brain tissues from euthanised mice were homogenized
with DMEM, and the supernatant was used for virus isolation.
A total of 200 μL supernatant was inoculated onto monolayer
Vero E6 cells in a 24-well plate. After 1 h
inoculation, the supernatant was removed and replaced with fresh DMEM
containing 2% FBS plus 1% Anti-Anti. CPE was monitored daily,
and the supernatant was clarified from debris and subjected to
viral RNA extraction after the onset of CPE. Viral RNA
copies were quantified by qPCR, and viral antigens were detected
using IFA. Images were collected by EVOS M5000 (Thermo). The
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed by using Illumina MiSeq 3000
sequencers and the sequences were analyzed by Geneious ®10.2.6 for
