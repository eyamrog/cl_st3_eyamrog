The observed and predicted incidence rates and numbers of new
cases for 21 selected cancer types plus other cancers as
a group are presented in figure 2 and the appendix
(pp 23–24, 27–28) . It is expected that prostate cancer
and female breast cancer will remain the most commonly diagnosed
cancers, with prostate cancer projected to account for 133 917
(23·9%) of 560 744 new cases for males and breast
cancer projected to account for 137 776 (28·5%) of 483
527 new cases for females in 2040–44 ( appendix pp
23–24, 32 ).
