EmCAB and CASABLANCA are prospective observational cohorts consisting of adult
patients (≥18 years of age) undergoing coronary angiography for suspected
ischemic heart disease. EmCAB enrolled patients at three Emory Healthcare
sites in Atlanta between 2003 and 2015, and CASABLANCA enrolled
patients at Massachusetts General Hospital in Boston between 2008 and
2011. EmCAB excluded patients with congenital heart disease, severe anemia,
a recent blood transfusion, myocarditis, or a history of active
inflammatory disease or cancer. The only exclusion criterion in CASABLANCA
was an unwillingness to participate.
