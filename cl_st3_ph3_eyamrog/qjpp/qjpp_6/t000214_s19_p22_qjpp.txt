Male 17E2’s neonatal line was preserved in his M 1
and C 1 , allowing calibration to his date of
birth. Although 17E2 had an open canine root when he
died (aged 8.34 years), the enamel was poorly preserved, and
we could not assess the lines in this root. His
accentuated line sequence therefore covers the period from birth until
the last increments measurable in his M 3 root at
age 6.58 years. This is 79% of his life span,
during which 31 accentuated lines formed ( Fig. 6 ;
SOM Table S3 ). However, his dental histology is unreliable
from age 4.74 years to the end of his life,
and we may have missed accentuated lines that formed during
