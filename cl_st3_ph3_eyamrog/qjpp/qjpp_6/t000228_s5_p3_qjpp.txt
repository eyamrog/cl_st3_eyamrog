Origin and taphonomy of plant sedaDNA and pollen Pollen and
sedaDNA records are differentially affected by source productivity and taphonomic
processes of dispersal, transfer, and deposition ( Niemeyer et al.,
2017 ; Alsos et al., 2018 ; Giguet-Covex et al.,
2019 ), and both proxies signify different records. SedaDNA is
generally considered of local origin ( Jørgensen et al., 2012
; Alsos et al., 2018 ), whereas pollen (especially from
wind-pollinated plants, such as many trees and grasses) may originate
from a wide area as it is distributed regionally through
the air ( Birks and Bjune, 2010 ; Parducci et
al., 2018 ). Moreover, plant DNA in sediments can originate
from most plant parts, but is unlikely to source from
pollen due to the robustness of the pollen grain wall
and the low amount of DNA it contains ( Parducci
et al., 2017 ; Sjögren et al., 2017 ; Clarke
et al., 2020 ). Furthermore, the primers used in this
study are unsuitable for analyzing DNA in pollen grains as
they target chloroplast DNA , which is especially low or
absent in pollen ( Zhang et al., 2003 ; Ellis
et al., 2008 ).
