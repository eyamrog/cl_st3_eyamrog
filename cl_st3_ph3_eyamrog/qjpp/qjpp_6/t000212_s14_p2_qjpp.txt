The lithic material presented in this article was collected from
the two main stratigraphic units (hereafter: SU) at the base
of the sequence, SU1 and SU2, which are securely documented
in terms of geomorphology . A total of 1136 lithics
was considered, of which 6.5% result from the 2016 test
excavations, and 93.5% was collected during the extended excavations led
in 2017. The assemblage originates from two adjoining squares: C3
and C4. These two squares were excavated in the slope
down to the sterile basal layer in the maximum thickness
of SU1 and SU2 deposits, reaching ca. 1m in C4
toward the section and ca. 0.8 m in C3. These
squares contain 81% ( n = 1136/1407) of the total
amount of material collected at the Ravin Blanc I site.
The rest of the material was not taken into account,
either because of the recent dates obtained from the SU
in which it was found or because of its unreliable
depositional history, in particular toward the center of the current
river bed.
