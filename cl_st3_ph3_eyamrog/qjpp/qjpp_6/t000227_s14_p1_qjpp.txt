Incisors The Boxgrove dental sample comprises two mandibular permanent incisors
tentatively assigned to H. heidelbergensis ( Hillson et al., 2010
). Boxgrove 2 is a right lower central incisor (NHMUK
PA EM 3567) exhibiting a wear grade 6 ( Molnar,
1971 ); the detached tip of the root (NHMUK PA
EM 3567b) was found nearby, in a bulk sample recovered
approximately 1 m away from the main portion of the
incisor (NHMUK PA EM 3567a; Hillson et al., 2010 ).
Boxgrove 3 (NHMUK PA EM 3568) is a left lower
lateral incisor with a wear grade 7 ( Molnar, 1971
). Both teeth have a large area of dentine exposed
on the incisal surface surrounded by a thin enamel rim
( Fig. 2 ). The wear pattern and context of
the finds in the same level and in close proximity
to each other suggest they belong to the same individual
