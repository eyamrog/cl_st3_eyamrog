To investigate plant assemblages, we used nonmetric multidimensional scaling (NMDS)
based on Bray–Curtis dissimilarities, allowing the visualization of dissimilarities in
composition across samples. A stress level of <0.2 for NMDS
is considered acceptable for this purpose ( Clarke, 1993 ).
We grouped samples according to their archaeological horizons and tested
for differences between groups by performing a permutational multivariate analysis
of variance (perMANOVA) with 999 permutations using the adonis function
in the ‘vegan’ package v. 2.5-7 ( Oksanen et al.,
2020 ). For pairwise perMANOVAs, we corrected p -values for
multiple testing using the Bonferroni method ( Bland and Altman,
1995 ) of the p.adjust function. We tested the perMANOVA
assumption of homogeneity of multivariate dispersions using the permutest function
with 999 permutations.
