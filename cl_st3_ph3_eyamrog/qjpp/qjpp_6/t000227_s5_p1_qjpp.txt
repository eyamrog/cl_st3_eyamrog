For extinct and extant late Homo , mandibular incisor morphology
is extremely similar, except for a generally larger size in
Neanderthals relative to RMH ( Bailey, 2006 ). Compared to
molars ( Olejniczak et al., 2008 ; Skinner et al.,
2015 ; Martín-Francés et al., 2018 , 2020 ; Lockey
et al., 2020 ) incisors are less taxonomically discriminative. As
shown in previous studies ( Lockey et al., 2020 ),
we found that extreme wear also reduces the utility of
crown dental tissue proportions for classification purposes.
