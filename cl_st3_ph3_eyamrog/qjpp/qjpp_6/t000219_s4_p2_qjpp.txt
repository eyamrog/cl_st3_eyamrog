In sum, although the pattern of integration is relatively constant
in mammals, we observed in our results subtle differences in
the craniocervical pattern and magnitude of integration between humans and
gorillas and chimpanzees. These differences are mainly circumscribed to cranial
and cervical characters implicated in maintaining head balance. However, whether
these relative differences are related to postural and/or locomotor factors
is something that requires more specific analyses to be answered.
Finally, we also found that the module comprising the atlas
and axis reflects a certain degree of lower magnitude of
shape change during evolution with the rest of the craniocervical
complex.
