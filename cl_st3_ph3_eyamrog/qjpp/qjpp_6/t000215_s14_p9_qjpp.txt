The absence or presence of the cultural striations was recorded.
In case of presence, it was also recorded in which
tooth appeared, the number of striations according to the orientation
(right-oblique, left-oblique, horizontal, vertical) in that tooth (following Lozano-Ruíz et
al., 2004 ; Estalrrich and Rosas, 2013 ). Chipped enamel
was scored following the three-stage scale proposed by Belcastro et
al. (2004) , which consists of: 1 = small crack
affecting only the enamel; 2 = square irregular lesion with
enamel deeply involved; 3 = affecting seriously both dentine and
enamel. For toothpicking grooves, we recorded absence/presence, and in this
last situation, the degree of groove development using a five-stage
scale proposed by Estalrrich et al. (2017b) . In the
case of the subvertical grooves, only absence/presence was recorded.
