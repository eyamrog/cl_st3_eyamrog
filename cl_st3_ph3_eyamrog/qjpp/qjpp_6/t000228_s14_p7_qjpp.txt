The cave sedimentary sequence was sampled while wearing personal protective
equipment (hairnets, facemasks, double gloves) and using sterile instruments. Sampling
tubes and scalpel holders were UV irradiated in preparation for
the sampling, and scalpel holders were cleaned with bleach in
between each sample to avoid cross-contamination of DNA. First, 1–2
cm of surface material was removed. Then two consecutive cuts
were made with sterile scalpels before sample collection in a
sample tube, following the procedure described by Epp et al.
(2019) . Samples were kept cool during transport to the
ancient DNA facilities at the University of Oslo, where they
remained at −20 °C until DNA extraction .
