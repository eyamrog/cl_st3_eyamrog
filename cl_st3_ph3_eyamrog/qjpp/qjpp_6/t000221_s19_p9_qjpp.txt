At the knee joint, there was a substantial positive power
peak in the first double-support period in both chimpanzees and
humans, followed by a complex, cyclical pattern of energy absorption
and generation through the single-support and second double-support periods. In
the middle of the single-support period, chimpanzees had a small
burst of energy generation, whereas humans were near zero power
output. Throughout limb swing, there was substantial negative power at
the knee in both species, especially in the second half
of the swing phase.
