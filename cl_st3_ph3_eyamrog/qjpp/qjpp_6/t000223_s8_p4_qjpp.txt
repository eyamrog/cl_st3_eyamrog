Riparo Mochi (43°47′3.66″ N, 7°32′4.18″ E) is a shallow rock
shelter located at the base of a broad dolomitic limestone
cliff called the Balzi Rossi, near the Italian-French border, in
the Italian province of Liguria in northeastern Italy ( Fig.
1 ). The site is part of a complex of
over 15 caves, rock shelters, and open-air sites, commonly known
as the Grimaldi sites. For a description of the site,
refer to the study by Douka et al. (2012) .
