The standard deviations of the natural logarithm of centroid size
obtained for the hominine taxa indicate that maxillary size is
significantly more variable in Gorilla and Pan than in Homo
( SOM Tables S3 and S4 ). At the species
level, G. gorilla and G. beringei are more variable in
size than H. sapiens , P. troglodytes , and P.
paniscus . Size variation does not differ significantly between the
species and subspecies of Pan or Gorilla .
