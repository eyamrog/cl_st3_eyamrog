Quartz and feldspar ages are consistent at 2 sigma through
the sequence. However, it is noticeable that in the lower
part of the sequence ( Fig. 4 ), the quartz
ages are systematically older than the K-feldspar pIRIR 290 ages
by ∼10–30% ( n = 6). Given the very different
bleaching rates (perhaps by a factor of ∼100; refer to
the study by Murray et al., 2012 ) of the
fast component in quartz and the pIRIR 290 signals in
feldspar, this is strong evidence that the quartz signals were
