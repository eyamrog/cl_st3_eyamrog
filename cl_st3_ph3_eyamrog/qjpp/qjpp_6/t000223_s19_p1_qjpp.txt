The four charcoal determinations ranged from 43 to 35 ka
BP (=thousands of uncalibrated 14 C years before AD1950; Table
3 ). The three determinations from the Mousterian unit agree
well with their stratigraphic position, whereas OxA-2579-40 from unit H,
which is intermediate between Late Mousterian I and Proto-Aurignacian G,
appears much younger. As suspected from previous work, in this
study, Unit H also provides anomalous results for radiocarbon ages.
It is possible that the material in this unit has
been affected by postdepositional diagenesis and mixing (see Discussion ).
