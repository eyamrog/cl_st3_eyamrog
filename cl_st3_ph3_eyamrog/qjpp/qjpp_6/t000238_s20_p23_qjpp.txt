On questions of confidence and trust, the therapists argue that
the clients respond positively when the therapists have confidence in
them and if they perceive the relationship to be based
on empathy. However, T1 adds, . . . if it
concerns clients who previously have not experienced trust in a
person it could, of course, be more difficult. Many of
the clients are in a crisis and want help. They
tell you that they want your help. If you are
willing to help them, trust is created along with the
hope of obtaining the help.
