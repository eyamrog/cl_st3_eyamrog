The category of repellents encompassed five taxa detected with sedaDNA
and three detected with pollen. These include several of the
taxa also categorized as bulk foods and/or flavoring ( Brassica
sp., Artemisia sp., Chenopodium sp., and Quercus sp.), and sedaDNA
from these categories showed similar patterns ( Fig. 4 ).
Repellent taxa found by pollen analysis were Acorus calamus (sweet
flag), Artemisia sp., and Matricaria matricarioides (wild chamomile) type (
Table 1 ).
