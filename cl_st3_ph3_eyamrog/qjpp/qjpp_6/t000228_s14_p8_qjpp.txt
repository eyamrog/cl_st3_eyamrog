DNA extraction and amplification DNA was extracted from 25 samples
and three negative controls at the dedicated ancient DNA laboratory,
Department of Biosciences, University of Oslo. We extracted DNA from
90 to 190 mg of sediment using the sediment extraction
protocol as described by Rohland et al. (2018) with silica
spin columns and binding buffer D. We increased the elution
buffer volume to 100 μL. See Supplementary Online Materials (SOM)
S1 for further details.
