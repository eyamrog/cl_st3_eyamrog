As all incisors, central and lateral, present extensive enamel wear
(>3 stage following Molnar, 1971 ), no reliable nor comparable
data on 2D and/or 3D crown measurements can be provided.
However, future studies may compare data on specimens showing similar
degree of wear across hominin species, and therefore we performed
the 2D and 3D measurements to be included as supplementary
material (see SOM Table S2 and SOM Fig. S1 ).
