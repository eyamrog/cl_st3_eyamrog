There are a number of data issues that I wish
to address. The first is the overall availability of data
in Canada. Then I move on to critique the existing
data that we have in terms of its quality and
its access. I also address how the potential for better
data exist, but that the will of those who can
make this happen does not appear to be apparent for
various reasons. In data that do exist, the issue of
a key missing demographic indicator—race—is discussed at length.
