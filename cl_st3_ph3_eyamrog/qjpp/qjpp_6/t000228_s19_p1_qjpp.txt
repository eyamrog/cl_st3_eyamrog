We obtained 168.8 million raw DNA sequence reads. After filtering,
the DNA data set consisted of 3.3 million reads from
21 samples, leaving four samples with no sequences ( SOM
Table S1 ). Read counts per sample, corrected for the
amount of input material, averaged 305 ± 38 log-transformed reads
per mg of sediment (excluding empty samples; Fig. 2 A;
SOM Table S4 ). The number of positive replicates per
sample averaged 4.3 ± 0.7 (excluding empty samples; Fig. 2
A; SOM Table S5 ). The filtered DNA data set
contained 128 unique MOTUs, corresponding to 10 plant families, 20
genera, and six species (see SOM Figs. S1 and S2
for detailed stratigraphic plots). Forbs (nongraminoid herbaceous plants) account for
116 MOTUs and more than 93% of the log-transformed read
abundance, followed by graminoids , with 11 MOTUs at approximately
6% and trees with 1 MOTU at <0.5%. Download: Download
