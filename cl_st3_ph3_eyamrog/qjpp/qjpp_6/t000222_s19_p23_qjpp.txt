Mean shape differences between P. troglodytes and P. paniscus show
that allometric adjustment has little impact, except for slightly more
projection of the anterior dental arcade and subnasal segment in
P. paniscus , and canine alveoli that are somewhat narrower
buccolingually in P. troglodytes ( SOM Fig. S5 ). Equivalent
comparisons between G. gorilla and G. beringei show no differences
after allometric adjustment ( SOM Fig. S6 ).
