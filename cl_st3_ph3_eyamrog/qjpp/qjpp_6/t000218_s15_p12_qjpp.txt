A comparison of the MAR-1-9B mandible with those of the
European M. sylvanus subspecies is limited because of the fragmentary
nature of the known specimens. Similar to MAR-1-9B, a mandibular
corpus fossa on the buccal side (for this character, refer
to the study by Delson, 1980 ) seems to be
present in the mandible of unidentifiable sex from Quecchia quarry
(Italy; Bona et al., 2016 : Fig. 3a), attributed to
M. s. cf. pliocena by Alba et al. (2019) ,
as well as a deeper one in the female mandible
of the same taxon from Lezetxiki II (Spain; Castaños et
al., 2011 : Fig. 2b). Likewise, a fossa is markedly
present in the holotype of M. s. florentina (IGF 10034)
of a male individual from Upper Valdarno (Italy; Fig. 4
C); however, a fossa is absent in the male mandible
NMB VA 352 also from Upper Valdarno ( Delson, 1980
: Fig. 2-2C). Compared with the mandible of M. s.
pliocena from Zlaty Kun C718 (Czechia), of unidentifiable sex, MAR-1-9B
shows a less inclined rostral margin of the ramus (
Fig. 4 E).
