Remarks The teeth are stained at the tip of their
cusps. The overall construction of the teeth is not as
robust as seen in B. fissidens , but these teeth
have rather a faint aspect. Beremendia minor shows teeth with
less rounded cusps and sharper crests than its larger congeneric
counterpart, B. fissidens . The identification of this species is
left at a tentative level (cf.) because the teeth of
the only specimen found are somewhat smaller than those frequently
reported in literature.
