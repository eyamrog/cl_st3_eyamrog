The production sequence of Aurignacian ivory beads is well understood
for Western and Central European sites ( Hahn, 1988 ;
Heckel, 2009 ; Heckel, 2018 ; White, 1995 ; Wolf,
2015a , b ; Wolf et al., 2013 ). To
make such beads, precision tools and ca. 15–45 min are
necessary. However, the use of pigments, specifically red ocher, during
the production of ivory beads has been previously proposed (
Heckel, 2009 ; Nivens, 2020 ; White, 1993 , 1995
), but never fully confirmed on artifacts. Our results show
that ocher was involved in the chaîne opératoire of ivory
bead production; however, there are several possibilities for which specific
purposes it was used for.
