What is most remarkable about our findings, however, is that
despite the Chagyrskaya individuals living on the far eastern edge
of the known Neanderthal range, their dietary suite is largely
indistinguishable from those of western Neanderthals when considering broad categories
of food types and macronutrients. While the specific plants and
animals that were consumed likely varied among habitats and potentially
through time, the Neanderthal niche seems to have involved a
high reliance on animal proteins and the consumption of a
variety of plant foods to meet their nutritional requirements.
