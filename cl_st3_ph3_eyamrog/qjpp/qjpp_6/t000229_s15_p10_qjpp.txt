Through the process of formulating and implementing the measures, a
risk which was previously presented as invisible was turned into
visible, responsibility and accountability had now an owner, and two
opposing groups were symbolically constructed. The risk of Covid-19 became
visible in the sense that it was a virus that
existed out-there and could spread through the help of people
who did not comply. So the invisible enemy was turned
into an enemy with allies, whose spread increased when people’s
sociality increased.
