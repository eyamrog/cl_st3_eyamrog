The ground reaction forces of humans and chimpanzees were different
in both magnitude and pattern ( Fig. 2 ). The
vertical force was monophasic in bipedal chimpanzees, lacking the second
peak from the single-support period into the second double-support period
that is characteristic of human walking. The bipedal chimpanzee's anterior-posterior
force was distinct from humans, as it exhibited a smaller
peak force in both posterior and anterior directions, whereas the
mediolateral force was larger, most notably during the single-support period.
