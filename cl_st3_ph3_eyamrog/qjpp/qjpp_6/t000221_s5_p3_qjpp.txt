Our results are consistent with earlier 2-D data on the
sagittal plane moments of humans and bipedal chimpanzees throughout stance
(Yamazaki et al., 1981; Sockol et al., 2007 ). In
particular, bipedal chimpanzees—like all facultative bipeds studied to date (
Yamazaki et al., 1979 ; Yamazaki, 1985 )—have a substantial
hip extension moment for nearly the full duration of stance.
