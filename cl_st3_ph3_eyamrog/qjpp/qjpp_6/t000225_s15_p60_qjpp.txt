Remarks The juvenile P 3 presents a triangular shape. It
is subdivided into three cuspids, as the anteroconid is isolated
and the talonid is not connected to the trigonid. In
lateral view, the P 3 shows the characteristic conical shape
of the juvenile teeth. The juvenile P 4 presents a
trigonid with lateral inflexions and is again isolated from a
slightly smaller talonid. The P 3 has an ellipsoidal surface,
with a poorly developed hypoflex. The paraflex is filled by
cement.
