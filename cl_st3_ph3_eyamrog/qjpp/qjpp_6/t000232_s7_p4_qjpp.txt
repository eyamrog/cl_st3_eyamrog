Crucial to this solution was an investigative strategy that could
articulate the complexities of the system while remaining grounded in
the practical need to solve a problem experienced by individual
clinicians—an applied approach that could enable decision-makers “to see relatively
autonomous human experiences. . . as social” and coordinated, or
capable of coordinating ( Deener 2018 :296). As an applied
sociologist, I knew that one promising approach was IE. This
is an account of how using an applied IE approach
can improve the success of what are traditionally understood to
be technology projects, such as developing systems of notification.
