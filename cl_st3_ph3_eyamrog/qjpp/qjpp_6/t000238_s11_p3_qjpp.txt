According to Scheff (1990, 2003 ; Retzinger 1995 ; Retzinger
and Scheff 2000 ; Scheff and Retzinger 1997 , 2000
, 2001 ), a relationship between two parties can be
based either on a solidarity social bond and feelings of
pride in belonging or on an alienated social bond and
feelings of shame in not belonging. Along the same lines,
the relationship can lead to both parties being isolated from
the other (i.e., over-differentiated) or being compelled to belong, as
engulfed by the other (i.e., under-differentiated). Both alternatives create an
unsafe social bond with the other person or group.
