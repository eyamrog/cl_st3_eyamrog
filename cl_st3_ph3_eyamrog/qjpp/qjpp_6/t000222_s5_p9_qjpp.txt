Finding similar levels of sexual dimorphism for the maxillae of
H. sapiens and Pan does obviously not mean that the
pattern of dimorphism is the same. The minimal canine sexual
dimorphism in H. sapiens ( Wood, 1976 ; Oxnard et
al., 1985 ; Wood et al., 1991 ; Scott and
Lockwood, 2004 ) is typically expressed in our results by
less difference in associated alveolar morphology than shown by Pan
. On the other hand, the latter lacks the clear
differences in facial height and palatal depth seen in H.
sapiens .
