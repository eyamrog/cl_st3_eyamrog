A typical strategy in IE is to follow a document
as it moves across locations to determine other standpoints to
examine, but I did not have a document to follow.
My strategy was instead to follow a chain of potential
responsibility, in line with methods employed in past ethnographic research
on health work practices (e.g. see S. Bishop and Waring
2016 ). For instance, identifying the need for an electronic
solution necessitated engaging the Privacy Office, because a Privacy Impact
Assessment is a procedural step in IMIT projects. This in
turn led to Medical Affairs since part of their role
in credentialing physicians is assigning role-based access to patient information.
