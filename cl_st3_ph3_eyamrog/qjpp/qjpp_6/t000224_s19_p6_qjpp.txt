Minimum temperature of the coldest month (Bio6) The MAT trends
are exacerbated in the case of the minimum temperatures of
the coldest month. These values are also at their largest
during MIS 12 (warmest and coldest intervals), with the largest
ranges occurring during MIS 12 on the south-north transect (13.2–14.5
°C; Table 7 ; Figure 11 , Figure 12 ).
However, in MIS 13 and 11 (warmest and coldest intervals)
there is greater variability on the east-west transect (e.g., range
values of 7.6 °C and 10.1 °C in the MIS
13 warmest and coldest intervals, respectively; Table 7 ). There
is also reduced variability in the Bio6 variable along the
south-north transect during the MIS 13 and 11 warmest intervals
when compared to the MAT data (e.g., a range value
of 4.3 °C compared to 5.0 °C for the MIS
13 warmest interval; Table 6 , Table 7 ). This
reduction in Bio6 variability relative to MAT is not apparent
on the east-west transect (e.g., a range value of 7.4
°C compared to 3.9 °C for the MIS 11 warmest
interval; Table 6 , Table 7 ). In all cases,
Bio6 values decreased to the north (Transect A; Fig. 12
) and increased to the west (Transect B; Fig. 12
). Statistical comparison of the Bio6 data for the 490
ka and 410 ka intervals indicated no significant differences on
either transect (Transect A [south-north]: U = 24.0 [ n
= 16], p = 0.442; Transect B [east-west]: U =
