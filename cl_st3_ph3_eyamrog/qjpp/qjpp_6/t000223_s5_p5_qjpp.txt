Placing the new chronostratigraphic sequence from Mochi in its regional
and broader context is important given that several northern Italian
Paleolithic sites have been reanalyzed and redated in recent years
( Fig. 8 ). Riparo Bombrini is part of the
same archaeological complex as Mochi and has recently been reevaluated.
The site was excavated in 1976 and again in the
early 2000s. Higham et al. (2014) and Benazzi et al.
(2015) presented a series of dates from the 1976 Vicino
excavations. The 1976 sequence was divided into four artificial spits
containing two cultural macrounits: the lower one (Upper and Lower
level IV) with Mousterian artifacts and the upper one (levels
III-I) containing UP artifacts. Levels III and II have yielded
Proto-Aurignacian artifacts, abundant Dufour bladelets, beads, bone points, as well
