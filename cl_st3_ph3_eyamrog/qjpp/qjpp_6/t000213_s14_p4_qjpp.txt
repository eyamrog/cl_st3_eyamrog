Materials studied To complement the isotope study, we retrieved and
analyzed plant microremains (starch grains and phytoliths) trapped in dental
calculus isolated from the teeth of the Chagyrskaya 6 mandible,
as well as from several Neanderthal isolated teeth and associated
faunal teeth ( Supplementary Online Material [SOM] Table S1 ).
One of the isolated teeth, the left I 2 Chagyrskaya
14, derives from the same individual as the mandible, based
on its find position and morphology, whereas the other teeth,
Chagyrskaya 12, 17, and 20, represent three different individuals based
on their estimated ages.
