Retouch has been recorded following the analytical grid suggested by
Inizan et al. (1999) , including retouch position (direct, inverse/ventral,
combined direct-inverse), their extent with respect to the center the
piece in the axis perpendicular to the length (short: 1/4,
medium: 2/4, invasive: 3/4 covering: 4/4), retouch inclination (abrupt: 90–71°,
semi-abrupt: 70–40°, low-angled: <40°), their morphology (scaled, subparallel, Clactonian), and
retouched edge delineation (straight, concave, convex, denticulated).
