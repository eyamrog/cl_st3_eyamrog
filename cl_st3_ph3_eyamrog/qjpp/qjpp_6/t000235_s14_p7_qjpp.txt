Three different outcomes will be used. The first, amount of
item nonresponse, is objective, while the other two are more
subjective and rely on the assessment of the interviewer. By
utilizing all three variables, we attempt to achieve a more
complete picture with regard to the interviewing situation and data
quality. Table 2 (p. 8) lists descriptive statistics for all
outcomes by countries. Table 3 lists all pairwise correlations for
the dependent variables. The results underline that there is correlation
present, which indicates that all three variables can be regarded
as appropriate measurements to assess the construct data quality.
