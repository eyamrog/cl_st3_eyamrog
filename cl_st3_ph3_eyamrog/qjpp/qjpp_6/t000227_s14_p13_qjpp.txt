As both Boxgrove incisor roots required reconstruction, an interobserver error
test was conducted by two of the authors (A.L.L and
L.M.-F.) and Clément Zanolli. For total root volume, Boxgrove 2
had <1% difference, and Boxgrove 3 <2% difference ( SOM
Table S3 ). Considering these low values, which are lower
than the reported 4% threshold error (e.g., Zanolli et al.,
2010 , 2018 ), our estimates from reconstructed roots therefore
appear accurate. Because of the extensive crown wear, we provide
descriptive statistics for the crown ( SOM Table S2 )
and a more in-depth analysis of the roots.
