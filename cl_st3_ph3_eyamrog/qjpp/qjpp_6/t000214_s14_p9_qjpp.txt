When animals were not recorded as present during daily censuses,
CIRMF staff searched the enclosures for dead animals. If they
found a dead animal, they collected the remains and placed
them in plastic bags for controlled decomposition in a designated
area, keeping track of their ID numbers for later reference.
Staff made every effort to keep material complete, but some
teeth were lost post-mortem. Staff cleaned the remains using salt
and alcohol, with minimal use of bleach.
