Previous sedaDNA studies on cave sites in New Zealand, Australia,
and North America demonstrate the potential to recover and identify
plant DNA and reconstruct plant assemblages from >20,000 years ago
( Willerslev, 2003 ; Haile et al., 2007 ; Haouchar
et al., 2014 ; Seersholm et al., 2020 ). Moreover,
a recent study at Solkota Cave in Georgia successfully retrieved
up to 84,000-year-old mammal and plant DNA from stalagmites (
Stahlschmidt et al., 2019 ), attesting to the unique conditions
of preservation in caves.
