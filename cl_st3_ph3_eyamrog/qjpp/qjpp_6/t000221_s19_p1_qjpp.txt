The average walking speeds of humans and chimpanzees were 1.66
± 0.06 and 1.09 ± 0.10 m s −1 ,
respectively. These correspond to similar dimensionless velocities ( v’ ;
H = 0.56 ± 0.01, C = 0.56 ± 0.06)
and Fr numbers (H = 0.31 ± 0.02, C =
0.31 ± 0.06) ( Table 1 ). These speeds are
close to—but a bit faster than—the preferred overground speeds for
human walking, and well below the expected walk-run transition speed
for terrestrial mammals (i.e., v’ = 0.7; Fr = 0.5;
Alexander, 1989 ; Kram et al., 1997 ).
