Plant taxa possibly used for dye were similarly detected throughout
the pollen record but limited to AH VII and VI
in the sedaDNA record. Those detected by sedaDNA included R.
scutatus , Lithospermum sp. (gromwell), Isatis sp., and Chenopodium sp.,
while the pollen record distinguished eight different taxa, of which
four trees (two Quercus types, Pinus sp., and Acer sp.),
Pinus sp. in particular, being present throughout most of the
sedimentary sequence.
