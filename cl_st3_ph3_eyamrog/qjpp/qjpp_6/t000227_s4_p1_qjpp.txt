When the Boxgrove finds were made and described in the
1990s, there was much less material known and published from
the MP of Europe, and the provisional assignment of the
Boxgrove fossils to H. heidelbergensis was made more on chronological
than morphological grounds. We are fortunately in a much better
position now in terms of fossil material, but the intervening
years have also led to a growing realization of the
complexity of human evolution during the MP, with the apparent
co-existence of different human lineages/species, and the taxonomically mosaic anatomies
found in some of the fossils.
