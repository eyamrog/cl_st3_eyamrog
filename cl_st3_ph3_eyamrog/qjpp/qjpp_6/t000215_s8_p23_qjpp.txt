Santa Catalina cave (Lekeitio, Biscay) was excavated between 1982 and
2000. Three archeological levels were identified: Upper Magdalenian (Level III),
Final Magdalenian (Level II) and Azilian (Level I; Berganza Gochi
and Arribas Pastor, 2014 ). Within the Magdalenian levels, three
human teeth were recovered. Two of them are deciduous teeth
( Table 1 ) found in the Upper Magdalenian (Level
III), being the level dated between 10,360 ± 90 and
12,425 ± 90 uncal BP ( Berganza Gochi and Arribas
Pastor, 2014 ; the complete set of chronology data for
Level III is provided on Table 1 ). The specimens
are housed at the Arkeologi Museoa in Bilbao.
