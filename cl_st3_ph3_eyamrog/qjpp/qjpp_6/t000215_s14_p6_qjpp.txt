The two deciduous teeth from the Upper Magdalenian level III
from Santa Catalina cave are a right dP 4 (SC.
C8. L31H. 1883) and a left dP 4 (SC. B6.
L30F. 815). Both individuals were estimated to be, approximately, 10
years of age and assigned to the juvenile category (
Albisu Andrade et al., 2014 ).
