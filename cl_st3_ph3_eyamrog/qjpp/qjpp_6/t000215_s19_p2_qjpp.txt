The Gravettian child from El Castillo, Castillo C, has very
little record of paramasticatory dental wear. The interproximal sides between
dP 3 and dP 4 present the expected interproximal contact
facets without subvertical grooves. The mesial side of the dP
3 was broken at some point and was recently reconstructed
by Garralda et al. (2019) but shows a slight trace
of chipped enamel on the occlusal surface of the dP
4 .
