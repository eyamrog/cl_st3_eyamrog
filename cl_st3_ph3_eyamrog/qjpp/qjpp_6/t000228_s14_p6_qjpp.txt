The Aghitu-3 excavation profile (>5.5 m) consists mainly of dry
silt, which is dominant throughout and interspersed with basalt debris
from the cave ceiling. We collected sedaDNA samples in September
2019 over the full depth of the profile. As AH
III contains high amounts of platy basalt ( Fig. 1
C), samples were taken from accessible sediments in between the
basalts. In the lower horizons (AH VII and VI), which
contain much less basalt, we did not sample close to
boulders and avoided including small rocks.
