With respect to the lower canine, other than a clear
separation of M. majori from the larger canines of M.
sylvanus , the canine dimensions overlap among the Macaca subspecies;
only the male canines of M. s. florentina tend to
be larger, in particular in MD ( Fig. 3 ).
However, the sample of M. s. prisca and M. s.
pliocena is too small to allow safe conclusions. Except for
M. majori , the MAR-1-9B P 3 and P 4
MD values fall below the mean and median values of
all M. sylvanus subspecies. Nonetheless, apart from the roughly similar
P 3 BL mean and median values with M. s.
florentina , the MAR-1-9B P 3 and P 4 BL
mean and median values surpass those of M. majori ,
M. s. sylvanus , M. s. prisca , M. s.
florentina, and M. s. pliocena , from the latter exceeding
its known maximum range for the P 4 BL (
