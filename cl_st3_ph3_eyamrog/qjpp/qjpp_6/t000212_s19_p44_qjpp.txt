Finally, the assemblage contains three bifacially shaped pieces. The invasive
removals show deep negative bulbs typical of hard hammer percussion.
Shaping removals partially cover the dorsal and ventral faces of
the blank, and two retain cortical areas on the dorsal
surface. One is cordiform ( Fig. 10 A) but the
two others are more irregular in shape and asymmetrical, which
has led us to qualify them as bifacial pieces rather
than as bifaces ( Fig. 10 B).
