In the stance phase, human negative work was predominately absorbed
at the knee (46.6 ± 3.0%), whereas chimpanzees absorbed most
of their mechanical work at the ankle (42.4 ± 2.0%).
In the swing phase, the knee accounted for nearly all
of the negative work in both species (H = 91.2
± 4.6%; C = 93.0 ± 2.1%).
