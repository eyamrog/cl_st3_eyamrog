As a result of application of the habitat weighting method,
the distribution of the relative abundances of rodent taxa and
their habitat preferences ( Table 2 ; Fig. 6 )
indicates a dominance of open dry meadows (36.5%) and rocky
habitats (21%). The rodent assemblage also indicates open herbaceous habitats
with some humidity requirements (15.5%) and areas surrounding water (25.7%).
Therefore, the rodent association from Dmanisi suggests the presence of
an open, steppic environment in the vicinity of Dmanisi, although
the scarce arvicolid and murid representation would indicate the existence
of wooded areas (1.3%) not far away from this steppic
landscape. Table 2 . Relative abundances of each rodent taxon
