RB5 thin section from SU6 shows sandy loamy sediment with
a poorly developed basal mass (<20% of the sediment). The
sand grains are quartz (200–500 μm) and also correspond to
pisoliths. The pedality of the sediment is well developed, corresponding
to a macroporosity drawing polyhedral aggregates and locally a microstructure
of chitonic type. Most voids are devoid of pedological features
and some secondary voids are covered with hyaline clay or
fine micritic coatings. The whole indicates a poorly developed pedogenesis
with a tendency to vertisolization.
