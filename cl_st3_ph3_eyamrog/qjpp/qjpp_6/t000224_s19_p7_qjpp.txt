The mean temperatures of the coldest quarters (Bio11; SOM Table
S2 ) similarly vary along the south-north and east-west transects
(with highest values in the south and the west), offering
a climatic perspective on the contrasting Lower Paleolithic record east
and west of the Rhine. The Bio11 variable also emphasizes
the potentially significant difference between minimum annual temperatures (which may
only need to be tolerated for a few days each
year) and the general conditions associated with the coldest periods
of the year (e.g., in south-east Britain during late MIS
13 [490 ka model], the latter values are just above,
rather than below, freezing).
