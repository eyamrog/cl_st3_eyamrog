The medians and means for the three performance measures (averaged
across all difficulty levels) show that participants needed, on average,
50 seconds longer and more attempts to imitate a knot
in the mismatching condition (demonstrated by a model of the
opposite handedness based on the median values; Table 2 ;
Fig. 1 ). Note that the variance of the medium
and difficult categories is much higher than of the easy
category. Subjects were also slightly more often successful in imitating
the knot when the demonstration was given by a model
who matched their handedness ( Table 2 ).
