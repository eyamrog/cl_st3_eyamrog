Finally, both Magdalenian juveniles Santa Catalina and the Magdalenian child
from Tito Bustillo display chipped enamel, bigger (following Belcastro et
al., 2004 grading scale) than in the other Magdalenian specimen
from Las Caldas, and toothpick grooves ( Fig. 3 C).
Also, we recorded subvertical grooves on the interproximal contact facets
of the right dP 4 from Tito Bustillo cave.
