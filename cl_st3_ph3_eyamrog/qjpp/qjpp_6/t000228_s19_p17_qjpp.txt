Ten taxa were assigned to the category of ‘other uses,’
of which two were found with both proxies ( Quercus
sp. and Artemisia sp.), two others uniquely with sedaDNA (
Cirsium sp. and Isatis sp.), and eight with pollen (
Table 1 ; Fig. 4 ). Several of the pollen
taxa belonging to this category are trees ( Pinus sp.,
Quercus sp., and Betula sp.), while others are grasses (
Phragmites sp.) and forbs ( A. calamus , Artemisia sp.,
Convolvulus sp. and Typha sp.).
