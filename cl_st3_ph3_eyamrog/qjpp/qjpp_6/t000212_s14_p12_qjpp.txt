Samples were prepared following standard procedures to extract 200–250 and
100–140 μm quartz grains: wet sieving, followed by HCl (10%),
then H 2 0 2 (30%) to remove carbonates and
organic material respectively. High-density liquid separation was performed using heteropolytungstate
of sodium at 2.72 and 2.62 g/cm 3 to separate
quartz, feldspars, and heavy minerals, although very few feldspar grains
were available in the 100–140 or 200–250 μm fractions. The
quartz grains were submitted to an HF (38%) etching for
60 min followed by a HCl treatment and a final
sieve at 80 μm or 180 μm. The quartz then
was mounted on single grain discs, presenting 100 holes with
diameter 150 μm and 150 μm depth (RB1, RB4, RB5)
or 300 μm diameter and 300 μm depth (RB2 and
RB3) so that there was only one grain per hole.
Multigrain discs (5-mm mask) were also prepared for preliminary tests.
