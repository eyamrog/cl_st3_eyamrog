Toothpicking is a cumulative process during the individual's life, and
is accepted as the oldest human habit, typically associated with
dental hygiene ( Frayer and Russell, 1987 ; Ungar et
al., 2001 ; Hlusko, 2003 ; Estalrrich et al., 2017b
), and sometimes as the result of a therapeutic or
palliative activity ( Lozano et al., 2013 ).
