Regarding the scrapers, two relevant characteristics are noticed. First, the
presence of end-scrapers with a large front made by semi-abrupt
direct retouch of medium extent and made on blanks of
uniform thickness resembling regular slabs, or heavily retouched flakes (
Fig. 10 C). Secondly, four of the seven side-scrapers show
short inverse retouch, usually semi-abrupt, either combined with dorsal retouch
or not ( Fig. 9 D). Among the blanks with
localized continuous retouch, ventral semi-abrupt modification is also observed on
4 of the 9 tools, most often not combined with
direct retouch. These inverse retouches, due to their short extension,
do not aim at thinning the blank but rather modify
the edge angle.
