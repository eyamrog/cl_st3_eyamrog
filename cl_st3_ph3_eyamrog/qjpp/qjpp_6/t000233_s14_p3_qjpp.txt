When framed in terms of organizational embeddedness, PEPiN brought together
NGO and academic actors. These two groups operate with different
logics and motivations as discussed earlier, as well as consisting
of members with different skills. Those from NGOs brought experiences
around grant-writing, messaging, community outreach, policy formulation, and outreach to
politicians. They had also experienced failed and successful past interventions
while positioned within a larger network of NGO and government
personalities. As scholars, we brought research skills and a “neutral”
and professional veneer which make findings more believable to skeptics.
