When harmonizing data between Toronto and Chicago ( Robson et
al. 2019 ), we were limited in the demographic variables
that they could provide for analysis, but had tremendous envy
on the detailed nature of the university and college enrollment
information they had access to. Because all their data were
of the administrative variety, they did not have many indicators
of demographic factors that would typically be reported in our
Toronto schools census (e.g. family size, parental education, country of
origin—variables that are typically measured through self-report questionnaires).
