Remarks The genus Crocidura is frequently regarded as an indicator
of mild climates according to the preferences of its present-day
representatives ( Reumer, 1984 ; Rofes and Cuenca-Bescós, 2011 ;
Furió et al., 2015 ). Not by chance, the genus
is widely distributed on the entire African continent and the
southern part of Eurasia, with presence in many Indonesian islands.
The northern -and colder- parts of the continent are not
suitable for the development of Crocidura species because of their
metabolic rates, which are rather slow if compared with their
red-toothed counterparts, the Soricinae ( Rofes and Cuenca-Bescós, 2011 ).
