The functional significance of the proximal femur of the Shuitangba
Mesopithecus is difficult to interpret, especially in the absence of
the acetabulum of the pelvis, distal femur, and most of
the remainder of the distal hind limb . Further relevant
information on hind limb morphology in the Shuitangba Mesopithecus is
contained in Ji et al. (2020) describing calcaneal morphology.
