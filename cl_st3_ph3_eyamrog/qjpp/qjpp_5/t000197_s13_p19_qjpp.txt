Several archaeologists have also applied the concepts of icon, index,
and symbol to the interpretation of architecture and the built
environment. Many of these studies focus on religious architecture (
Baron 2014 , 2016 ; Coben 2006 ; Fogelin 2014
, 2015 ; Schoenfelder 2011 ; Stanish 2012 ; Swenson
2018 ). Other interesting applications include the houses of missionaries
in nineteenth-century southern Africa ( Crossland 2013 ), changing site
plans in the wake of the Pueblo Revolt ( Liebmann
2008 ), and conflicting ideologies of house configuration at a
nineteenth-century commune in Massachusetts ( Preucel 2006 , chapter 8).
