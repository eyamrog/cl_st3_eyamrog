The data set had a hierarchical structure, consisting of camera
trap deployments (the smallest sampling units) nested within unique sampling
stations, which were in turn nested within each study site
( Table 1 ). We therefore used hierarchical (mixed) models
to study site-level properties while accounting for lower-level nested effects.
Random intercepts for stations (cameras) were incorporated to capture camera-to-camera
heterogeneity, to accommodate the possibility that repeated deployments within stations
in the continental sites are not independent, and to allow
unique camera-specific baselines to contribute to model predictions. We fit
models for detection rates, party size, and duration of terrestrial
events in this hierarchical context ( Monteza-Moreno et al., 2020
).
