The first evidence of hominins in Asia, either fossil or
archaeology, may date to as early as two million years
ago. The earliest evidence for a hominin presence in Asia
comes from archaeological sites in northern China without hominin fossils:
the Nihewan Basin sites such as Majuangou (Site 21) 1
( Ao et al. 2013 ) dated 1.32–1.66 Ma based
on magnetostratigraphic correlation ( Zhu et al. 2004 ) and
Shangchen (Site 38) dated 2.1 Ma based on loess (
Zhu et al. 2018 ).
