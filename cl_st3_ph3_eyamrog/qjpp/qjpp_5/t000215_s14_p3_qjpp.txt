Castillo C mandible has been assigned to an individual between
4 and 5 years old, due to the presence of
all the deciduous dentition in occlusion (only right dP 3
and dP 4 were preserved) and the development of the
permanent dentition in their respective alveoli ( Garralda et al.,
2019 ). We considered this individual as a child. Besides,
two teeth left dI 1 (catalogue number 924) and left
dP 4 (catalogue number 292) were assigned to a child
between 4.5 and 6.5 years, based on the state of
the root (according to AlQahtani et al., 2010 ). The
last tooth is a left dP 3 (catalogue number 492),
still in formation. It has been estimated to belong to
an individual younger than 1.5 years old (an infant; according
to the atlas proposed by AlQahtani et al., 2010 ).
