Although it is now widely accepted that hominins dispersed beyond
Africa in the Early Pleistocene ( Table 2 ), the
idea of an early dispersal became generally agreed on only
after the 1990s, starting with the acceptance of the radiometric
dates for Dmanisi ( Antón & Swisher 2004 ). Until
the 1990s, the consensus was that hominins were limited to
Africa during the Early Pleistocene and that hominin habitation of
Eurasia took place during the Middle Pleistocene between 500 and
800 ka ( Klein 1989 , Pope 1988 ).
