The avian and nonprimate mammal fauna of Shuitangba also supports
an interpretation that the site represents a continuously wet freshwater-margin
environment, as evidenced by copious fossils of waterfowl and remains
of beavers, giant otters, and swamp rabbits ( Zhang et
al., 2013 ; Jablonski et al., 2014 ; Wang et
al., 2018 ; Flynn et al., 2019 ). The recent
recognition of the swamp rabbit, Nesolagus , at Shuitangba and
Lufeng is consistent with a wet, swamp-margin environment ( Flynn
et al., 2019 ).
