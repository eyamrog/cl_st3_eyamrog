RB2 thin section from SU2 shows a basal mass consisting
of a micritic carbonate mud with fluvial detrital sediments consisting
of fine to medium sands (100–200 μm), pisolitic gravels (300
μm to 2–3 cm) and fine- and coarser-grained yellowish sandstone
pebbles of gray color. The sand contains grains of quartz,
polycrystalline quartz, and feldspar. The porosity is mainly mechanical and
the voids are filled with microsparite (20–50 μm) indicating partial
leaching of carbonates from the basal mass. This indicates a
drying up of the sediment, at least seasonally.
