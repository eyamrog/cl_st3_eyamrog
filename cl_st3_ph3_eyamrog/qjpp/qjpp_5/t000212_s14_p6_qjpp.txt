Phytolith assemblages have been analyzed for four samples (RB1–RB4). Phytolith
extraction has been conducted on dried samples of 30 g
and treated through standard procedures: 1) destruction of organic matter
with hydrogen peroxide (H 2 O 2 33%, at 90
°C); 2) clays deflocculation with sodium hexametaphosphate (40 g/L); 3)
sands removal by sieving at 250 μm and clay removal
by decantation ; and 4) densiometric separation with zinc bromide
heavy liquid (ZnBr 2 ) set at d = 2.3.
Immersion oil was used as mounting medium to allow 3D
observations. Phytoliths were counted at 400× and 630× magnification. Absolute
phytolith counts range from 205 to 319 diagnostic morphotypes depending
on phytolith concentration and corrosion.
