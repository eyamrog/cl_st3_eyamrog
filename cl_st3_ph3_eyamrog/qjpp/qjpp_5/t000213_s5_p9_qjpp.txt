Analysis of dental wear and dental calculus has already verified
that some Neanderthals consumed plants in the more temperate western
and southern areas of their range ( Henry et al.,
2011 ; El Zaatari et al., 2011 , 2016 ;
Salazar-García et al., 2013 ; Fiorenza, 2015 ; Estalrrich et
al., 2017 ; Power et al., 2018 ). The identified
microremains at Chagyrskaya indicate that, even at the easternmost limit
of their range, and in the most continental environment occupied,
Neanderthals may have retained elements of a diverse dietary strategy
that incorporated plant foods into their diet.
