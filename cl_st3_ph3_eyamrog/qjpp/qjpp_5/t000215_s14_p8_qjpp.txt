High-resolution replicas of the dentition were prepared following conventional procedures
( Bromage, 1987 ; Teaford and Oyen, 1989 ; Martínez-Maza
et al., 2011 ; Estalrrich and Rosas, 2015 ). The
museums’ curators previously cleaned and prepared all teeth. Teeth were
molded using Coltène/Whaledent President Jet Polyvinylsiloxane dental impression material, and
then molds were cast using Epotek 301 high-resolution epoxy and
hardener and allowed to dry before analysis.
