Our tests for recombination suggest that these data do not
appear to be substantially affected by recombination in mitochondrial DNA,
which increases branch lengths and leads to exaggerated divergence times.
However, there are several cautionary notes with respect to the
divergence estimates in this study. Perhaps most importantly (and as
illustrated by this study compared to previous ones), unsampled mitochondrial
diversity from extant and prehistoric macaque populations on these islands
could identify new variation that influences phylogeographic interpretations.
