We calibrated female 5D3A’s dental development to her date of
birth using the neonatal line. Her dentition covered from 3
weeks before birth to age 8.55 years. This is 81%
of her life span, during which 45 accentuated lines formed
in her teeth ( Fig. 5 ; SOM Table S2
). We could not assess her dental histology when she
was aged 7.02–7.35 years, due to the poor quality of
