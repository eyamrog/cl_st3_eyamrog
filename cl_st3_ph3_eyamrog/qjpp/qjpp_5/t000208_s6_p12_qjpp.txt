A recent study of the putative diet of Lufengpithecus at
Shuitangba revealed the presence of edible nuts and seeds including
hickory ( Carya ) and hazelnut ( Corylus ; Huang
et al., 2015 ; Huang et al., 2017 ). These
materials would have been good sources of protein and energy
for Mesopithecus. Studies of dental microwear of Mesopithecus specimens from
eastern Europe show features consistent with a diet including hard
and brittle foods such as nuts and seeds ( Merceron
et al., 2009 ).
