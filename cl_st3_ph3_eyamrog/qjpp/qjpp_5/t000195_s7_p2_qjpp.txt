The earliest South African hominids belong to the genera Australopithecus
, Paranthropus , and Homo . We first summarize historical
views of these taxa and then discuss how more recent
discoveries and research have introduced new perspectives concerning the systematics,
dating, and taphonomy of these hominids. For clarity, recent discoveries
and interpretations must be framed in the context of earlier
concepts and misinterpretations of fossils.
