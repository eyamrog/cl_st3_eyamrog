As the bulk of the sequences analyzed here have been
published elsewhere ( Liedigk et al., 2015 ; Yao et
al., 2017 ; Roos et al., 2019 ), we present
our results in terms of the new insights gained from
the additional 60 new genomes we collected. Specifically, these new
data provide novel perspectives into (1) the timing and nature
of M. fascicularis dispersal across Wallace's Line into the Lesser
Sunda Islands and (2) phylogenetic resolution among and within M.
nemestrina and Sulawesi macaques using mitochondrial genomes.
