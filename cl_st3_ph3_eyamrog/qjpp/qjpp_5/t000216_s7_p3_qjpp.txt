Here, we build on existing research using nonhuman primates to
inform our understanding of hominin hybrids (e.g., Ackermann et al.,
2006 , 2014 ; Boel et al., 2019 ; Martinez
et al., 2019 ). We use a large sample of
captive full-bred and admixed Chinese and Indian rhesus macaques (
Macaca mulatta ) to elucidate the expected morphological consequences of
admixture between closely related primate species, including hominins. Of particular
interest because Chinese and Indian M. mulatta have been mixing
in this captive colony for decades, the study also examines
how admixture effects on phenotype vary with ancestry contributions from
the two subspecies lines.
