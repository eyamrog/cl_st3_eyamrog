In Brockmeier's novel, wounds open and close. But regardless of
their status as healed or not, the light still radiates.
At no point does the light from a wound extinguish.
A cut can heal but it still shines; it just
shines less brightly. The walking wounded are the lit-up wounded.
It is not their wounds per se, but rather their
different intensities of light-shining that reveal how wounding is thoroughly
uneven. What might such unequal illuminations suggest in terms of
the earlier provocation to loosen attachments to wounds?
