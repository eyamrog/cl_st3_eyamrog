Little is known about the behaviors of the Neanderthals who
lived in the eastern part of their range. By studying
the material from Chagyrskaya in a multidisciplinary context, we are
able to explore if, and to what degree, their lifeways
and subsistence patterns varied from those in western Eurasia .
Characterizations of Neanderthal diet more generally have been of recent
interest because some have proposed that dietary differences may have
contributed to Neanderthals being replaced by modern humans ( Fabre
et al., 2011 ; Power and Williams, 2018 ).
