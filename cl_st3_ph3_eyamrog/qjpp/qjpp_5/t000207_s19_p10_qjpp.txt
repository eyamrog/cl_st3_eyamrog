For duration of terrestrial events, the contrast between island and
mainland sites, adjusted for party size and behavior, is −0.2,
with an SE of 0.4. A 95% credibility interval for
the contrast is then (−1.0, 0.6). The null value zero
is contained in the interval, so the hypothesis that average
durations of terrestrial events are the same in island and
mainland sites—party size and behavior held equal—is not rejected.
