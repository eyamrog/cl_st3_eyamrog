The model estimates ( Fig. 3 B) echo the empirical
record of party size, with means slightly higher for Coiba
and Jicaron than for GP and BCI. The credibility interval
for SNP (having only 5 detection events) is wide compared
with the other sites, and so, this site effect should
be interpreted with caution. Across all sites, most observations consisted
of one or two individuals. Thus, there is little evidence
that mean party size varies with the presence or absence
of predators.
