Paleoanthropologists have looked to the fossil record for evidence about
when infant helplessness in humans and the need for high
levels of parental and alloparental care may have evolved (
Halcrow et al. 2020 ). This area of study has
involved looking at aspects of skeletal anatomy that are correlated
with one of the traits linked to helplessness, such as
birth mechanism, brain advancement factor, or dental development. 5
