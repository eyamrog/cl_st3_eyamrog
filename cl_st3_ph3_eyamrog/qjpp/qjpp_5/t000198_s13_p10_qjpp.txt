In this section, investigations into when, where, and how humans
and primates interacted during the Pleistocene-Holocene reveal patterns of interconnections
in regions where primates occurred and where they were not
naturally present. Primates were socially and culturally constructed differently by
humans in various places, while imageries of primates were exchanged
across long distances. Thus, there is both ample evidence of
primate depictions in ancient material culture and biological evidence of
primates in the global zooarchaeological record.
