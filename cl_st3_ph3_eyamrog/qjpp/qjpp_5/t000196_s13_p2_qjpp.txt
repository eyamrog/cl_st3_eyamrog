Stone tools found from these early archaeological sites are Mode
1, chopper-chopping tool industry, using quartz as the material. Mode
1 chopper-chopping tools are associated with processing bones for marrow
from a scavenging adaptation, in contrast to Mode 2 handaxes,
which are associated with butchering and processing meat from big-game
hunting. Mode 1 technology continued in Asia for a long
time, even after Modes 2 and 3 tools were made.
Debates about the different stone tool sequences and what they
mean in terms of adaptive strategies are beyond the scope
of this review. Interested readers can refer to a review
by Bar-Yosef & Wang (2012) .
