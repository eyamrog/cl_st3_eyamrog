Data processing and statistical analyses were performed using R v.
3.4.4 ( R Core Team, 2016 ). To investigate species
and sex differences during ontogeny of HPA axis activity in
young chimpanzees and bonobos, we fitted a linear mixed model
( Baayen, 2008 ) with Gaussian error structure (‘age variation
model’). The dependent variable was the SG-corrected cortisol concentration (ng/ml
SG) of urine samples. We included as test predictors population
(coded with three levels to differentiate between LuiKotale bonobos, Taï,
and Budongo chimpanzees), sex, age, and their two- and three-way
interactions. As we anticipated a weaning-related peak in cortisol levels
for both species, we included the cubic function of age
(and thus also the linear and quadratic functions) to effectively
model cortisol levels across ontogeny.
