Mammoth ivory was also a medium for personal ornaments in
the Gravettian, although on a smaller scale, and was dominated
by teardrop shapes and basket forms also found in Bavaria
and Poland ( Vercoutère and Wolf, 2018 ). During the
Gravettian, mammoth ivory remained the preferred raw material for carving
personal ornaments, although its use in general in the Swabian
Jura decreased ( Wolf et al., 2016 ; Münzel et
al., 2017 ) and was subsequently replaced by other faunal
elements, such as animal teeth ( Camarós et al., 2016
).
