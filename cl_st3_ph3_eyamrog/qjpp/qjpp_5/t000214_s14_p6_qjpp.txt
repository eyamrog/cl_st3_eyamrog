We obtained the dates of births of the subjects’ younger
siblings and dates on which the two females themselves gave
birth from colony records. Dates are accurate to the day
for all births that took place before 2006 (end of
data collection by Setchell), after which dates of birth are
accurate to the week, based on the first observation of
the new infant by CIRMF staff, the known gestation period
of mandrills (175 days; Setchell et al., 2002 ) and
female cycling data.
