The transition to protostatehood could be made only when some
of these petty kingdoms were able to achieve a military
superiority over their rivals for long enough to involve themselves
directly in building dikes and draining marshes. The higher productivity
thereby made possible triggered in its turn a need both
for increased corvee or slave labor and for a permanent
bureaucracy located in a central capital. ( Runciman 1982 ,
p. 364)
