Hodgson (2014) questions whether iconicity (typically regarded as the most
fundamental kind of semiotic ground) might not in fact be
preceded by indexicality. After all, an indexical ground can be
based on simple copresence, whereas an iconic ground requires the
recognition of similarity and is thus more abstract. This position
is supported by the observation that iconic figural representation appears
to postdate early examples of indexical signs such as those
of Blombos Cave ( Hodgson & Pettitt 2018 ).
