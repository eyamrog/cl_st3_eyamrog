In this review, I discuss how the hygiene hypothesis fits
within this broader framework of life history theory (LHT) and
ecoimmunology. I first discuss the core principles that link environmental
exposure to immune plasticity and immune-related disease. I then discuss
how biotic, abiotic, and multigenerational factors feed into these mechanisms.
Finally, I discuss how these factors lead to disease primarily
through two pathways: dysregulated inflammation and mistargeted immune responses. Figure
