Life history allocation problems are central to understanding plasticity in
immune responses. Energy budgets are constrained, at least over long
periods of time ( Pontzer 2018 , Thurber et al.
2019 ), and immune responses are costly. For example, fighting
an infection raises basal metabolic rates ( Lochmiller & Deerenberg
2000 , Muehlenbein et al. 2010 ), and immune responses
often necessitate reductions in other costly allocations, such as growth
and reproduction ( Blackwell et al. 2010 , McDade 2003
, Sheldon & Verhulst 1996 , Urlacher et al. 2018
).
