Here, we present a study of ocher residues on personal
ornaments from the Central European Aurignacian. The personal ornaments are
beads carved from mammoth ivory from the sites of Hohle
Fels and Vogelherd in the Swabian Jura. The sites found
here have yielded some of the earliest evidence (ca. 43
ka) for the arrival of anatomically modern humans into central
Europe ( Conard and Bolus, 2015 ). Our main goal
is to determine if the residues on the Aurignacian ivory
beads contain iron oxide mineral phases (ocher). Our secondary goal
is to then investigate the iron oxide content of the
associated archaeological sediments to determine whether the residues result from
postdepositional processes. If the sediments are devoid of iron oxides,
the most likely interpretation is that the residues are the
result of anthropogenic actions.
