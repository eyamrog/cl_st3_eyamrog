Although no hominin fossil has been discovered in the archaeological
sites discussed above, Gongwangling, Shaanxi (Site 15), a fossil hominin
site, is in the vicinity of Shangchen, dated to as
old as 1.62 Ma based on the paleomagnetic dating of
loess-paleosol sequences ( Zhu et al. 2015 ). The new
name given at first, Sinanthropus lantianensis , does not signify
a new species (see below). The Gongwangling cranium shows traits
consistent with the later Homo erectus specimens in Asia—namely, a
long and low frontal, thick supraorbital torus, and thick cranial
bones. The cranial capacity estimated at 780 cc is similar
to that of another early hominin sample of similar date
and latitude outside Africa, Dmanisi, Georgia (Site 6), with cranial
capacity estimates in the range of 546–730 cc ( Lordkipanidze
et al. 2013 ).
