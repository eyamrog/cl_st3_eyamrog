One contrast involves differences in concealment. Consider the light of
The Illumination in comparison with another proxy for wounds, the
scar. Medical anthropologists have often pointed to human scars as
evidence of the nefarious forces of structural violence. Scholars of
nonhuman animals have shown that scars can illuminate the scope
of biopolitics and its injurious forces ( Blanchette 2020 ,
Parreñas 2018 , Rosenberg 2017 ). But different from the
light of wounds, scars can be hidden (sometimes). In The
Illumination, the light cannot be concealed.
