All the specimens were inspected and photographed with a binocular
lens up to 80× of magnification. For the samples from
Axlor, Santa Catalina and Castillo C, the preservation of the
fossils prevented any manipulation, so the study was performed at
their respective museum facilities. For the specimens from El Castillo
isolated teeth, Las Caldas and Tito Bustillo, it was possible
to perform high-resolution replicas of the teeth and then, to
the study of dental modifications with a Leica DVM6 Digital
Microscope (Leica Microsystems, Heerburgg, Switzerland) at the EvoAdapta Group facilities
of the University of Cantabria.
