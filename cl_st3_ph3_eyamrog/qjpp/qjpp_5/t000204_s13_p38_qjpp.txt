Although there are some positive examples of collaboration in ways
that do not replicate colonial science ( Kemp et al.
2007 , Moreno-Mayar et al. 2018 , Travis 2010 ),
some have called for a much deeper disciplinary reflection and
revision to genomic methods and ethics ( Bardill 2014 ,
2018 ; Claw et al. 2017 ). Although genetics can
be only one line of evidence, because of its perceived
clarity and apparent scientific certainty, it can come to dominate
cultural affiliation determinations under NAGPRA ( Beckenhauer 2003 , Callaway
2015 , Mayes 2010 ).
