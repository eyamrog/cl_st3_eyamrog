Thus this review queries attachments to wounds retrospectively, currently, and
also speculatively to imagine what it might mean to loosen
ethnographic attachments to wounds in order to better understand contemporary
body politics. This exercise is not intended to be prescriptive.
Wounds should not necessarily be a new target upon which
we should focus our sights, nor should wound culture necessarily
be the stuff of new grant proposals and fieldwork projects.
Instead, the review considers wounds as a bellwether of ethnographic
relations. It considers wounds as attachment points for ethnographic curiosity.
It asks that we pause and ask how and why
wounds effect such magnetism and that we consider loosening some
attachments to wounds in order to see them anew.
