The fact that the MSA archaeological occurrence at the Ravin
Blanc I can be considered dating to an early humid
stage of MIS 5 on solid grounds raises several questions.
MIS 5 is seen as a period of behavioral changes
in the evolution of H. sapiens in other parts of
Africa where archaeological data are more abundant for this period,
and the impact of the changing climatic conditions of MIS
5 on human behavior is a frequently addressed topic. At
the scale of West Africa there is no other dated
site relevant to MIS 5 and proposing a human evolution
scenario based on the Ravin Blanc I site alone is
currently not straightforward. However, some hypotheses are discussed below.
