The final estimates are given in Figure 7 . For
RB1, the age estimates range between 124 ± 12 ka
and 128 ± 12 ka. For RB2 and RB3 the
minimal estimates are 99 ± 12 ka and 102 ±
10 ka, respectively, while the higher estimates go up to
137 ± 17 and 148 ± 15 ka, respectively. Since
the age of RB2 and RB3 are necessarily younger than
RB1, it can be concluded that RB1 (SU1) was deposited
at the beginning of stage 5, during MIS 5e (130–123
ka), while RB2 (SU2) and RB3 (SU3) were deposited sometime
between the beginning of stage 5 and 92 ka, probably
between MIS 5d (123–109 ka) and MI 5b (96–87 ka).
