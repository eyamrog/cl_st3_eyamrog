The first scenario involves a late persistence of the Acheulean
during most of the Middle Pleistocene followed by transitional industries
as captured at the Ravin Blanc I site at the
beginning of MIS 5, before the full development of the
‘Mousteroid’ MSA. In this case, the Ravin Blanc I assemblage
would show a much-reduced emphasis on bifacial pieces in terms
of technicity and abundance compared with the Acheulean, possibly compensated
by the development of new tool types such as the
pieces with large notches, and an increased importance of flake
production through low-standardized reduction methods.
