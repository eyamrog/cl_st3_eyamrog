A preliminary study of the micromammals from the site revealed
an inventory similar to that of Lufeng ( Storch and
Qiu, 1991 ; Storch and Ni, 2002 ), which is
dated between 6.9 and 6.2 Ma ( Dong and Qi,
2013 ), but containing more derived species of murine, bamboo
rats ( Miorhizomys ), beavers, and the large mole shrew
Anourosorex, all suggesting a slightly younger age for Shuitangba than
Lufeng.
