Thus far, I have been discussing how European scientific racist
ideas were applied to Indian caste groups during colonialism, and
much like the narratives of my predecessors, this story has
been dominated by European men. But, as I proposed in
the introduction, the racial science of India did not consist
of merely the application of already formed ideas to a
foreign human laboratory; it was also a vehicle through which
Western racial ideas were tested, refined, and then reapplied to
Europeans.
