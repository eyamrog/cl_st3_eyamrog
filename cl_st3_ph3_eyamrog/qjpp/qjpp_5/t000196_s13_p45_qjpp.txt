Modern humans in Asia, therefore, have come to existence from
a dynamic admixture with local populations, rather than originating as
a new single species from Africa. A model that posits
Homo sapiens as a new species replacing every population (outside
Africa) without any genetic exchange is refuted; admixed populations contributed
to the evolution of modern humans. The mixture of archaic
and modern features can be explained by ancestry (or introgression,
if they are two different species).
