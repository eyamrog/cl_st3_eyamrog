An alternative way to investigate how predators shape primate behavior
is through natural experiments, by comparing systems with and without
predators. van Schaik and van Noordwijk (1985) , for example,
used this approach to link a release from felid predation
to changes in the social structure in the crab-eating macaque
( Macaca fascicularis ) in Southeast Asia. Macaques on the
island of Simeulue, where no cats are found, lived in
smaller groups and had smaller home ranges than their counterparts
living on Sumatra, where a variety of felids are found.
