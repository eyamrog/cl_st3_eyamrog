Tam Pa Ling (Site 41), a limestone karst cave site
in northern Laos, yielded hominin fossils argued to be modern
humans ( Demeter et al. 2012 ). A partial cranium
and several mandibles have been reported. The partial cranium has
modern human morphology, while the mandibles show a mixture of
archaic and modern features in a robust mandible with a
chin ( Demeter et al. 2015 ). The date of
70–46 ka (OSL) is supported by U/Th-series dates ( Demeter
et al. 2012 , 2015 ).
