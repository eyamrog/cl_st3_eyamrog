Toothpick grooves Toothpick grooves are modifications located on the mesial
and distal surfaces between two adjacent teeth, commonly premolars and
molars, and, at a lower frequency, on incisors and canines
( Formicola, 1988 , 1991 ; Frayer, 1991 ; Ravy
et al., 1996 ; Ungar et al., 2001 ; Estalrrich
et al., 2017b ). The surface of the grooves shows
numerous parallel buccolingually oriented microstriations ( Grine et al., 2000
; Ungar et al., 2001 ; Hlusko, 2003 ; Bouchneb
and Maureille, 2004 ; Lozano et al., 2013 ; Estalrrich
et al., 2017b ).
