The Antiquities Act, which was intended to protect the cultural
resources of the United States by creating a permitting process
for archaeological excavation and establishing punishments for looting, in effect
further reified the authority that anthropologists held over Native American
material culture, including human remains.…In effect [the Antiquities Act] turned
Native American human remains into archaeological resources and the property
of the federal government. ( Daehnke & Lonetree 2011 ,
p. 90)
