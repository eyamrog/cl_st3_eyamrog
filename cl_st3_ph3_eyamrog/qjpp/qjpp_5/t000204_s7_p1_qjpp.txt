US President George H.W. Bush signed into law the Native
American Graves Protection and Repatriation Act (NAGPRA) on November 16,
1990, marking a quiet start to a law that was
the result of decades of vigorous debates and a law
that had the potential to radically transform America's art, anthropology,
and natural history museums ( Bray 2001 , Fine-Dare 2002
, Killion 2008 , Mihesuah 2000 ).
