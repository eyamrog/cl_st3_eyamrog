Previously, a signature of recombination was detected in partial mitochondrial
sequences from M. nemestrina from Borneo and Sulawesi macaques (
Piganeau et al., 2004 ; Tsaousis et al., 2005 ).
To evaluate whether this signature was present in the mitochondrial
genomes studied here, we analyzed these data using nine methods
(RDP, GENECONV, Bootscan, Maxchi, Chimaera, SiSscan, PhylPro, LARD, 3Seq) as
implemented by the Recombination Detection Program ( Martin and Rybicki,
2000 ), version 4.97.
