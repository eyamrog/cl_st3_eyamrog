Knight explores one museum's register (its record of acquisitions) to
show how it navigated a repatriation claim in the 1960s.
In so doing, she determines that the register allows us
to “witness the confrontations and resolutions within museums” throughout the
repatriation process ( Knight 2017 , p. 45). Indeed, historians
and historians of science will find rich archival data sets
with which to analyze NAGPRA-related work in the coming years.
