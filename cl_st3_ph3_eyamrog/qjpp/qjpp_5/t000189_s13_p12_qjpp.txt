Last, the potential host range of SARS-CoV-2 is exceptional. Mammalian
vascular endothelium carries high levels of the SARS-CoV-2 mandatory receptor
for cellular entry, ACE2. ACE2 is central to stabilizing blood
pressure, and its conserved sequence in mammals likely explains the
large known and potential host range of SARS-CoV-2. So far,
more than 20 mammalian species have been confirmed as hosts
of SARS-CoV-2 and can potentially serve as host pools from
which competitive variants can spill back and forth to and
from humans, creating a persistent threat to human health, animal
conservation, and agricultural industries ( Figure 2 ). These combined
characteristics make SARS-CoV-2 not only a more successful zoonotic virus
than SARS-CoV but a greater threat to human health than
most currently known circulating viruses.
