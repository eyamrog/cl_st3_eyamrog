The aim of this paper is thus to follow up
on the trends and developments established in previous work by
exploring the patterns of intensification in eighteenth century medical writing,
using Late Modern English Medical Texts (LMEMT; Taavitsainen et al.
2019 ), which contains a large collection of texts representing
different areas of medicine. Methodologically, the chosen approach is motivated
by two specific ideas from recent historical corpus research: that
specific sub-register differences are crucial in studying historical language use
( Biber & Gray 2013 ), and that intensifiers are
often intricately linked to the lexical items they modify and
should therefore not be studied separately from them ( Wagner
2017 ).
