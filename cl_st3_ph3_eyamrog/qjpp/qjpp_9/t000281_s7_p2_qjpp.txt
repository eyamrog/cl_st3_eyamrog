This paper adopts a corpus-based approach to explore one of
the above-mentioned peripheral adjective intensifying options; namely, what Huddleston and
Pullum (2002 :561) label “intensificatory tautology” (e.g., “ big huge
house,” “ tiny little bird”). My aim is twofold: to
chart the diachronic development of tautological intensification in English and
to contribute to a wider understanding of the role of
collocation in processes of intensifier-formation.
