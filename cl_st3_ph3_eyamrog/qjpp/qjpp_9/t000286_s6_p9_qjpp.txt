Need -passives occupy a very small niche of English grammar.
This study has revealed that the small space is a
complex one, with a richer profile of variation across Englishes
than has previously been recognized in dialectology, sociolinguistics, and micro-syntax,
as well as in major grammars. I have argued that
low-frequency features like need -passives are naturally subject to such
mis- or under-analysis because linguists’ tools are not well-suited for
studying them. While my approach in this paper was simply
to collect enough naturalistic language to examine a low-frequency feature
along the lines of higher-frequency features, I hope the approach
will foster additional creativity and innovation in methods to study
low-frequency features. The enriched account of need -passives provided by
this work illustrates the possibilities for enriching descriptions and theories
of English grammar, language variation and change, and the language
faculty more generally through intensive attention to low-frequency features.
