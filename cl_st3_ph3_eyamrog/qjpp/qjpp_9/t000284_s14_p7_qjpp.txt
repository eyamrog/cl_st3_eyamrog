Because back vowels tend to be more fronted after coronal
sounds (e.g., /t/), the lexical sets of goose , foot
, and goat were each split into two allophones based
on whether the preceding sound was a coronal consonant. 3
The labels we assign for the postcoronal allophones are toot
, took , and toe , respectively, and for the
elsewhere allophones, they are boot , put , and boat
. We retain the use of the labels goose ,
foot , and goat when referring to the vowel class
as a whole rather than specific context-dependent allophones of those
vowels.
