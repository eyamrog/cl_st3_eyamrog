As can be seen, despite the differences of meaning and
grammatical patterning between the three amplifiers, they in fact share
many of the frequently co-occurring (italicized) adjectives. A case in
point is great : despite being the adjective with the
highest value of attraction across the board, its occurrence is
not particularly dependent on any one of the three amplifiers,
as shown by the comparatively low value for reliance in
all three plots.
