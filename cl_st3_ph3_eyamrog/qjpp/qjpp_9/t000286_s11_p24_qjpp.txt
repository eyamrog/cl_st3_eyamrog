This places research on need +ED, on need -passives more
generally, and potentially on other low-frequency features in a paradox.
Because they occur infrequently in natural-language corpora, these features may
be examined through the elicitation of conscious judgments. However, (perhaps
because they occur infrequently) conscious judgments of these features may
