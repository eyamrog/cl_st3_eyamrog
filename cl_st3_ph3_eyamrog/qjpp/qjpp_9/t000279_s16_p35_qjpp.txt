The reported distributions might be looked at with respect to
the concepts of “powerless” versus “powerful” language introduced by O’Barr
(1982 :61-86), where the powerless style is characterized by a
high frequency of intensifiers and hedges. While the higher use
of hedgy little might characterize witness and victim styles as
powerless, the lower use by the professionals is offset by
their higher use of intensifying maximizers, thus producing a mixed
powerless/powerful picture. This together with problems inherent in O’Barr’s concept
(e.g., problematic category definitions, neglect of contextual microfunctions) clearly needs
more investigation (cf. Claridge, Jonsson & Kytö forthcoming ).
