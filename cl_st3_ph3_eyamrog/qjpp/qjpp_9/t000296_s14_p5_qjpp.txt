To compare learners’ make + noun collocations with those of
English native speakers (hereafter, ens ), make + noun collocations
were extracted from icnale ( Ishikawa, 2013 ), which categorises
native speakers into three groups: ens others, ens students and
ens teachers. According to Ishikawa (2018) , ens others are
not English teachers, but their English may represent natural usage.
ens students, meanwhile, are college students in English-speaking countries whose
English is suitable for comparison with learners of about the
same age. Finally, ens teachers are experienced teachers whose English
could be regarded as an ideal model for English learners.
