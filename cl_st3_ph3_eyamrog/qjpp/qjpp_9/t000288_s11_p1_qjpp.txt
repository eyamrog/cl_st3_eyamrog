The present study takes usage-based Construction Grammar as its theoretical
framework. Following Goldberg (2013 :17), constructions are form-meaning pairings at
different levels of abstraction displaying different degrees of idiomaticity, ranging
from individual words to abstract patterns (see Table 1 ).
At higher degrees of schematicity, constructions have functional slots that
can be partially filled or completely open; the benefactive and
transitive constructions are thus highly schematic constructions which are not
