The proportions reported in Figure 4 confirm previous characterizations of
need +ED as a regional grammatical feature of Belfast, Scotland,
Newcastle, and the US Midland (e.g., Strelluf 2020 ). They
also reveal variation across Englishes in need +ING, with these
constructions occurring in greater proportions in the English North particularly
and in England and Wales more generally. They show need
+TO to be overwhelmingly preferred in a range of Englishes
that includes Cape Town, Islamabad, and New Delhi, as well
as most US cities.
