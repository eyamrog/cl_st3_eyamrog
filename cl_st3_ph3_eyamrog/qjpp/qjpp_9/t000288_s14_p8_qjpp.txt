frequency per variety). In sampling the material, great care was
taken to exclude non-regional material, such as hits from the
Tokyo Reporter in the original Singapore concordance. One general strategy
in sampling the data was to give preference to regional
news outlets (e.g., the Daily Post , a Laos-based Nigerian
newspaper) rather than those that cater to a wider audience
(e.g., The News Guru , which—while based in Laos—self-advertises as
“Africa’s number one news portal”). The two types of online
publication are generally distinguished by a domain name which is
regional and one that ends in .com. This is not
to say that there is no local news copy produced
for an outlet such as Nirametrics.com .
