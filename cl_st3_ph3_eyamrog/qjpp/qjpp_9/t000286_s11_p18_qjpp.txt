N eed -passives are described in sociolinguistic and dialectological literature
as occurring infrequently in natural-language corpora. Murray, Frazer, and Simon
(1996 :258), for instance, relied on conscious judgments from respondents
on the acceptability of need +ED sentences as “a pragmatic
decision based on the great difficulty we had in eliciting
large quantities of information about [ need +ED] through more
traditional atlas-type methods or through relatively brief periods of free
conversation.”
