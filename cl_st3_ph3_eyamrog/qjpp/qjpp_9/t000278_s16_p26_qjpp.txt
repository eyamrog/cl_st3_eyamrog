This section complements the preceding information on type and token
frequency by looking at productivity, “the likelihood that a construction
will apply to a new item” ( Bybee 2010 :94).
Productivity has been studied in the morphological domain more than
any other ( Baayen & Lieber 1991 ; Baayen 1992
), and increasingly with regard to morphosyntactic constructions as well,
in order to assess their degree of “entrenchment” ( Langacker
1987 :59-60) or of grammaticalization (see, e.g., Coussé, Andersson &
Olofsson 2018 ).
