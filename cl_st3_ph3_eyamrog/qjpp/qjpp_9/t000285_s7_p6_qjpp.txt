The paper proceeds by considering previous accounts of negative tag
variation in English ( section 2 ) and the mechanisms
of grammaticalization and their relevance to negative tag variation (
section 3 ). Details of the data and methods used
in the current study are given in section 4 .
Section 5 presents the results of the quantitative variationist analysis.
Section 6 presents discussion of the findings and is followed
by the conclusion in section 7 .
