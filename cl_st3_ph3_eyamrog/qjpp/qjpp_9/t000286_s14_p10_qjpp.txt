A small set of interesting but irrelevant need constructions were
erroneously tagged as containing need +ED or need +ING. In
(22) and (23), need means “never.” Elsewhere, need occasionally took
a non-passive non-finite complement headed by a progressive verb (24)
or plain form (25). Such constructions were excluded. (22) We
are need drinking ever again. (Edinburgh; tagged as need +ING)
(23) I’ve need had a pigeon poop on me. (Minneapolis;
tagged as need +ED) (24) That needs going in the
rubbish. (Manchester; tagged as need +ING) (25) He need put
more swing in the hips. (Philadelphia; tagged as need +ED)
