The first construction could be more accurately described as NA
(stage) , whose meaning would be ‘the noun is modified
by an adjective referencing stage-level properties.’ It is the only
pattern analyzed which clearly prefers stage-level adjectives, and this fact
alone would be reason enough to see NA as a
construction different from both ANA and ANCA, regardless of whether
the latter two are treated together (cf. Fischer 2012 )
or separately (cf. Haumann 2003 , 2010 ; Pysz 2009
). The corpus data suggest that Fischer’s (2000 , 2001
) proposal for NA is at least partly well-founded: the
adjective’s verbalness, understood as its clearly adverbial interpretation or a
reference to an “appropriate action involving the thing” ( Fischer
2001 :273n3, after Vendler 1967 :175), is difficult to argue
with in the majority of the examples.
