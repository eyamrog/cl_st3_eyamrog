Research questions addressed in this paper are: 1) What are
the challenges in data preparation for archiving hard-to-access, sensitive textual
data, particularly around anonymisation coding? 2) Given the adopted solution
of creating a separate corpus for archiving, how do the
differences between the two versions of the WiSP corpus affect
findings? 3) To what extent do the issues raised and
solutions found problematise conventionally-accepted principles in corpus building?
