To date, researchers have not agreed on a definition of
the term ‘collocation’ and have rather attempted to define it
by using different approaches (e.g., Sinclair, 1991 ; Cowie, 1994
; Hill, 2000 ; and Nesselhauf, 2005 ). Nesselhauf (2005
: 12) defined collocations ‘as a type of word combination,
most commonly as one that is fixed to some degree
but not completely’.
