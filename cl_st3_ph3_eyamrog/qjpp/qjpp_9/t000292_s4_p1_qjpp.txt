The corpus comprises 4,448 debates, divided into thirteen legislatures (see
Table 1 ), each of which may contain up to
four legislative sessions. The average number of utterances per debate
is 305.12 (with median 273) and the standard deviation is
194.38. The maximum number of utterances in a single debate
is 2,104. There are, on average, 95.20 words per utterance
(median of 19 and standard deviation of 198.30), with the
longest utterance having 16,558 words.
