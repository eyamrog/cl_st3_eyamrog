the BNC showed that in PDE run (107 occurrences) is

(17 occurrences). These findings are in accordance with the data
in Matsumoto (2016 :156, 160) and Broccias and Torre (2018
:89), who list come , go , and run as
the three verbs most frequently attested in their samples, as
also with Goldberg’s (2006) observations quoted in section 2. 5
The discussion that follows, which proceeds in chronological order, will
therefore focus only on those three predicates; semantically, come and
go are path verbs coding the deictic, one of the
three components of path in Talmy’s (2000 :II, 53) formulation,
while run is a general verb of manner of motion.
