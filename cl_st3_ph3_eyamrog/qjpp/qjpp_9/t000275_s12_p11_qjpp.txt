These previous studies show that both micro-analysis and digital CA
of online discussion are useful in revealing discourse practices among
users in their interactions. Therefore, conducting either analysis on top
of corpus analysis might reveal further the interactional functions of
the keywords found. In short, to effectively conduct a corpus
analysis of online discussions with an interactional approach, a corpus
tool or procedure that allows exploration of various aspects of
online interactions, including different variables (e.g., topics of discussions, role
of internet users, types of postings) and units of analysis
(e.g., subforums, threads, individual postings, words) is needed. In the
following, I show how I utilized CWB to compile and
explore a 12-million-word corpus of threaded online discussions. I elaborate
on how the tool can be used to facilitate a
synergy of corpus analysis and interactional approach in addition to
what has been proposed by O'Keeffe & Walsh's (2012) CLCA
approach.
