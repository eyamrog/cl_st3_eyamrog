Korea lags a little behind some other Asian countries like
Japan and China in the compilation of learner corpora and
learner corpus research. There is a pressing need for Korean
academics to keep abreast with the latest developments in this
field. The availability of high-quality learner corpora will no doubt
promote the collaborative research of scholars and lead to the
development of new teaching and learning methods. It is hoped
that this survey will provide some inspiration and insights for
compiling learner corpora and fostering the academic motivation to embark
on learner corpus studies in Korea.
