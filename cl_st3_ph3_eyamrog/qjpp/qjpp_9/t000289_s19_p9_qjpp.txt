Editorials are fewer in number but usually greater in length
than news reports, which means that they are likely less
diversified in terms of authors. Consequently idiosyncratic uses may play
a greater role in the editorial data. As mentioned in
section 3 , editorials from 1939 and 1968 are never
signed, and many in the SAVE subcorpus are not either,
which renders more precise considerations of individuations impossible.
