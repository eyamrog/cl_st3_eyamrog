I deleted any tweet that was sent as identical or
nearly identical text from a single handle (i.e., cases where
a single author tweeted basically the same text more than
once). I did not sample tweets that Twitter classified as
retweets. However, in cases where more than one author tweeted
very similar text as an original message from their own
handle, I kept these in the dataset. I made this
decision because an author’s re-broadcasting of a tweet creates ownership
over the message that is not present in a retweet
(e.g., it appears on Twitter as a message from their
handle), and because it would have been possible for authors
to edit a need -passive construction if they had objected
to it.
