Other studies on the receptive collocational knowledge of Japanese efl
learners (e.g., Murao, 2004 ; and Yamashita and Jiang, 2010
) have produced similar results to Noji and Kishida’s (2016)
. Thus, previous studies on Japanese learners’ receptive collocational knowledge
have corroborated that L1 influence continues to dominate learners’ receptive
collocational knowledge although they acquire incongruent English collocations with Japanese
as their proficiency levels increase.
