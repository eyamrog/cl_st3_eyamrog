By contrast, adjectives which most rely on very for their
occurrence include troublesome, painful , and slight (as in 14
and 15). These occur in descriptions of patients’ symptoms and
the degrees of discomfort. The frequency of slight is increased
by the fact that it also regularly occurs in another
context, namely in descriptions involving a high degree of precision.
In this sense it behaves in a similar way to
fine , which is also frequently modified by very in
the data (as in 16).
