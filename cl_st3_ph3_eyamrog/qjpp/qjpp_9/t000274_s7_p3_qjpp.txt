The paper discusses the methodological, ethical and epistemological considerations around
anonymising a relatively small corpus (1 million words) of sensitive
texts, considering institutional access issues, our values and commitments as
researchers to participants (including social workers, service users and third
parties such as health visitors or clients’ family members) and
the imperative to archive the resulting datasets. One significant solution
adopted for WiSP was the creation of two corpora: ‘WiSP
Corpus 1′ for use by the research team and 'WiSP
Corpus 2′ for deposit in the grant funders’ repository: ESRC
ReShare archive.
