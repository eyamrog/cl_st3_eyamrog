Tenny (1998 :593) and Edelstein (2014 :260) claim that need
+ED cannot take a passive complement with a non-reversive un
- prefix. Because non-reversive un - can only affix to
adjectives, its non-occurrence in the AEP provides further evidence that
need +ED passive complements are always verbal. Tweets support this
analysis. There are no need +ED or need +ING tweets
where the passive complement has a non-reversive un - prefix.
