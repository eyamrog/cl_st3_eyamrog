Many health care advisors advocate eating virgin/organic coconut oil for
it's health benefits even though it is high in saturated
fats. Coconut oil contains lauric acid, which is a medium-chain
fatty acid, that converts to monolaurin. Monolaurin is the compound
found in breast milk that strengthens a baby's immunity, and
a great deal of research has been done to establish
the ability of lauric acid to enhance immunity. This medium-chain
fatty acid (MCFA) actually disrupts the lipid membranes of offending
organisms such as yeast, fungal and bacteria living in our
gut. This is the main reason why I consume it.
