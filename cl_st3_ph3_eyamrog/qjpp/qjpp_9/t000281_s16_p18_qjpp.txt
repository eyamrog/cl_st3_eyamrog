Two other functions are, however, recorded in PDE. First, as
has been suggested in previous literature, emphasizers are “epistemic markers”
( Paradis 2000 :233; see also Quirk, Greenbaum, Leech &
Svartvik 1985 :429). They cut across noun modifier and intensifier
functions in their emphatic attribution of a speaker-based judgement on
to a nominal referent. Syntactically, emphasizers are attributive-only elements and,
unlike noun modifiers, do not accept degree modification (as they
themselves act as specifiers of degree). While not very frequent,
emphasizer uses are attested among the size -cluster examples, as
in (12)-(14). The baby in (12) does not refer to
a “large-size infant” but to an adult who the speaker
is not very sympathetic towards. In this context, great big
works with the noun to convey both degree emphasis and
negative affection. Similar emphatic negative readings can be obtained from
(13), whereas (14) works the opposite way, i.e., with whacking
great conveying the speaker’s emphatic positive appraisal of the alibi,
rather than a qualification of its (metaphorical) dimension (see González-Díaz
2018 ).
