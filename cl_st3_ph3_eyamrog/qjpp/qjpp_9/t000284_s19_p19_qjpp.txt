It is tempting to think that this stability in formant
curves is an artifact of modeling, and that a more
flexible statistical model is required. However, recall that in the
model specification, each combination of gender, generation, vowel, and formant
was fit independently; the best fit formant curves were not
constrained by a particular location in the F1-F2 space nor
were they forced to conform to a particular shape by
gender or vowel. In other words, the predicted values for
the Lost Generation women’s boat were not tethered to any
other generation, gender, or allophone, and the same holds for
all other levels of the four-way interaction term. Thus, the
similarities among formant trajectories predicted for each vowel are not
due to any bias or constraints in the model.
