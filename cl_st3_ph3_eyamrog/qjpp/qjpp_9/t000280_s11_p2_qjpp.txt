The register of medical writing has been the focus of
some historical studies. Pahta (2006a) studied texts representing Late Middle
English, finding that full is the main intensifier followed by
right, so, all , and well , and the study
by Méndez-Naya and Pahta (2010 :194) documented the dramatic rise
of very towards the beginning of the Early Modern English
period and the concomitant decline of the main intensifiers in
the Middle English period.
