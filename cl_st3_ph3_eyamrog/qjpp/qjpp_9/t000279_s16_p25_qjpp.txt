Figure 5 presents an overview of the results of the
analysis, a summary of how the usage rate of adverb
little as a downtoner or durative varies across time and
as a function of the socio-pragmatic parameters, the speakers’ role,
class, and gender. Each pane in the four-pane panel displays
the unique contribution of the predictor (e.g., time), holding the
other variables constant, i.e., set to their average values. Please
note that the quantities in the figure are model-based estimated
rates (per 100,000 words) for each condition, while previous graphs
(e.g., Figure 1 ) have shown normalized frequencies that compound
all conditions. The error band/bars in Figure 5 show 50-percent
and 95-percent credible intervals, reflecting the statistical uncertainty in the
estimates.
