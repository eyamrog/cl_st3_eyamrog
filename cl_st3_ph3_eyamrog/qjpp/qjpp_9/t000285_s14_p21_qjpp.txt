they differ in one key respect: challenging tags are impatient
or even aggressive ( Millar & Brown 1979 :43; Algeo
1990 :448). Although Pichler (2013 :193) found no challenging tags
in her data, they have been attested in other studies
( Algeo 1988 , 1990 ; Tottie & Hoffmann 2006
). In (22), Sasha states that dominoes players use “them

really have known this fact. Charlotte’s response is said with
increased pitch to convey annoyance at Sasha’s previous challenge. (22)
