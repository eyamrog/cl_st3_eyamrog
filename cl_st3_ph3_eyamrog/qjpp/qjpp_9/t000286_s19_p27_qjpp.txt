Edelstein’s (2014) indicators of the validity of her unique AEP
derivation are upheld for need +ED in tweets. Moreover, these
indicators appear to be present for need +ING too. For
some language users, at least, the passive complement in need
+ING appears to hold a tighter syntactic relationship to the
matrix verb than does the passive complement in need +TO.
This mirrors Edelstein’s (2014) analysis of need +ED syntax.
