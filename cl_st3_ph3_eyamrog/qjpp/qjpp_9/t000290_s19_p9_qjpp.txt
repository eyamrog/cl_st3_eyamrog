To summarize the main findings regarding static mid-point vowel positions:
(i) Women’s /æ/ vowels are lower and more retracted than
men’s (ii) European /e/ is higher than Mennonite /e/ (iii)
Filipino /e/ is higher and more advanced than Mennonite /e/
(iv) Coda /k/ correlates with lowered /æ, ɛ/, and raised
/e/ (v) Coda /ɡ/ correlates with raised and more advanced
/æ, ɛ/, and raised /e/ (vi) Coda /ŋ/ correlates with
raised and more advanced /æ/ (vii) B ag and beg
show substantial overlap, but there does not appear to be
a three-way merger with bagel
