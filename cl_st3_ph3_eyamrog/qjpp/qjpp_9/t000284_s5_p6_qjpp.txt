Our analysis reinforces existing transcriptions of Southern vowels while contributing
additional phonetic detail about their trajectories. There is change in
apparent time for boot , took , boat , toe
, and thought , and stability in toot , put
, and lot . When comparing the relative positions of
the shifting vowels to the stable vowels, which we assume
occupy the same approximate position in perceptual space across genders,
women appear to have led the changes.
