Another group of adjectives which Bech (2017 :12) quite regularly
(sixty-four instances) finds in prenominal stacks are adjectives denoting type
or origin, which she describes, after Quirk, Greenbaum, Leech, and
Svartvik (1985) , as the “least adjectival and most nominal”
of all adjectives, so they essentially form compounds with their
nouns and as such might not have required a conjunction,
as in (48).
