As discussed in section 2, Goldberg (2006 :52) argues that
the VV ing OBL pattern is a construction in the
sense of CxG, that is, a form-meaning pairing whose overall
event meaning can be glossed as ‘move in a manner
along a path.’ The corpus-based study presented here enables us
to refine this characterization and propose that the construction codes
‘deictic motion along a path, which takes place simultaneously with
a manner of action involving manner of motion proper, concurrent
result, or concomitance.’
