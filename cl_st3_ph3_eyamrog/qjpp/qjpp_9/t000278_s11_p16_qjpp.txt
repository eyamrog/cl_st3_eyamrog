As happened with manner of motion verbs, the inventory of
clink verbs in English has expanded considerably. Thus, Fanego (2017
:52-53, 58-61) lists thirty-seven new clink verbs dating from 1500
onwards, such as fizz (1685), patter (1611), spatter (1586), splash
(1699), swish (1756), thump (1563), whizz (1547), zip (1852).
