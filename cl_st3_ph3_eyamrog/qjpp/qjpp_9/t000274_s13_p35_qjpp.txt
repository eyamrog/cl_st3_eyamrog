Within our research team of three, we held differing views
over the extent to which the data should be anonymised
for archiving. After extensive discussion, we decided to create two
versions of the corpus: a full version for the research
team's use which included engagement with participants about the use
of any data extracts, and a version for archiving which
was effectively a stand-alone corpus (henceforth respectively ‘Corpus 1′ and
‘Corpus 2′).
