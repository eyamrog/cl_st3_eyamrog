Turning to boat , Figure 4 shows that all three
generations of women had different realizations of the vowel. The
Lost Generation of women had the highest vowel while the
G.I. and Silent/Boomer Generations used a lower variant. The G.I.
group also had the most diphthongal realization with an offglide
somewhat further back than the other groups, though this is
not retained in the youngest generation. Therefore, the bulk of
the change in boat lowering by women occurred between the
Lost and the G.I. Generations, or roughly around 1900.
