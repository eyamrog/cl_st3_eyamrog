Data extraction was based on six shell nouns ( fact
, problem , thing , truth , trouble , and
word ) that were attested with a text frequency of
at least ten instances in the press section of COCA
(see Hundt & Oppliger 2022 ). The retrieval algorithm made
use of the shell noun directly followed by the copula
in the present tense. The resulting concordance files were randomized
and manually post-edited to exclude false positives.
