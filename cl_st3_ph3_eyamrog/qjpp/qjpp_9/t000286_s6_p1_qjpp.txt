This study has revealed need -passives to be a complex
system of inter-variety variation in Englishes around the world. It
has recast need +ING as a regional syntactic variant that
is distinctive to England and Wales in the same way
that need +ED is distinctive to Northern Ireland, Scotland, and
the US Midland. It has also united most Englishes in
North America, India, Pakistan, and South Africa in an overwhelming
preference for need +TO.
