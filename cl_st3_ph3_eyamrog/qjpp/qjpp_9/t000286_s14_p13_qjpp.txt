The need +TO sample included tweets where the complement was
a participial adjective rather than a verb. Because need +ED
prohibits adjectival complements (see discussion of Tenny [1998] and Edelstein
[2014] above), need +TO tweets with adjectival complements were excluded.
Among the most frequently excluded complements were done with ,
concerned , gone , lit (“drunk,” “fun”), married , and
worried .
