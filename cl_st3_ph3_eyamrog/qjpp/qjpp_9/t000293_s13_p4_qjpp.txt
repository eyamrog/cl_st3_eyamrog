The Gachon Learner Corpus ( glc ) was collected from
2012 through 2014 at Gachon University in Korea ( Carlstrom
and Price, 2012 –2014). The texts were collected using twenty
topic questions. Participants were asked to write about one of
the topics in Google Forms using 100 to 150 words
per text. This corpus is available in two versions: Version
2.1 and the Final Version. Version 2.1 released in 2013
contains 1,609,517 words from 16,113 texts produced by 1,607 participants
and the Final Version released in 2015 consists of over
2.5 million words from 25,073 texts produced by 2,500 participants.
All versions are freely downloadable online in text or Excel
format from the website. 7
