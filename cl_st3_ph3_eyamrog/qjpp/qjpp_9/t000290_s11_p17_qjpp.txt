Unlike most Mennonites, in the case of Filipino-Manitobans ethnic identity
can also pertain to phenotypic differences, as mentioned by another
participant: “I would say I’m Canadian-Filipino, I would be Canadian
first ‘cause I feel like that’s my identity, like I
am Filipino background, but in terms of culture I’m more
Canadian but my skin is just brown so I’m Filipino”
(LIPP interview #212).
