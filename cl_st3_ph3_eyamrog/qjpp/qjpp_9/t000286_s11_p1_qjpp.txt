This section provides context for the dialectological, sociolinguistic, and micro-syntactic
exploration of need -passives. I describe previous research on the
distribution and syntax of need +ED, need +ING, and transitive-
need . I do not provide focused discussion of need
+TO because, in the context of need -passive variation, need
+TO has primarily been positioned as a standard alternative to
need +ED. The section concludes with discussion of low-frequency features
and the exigency of examining them.
