An undergraduate research assistant transcribed the conversation section of the
interviews, beginning ten minutes into the recording in order to
ensure the collection of casual speech data and continuing for
fifteen minutes. I transcribed the reading passage, word list, and
minimal pair tasks. 5 The transcriptions and audio were then
forced aligned using the FAVE program ( Rosenfelder et al.,
2014 ). A customized script was used to extract F1
values at 40 percent of the vowel in Praat (
Boersma & Weenink 2017 ). Formants were Lobanov normalized (
Lobanov 1971 ) and rescaled using the overall mean and
overall standard deviations of the speaker set analyzed in Duncan
(2018) . 6
