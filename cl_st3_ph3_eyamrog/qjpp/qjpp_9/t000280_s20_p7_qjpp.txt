Like the analysis presented in section 4.1, this part relies

Figures 3 - 5 show how the frequencies of so
( Figure 3 ), very ( Figure 4 ), and
too ( Figure 5 ) vary as a function of
time, using LOESS scatterplot smoothers and 95 percent confidence intervals
(grey ribbon); Pearson correlation coefficients are also displayed for each
category. The smoother represents the relationship between the two variables
(year and normalized frequency) visually, and the Pearson correlation coefficient
(R) shows the strength and direction of linear correlation between
them (values range from -1 to +1).
