Following the implication of complementarity in Murray, Frazer, and Simon
(1996) , Doyle (2014 :104) searched for tweets containing the
strings needs to be done , needs done , and
needs doing . He mapped locations of tweets from the
continental United States that contained these three strings and found
that the need +TO string “is acceptable in most locations,”
whereas [ need +ING] “is strongest in the areas where
[ need +ED] is not used,” providing empirical evidence that
need +ING and need +ED are complementary variants in the
United States ( Doyle 2014 :104-105).
