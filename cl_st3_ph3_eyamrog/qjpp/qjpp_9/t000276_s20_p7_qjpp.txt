The results suggest that the postnominal adjectives in NA and
ANCA belong to different categories: the former are predominantly stage-level,
while the latter are almost exclusively individual-level, these differences being
statistically significant (chi-square = 235.203, p < .00001). The third
pattern, ANA, is closer to ANCA, at least in terms
of the adjectives it allows into the postnominal position: the
preference is rather for individual-level adjectives. Examples follow in (19)-(21).
