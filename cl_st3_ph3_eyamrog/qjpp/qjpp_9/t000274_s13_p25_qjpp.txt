Initially, coders replaced direct identifiers such as names and locations,
choosing to either manually replace words with codes, or use
the ‘find and replace’ feature in MS Word. The latter
was occasionally problematic as, for example, replacing all instances of
the name ‘Reg’ with [PERSON] also changed ‘regarding’ to ‘[PERSON5]arding’,
and similarly ‘Aila’ changed ‘available’ to ‘av[PERSON2]ble’. In each case,
this rendered the individual service user's name recoverable from the
text.
