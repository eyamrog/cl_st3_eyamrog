These two measures provided a visual representation of the differences
in the use of individual adjectives together with the three
amplifiers in focus. “Attraction” on the horizontal axis shows the
token frequencies relative to the total number of amplifier +
adjective pattern: in Figure 6 , for example, great is
shown to be the most important filler of the adjective
slot with a proportion of 7.3 percent of total instances
of the pattern, ten times larger than the attraction score
for extensive (0.62 percent). By contrast, “Reliance” measures to what
extent the use of an adjective depends on the intensifier:
the reliance score for great in Figure 6 is 3.4
percent (meaning that 3.4 percent of the instances of great
in the corpus are preceded by so ), which is
much lower than the score for extensive (13.3 percent).
