The 1034 negative tag tokens within the variable context were
coded for various factors that were deemed likely to affect
the choice of variant and which will facilitate the analysis
of how grammaticalization mechanisms operate in the negative tag systems
of different English dialects. The first of these coded factors
is paradigmaticity, which refers to the extent to which a
tag agrees in person, number, and gender features with the
anchor clause subject ( Andersen 2001 ; Pichler 2013 ).
Lack of agreement between the tag and the anchor clause
can indicate decategorialization as it shows “invariabilization,” whereby a form
becomes less restricted in its syntactic distribution within a paradigm
( Andersen 2001 :98). Non-paradigmatic innit , shown in (16),
is common in London teenagers’ speech ( Andersen 2001 ;
Cheshire, Kerswill & Williams 2005 ; Torgersen, Gabrielatos, Hoffmann &
Fox 2011 ; Pichler 2021a ). (16) I was talking
to you earlier on innit (COLT; Stenström 1997 :141)
