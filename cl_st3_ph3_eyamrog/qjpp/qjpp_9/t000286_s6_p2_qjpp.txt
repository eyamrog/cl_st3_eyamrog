These results call for reanalysis of previous research on need
-passives, especially in the United States. They also open space
for new investigations. Need +ING should be brought squarely into
the fold of British dialectology. Newcastle bears examination as a
space where all three need -passives appear to be on
a fairly equal footing. And the limited data in this
study for Englishes outside the United States and United Kingdom
suggest that need -passives should be examined more broadly as
a variable across global Englishes.
