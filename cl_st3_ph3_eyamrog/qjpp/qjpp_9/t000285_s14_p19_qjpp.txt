Epistemic tags are used to “reduce speakers’ commitment to their
propositions and seek verification of these propositions from addressees” (
Pichler 2013 :187). These tags therefore function as information-seeking devices.
This function is consistently attested in previous literature, albeit with
varying labels ( Millar & Brown 1979 ; Cheshire 1981
; Algeo 1990 ; Tottie & Hoffmann 2006 , 2009
). Example (20) shows the use of an epistemic tag—in
this case, innit . Speaker 00-G1-m04 introduces someone named Bolan,
to which 00-G1-m03 suggests “Aye, Sam Bolan, innit ?,” seeking
verification. (20) 00-G1-m04: Know that wee Bolan? 00-G1-m03: Aye, Sam
Bolan, innit ? 00-G1-m04: Jim 00-G1-m03: Jim (Glasgow)
