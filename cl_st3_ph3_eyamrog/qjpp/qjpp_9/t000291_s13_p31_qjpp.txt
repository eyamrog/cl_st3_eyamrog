To summarise, representations of women as weak and subject to
discrimination in gkis appears to be a rhetorical strategy that
seeks to elicit moral outrage in readers, and to inspire
them to become involved in the left's global political struggle
against state-sponsored violence and patriarchy. Women are associated with children
as both are portrayed as weak and helpless (but more
on strong women later). Women are also portrayed as suffering
because of the care they provide to their children.
