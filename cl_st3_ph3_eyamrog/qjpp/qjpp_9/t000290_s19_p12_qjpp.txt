Looking across vowels , /æ/ (the intercept) trends towards longer
durations than the other two vowels in nearly every case
(see Figure 5 ). This difference is significant in comparison
with both /e/ and, more substantially, /ɛ/ (the briefest of
the three vowels) before /k/ and other codas. Before /ɡ/,
the difference in duration between /æ/ and the other vowels
is substantially reduced and does not reach the level of
significance for /e/. Only before /ŋ/ does no significant durational
difference occur at all (between /æ/ and /ɛ/).
