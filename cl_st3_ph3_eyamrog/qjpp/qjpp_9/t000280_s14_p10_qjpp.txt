The analysis follows a corpus-pragmatic approach, which integrates “vertical” reading
associated with concordances and frequency data, and “horizontal” reading associated
with qualitative pragmatic analysis of individual examples in context (
Rühlemann & Aijmer 2015 ). Vertical reading captures the main
trends in the material and allows generalizations to be made,
while horizontal reading enables the investigation into how individual surface
forms are linked to specific contextual configurations and textual meaning
expressed in medical texts from this period.
