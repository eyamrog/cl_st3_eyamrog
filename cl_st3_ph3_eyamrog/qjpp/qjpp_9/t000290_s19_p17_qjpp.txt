To summarize the main findings regarding vowel trajectories: (i) F1
and F2 trajectory differences are generally minimized before voiced velar
codas /ɡ, ŋ/ (ii) F1 and F2 trajectory differences are
generally maximized before voiceless velar /k/ (iii) F2 trajectories of
/ɛ/ and /e/ fully overlap before /ɡ/ (iv) F2 trajectories
of /ɛ/ and /æ/ fully overlap before /ŋ/ (v) F1
and F2 trajectories of /æ, ɛ/ show full or near-full
overlap between both voiced velar coda /ɡ, ŋ/ contexts (vi)
F1 and F2 trajectories of /e/ are most divergent before
/ɡ/
