Finally, our results fill in several gaps in a description
of SAE. First, to our knowledge, this study is the
first analysis to focus on the dynamics of back vowels
in the US South collectively, especially in the area covered
by DASS. The second gap we fill is temporal: this
study has uncovered detail about back vowel formant dynamics in
Southerners born in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries.
By filling in our knowledge of the phonetic past, we
can better understand the phonetic present, and anticipate the phonetic
future.
