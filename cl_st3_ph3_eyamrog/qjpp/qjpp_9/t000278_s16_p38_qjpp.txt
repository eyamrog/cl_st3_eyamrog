The parameter of desemanticization ( Lehmann 1995 :127-128), also often
termed semantic bleaching, can be seen at work in processes
of auxiliation (i.e., the development of lexical verbs into auxiliaries),
and relates to the degree in which one of the
verb forms involved is bleached of lexical meaning. It applies
very clearly, for instance, to the VV ing sequence discussed
in section 2 as modality go (“Don’t go behaving like
that to poor Freddy!”), where the motion verb go has
been stripped of its spatial dimension to develop into an
auxiliary of evaluative modality. By contrast, in the VV ing
OBL construction, come and go retain their spatial, propositional meaning
so that in terms of desemanticization they cannot be said
to have grammaticalized.
