The ESRC ReShare archive ( https://reshare.ukdataservice.ac.uk/ ) features three levels
in which data collections can be deposited: open access (anyone
can download), ‘safeguarded’ (data users must be registered) and ‘controlled’
(unavailable, though potential users can contact the data controller). Searching
in the ESRC ReShare archive indicates that of the 7000+
data collections, just 31 are tagged as a corpus (search
conducted in April 2021). Of these, 12 are open access
(including BAWE), 15 are ‘safeguarded’, of which just two require
permission from the data controller (one of these is WiSP).
A further three are not available ‘due to legal, ethical
or commercial contracts’ with only metadata and documentation such as
interview protocols and descriptions of the method deposited in the
archive (allowed by funders under particular circumstances).
