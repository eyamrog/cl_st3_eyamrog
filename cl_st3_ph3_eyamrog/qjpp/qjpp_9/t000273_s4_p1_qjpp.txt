This study has examined patient feedback on NHS cancer care
in England, comparing written comments from patients who have been
categorised according to the length of time from their first
treatment. For this, we grouped the patient feedback into three
categories reflecting treatment duration: under 1 year, 1-5 years, and
over 5 years. First, we explored the satisfaction rates of
each of these groups by comparing what proportion of each
group gave each of the satisfaction scores between 0 and
10 in their feedback. Patients in each group gave ratings
in approximately the same proportions, with around 86% of patients
who provided comments giving scores of at least 8, regardless
of the amount of time they have been receiving treatment.
While the highest rating of 10 was the most popular
score for patients at all treatment stages, those who have
received treatment for less than 1 year were the most
enthusiastic, being around 4% more likely to give this highest
score than the other groups. This suggests, then, that patient
satisfaction rates are very high overall and have remained relatively
stable for patients across the duration of their treatment, at
least during the period under study (2015-2018).
