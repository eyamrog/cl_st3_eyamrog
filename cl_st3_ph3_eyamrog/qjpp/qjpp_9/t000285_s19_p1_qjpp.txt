Having outlined the coding of internal and external factors, this
section turns to the quantitative analysis, firstly presenting distributional results
which provide insight into the four mechanisms of grammaticalization and
their relevance to negative tag variation: erosion ( section 5.1
), decategorialization ( section 5.2 ), and desemanticization and extension
( section 5.3 ). The relative impact of factors on
the variation will be examined in mixed-effects logistic regression models
in section 5.5 .
