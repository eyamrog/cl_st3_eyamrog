Analysis of formant dynamics requires the consideration of multiple formant
measurements per vowel token. We argue that this technique contributes
to our understanding of phonetic and sociolinguistic variation in human
speech, and we use it to illustrate diachronic change in
Southern US vowel systems. We use measurements from five time
points across each vowel token, and we apply generalized additive
mixed-effects models (or GAMMs; Wood 2017a ) to nearly 90,000
back‐vowel tokens.
