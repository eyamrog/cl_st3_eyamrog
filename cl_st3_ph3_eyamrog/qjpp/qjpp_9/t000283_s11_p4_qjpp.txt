In sum, very little empirical evidence substantiates claims of either
merged or separate vowels for words such as fir ,
fern , and fur in Scottish English: the nurse merger
and social stratification in the realization of pre-rhotic vowels in
Scottish English are understudied. The present investigation aims to begin
to fill this gap with a large-scale analysis of SSE
that examines both the realization of the individual vowels in
