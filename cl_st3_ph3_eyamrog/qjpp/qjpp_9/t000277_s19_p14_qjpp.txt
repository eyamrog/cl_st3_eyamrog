There is a layer of complexity here as the pronoun
actually refers to a character in the film Shortbus named
Justin Bond , not the real-life Justin Bond who portrays
said character; nevertheless the pronoun is used for an individual
as opposed to a generic referent. However, the acceptability of
using s/he to refer to someone who is transgender is
highly questionable, and further investigation indicates that v is Bond’s
preferred pronoun ( Steel 2011 ). Overall then, close analysis
of the antecedents of woman-first combined pronouns has shown no
strong preference for (feminine) gender-stereotyped antecedents.
