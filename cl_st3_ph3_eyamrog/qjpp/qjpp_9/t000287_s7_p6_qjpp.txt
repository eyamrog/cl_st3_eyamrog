This paper proceeds as follows. In section 2 , I
review background literature on the start - north merger in
St. Louis and elsewhere, as well as merger reversals more
generally. Section 3 introduces the sampling methodology, while section 4
reports results that show the start - north merger to
be a near-merger and its reversal to be socially motivated.
Section 5 discusses how the results show a role for
perceptual salience in merger reversal.
