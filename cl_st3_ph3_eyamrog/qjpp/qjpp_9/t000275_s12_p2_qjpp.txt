Many researchers have utilized corpus linguistics methodology that affords both
quantitative analysis and qualitative analysis to investigate the big textual
data in online discussions ( Drasovean and Tagg, 2015 ;
Hunt and Harvey, 2015 ; McDonald and Woodward-Kron, 2016 ;
Paterson, 2020 ; Sotillo and Wang-Gempp, 2016 ). For example,
by investigating collocates of the keywords referring to the main
topics in the respective discussions, that is anorexia and politicians
, Hunt & Harvey (2015) and Sotillo & Wang-Gempp (2016)
provided insights into users’ experience and conceptualization of the topics,
while Drasovean & Tagg (2015) revealed language practices that establish
online communities.
