This section applies syntactic tests from Tenny (1998) and Edelstein
(2014) to tweets. In the case of need +ED, these
tests check whether productions in Twitter align with Tenny’s (1998)
and Edelstein’s (2014) (primarily) judgment-based data. In the case of
need +ING, the tests check whether need +ING follows a
similar set of syntactic constraints to need +ED.
