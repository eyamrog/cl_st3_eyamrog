We refrained from using a more fine-grained distinction of rhotic
realizations because it would have made it difficult to achieve
sufficient interrater reliability (as in Dickson & Hall-Lew 2017 ).
Following this method, we achieved good interrater reliability of about
89 percent between the first and the second rater for
identification of /r/ variants. In case of disagreement between the
two raters, a third rater, also phonetically trained, made the
final decision. The auditory analysis was complemented with a visual
spectrographic analysis using Praat ( Boersma & Weenink 2017 )
for less clear tokens or when in doubt.
