In the lower (left) panel of Figure 3 , the
tendency for both /ɡ/ and /ŋ/ to induce raising and
advancement of /æ/ relative to non-velar other codas is very
clear, and contrasts markedly with /k/ which has a diametrically
opposed pattern showing lowering and some retraction relative to the
other category. In the upper right panel, /ɛ/ exhibits a
similar pattern as /æ/ albeit with a reduction in magnitude
of positional difference between codas. (As mentioned before, tokens of
/ɛ/ before /ŋ/ were very rare in the data and
should not be relied upon too heavily; this is apparent
in its uneven density distribution here.) The preceding patterns strongly
contrast with those for /e/ as depicted in the upper
left panel, which shows a much more cohesive arrangement across
all coda types.
