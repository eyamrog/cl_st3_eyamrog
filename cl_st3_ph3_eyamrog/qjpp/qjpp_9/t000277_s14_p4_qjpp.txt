To determine the antecedents of the combined pronouns, all of
the hits returned by the query were downloaded as concordance
lines with a span of fifty words either side. Manual
analysis of the hundred-word co-text was enough to match each
combined pronoun to its antecedent. In all of the corpora,
there were only two erroneous hits where the query results
were not combined pronouns but were actually two different pronouns
referring to separate entities (as in 4).
