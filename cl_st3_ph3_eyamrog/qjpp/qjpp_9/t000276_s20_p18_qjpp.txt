These examples upset the “clear division of labor” that Haumann

Based on this tendency, the meaning of the ANA schema
could be ‘the adjectives describe the noun hierarchically.’ In a
sense, then, ANA seems to be functionally closest to AAN,
which Bech (2017 :15) has discovered to specialize in describing
hierarchical relations. Table 6 below compares the two patterns. Similarly
