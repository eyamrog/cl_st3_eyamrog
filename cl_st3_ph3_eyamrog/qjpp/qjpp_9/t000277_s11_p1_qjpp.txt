Historically, it has been argued that English does not have
a (formally-endorsed) gender-neutral third-person animate singular pronoun. Based on the
established third-person pronoun paradigm ( Figure 1 ), as printed
in grammar guides and taught in schools (in both L1
and L2 contexts), speakers of English must decide between he
and she when referring to any animate third-person singular referent.
In most cases this choice is unproblematic; if you know
the (binary) gender of the intended referent then you can
choose the matching pronoun.
